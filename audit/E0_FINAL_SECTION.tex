\subsection{E0: Schema Design from Natural Language Requirements}
\label{sec:e0}

\paragraph{Setup.}
E0 evaluates Text2Schema constructors that map a dataset-authored natural-language
requirement to a relational schema.
We use five constructors---CodeLlama-13B, Llama-3.1-8B, DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B,
Claude Sonnet~4.6 and GPT-5.4-mini---with a fixed Direct constructor prompt.
Each model receives only the gold NL text (never gold DDL) and emits MySQL-style DDL
with structured metadata.
RSchema contributes 518 logical references for Tables/Attributes/Entity/Referential
$F_1$, Hallucination and type-independent nGED, and 381 ID-matched genuine DDL
references for Domain $F_1$.
SchemaBench contributes 239 DDL-backed references for all five structural dimensions.
We report micro-averaged $F_1$ on $T,A,D,E,R$, the four-dimensional hallucination rate
\[
H=\tfrac14\sum_{d\in\{T,A,E,R\}} \max(0,P_d-M_d)/P_d
\]
(Domain excluded; $P_d{=}0\Rightarrow H_d{=}0$), and mean names-only nGED over tables,
table-qualified attribute names and directed foreign keys.
Failed or unparseable generations remain in the population as empty predictions
(structural matches zero; nGED${=}1$ on nonempty gold; Hallucination not forced to 1).

\paragraph{Requirements under-specify schema content.}
Table~\ref{tab:e0_overall} shows that even strong constructors recover tables more
reliably than domains or relationships.
On RSchema, GPT-5.4-mini attains the best nGED (0.553), Domain $F_1$ (0.223) and
Referential $F_1$ (0.225); Claude Sonnet~4.6 leads Tables (0.602) and Entity (0.407);
DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B is the strongest open-weight model on hallucination (0.595) and
Attributes (0.443), with nGED close to the closed models (0.561).
On SchemaBench, Claude Sonnet~4.6 is best on every reported column (nGED 0.574,
Hall.\ 0.598, and all five $F_1$ scores).
Across both datasets, Domain and Referential $F_1$ remain low relative to Tables
(maximum Referential $F_1$ is 0.263), which is consistent with NL requirements that
name entities more clearly than types, nullability and foreign keys.
No single constructor dominates every RSchema metric; SchemaBench is more
one-sided toward Claude.

\paragraph{Prompting baselines.}
Table~\ref{tab:e0_baselines} contrasts PaperMethods prompting with Direct on RSchema.
Direct rows are copied from the verified Table~\ref{tab:e0_overall} evaluation.
Archive micro-$F_1$ and Hallucination indicate that One-shot+CoT weakens structural
fidelity relative to One-shot, while Few-shot improves several $F_1$ columns among
open-weight models.
We do \emph{not} claim that archive Few-shot nGED is directly comparable with Direct
names-only nGED: baseline nGED remains a historical macro aggregate pending recovery
of per-schema PaperMethods trees.
Domain is reported as ``--'' for untyped prompting outputs.
