## Metrics (summary)

- Micro F1 over eligible populations (RSchema 518 structural / 381 Domain; SchemaBench 239).
- Hallucination: 4-dim micro mean over T/A/E/R (Domain excluded).
- nGED: structural `names_only` mean; empty prediction → 1.
- Domain: genuine typed gold with known nullability only; no synthetic VARCHAR gold.
- Coverage is tracked separately from scored population size (`COMPLETE` / `INCOMPLETE` / `BLOCKED`).
- Direct rows of Table VI equal Table V exactly.
