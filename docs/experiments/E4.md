## Pipeline

Gold DDL → mask one structural dimension (MT–MR) → Direct GPT-4o-mini reconstruction **or** Schema2Text (open describers) → GPT-4o-mini Round-trip → score vs gold with heuristic inferability labels.

## Metrics

- **Split:** Direct recall by inferable / non-inferable class.
- **Recovery:** unique inferable Gold N; Direct; RT (per-element mean over available describers); Retention = RT/Direct.
- **Domain:** joint type + nullability (paper). Legacy Exp5 `domain_facts` is historical only.
