\subsection{E5: Scalability and Cost}
\label{sec:e5}

\paragraph{Setup.}
We analyze computational efficiency and structural scalability using
\emph{existing} E0--E2 artifacts rather than a new full-corpus inference pass.
Structural size effects reuse E2 round-trips (describer $\rightarrow$ fixed
GPT-4o-mini constructor) stratified by schema size
(Small: ${\le}5$ tables and ${\le}50$ columns; Medium: not Small but
${\le}15$ tables and ${\le}150$ columns; otherwise Large).
Efficiency combines (i)~a DeepSeek-Coder basic-prompt sample of up to 40
schemata per dataset with archived reconstructions,
(ii)~a small DeepSeek Schema2Text latency remount ($N{=}5$ per prompt/dataset),
and (iii)~billed API pilots on two schemata
(GPT-5.4-mini basic/detailed; Claude Sonnet~4.6 basic; constructor GPT-4o-mini).
Pilot rows are never pooled with sample- or full-corpus averages.
Provider usage logs for archived E0/E2 runs are absent; open-weight inference
is reported as monetary N/A.

\paragraph{Results.}
Table~\ref{tab:e5_scalability} shows that referential fidelity declines with
schema size on SchemaBench under DeepSeek detailed descriptions
(Ref.\ F1 $0.864\rightarrow0.654$ from Small to Large; nGED
$0.324\rightarrow0.402$), while RSchema Small/Medium remain high
(Ref.\ F1 ${\approx}0.92$) with Domain F1 persistently low (${\approx}0.15$--$0.17$).
Table~\ref{tab:e5_cost} reports recoverable costs and latencies.
On the DeepSeek-basic sample, referential F1 is $0.887$ (RSchema, $N{=}40$) and
$0.674$ (SchemaBench, $N{=}38$ valid archives; two missing slots are excluded
rather than zero-scored).
Estimated GPT-4o-mini constructor cost is ${\approx}\$0.002$--$0.003$ per schema;
DeepSeek dollars are N/A.
API pilots bill ${\approx}\$0.002$--$0.003$ per schema for GPT-5.4-mini round-trips
and ${\approx}\$0.007$--$0.008$ for Claude basic round-trips on the same two
small schemata, with saturated fidelity (Ref.\ F1~$=1$, nGED~$=0$) that does
\emph{not} generalize to the corpus.
DeepSeek Schema2Text remounts show detailed prompts roughly $2$--$3\times$
slower than basic ($54.6$\,s vs.\ $120.2$\,s mean on RSchema, $N{=}5$).
Manuscript E0 wall-clock claims (e.g., CodeLlama $390.5$\,s/schema) could not
be matched to packaged logs and are omitted from Table~\ref{tab:e5_cost}.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
E3 constructor isolation and Enterprise-145 round-trip archives are unavailable
on disk; closed-model full-corpus costs remain unrecovered.
E0 direct-construction latency/\$ and E2 round-trip fidelity must not be read
as measurements from the same pipeline.
