This database schema, named rschema_0004, is used to store information about customers, their service plans, and their subscriptions. It contains three tables: Customer, Service_Plan, and Subscription_Record.
The Customer table stores information about each customer, including their address, customer number, email, name, and phone number. The customer number is the primary key and is unique for each customer.
The Service_Plan table stores information about each service plan, including the plan name, plan number, price, and service content. The plan number is the primary key and is unique for each plan.
The Subscription_Record table stores information about each subscription, including the customer number, plan number, subscription time, cancellation time, and operating personnel. The primary key for this table is a composite key consisting of the customer number, plan number, and subscription time. The customer number and plan number are foreign keys that reference the corresponding columns in the Customer and Service_Plan tables, respectively. The update and delete rules for these foreign keys are set to CASCADE, meaning that if a customer or plan is deleted, all related subscriptions will also be deleted.
In addition to the primary and foreign keys, there are no other indexes or constraints defined in this schema.
