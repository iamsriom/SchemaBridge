The meeting table has six columns: meeting_type, time, location, meeting_content, meeting_id, and meeting_topic. The meeting_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are not null.
The meeting_participation table has four columns: member_id, meeting_id, remarks, and attendance_time. The member_id and meeting_id columns are the primary key, and the other columns are not null.
