The rschema_0012 database contains three tables: Warehouse, Product, and Product_Circulation_Record. The Warehouse table stores information about warehouses, including their address, capacity, current inventory, and name. The Product table contains information about products, including their ID, inventory, name, and specification. The Product_Circulation_Record table tracks changes in product inventory and stores information about the date, operator, and quantity of changes. The Warehouse_ID and Product_ID columns in the Product_Circulation_Record table are foreign keys that reference the corresponding columns in the Warehouse and Product tables, respectively. The foreign key constraints ensure that the data in the Product_Circulation_Record table is consistent with the data in the Warehouse and Product tables. The schema also includes primary keys and unique constraints to ensure the integrity of the data.
