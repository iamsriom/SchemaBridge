This database schema, named rschema_0019, contains four tables: Customer, Dish, Order, and Order_Details. The Customer table stores information about customers, including their names and phone numbers. Each customer has a unique identifier, which is the primary key. The Dish table contains information about the dishes available for ordering, including their names and prices. Each dish has a unique identifier, which is the primary key. The Order table stores information about orders placed by customers, including the customer's identifier, the order's unique identifier, the order's status, the time the order was placed, the total amount, and the foreign key to the customer. The Order_Details table stores information about the dishes ordered, including the dish's identifier, the order's identifier, the quantity ordered, and the foreign keys to the dish and order. The primary key of the Order_Details table is a composite key consisting of the order identifier and the dish identifier. The foreign keys in the Order_Details table reference the Dish and Order tables. The Order_Details table also has a unique constraint on the composite key.
