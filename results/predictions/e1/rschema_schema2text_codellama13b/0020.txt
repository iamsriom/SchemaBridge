This database schema, named rschema_0020, contains three tables: Supplier, Ingredient, and Purchase_Record. The Supplier table has four columns: Address, Contact_Information, Name, and Supplier_ID, which is the primary key. The Ingredient table has four columns: Ingredient_ID, Ingredient_Name, Ingredient_Specification, and Price, with Ingredient_ID being the primary key. The Purchase_Record table has six columns: Ingredient_ID, Purchase_Amount, Purchase_Date, Purchase_ID, Purchase_Quantity, and Supplier_ID, with a composite primary key made up of Supplier_ID and Ingredient_ID. The Ingredient_ID and Supplier_ID columns are foreign keys referencing the Ingredient and Supplier tables, respectively. The Purchase_Record table also has a foreign key relationship with the Ingredient table, with the Ingredient_ID column referencing the Ingredient table's Ingredient_ID column.
