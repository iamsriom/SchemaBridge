The database name is rschema_0021, and it has three tables: Employee, Shift, and Schedule. The Employee table has columns for Date_of_Birth, Employee_ID (primary key), Employee_Name, Gender, Phone_Number, and Position. The Shift table has columns for End_Time, Shift_ID (primary key), Shift_Name, and Start_Time. The Schedule table has columns for Employee_ID (not null), Scheduled_Time, Shift_ID (not null), and primary key (Employee_ID, Shift_ID, Scheduled_Time). The Schedule table has foreign keys referencing the Employee and Shift tables. The foreign key constraints are set to CASCADE on update and delete.
