The rschema_0025 database is used to store information about tobacco products, departments, employees, production records, and production record details. The tobacco products table contains information about the products, including their ID, name, type, production date, shelf life, sales price, and production batch number. The department table contains information about the departments, including their ID, name, total number of people in the department, person in charge ID, and department responsibilities. The employee table contains information about the employees, including their ID, name, gender, phone, position, joining date, and department ID. The production record table contains information about the sales records, including the product ID, sales time, salesperson ID, customer name, sales quantity, sales price, customer phone, customer address, and sales type. The production record details table contains information about the production record details, including the production record ID, producer ID, and production record ID. The database also has foreign key constraints to ensure referential integrity, with the employee table referencing the department table, the production record table referencing the tobacco products table and the employee table, and the production record details table referencing the production record table and the employee table.
