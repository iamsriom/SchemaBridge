The database named rschema_0026 contains seven tables, each representing a different entity or relationship in the system. The Textile table stores information about the textiles produced by the company, including their name, price, production date, and type. The Department table contains information about the different departments within the company, including their contact information, department name, and manager. The Inventory table tracks the quantity of textiles in stock and the date they were stocked in. The Employee table contains information about the employees within the company, including their department, name, gender, phone number, position, and employee ID. The Production_Relationship table tracks the production process, including the producer, production time, and quantity produced. The Sales_Relationship table tracks the sales process, including the customer, quantity sold, sales personnel, and sales time. The Performance_Evaluation table contains information about the performance of each employee, including their evaluation date, performance ID, score, and employee ID. Foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables, ensuring data consistency and integrity.
