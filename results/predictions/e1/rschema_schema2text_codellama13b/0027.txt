The rschema_0027 database is used to store information about a clothing company's departments, employees, products, production records, and sales records. The database contains five tables: Department, Clothing, Employee, Production_Record, and Sales_Record.
The Department table contains information about the company's departments, including the department's contact information, department ID, department name, and person in charge. The Department_ID column is the primary key, and the Contact_Information, Department_Name, and Person_in_Charge columns are all strings.
The Clothing table contains information about the company's products, including the product's inventory, name, price, product ID, production date, and style. The Product_ID column is the primary key, and the Inventory, Name, Price, Production_Date, and Style columns are all strings.
The Employee table contains information about the company's employees, including their department ID, employee ID, gender, name, phone number, position, and foreign key to the Department table. The Employee_ID column is the primary key, and the Department_ID column is a foreign key that references the Department table.
The Production_Record table contains information about the company's production records, including the producer employee ID, product ID, production time, quantity, and foreign keys to the Employee and Clothing tables. The primary key is a composite key consisting of the Product_ID, Producer_Employee_ID, and Production_Time columns.
The Sales_Record table contains information about the company's sales records, including the customer, product ID, sales quantity, sales time, salesperson employee ID, and foreign keys to the Clothing and Employee tables. The primary key is a composite key consisting of the Product_ID, Salesperson_Employee_ID, and Sales_Time columns.
In summary, the rschema_0027 database is a comprehensive database that stores information about a clothing company's departments, employees, products, production records, and sales records. The database is designed to ensure data integrity by using primary keys, foreign keys, and composite keys to enforce entity and referential integrity.
