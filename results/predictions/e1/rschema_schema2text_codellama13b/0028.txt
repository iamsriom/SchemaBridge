The database schema "rschema_0028" is used to store information about a timber company. It consists of five tables: "timber," "department," "employee," "timber_procurement," and "timber_processing."
The "timber" table contains information about different types of timber, including their names, types, procurement prices, processing costs, and sales prices. The "timber_id" column is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each type of timber.
The "department" table contains information about the different departments within the company, including their names, supervisors, contact information, and department IDs. The "department_id" column is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each department.
The "employee" table contains information about the employees within the company, including their names, genders, phone numbers, positions, and department IDs. The "employee_id" column is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each employee.
The "timber_procurement" table contains information about the procurement of timber, including the type of timber, the quantity procured, the supplier, and the time of procurement. The "employee_id" and "timber_id" columns are used to link the procurement to the appropriate employee and timber type.
The "timber_processing" table contains information about the processing of timber, including the type of timber, the quantity processed, and the time of processing. The "employee_id" and "timber_id" columns are used to link the processing to the appropriate employee and timber type.
The schema also includes foreign key constraints to ensure referential integrity, which means that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate. For example, the "timber_id" column in the "timber_procurement" table references the "timber_id" column in the "timber" table, ensuring that the procurement of timber is linked to the appropriate timber type. Similarly, the "employee_id" column in the "timber_procurement" and "timber_processing" tables references the "employee_id" column in the "employee" table, ensuring that the procurement and processing of timber are linked to the appropriate employee.
