This database schema, named "rschema_0030," is used to store information about a company's products, raw materials, production records, inventory records, sales records, and employees. The schema consists of six tables: raw_material_table, product_table, production_record_table, inventory_record_table, sales_record_table, and employee_department_table.
The raw_material_table contains information about the raw materials used in the production of the company's products, including the raw material's ID, name, production date, and shelf life. The product_table contains information about the company's products, including the product's ID, name, composition, production date, shelf life, and price. The production_record_table contains information about the production process, including the product ID, quantity produced, producer, and production time. The inventory_record_table contains information about the inventory of products, including the product ID, stocking person, quantity, and stocking time. The sales_record_table contains information about the sales of products, including the product ID, quantity sold, salesperson, and sale time. The employee_department_table contains information about the employees in the company, including their ID, name, gender, phone number, department ID, and position.
Each table has primary keys and foreign keys to enforce entity and referential integrity. For example, the product_table has a primary key on the product ID column, and the production_record_table has a foreign key on the product ID column that references the product_table. Similarly, the inventory_record_table has a foreign key on the product ID column that references the product_table, and the sales_record_table has a foreign key on the product ID column that references the product_table.
The schema also includes indexes and constraints to optimize query performance and ensure data consistency. For example, the production_record_table has a composite primary key on the product ID, producer, and production time columns, and the inventory_record_table has a composite primary key on the product ID, stocking time, and stocking person columns. The sales_record_table has a composite primary key on the product ID, salesperson, and sale time columns.
Overall, this schema provides a comprehensive and well-organized structure for storing and managing the company's data, including information about products, raw materials, production records, inventory records, sales records, and employees.
