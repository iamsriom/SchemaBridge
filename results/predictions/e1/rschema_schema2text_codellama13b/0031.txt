The database name is rschema_0031 and it is used to store information about a moving company. It contains three tables: Moving_Team, Mover, and Moving_Task.
The Moving_Team table has two columns: Team_ID (primary key) and Team_Name. It stores information about the different teams that make up the moving company, such as their name and ID number.
The Mover table has seven columns: Age, Assigned_Team_ID (foreign key referencing Moving_Team), Gender, Name, Phone_Number, Worker_ID (primary key), and FOREIGN KEY (Assigned_Team_ID) referencing Moving_Team(Team_ID). It stores information about the individuals who make up the moving teams, such as their age, gender, name, and phone number.
The Moving_Task table has ten columns: Assigned_Team_ID (foreign key referencing Moving_Team), Customer_Feedback, Customer_Name, Customer_Phone_Number, Destination_Address, Moving_Cost, Moving_Date, Quantity_of_Items_Moved, Starting_Address, and Task_ID (primary key). It stores information about the moving tasks that the moving company has completed, such as the customer's feedback, name, and phone number, as well as the starting and destination addresses, moving date, and quantity of items moved.
The foreign keys in the Moving_Task table reference the primary keys in the Moving_Team table, ensuring that the tasks are assigned to the correct teams. The foreign keys also specify the update and delete rules, such as CASCADE or RESTRICT, which determine what happens to the tasks when the teams are updated or deleted.
In summary, the rschema_0031 database schema is used to store information about a moving company, including the different teams, the individuals who make up those teams, and the moving tasks that the company has completed. It includes primary keys, foreign keys, and other constraints to ensure data integrity and consistency.
