This database schema, named rschema_0037, contains three tables: Civil_Engineering_Project, Inspector, and Project_Quality_Inspection.
The Civil_Engineering_Project table contains information about ongoing or completed civil engineering projects. It has the following columns: Actual_Completion_Date, Expected_Completion_Date, Project_Budget, Project_ID (primary key), Project_Location, Project_Manager, Project_Name, and Start_Date. The Project_ID column is a unique identifier for each project.
The Inspector table contains information about individuals who inspect the quality of civil engineering projects. It has the following columns: Contact_Information, Inspector_ID (primary key), Name, and Position. The Inspector_ID column is a unique identifier for each inspector.
The Project_Quality_Inspection table contains information about the quality inspections performed on civil engineering projects. It has the following columns: Inspection_Content, Inspection_Date, Inspection_Result, Inspector_ID (foreign key referencing Inspector table), Project_ID (foreign key referencing Civil_Engineering_Project table), and a composite primary key consisting of Project_ID, Inspector_ID, and Inspection_Date. The composite primary key ensures that each inspection is unique and identifiable.
The foreign key constraints ensure referential integrity, ensuring that the Inspector_ID and Project_ID columns in the Project_Quality_Inspection table reference the corresponding primary keys in the Inspector and Civil_Engineering_Project tables, respectively. The foreign key constraints also specify the update and delete rules, which are CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
In summary, this database schema contains three tables that store information about civil engineering projects, inspectors, and quality inspections. The tables are linked through foreign key constraints, ensuring referential integrity and data consistency.
