This database schema, named rschema_0038, contains three tables: designer, customer, and project. The designer table has five columns: designer_id, designer_name, phone, email, and department. The designer_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are required and non-null. The customer table has four columns: customer_id, name, phone, and email. The customer_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are required and non-null. The project table has 13 columns: project_id, designer_id, customer_id, project_manager, project_name, start_date, estimated_completion_date, actual_completion_date, project_budget, design_plan_date, design_plan_name, design_plan_content. The project_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are required and non-null. The project table also has two foreign key constraints: project_ibfk_customer_id and project_ibfk_designer_id. The project_ibfk_customer_id constraint references the customer table's customer_id column, and the project_ibfk_designer_id constraint references the designer table's designer_id column. The delete and update rules for these constraints are CASCADE.
