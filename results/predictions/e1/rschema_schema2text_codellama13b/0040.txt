This database schema, named rschema_0040, is used to store information about real estate properties, salespeople, customers, sales contracts, and the properties sold by each salesperson. The schema consists of five tables: Property_Information, Salesperson, Customer_Information, Sales_Contract, and Salesperson_Sold_Property.
The Property_Information table contains information about real estate properties, including their addresses, areas, and sales prices. The table has five columns: Property_Address, Property_Area, Property_ID (primary key), Sales_Price, and Sales_Status. The Property_ID column is an integer that uniquely identifies each property, and the Sales_Price column stores the sales price of the property.
The Salesperson table contains information about salespeople, including their names, emails, and phone numbers. The table has four columns: Email, Name, Phone, and Salesperson_ID (primary key). The Salesperson_ID column is an integer that uniquely identifies each salesperson.
The Customer_Information table contains information about customers, including their addresses, emails, and phone numbers. The table has five columns: Address, Customer_ID (primary key), Email, Name, and Phone. The Customer_ID column is an integer that uniquely identifies each customer.
The Sales_Contract table contains information about sales contracts, including the date of signing, the price of the contract, and the specific content of the contract. The table has seven columns: Contract_Date, Contract_Price, Contract_Signing_Time, Contract_Specific_Content, Customer_ID (not null), Property_ID (not null), and Sales_Contract_ID (primary key). The Customer_ID and Property_ID columns are foreign keys that reference the Customer_Information and Property_Information tables, respectively.
The Salesperson_Sold_Property table contains information about the properties sold by each salesperson. The table has three columns: Sales_Contract_ID (not null), Salesperson_ID (not null), and a composite primary key consisting of Salesperson_ID and Sales_Contract_ID. The Sales_Contract_ID and Salesperson_ID columns are foreign keys that reference the Sales_Contract and Salesperson tables, respectively.
In summary, this schema stores information about real estate properties, salespeople, customers, sales contracts, and the properties sold by each salesperson. The schema is designed to ensure entity integrity, referential integrity, and domain integrity by using primary keys, foreign keys, and constraints such as NOT NULL and UNIQUE.
