The database schema "rschema_0042" is used to store information about a community, its residents, property managers, and maintenance records. The schema consists of seven tables: "community," "property_manager," "resident," "maintenance_record," "maintenance_personnel," "building," and "worker_handles_maintenance_records."
The "community" table stores information about the community, including its name, location, and property manager. The "community_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each community. The "property_manager_number" column is a foreign key that references the "property_manager" table.
The "property_manager" table stores information about the property managers, including their name, phone number, and email. The "property_manager_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each property manager.
The "resident" table stores information about the residents, including their name, phone number, email, and building number. The "resident_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each resident. The "community_number" and "building_number" columns are foreign keys that reference the "community" and "building" tables, respectively.
The "maintenance_record" table stores information about maintenance records, including the maintenance order number, community number, resident number, and maintenance content. The "maintenance_order_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each maintenance record. The "community_number" and "resident_number" columns are foreign keys that reference the "community" and "resident" tables, respectively.
The "maintenance_personnel" table stores information about the maintenance personnel, including their name, phone number, and email. The "maintenance_personnel_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each maintenance personnel.
The "building" table stores information about the buildings in the community, including their name and location. The "building_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each building.
The "worker_handles_maintenance_records" table stores information about the maintenance personnel who handle maintenance records, including the maintenance order number, maintenance date, maintenance cost, and maintenance personnel number. The "maintenance_order_number" column is the primary key and is used to identify each maintenance record. The "maintenance_staff_number" column is a foreign key that references the "maintenance_personnel" table.
In summary, the schema stores information about a community, its residents, property managers, maintenance records, and maintenance personnel. The schema uses foreign keys to establish relationships between the tables and ensure data consistency and integrity.
