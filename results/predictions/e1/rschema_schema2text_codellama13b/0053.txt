The rschema_0053 database is used to store information about a processing pool, including the pool's name, location, and the person responsible for its maintenance. The database contains four tables: processing_pool, staff, processing_pool_record, and processing_pool_maintenance.
The processing_pool table contains information about the processing pool, including its ID, name, location, and the ID of the person responsible for its maintenance. The table has a primary key on the processing_pool_id column, and a foreign key on the responsible_person_id column that references the staff table.
The staff table contains information about the people who work at the processing pool, including their name, phone number, email, responsibilities, and staff ID. The table has a primary key on the staff_id column.
The processing_pool_record table contains information about the processing capacity and efficiency of the pool, as well as the time and date of the record. The table has a primary key on the recording_staff_id, record_time, and processing_pool_id columns, and foreign keys on the processing_pool_id and recording_staff_id columns that reference the processing_pool and staff tables, respectively.
The processing_pool_maintenance table contains information about maintenance activities performed on the pool, including the time and date of the maintenance, the content of the maintenance, and the cost of the maintenance. The table has a primary key on the maintenance_staff_id, maintenance_time, and processing_pool_id columns, and foreign keys on the processing_pool_id and maintenance_staff_id columns that reference the processing_pool and staff tables, respectively.
In summary, the rschema_0053 database is used to store information about a processing pool, including the pool's name, location, and the person responsible for its maintenance. The database contains four tables that store information about the pool, the people who work there, and the maintenance activities performed on the pool. The tables are linked together using foreign keys to ensure data integrity and consistency.
