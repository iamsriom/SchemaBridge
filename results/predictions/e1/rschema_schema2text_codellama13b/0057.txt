This database schema, named rschema_0057, contains three tables: Equipment, Maintainer, and Maintenance_Record. The Equipment table stores information about the equipment, including its ID, installation date, location, model, and name. The Maintainer table contains the contact information, department, ID, and name of the maintainer. The Maintenance_Record table stores information about the maintenance performed on the equipment, including the equipment ID, maintainer ID, maintenance content, and date. The primary key of the Maintenance_Record table is a composite key consisting of the maintainer ID and equipment ID, and it has foreign keys referencing the Maintainer and Equipment tables. The foreign keys are set to cascade on update and delete, meaning that if the maintainer or equipment is updated or deleted, the corresponding records in the Maintenance_Record table will also be updated or deleted.
