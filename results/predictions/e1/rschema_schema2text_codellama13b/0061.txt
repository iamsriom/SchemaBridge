This database schema, named "rschema_0061," contains three tables: "patent," "inventor," and "patent_application_record." The "patent" table stores information about patents, including the patent number, name, application date, and status. The "inventor" table contains information about inventors, including their number, name, date of birth, gender, phone number, and position. The "patent_application_record" table links patents to their corresponding inventors, with each record containing the patent number, inventor number, and application date. The primary key for each table is indicated by the PRIMARY KEY constraint. The "patent_application_record" table also has a composite primary key consisting of the patent number, inventor number, and application date. Foreign key constraints are used to ensure referential integrity, with the "patent_application_record" table referencing the "patent" and "inventor" tables. The ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE rules ensure that if a patent or inventor is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the "patent_application_record" table are also affected.
