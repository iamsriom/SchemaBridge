The database name is rschema_0066, and it contains four tables: community, owner, house, and repair_application_record. The community table has columns for community_id, community_name, address, and manager, with community_id being the primary key. The owner table has columns for owner_id, name, phone, house_number, and community_id, with owner_id being the primary key and community_id being a foreign key referencing the community table. The house table has columns for house_id, house_name, and location, with house_id being the primary key. The repair_application_record table has columns for owner_id, house_id, application_time, repair_content, and repair_cost, with a composite primary key of (house_id, owner_id) and foreign keys referencing the owner and house tables.
