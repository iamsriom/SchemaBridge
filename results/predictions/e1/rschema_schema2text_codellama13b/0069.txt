The database name is rschema_0069, and it is used to store information about customers, rental items, and rental records. The database contains three tables: customer, rental_item, and rental_record.
The customer table has six columns: customer_id, customer_name, customer_phone, customer_age, customer_gender, and customer_address. The customer_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are required and cannot be null.
The rental_item table has six columns: rental_item_id, item_name, item_category, single_rental_fee, total_inventory, and remaining_inventory. The rental_item_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are required and cannot be null.
The rental_record table has seven columns: customer_id, rental_item_id, rental_start_time, rental_end_time, number_of_rented_items, rental_payment_fee, and rental_record_id. The primary key is a composite key consisting of rental_start_time, customer_id, and rental_item_id. The customer_id and rental_item_id columns are foreign keys that reference the customer and rental_item tables, respectively. The ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE clauses specify that if a customer or rental item is deleted, any related rental records should also be deleted or updated accordingly.
