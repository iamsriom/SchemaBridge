This database schema is called rschema_0071 and it has three tables: mine, safety_officer, and safety_inspection_record. The mine table has five columns: mine_number, mine_name, mine_location, person_in_charge, and a primary key constraint on mine_number. The safety_officer table has six columns: safety_officer_number, name, gender, telephone, position, and mine_number, with a primary key constraint on safety_officer_number and a foreign key constraint on mine_number that references the mine table. The safety_inspection_record table has four columns: inspection_date, inspection_content, inspection_results, and inspector, with a primary key constraint on mine_number and inspection_date and a foreign key constraint on mine_number that references the mine table.
