The rschema_0086 database contains seven tables, each representing a different entity or relationship in the system. The Material table stores information about the materials used in the procurement process, including their description, ID, name, type, and unit. The Region table contains information about the regions in which the companies are located, including their ID and name. The Country table stores information about the countries in which the companies are located, including their code, ID, name, and tax ID. The Procurement_Application table contains information about the procurement applications, including the quantity, time, material ID, ID, remarks, and foreign key references to the Material table. The Supplier table contains information about the suppliers, including their address, country ID, fax, name, phone, region ID, ID, tax ID, and foreign key references to the Country and Region tables. The Company table contains information about the companies, including their activation time, address, ID, country ID, creation time, ERP system identifier, email, name, password, phone, region ID, security answer, security question, status, tax ID, update time, username, VAT registration number, and foreign key references to the Country and Region tables. The Supplier_Production_Material table contains information about the materials produced by the suppliers, including the material ID, production quantity, production time, remarks, and foreign key references to the Material and Supplier tables.
