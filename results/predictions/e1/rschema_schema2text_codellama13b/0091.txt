The database schema rschema_0091 is used to store information about a company's departments, employees, trainings, and attendance records. The schema consists of five tables: department, training, employee, attendance, and employee_training.
The department table contains information about each department in the company, including the department ID, department name, person in charge, and phone number. The department ID is the primary key, and the department name and person in charge are strings. The phone number is a string, and the person in charge is a float.
The training table contains information about each training session, including the training ID, training name, training content, and training time. The training ID is the primary key, and the training name and training content are strings. The training time is a datetime.
The employee table contains information about each employee, including the employee ID, name, gender, position, salary, and department ID. The employee ID is the primary key, and the name, gender, position, and salary are strings. The department ID is an integer, and the employee is associated with a specific department.
The attendance table contains information about each employee's attendance, including the attendance ID, employee ID, attendance date, check-in and check-out times, whether the employee was late or early, and whether the employee was absent. The attendance ID is the primary key, and the employee ID is an integer that references the employee table. The attendance date is a datetime, and the check-in and check-out times are datetimes. Whether the employee was late or early is a string, and whether the employee was absent is a boolean.
The employee_training table contains information about each employee's training, including the employee ID, training ID, and whether the employee has completed the training. The employee ID and training ID are integers that reference the employee and training tables, respectively. The employee_training table has a composite primary key consisting of the employee ID and training ID.
In summary, the rschema_0091 database schema is used to store information about a company's departments, employees, trainings, and attendance records. The schema consists of five tables, each with its own set of columns and constraints. The tables are related to each other through foreign keys, and the schema enforces entity and referential integrity.
