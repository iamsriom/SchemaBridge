The post table has the following columns: post_id, post_title, post_description, image_path, thumbnail_path, activation_status, creation_time, and user_id. The post_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are not null. The comment table has the following columns: comment_content, author_id, post_id, and creation_time. The primary key is a composite key consisting of comment_creation_time, author_id, and post_id. The author_id and post_id columns are foreign keys that reference the user_id and post_id columns in the user and post tables, respectively.
