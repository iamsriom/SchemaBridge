The rschema_0109 database is a schema that contains four tables: Country, Member, Address, and Member_Address_Management. The Country table contains two columns: Country_Code, which is a primary key, and Name. The Member table contains seven columns: Company_Name, Country_Code, Member_ID, Name, Name_Pronunciation, Public_Status, and a foreign key to the Country table. The Address table contains five columns: Address_ID, Contact_Number, Country_Code, Postal_Code, and Specific_Address, with a foreign key to the Country table. The Member_Address_Management table contains two columns: Address_ID and Member_ID, with a composite primary key and foreign keys to the Address and Member tables. The schema is designed to store information about members of a company, including their addresses, and the countries they are from. The foreign keys ensure referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate.
