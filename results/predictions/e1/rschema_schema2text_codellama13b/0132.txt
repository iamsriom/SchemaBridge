The database schema "rschema_0132" contains five tables: Student, Course, Teacher, Course_selection, and Teaching. The Student table has columns for Age, Class, Enrollment_time, Gender, Name, Place_of_residence, and Student_ID, with Student_ID being the primary key. The Course table has columns for Course_ID, Course_introduction, Course_name, and Course_status, with Course_ID being the primary key. The Teacher table has columns for Gender, Name, Teacher_ID, and Title, with Teacher_ID being the primary key. The Course_selection table has columns for Course_ID and Student_ID, with a composite primary key consisting of both columns. The Teaching table has columns for Course_ID, Start_time, Teacher_ID, and a composite primary key consisting of both columns. Foreign keys are defined in the Course_selection and Teaching tables, referencing the Course and Teacher tables, respectively. The foreign key constraints are defined with update and delete rules of CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
