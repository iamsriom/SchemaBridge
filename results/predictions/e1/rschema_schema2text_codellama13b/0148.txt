The database named rschema_0148 is used to store information about a college and its students, teachers, and courses. It has six tables: College, Major, Teacher, Student, Course, and Student_Course_Selection.
The College table contains information about the college, including its name and ID. The Major table contains information about the majors offered by the college, including the ID, name, and foreign key reference to the College table. The Teacher table contains information about the teachers at the college, including their ID, name, contact information, gender, hire date, position, and foreign key reference to the College table.
The Student table contains information about the students at the college, including their ID, name, gender, and foreign key reference to the Major table. The Course table contains information about the courses offered by the college, including its ID, name, credit hours, class hours, and foreign key references to the Major and Teacher tables.
The Student_Course_Selection table contains information about the courses selected by students, including the course ID, selection time, grade, and foreign key references to the Course and Student tables.
Each table has primary keys and foreign key constraints to ensure data integrity. The primary keys are used to uniquely identify each row in the table, while the foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables. The foreign key constraints ensure that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate.
In summary, the rschema_0148 database schema is used to store information about a college and its students, teachers, and courses. It has six tables that are linked together through primary and foreign keys to ensure data integrity and consistency.
