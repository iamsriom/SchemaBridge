The database name is rschema_0154, and it contains three tables: user, product, and supply_and_demand_relationship. The user table has the following columns: username, password, gender, phone_number, id_number, email, and user_id. The user_id column is the primary key. The product table has the following columns: title, category, price, images, content, validity_period, contact_person, phone_number, and product_id. The product_id column is the primary key. The supply_and_demand_relationship table has the following columns: user_id, product_id, supply_and_demand_id, and supply_and_demand_type. The supply_and_demand_id column is the primary key. The user_id and product_id columns are foreign keys that reference the user and product tables, respectively. The ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE clauses specify that if a user or product is deleted or updated, the corresponding supply_and_demand_relationship records should also be deleted or updated.
