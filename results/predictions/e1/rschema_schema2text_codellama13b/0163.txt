This database schema, named rschema_0163, is used to store information about sugarcane farming operations. It consists of five tables: Sugar_cane_variety, Employee, Plot, Planting_record, and Fertilization_record.
The Sugar_cane_variety table contains information about different sugarcane varieties, including their maturation cycle, suitable climate, soil, variety ID, and variety name. The table has five columns: Maturation_cycle, Suitable_climate, Suitable_soil, Variety_ID, and Variety_name. The Variety_ID column is the primary key, and the other columns are not null.
The Employee table contains information about the employees working on the sugarcane farm, including their employee ID, gender, name, phone number, and working hours. The table has five columns: Employee_ID, Gender, Name, Phone, and Working_hours. The Employee_ID column is the primary key, and the other columns are not null.
The Plot table contains information about the plots of land used for sugarcane farming, including their area, location, organic matter content, person in charge ID, plot ID, soil pH, soil type, and any foreign keys referencing the Employee table. The table has eight columns: Area, Location, Organic_matter_content, Person_in_charge_ID, Plot_ID, Soil_pH, Soil_type, and FOREIGN KEY (Person_in_charge_ID) REFERENCES Employee(Employee_ID). The Person_in_charge_ID column is not null, and the other columns are not null.
The Planting_record table contains information about the planting of sugarcane, including the planter ID, planting method, planting quantity, planting time, plot ID, variety ID, and any foreign keys referencing the Employee and Sugar_cane_variety tables. The table has seven columns: Planter_ID, Planting_method, Planting_quantity, Planting_time, Plot_ID, Variety_ID, and PRIMARY KEY (Plot_ID, Variety_ID, Planting_time). The Planter_ID, Plot_ID, and Variety_ID columns are not null, and the other columns are not null.
The Fertilization_record table contains information about the fertilization of sugarcane, including the fertilization amount, fertilization method, fertilization time, fertilization type, person in charge ID, plot ID, remarks, and any foreign keys referencing the Employee and Plot tables. The table has eight columns: Fertilization_amount, Fertilization_method, Fertilization_time, Fertilization_type, Person_in_charge_ID, Plot_ID, Remarks, and PRIMARY KEY (Person_in_charge_ID, Plot_ID, Fertilization_time). The Person_in_charge_ID and Plot_ID columns are not null, and the other columns are not null.
In summary, this database schema is used to store information about sugarcane farming operations, including different sugarcane varieties, employees, plots of land, planting, and fertilization records. The schema is designed to ensure data integrity by using primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints to enforce entity and referential integrity.
