The rschema_0164 database contains six tables, each with its own unique purpose. The Warehouse table stores information about the physical location of the warehouse, including its name, location, description, and image. The User table contains information about the users who interact with the warehouse, including their email, phone number, role, and username. The Stack_Position table stores information about the specific locations within the warehouse, such as the type of stack, the location of the stack, and the description of the stack. The Sensor table stores information about the sensors that are used to monitor the stacks, including the type of sensor, the data it collects, and the status of the sensor. The User_Operation_Stack_Position table stores information about the operations that are performed on the stacks, including the type of operation, the time of the operation, and the user who performed the operation. Finally, the Stack_Position_Alert table stores information about any alerts that are triggered by the sensors, including the type of alert, the level of the alert, and the time of the alert. Each table is linked to the others through foreign keys, ensuring that the data is consistent and accurate.
