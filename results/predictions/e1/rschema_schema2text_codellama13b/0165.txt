The rschema_0165 database is a collection of tables that store information related to meteorological stations, operators, historical evolution information, and audit information. The Meteorological_Station table contains information about the physical location and characteristics of each station, including its elevation, geographic environment, latitude, longitude, observation elements, instrument name and model, observation record medium, observation standards, observation time, station ID, station name, and station type. The Operator table contains information about the people responsible for maintaining and operating the stations, including their name, email, phone number, role, and operator ID. The Historical_Evolution_Information table contains information about changes made to the station's information over time, including the change type, end time, new value, old value, operation status, operation time, reason for change, start time, station ID, and belonging station ID. The Audit_Information table contains information about the audit process, including the audit comments, audit status, audit time, change ID, operator ID, and primary key. The foreign keys in the tables ensure referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate.
