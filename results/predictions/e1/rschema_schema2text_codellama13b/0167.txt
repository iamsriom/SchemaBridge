The database schema named rschema_0167 is used to store information about natural disasters, including their data, source, and format. It consists of five tables: natural_disaster_data, region_information, department, user, and region_and_department.

The region_information table contains information about the regions affected by the disasters, including their ID, name, administrative division, latitude and longitude, area, and disaster risk level. The department table contains information about the departments responsible for handling the disasters, including their ID, name, person-in-charge, contact information, functional scope, and department ID. The user table contains information about the users who have access to the system, including their ID, username, role, contact information, permissions, and department ID. The region_and_department table contains information about the division of regions and departments, including their ID, department ID, and division scope.
