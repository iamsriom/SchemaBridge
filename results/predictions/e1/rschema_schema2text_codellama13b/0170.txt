The database name is rschema_0170, and it has three tables: store, department, and employee. The store table has five columns: store_id, name, address, contact_phone_number, and manager. The department table has nine columns: department_id, department_name, distribution_location, contact_phone_number, manager, manager_tenure, belonging_store, and employee_id. The employee table has eight columns: employee_id, name, position, phone_number, salary, emergency_contact, belonging_department, and employee_id. The store table has a primary key of store_id, and the department table has a primary key of department_id. The employee table has a primary key of employee_id. The department table has a foreign key of manager that references the employee table, and the department table has a foreign key of belonging_store that references the store table. The employee table has a foreign key of emergency_contact that references the employee table, and the employee table has a foreign key of belonging_department that references the department table.
