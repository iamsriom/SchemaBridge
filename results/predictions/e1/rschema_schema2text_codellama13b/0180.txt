This database schema, named "rschema_0180," is used to store information about a pizza delivery service. It consists of four tables: Pizza, Customer, Order, and Ordered_Pizza.
The Pizza table contains information about the different types of pizzas available for delivery, including their names, prices, and stock quantities. The Customer table stores information about the customers who place orders, including their names, addresses, phone numbers, and IDs.
The Order table contains information about the orders placed by customers, including the order date, expected delivery date, order status, and the customer ID. The Ordered_Pizza table contains information about the pizzas ordered by customers, including the order ID, pizza ID, and quantity.
The primary keys for each table are highlighted in bold, and the foreign keys are denoted by the FOREIGN KEY keyword. The foreign keys in the Ordered_Pizza table reference the Order and Pizza tables, respectively. The update and delete rules for the foreign keys are CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
In addition to the primary and foreign keys, the Ordered_Pizza table has a composite primary key consisting of the Order ID and Pizza ID. This ensures that each pizza ordered by a customer is unique and cannot be duplicated.
Overall, this schema provides a comprehensive and organized way to store and manage information about a pizza delivery service, including the different types of pizzas available, the customers who place orders, and the orders themselves.
