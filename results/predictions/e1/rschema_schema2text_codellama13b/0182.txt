The database name is rschema_0182. It is used to store information about agricultural products and their sales records. The database has three tables: market, agricultural_products, and sales_record.
The market table has four columns: market_id, name, address, and contact_information. The market_id column is the primary key and is a unique identifier for each market. The name, address, and contact_information columns contain information about the market.
The agricultural_products table has five columns: product_id, name, specification, price, and current_stock. The product_id column is the primary key and is a unique identifier for each product. The name, specification, price, and current_stock columns contain information about the product.
The sales_record table has six columns: market_id, product_id, sales_date, sales_quantity, sales_unit_price, and operator. The sales_date column contains the date of the sale, the sales_quantity column contains the quantity of the product sold, the sales_unit_price column contains the unit price of the product, and the operator column contains the name of the person who made the sale. The primary key of the sales_record table is a composite key consisting of the product_id, sales_date, and market_id columns.
The sales_record table has two foreign key constraints: sales_record_ibfk_product_id and sales_record_ibfk_market_id. The sales_record_ibfk_product_id constraint references the agricultural_products table and specifies that the product_id column in the sales_record table must match the product_id column in the agricultural_products table. The sales_record_ibfk_market_id constraint references the market table and specifies that the market_id column in the sales_record table must match the market_id column in the market table. The ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE clauses specify that if a record in the market or agricultural_products table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the sales_record table should also be deleted or updated.
