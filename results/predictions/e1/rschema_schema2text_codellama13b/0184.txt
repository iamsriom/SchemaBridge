The database name is rschema_0184 and it is used to store information about hotel guests and their bookings. The schema consists of three tables: member, room, and booking.
The member table contains information about each guest, including their member ID, name, phone number, registration date, and points earned. The member ID is the primary key and is used to identify each guest.
The room table contains information about each hotel room, including the room number, room type, and price. The room number is the primary key and is used to identify each room.
The booking table contains information about each booking, including the member ID, room number, booking date, check-in date, check-out date, booking status, and points change. The member ID and booking date are the primary key, and the room number is a foreign key that references the room table. The booking table also has foreign keys to the member and room tables, which are used to enforce referential integrity.
In addition to the primary and foreign keys, the booking table has a composite unique constraint that ensures each booking is unique based on the member ID, booking date, and room number.
Overall, the schema is designed to store information about hotel guests and their bookings, and to enforce data integrity through the use of primary and foreign keys, unique constraints, and referential integrity.
