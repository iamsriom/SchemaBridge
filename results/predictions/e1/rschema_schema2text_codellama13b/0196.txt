The rschema_0196 database contains four tables: Applicant, Approver, Loan_Application, and Loan_Approval. The Applicant table stores information about loan applicants, including their email address, identification number, name, and phone number. The Approver table contains information about loan approvers, including their email address, name, and phone number. The Loan_Application table stores information about loan applications, including the applicant's ID, application number, approval status, loan amount, loan term, and the foreign key referencing the Applicant table. The Loan_Approval table stores information about loan approvals, including the application number, approval date, approval result, approver ID, and the primary key consisting of the approver ID, application number, and approval date. The foreign keys in the Loan_Approval table reference the Loan_Application table and the Approver table, and the update and delete rules are set to CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
