The database named rschema_0209 is used to store information about laboratories, equipment, and maintenance records. The laboratory table contains information about each laboratory, including its number, name, and location. The equipment table contains information about each piece of equipment, including its number, name, model, purchase date, status, and the laboratory it belongs to. The maintenance_record table contains information about each maintenance record, including the equipment it is associated with, the date of the maintenance, the personnel responsible, and the content of the maintenance. The laboratory_number column in the equipment table is a foreign key that references the laboratory table, and the equipment_number column in the maintenance_record table is a foreign key that references the equipment table. The foreign key constraints ensure that the data in the child tables (equipment and maintenance_record) is consistent with the data in the parent tables (laboratory and equipment).
