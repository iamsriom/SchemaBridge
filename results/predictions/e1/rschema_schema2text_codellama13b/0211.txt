The Client_Name and Name columns are strings, and the End_Date, Start_Date, and Project_ID columns are integers. The Project_ID column is the primary key, and the Start_Date column has a default value of the current date and time. The consultant table has six columns: Consultant_ID, Date_of_Birth, Gender, Name, Phone_Number, and Professional_Field. The Consultant_ID column is the primary key, and the Date_of_Birth, Gender, Name, Phone_Number, and Professional_Field columns are strings. The service_record table has six columns: Consultant_ID, Project_ID, Service_Content, Service_Date, Service_ID, and FOREIGN KEY (Consultant_ID) REFERENCES consultant(Consultant_ID), FOREIGN KEY (Project_ID) REFERENCES project(Project_ID). The Consultant_ID and Project_ID columns are integers, and the Service_Content and Service_Date columns are strings. The Service_ID column is the primary key, and the FOREIGN KEY constraints ensure that the Consultant_ID and Project_ID columns reference the corresponding columns in the consultant and project tables, respectively.
