The rschema_0213 database is used to store information about customers, their contracts, and payments made for those contracts. The database contains three tables: Customer, Contract, and Payment_Record.
The Customer table has four columns: Customer_ID, Email, Name, and Phone. Customer_ID is the primary key and is an integer. Email, Name, and Phone are all strings.
The Contract table has seven columns: Contract_Amount, Contract_ID, Customer_ID, End_Time, Service_Content, Start_Time, and Payment_ID. Contract_Amount is a float, Contract_ID is an integer, and Customer_ID is an integer that references the Customer table. End_Time and Start_Time are both datetimes, and Service_Content is a string. Payment_ID is an integer that references the Payment_Record table.
The Payment_Record table has four columns: Contract_ID, Payment_Amount, Payment_Date, and Payment_ID. Contract_ID is an integer that references the Contract table, Payment_Amount is a float, Payment_Date is a datetime, and Payment_ID is an integer that is the primary key.
The foreign keys in the Contract table reference the Customer table, and the foreign keys in the Payment_Record table reference the Contract table. The update and delete rules for these foreign keys are CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
There are no other indexes or constraints in the schema.
