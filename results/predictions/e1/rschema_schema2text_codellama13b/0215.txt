The database schema rschema_0215 is used to store information about a mining operation, including the materials used, the mines themselves, and the records of purchases and distributions of those materials. The schema consists of four tables: material_administrator, mine, purchase_record, and material_distribution_record.
The material_administrator table contains information about the individuals responsible for managing the mining operation, including their names, genders, phone numbers, positions, and the mine they are responsible for. The mine table contains information about the mines themselves, including their names, locations, and the person in charge.
The purchase_record table contains information about the materials purchased, including the date of purchase, the material name, the quantity purchased, the supplier, and the cost. The material_distribution_record table contains information about the distribution of materials, including the date of distribution, the material name, the quantity distributed, the distributor, and the recipient.
Each table has a primary key, which is a unique identifier for each row in the table. The material_administrator table has a foreign key that references the mine table, indicating that each material administrator is responsible for a specific mine. The material_distribution_record table has a foreign key that references the material_administrator table, indicating that each distribution is made by a specific material administrator.
In addition to the primary and foreign keys, each table has constraints that ensure the integrity of the data. For example, the material_administrator table has a constraint that ensures that each administrator is responsible for a specific mine, and the material_distribution_record table has a constraint that ensures that each distribution is made by a specific material administrator.
Overall, the schema is designed to provide a comprehensive and accurate record of the mining operation, including the materials used, the mines themselves, and the records of purchases and distributions of those materials.
