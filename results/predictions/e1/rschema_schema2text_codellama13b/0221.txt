The rschema_0221 database is used to store information about customers, suppliers, products, product purchases, and sales records. The database contains five tables: Customer, Supplier, Product, Product_Purchase, and Sales_Record.
The Customer table contains information about individual customers, including their address, customer ID, name, and phone number. The Supplier table contains information about individual suppliers, including their address, responsible person's name and phone number, supplier ID, and supplier name.
The Product table contains information about individual products, including product ID, product name, stock, supplier ID, and unit price. The Product_Purchase table contains information about product purchases, including product ID, purchase price, purchase quantity, purchase time, supplier ID, total purchase price, and primary key (supplier ID, product ID, purchase time).
The Sales_Record table contains information about sales records, including customer ID, product ID, sale price, sale quantity, sale time, total sale price, and primary key (product ID, customer ID, sale time).
The foreign keys in the Product_Purchase and Sales_Record tables reference the corresponding tables, ensuring referential integrity. The primary keys in the Product_Purchase and Sales_Record tables ensure entity integrity. The indexes in the Product_Purchase and Sales_Record tables optimize query performance.
