This schema is for a database that manages the maintenance and repair of vehicles. It consists of seven tables: vehicle_model, mechanic, customer, maintenance_record, part, order, and order_detail.
The vehicle_model table contains information about different types of vehicles, including their model names and IDs. The mechanic table contains information about the mechanics who work on the vehicles, including their employee IDs, names, and contact information. The customer table contains information about the customers who own the vehicles, including their customer IDs, names, phone numbers, and contact addresses.
The maintenance_record table contains information about the maintenance and repair tasks that have been performed on the vehicles. It includes the record ID, the associated customer, the vehicle model, the task status, the start and end dates, and the mechanic who performed the task. The maintenance_record table also includes foreign key constraints to reference the customer and mechanic tables.
The part table contains information about the parts that are used in the maintenance and repair tasks. It includes the serial number, price, stock quantity, and supplier of each part.
The order table contains information about the orders placed by customers for parts. It includes the order ID, the dispatch date, and the order status.
The order_detail table contains information about the parts that are included in each order. It includes the order ID, the part serial number, and the purchase quantity. The order_detail table also includes foreign key constraints to reference the order and part tables.
Overall, this schema is designed to manage the maintenance and repair of vehicles, including tracking the tasks performed, the parts used, and the orders placed by customers. It is a comprehensive and well-structured database that can be used to efficiently manage a vehicle maintenance and repair business.
