The primary key is a composite of the User_ID, Scholarship_ID, and Like_Time columns.
The Scholarship_Category table contains information about which scholarships belong to which categories. The Scholarship_ID column references the scholarship, and the Category_ID column references the category. The primary key is a composite of the Scholarship_ID and Category_ID columns.
In addition to the primary keys, there are foreign keys that reference the User, Scholarship, and Category tables.
