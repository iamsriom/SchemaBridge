The database name is rschema_0242, and it contains four tables: Employee, Member, Administrator, and Order. The Employee table has three columns: Employee_ID, Password, and Username. The Member table has eight columns: Balance, Birth_Month_and_Year, Gender, Member_ID, Password, Phone_Number, Username, and Administrator_ID. The Administrator table has three columns: Administrator_ID, Password, and Username. The Order table has five columns: Employee_ID, Member_ID, Order_ID, Order_Time, and FOREIGN KEY (Employee_ID) REFERENCES Employee(Employee_ID) and FOREIGN KEY (Member_ID) REFERENCES Member(Member_ID).
