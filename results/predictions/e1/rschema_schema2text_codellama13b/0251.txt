The database name is rschema_0251, and it contains three tables: Groomer, Pet, and Grooming_service. The Groomer table contains information about pet groomers, including their name, rating, specialization, and years of experience. The Pet table contains information about the pets being groomed, including their age, breed, fur characteristics, weight, and ID number. The Grooming_service table contains information about the grooming services provided by the groomers, including the customer rating, groomer ID, pet ID, price, service items, service market, and service time. The primary key for the Grooming_service table is a composite key consisting of the groomer ID, pet ID, and service time. The foreign keys in the Grooming_service table reference the Groomer and Pet tables, and the update and delete rules for these foreign keys are CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively. Additionally, there are no other indexes or constraints in this schema.
