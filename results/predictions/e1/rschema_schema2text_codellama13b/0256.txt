The database name is rschema_0256, and it contains three tables: Mall, Department, and Employee. The Mall table has four columns: Address, Contact_number, Mall_ID, and Mall_name. The Department table has seven columns: Contact_number, Department_ID, Department_name, Location_distribution, Mall_ID, Manager_employee_ID, and two foreign keys, one referencing the Mall table and the other referencing the Employee table. The Employee table has six columns: Department_ID, Employee_ID, Name, Phone_number, Position, and Salary. The primary key of the Employee table is a composite key of Employee_ID and Department_ID, and it has a foreign key referencing the Department table.
