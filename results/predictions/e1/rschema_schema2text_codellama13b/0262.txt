The database name is rschema_0262, and it contains eight tables. The tables are:
1. Company: This table contains information about companies, including their address, company ID, company name, and telephone number. The company ID is the primary key.
2. Product: This table contains information about products, including their model, name, product ID, and unit price. The product ID is the primary key.
3. Salesperson: This table contains information about salespeople, including their contact information, name, and salesperson ID. The salesperson ID is the primary key.
4. Supermarket: This table contains information about supermarkets, including their address, company ID, manager, supermarket ID, telephone number, and foreign key to the Company table. The supermarket ID is the primary key.
5. Allocation_Record: This table contains information about product allocations, including the allocation ID, allocation quantity, allocation time, product ID, salesperson ID, and foreign keys to the Product and Salesperson tables. The allocation ID is the primary key.
6. Department: This table contains information about departments, including their contact telephone number, department ID, department name, manager, supermarket ID, and foreign key to the Supermarket table. The department ID is the primary key.
7. Product_Inventory: This table contains information about product inventory, including the inventory ID, inventory quantity, product ID, and supermarket ID, and foreign keys to the Product and Supermarket tables. The inventory ID is the primary key.
8. Employee: This table contains information about employees, including their contact information, department ID, employee ID, name, position, salary, and foreign key to the Department table. The employee ID is the primary key.
Each table has its own set of columns, with some columns having constraints such as primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints. The primary keys are used to uniquely identify each row in the table, while the foreign keys establish relationships between tables. The unique constraints ensure that each row in the table has a unique combination of values in the specified columns.
