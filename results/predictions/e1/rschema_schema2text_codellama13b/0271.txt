This database schema, named rschema_0271, contains three tables: Service, Client, and Service_Subscription. The Service table stores information about different services offered by the company, including their price, service ID, service name, transmission rate, and transmission type. The Client table contains information about the clients who subscribe to these services, including their client ID, client name, client address, client email, and client phone number. The Service_Subscription table links clients to the services they subscribe to, including the operator, service ID, subscription start and end times, and client ID. The primary key for this table is a composite of service ID, client ID, and subscription start time, and it also has foreign keys referencing the Client and Service tables. The foreign keys are set to restrict updates and deletes, ensuring referential integrity.
