The database schema rschema_0277 is used to store information about a cleaning service company. It consists of seven tables: Service_team, Customer, Service_evaluation, Housekeeper, Service_task, Staff_task, and Customer_task.
The Service_team table contains information about the different cleaning teams, including their unique identifiers and names. The Customer table contains information about the customers, including their unique identifiers and names. The Service_evaluation table contains information about the evaluations of the cleaning services provided, including the customer's evaluation, the effectiveness of the service, and the service fee.
The Housekeeper table contains information about the cleaning staff, including their contact information, name, professional skills, service type, unique identifier, team identifier, years of experience, and foreign key references to the Service_team table. The Service_task table contains information about the cleaning tasks, including the unique identifier, service address, service date, service duration, service type, and foreign key references to the Service_evaluation table.
The Staff_task table contains information about the cleaning staff and their tasks, including the unique identifier of the staff member, the unique identifier of the task, and foreign key references to the Housekeeper and Service_task tables. The Customer_task table contains information about the customers and their tasks, including the unique identifier of the customer, the unique identifier of the task, and foreign key references to the Customer and Service_task tables.
The schema enforces data integrity through primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints. The primary keys ensure that each table has a unique identifier for each row, while the foreign keys ensure that the data in one table is related to the data in another table. The unique constraints ensure that no duplicate data is entered into the database.
