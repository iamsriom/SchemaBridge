The database schema is named "rschema_0293" and contains four tables: Mine_Table, Maintenance_Personnel_Table, Equipment_Table, and Maintenance_Record_Table.
The Mine_Table contains columns for location, mine_ID (primary key), mine_name, and person_in_charge. The Maintenance_Personnel_Table contains columns for contact_number, maintenance_personnel_ID (primary key), and name. The Equipment_Table contains columns for equipment_ID (primary key), mine_ID (foreign key referencing Mine_Table), model, name, purchase_date, and foreign key referencing Maintenance_Personnel_Table. The Maintenance_Record_Table contains columns for equipment_ID (foreign key referencing Equipment_Table), maintenance_content, maintenance_cost, maintenance_date, maintenance_personnel_ID (foreign key referencing Maintenance_Personnel_Table), and primary key (equipment_ID, maintenance_date).
The primary keys in each table are enforced by the database, and the foreign keys in the Equipment_Table and Maintenance_Record_Table reference the primary keys in the Mine_Table and Maintenance_Personnel_Table, respectively. The foreign keys have update and delete rules of CASCADE, meaning that if the referenced record is deleted or updated, the referencing record will also be deleted or updated.
The Maintenance_Record_Table has a composite primary key of equipment_ID and maintenance_date, which ensures that each record is unique. The foreign keys in the Maintenance_Record_Table also have update and delete rules of CASCADE.
Overall, the schema is designed to store information about mines, maintenance personnel, equipment, and maintenance records. The relationships between the tables are enforced by the primary and foreign keys, and the data types of the columns are appropriate for the data they contain.
