The rschema_0299 database contains three tables: City, Weather_Description, and Weather_Forecast. The City table has the following columns: city_ID (primary key), city_name, country, latitude, longitude, population, and timezone. The Weather_Description table has the following columns: detailed_description, icon, main_description, and weather_type_ID (primary key). The Weather_Forecast table has the following columns: afternoon_temperature, atmospheric_pressure, city_ID (foreign key referencing City.city_ID), cloud_cover, date, feels_like_temperature, forecast_ID (primary key), humidity, morning_temperature, noon_temperature, precipitation_probability, sunrise_time, sunset_time, weather_type_ID (foreign key referencing Weather_Description.weather_type_ID), wind_direction, and wind_speed. The City table has a foreign key referencing the Weather_Forecast table, and the Weather_Description table has a foreign key referencing the Weather_Forecast table.
