The Album_ID column is a foreign key that references the Album table, and the Role_ID column is a foreign key that references the Role table. The PRIMARY KEY constraint ensures that each combination of Album_ID and Role_ID is unique.
The foreign key constraints ensure that the data in the Permission table is consistent with the data in the Album and Role tables. If a role is deleted from the Role table, any permissions associated with that role in the Permission table are also deleted. Similarly, if an album is deleted from the Album table, any permissions associated with that album in the Permission table are also deleted.
