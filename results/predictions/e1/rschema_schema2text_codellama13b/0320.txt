The database name is rschema_0320, and it contains five tables. The first table, Product, has six columns: Default_Price, Product_Category, Product_Description, Product_ID (primary key), Product_Name, and Product_Slogan. The second table, Style, has seven columns: Associated_Product_ID (foreign key referencing Product), Is_Default_Style, Original_Price, Sale_Price, Style_ID (primary key), Style_Name, and FOREIGN KEY (Associated_Product_ID) REFERENCES Product(Product_ID). The third table, Feature, has five columns: Associated_Product_ID (foreign key referencing Product), Feature_ID (primary key), Feature_Name, Feature_Value, and FOREIGN KEY (Associated_Product_ID) REFERENCES Product(Product_ID). The fourth table, SKU, has four columns: Associated_Style_ID (foreign key referencing Style), SKU_ID (primary key), Size, and Stock_Quantity, and the fifth table, Photo, has five columns: Associated_Style_ID (foreign key referencing Style), Photo_ID (primary key), Photo_URL, Thumbnail_URL, and FOREIGN KEY (Associated_Style_ID) REFERENCES Style(Style_ID).
