The fourth table, Football_Player_Information, stores information about football players, including their affiliated team ID, football player ID, and primary key (football player ID and affiliated team ID). The fifth table, Match_Information, stores information about matches, including the away team ID, home team ID, match ID, match location, match result, match time, and primary key (match ID). The primary keys in each table are indicated by the PRIMARY KEY keyword. The foreign keys in each table are indicated by the FOREIGN KEY keyword, and they reference the primary keys in other tables.
