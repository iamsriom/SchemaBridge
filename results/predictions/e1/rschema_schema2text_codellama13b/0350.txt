The rschema_0350 database contains eight tables that are used to store information about a conference. The Ticket_Type table contains information about the different types of tickets that can be purchased for the conference, including the name, description, price, and whether or not it includes a workshop. The Discount_Code table contains information about the different discount codes that can be used to get a discount on the cost of a ticket, including the name, type, and amount of the discount. The Meeting table contains information about the different meetings that will be held during the conference, including the date, time, location, and description. The Workshop table contains information about the different workshops that will be held during the conference, including the date, time, location, and description. The Speaker table contains information about the different speakers who will be giving presentations during the conference, including their name, company, and bio. The Participant table contains information about the different participants who will be attending the conference, including their name, company, email, and discount code. The Meeting_Speaker table contains information about the speakers who will be giving presentations at each meeting, including the meeting and speaker. The Workshop_Speaker table contains information about the speakers who will be giving presentations at each workshop, including the workshop and speaker.
