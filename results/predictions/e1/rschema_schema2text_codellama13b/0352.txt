The database name is rschema_0352. It is used to store information about suppliers, customers, products, invoices, and the products on those invoices. The database has five tables: Supplier, Customer, Product, Invoice, and Invoice_Product.
The Supplier table contains information about each supplier, including their address, ID, name, and phone number. The Supplier_ID is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each supplier.
The Customer table contains information about each customer, including their address, ID, email, first name, last name, and phone number. The Customer_ID is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each customer.
The Product table contains information about each product, including its description, minimum stock quantity, name, ID, purchase price, selling price, stock quantity, and supplier ID. The Product_ID is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each product. The Supplier_ID is a foreign key that references the Supplier table.
The Invoice table contains information about each invoice, including the customer ID, date, invoice ID, tax amount, and total value. The Invoice_ID is the primary key and is used to uniquely identify each invoice. The Customer_ID is a foreign key that references the Customer table.
The Invoice_Product table contains information about the products on each invoice, including the invoice ID, product ID, and product quantity. The primary key is a composite key made up of the Invoice_ID and Product_ID. The Invoice_ID and Product_ID are foreign keys that reference the Invoice and Product tables, respectively.
In summary, the rschema_0352 database stores information about suppliers, customers, products, invoices, and the products on those invoices. Each table has its own set of columns, with the primary key serving as a unique identifier for each row. Foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables, and the database is designed to ensure data integrity through the use of constraints such as primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints.
