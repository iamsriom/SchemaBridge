The database name is rschema_0361, and it contains four tables: Restaurant, User_review, Dish, and Review_reply. The Restaurant table contains information about restaurants, including their address, average cost, business hours, name, phone number, and restaurant ID. The User_review table contains information about user reviews, including actual photos, dining time, rating, review ID, and review content. The Dish table contains information about dishes, including category, description, dish ID, dish image URL, monthly sales, name, price, restaurant ID, satisfaction rate, and foreign key referencing the Restaurant table. The Review_reply table contains information about review replies, including reply ID, reply content, reply time, review ID being replied to, and foreign key referencing the User_review table. The primary keys for each table are Restaurant_ID, Review_ID, Dish_ID, and Reply_ID. The foreign keys are Restaurant_ID referencing the Restaurant table, Review_ID referencing the User_review table, and Review_ID_being_replied_to referencing the User_review table. The tables have composite or unique constraints, such as the primary keys, and referential integrity, such as the foreign keys.
