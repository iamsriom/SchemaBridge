The database name is rschema_0363, and it contains four tables: Book, Reader, Reader_book_borrowing, and Reader_book_reservation. The Book table contains information about books, including their authors, available and circulating copies, ISBN, library location, price, publication date, publisher, shelf number, and title. The Reader table contains information about readers, including their addresses, ages, emails, genders, library card numbers, loan periods, maximum borrowing limits, names, phones, reader IDs, and reader types. The Reader_book_borrowing table contains information about book borrowings, including the actual return date, borrowing status, borrowing time, due date, ISBN, reader ID, and primary key (Reader_ID, ISBN, Borrowing_time). The Reader_book_reservation table contains information about book reservations, including the ISBN, reader ID, reservation expiration time, reservation status, reservation time, and primary key (Reader_ID, ISBN, Reservation_time). The foreign keys in the Reader_book_borrowing and Reader_book_reservation tables reference the primary keys in the Book and Reader tables, respectively.
