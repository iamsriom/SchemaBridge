This database schema, named "rschema_0365," contains four tables: Customer, Product, Order, and Order_Item. The Customer table stores information about customers, including their detailed address, email, name, and password. The Product table contains information about the products available for purchase, including their description, image path, name, price, stock quantity, and product ID. The Order table stores information about orders placed by customers, including the customer ID, order ID, order time, status, and foreign key referencing the Customer table. The Order_Item table stores information about the items in each order, including the order ID, product ID, quantity, and primary key referencing the Order and Product tables. The schema also includes foreign key constraints to enforce referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the Order and Order_Item tables is consistent with the data in the Customer and Product tables.
