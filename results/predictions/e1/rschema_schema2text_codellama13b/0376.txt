The "order_parts" table stores information about the parts that are ordered by customers, including the order ID, part ID, and quantity. The "task_parts" table stores information about the parts that are used in repair tasks, including the task ID, part ID, and quantity.

The "supplier" table stores information about the suppliers of parts, including the supplier ID and name. The "part" table stores information about the parts that are used in the repair shop, including the part ID, serial number, description, price, supplier ID, and stock quantity.
