The database name is rschema_0389 and it contains four tables: Student, Teacher, Course, and Student_Course_Selection. The Course table has six columns: Class_Hours, Class_Location, Course_ID, Credits, Number_of_Students_Enrolled, and Teaching_Teacher_ID, where Course_ID is the primary key and Teaching_Teacher_ID is a foreign key referencing the Teacher table. The Student_Course_Selection table has four columns: Course_ID, Grades, Selection_Time, and Student_ID, where Student_ID and Course_ID are the primary key and foreign keys referencing the Student and Course tables, respectively. The primary key of the Student_Course_Selection table is a composite key consisting of Student_ID, Course_ID, and Selection_Time.
