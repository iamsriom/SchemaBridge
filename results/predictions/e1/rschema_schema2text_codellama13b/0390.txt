The rschema_0390 database contains five tables: Consumer, Courier_Company, Courier_Tracking, Administrator, and Cooperation. The Consumer table contains information about consumers, including their address, phone number, and ID. The Courier_Company table contains information about courier companies, including their name, arrival time, and charging price. The Courier_Tracking table contains information about courier tracking numbers, including the recipient's ID, name, and phone number. The Administrator table contains information about administrators, including their ID, username, password, and name. The Cooperation table contains information about the cooperation between courier companies and administrators, including the administrator's ID and the company's name. The Cooperation table has a composite primary key consisting of the company name and the administrator ID, and it has foreign key constraints referencing the Administrator and Courier_Company tables.
