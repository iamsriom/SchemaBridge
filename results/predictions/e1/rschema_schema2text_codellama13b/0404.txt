This database schema, named "rschema_0404," is used to store information about employees, suppliers, products, and sales. It contains five tables: Employee, Supplier, Product, Employee_Sales, and Inventory.
The Employee table contains information about employees, including their contact number, employee ID, password, position, and username. The Employee ID is the primary key, and the table has no foreign keys.
The Supplier table contains information about suppliers, including their supplier ID, supplier name, supplier number, and supplier phone number. The Supplier ID is the primary key, and the table has no foreign keys.
The Product table contains information about products, including their product ID, product name, product number, purchase price, sales price, stock quantity, and supplier ID. The Product ID is the primary key, and the table has a foreign key referencing the Supplier table.
The Employee_Sales table contains information about sales made by employees, including the employee ID, product ID, sales date, sales price, and sales quantity. The primary key is a composite of the employee ID and product ID, and the table has foreign keys referencing the Employee and Product tables.
The Inventory table contains information about inventory, including the inventory ID, product ID, sales price, and stock quantity. The inventory ID is the primary key, and the table has a foreign key referencing the Product table.
Overall, this schema is designed to store and manage information about employees, suppliers, products, and sales, ensuring that data is accurate and consistent across all tables.
