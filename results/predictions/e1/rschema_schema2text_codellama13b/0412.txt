The course table has four columns: course_id, course_name, course_credit, and teacher_id. The course_id column is the primary key, and the other columns are not null. The student_grade table has four columns: student_id, course_id, enrollment_time, and grade. The primary key is a composite key consisting of student_id, enrollment_time, and course_id.
