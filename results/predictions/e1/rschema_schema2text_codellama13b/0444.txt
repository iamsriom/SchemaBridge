The Instructor table has five columns: Gender, Instructor_ID, Name, Title, and Work_Duration. The Instructor_ID column is the primary key, and the other columns are strings.
The Course table has six columns: Course_ID, Course_Name, Credits, Instructor_ID, Location, and FOREIGN KEY (Instructor_ID) REFERENCES Instructor(Instructor_ID). The Course_ID column is the primary key, and the other columns are strings. The Instructor_ID column is a foreign key that references the Instructor table.

The Leave_Record table has seven columns: Class_Time, Course_ID, Leave_Reason, Leave_Status, Leave_Time, Student_ID, and PRIMARY KEY (Student_ID, Course_ID, Leave_Time). The Leave_Reason and Leave_Status columns are strings, and the other columns are dates. The PRIMARY KEY constraint ensures that each combination of Student_ID, Course_ID, and Leave_Time is unique. The FOREIGN KEY constraints ensure that the Course_ID and Student_ID columns reference the Course and Student tables, respectively.
