The user_ibfk_belonging_department column in the user table is a foreign key that references the department_id column in the department table. The ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE clauses in the foreign key constraints ensure that if a record is deleted or updated in the referenced table, the corresponding records in the referencing table are also deleted or updated.
