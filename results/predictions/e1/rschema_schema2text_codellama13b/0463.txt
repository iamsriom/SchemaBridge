The database name is rschema_0463, and it contains three tables: Mall, Department, and Employee. The Mall table has four columns: Address, Contact_Number, Mall_ID, and Mall_Name. The Department table has eight columns: Contact_Number, Department_ID, Department_Name, Location_Distribution, Mall_ID, Manager_Employee_ID, Tenure, and Foreign keys to the Mall and Employee tables. The Employee table has seven columns: Department_ID, Emergency_Contact_Name, Emergency_Contact_Phone, Employee_ID, Name, Phone_Number, Position, and Salary. The Mall_ID and Manager_Employee_ID columns in the Department table are foreign keys that reference the Mall and Employee tables, respectively. The Department_ID column in the Employee table is a foreign key that references the Department table. The Mall, Department, and Employee tables all have primary keys, and the Department and Employee tables have foreign keys that reference each other.
