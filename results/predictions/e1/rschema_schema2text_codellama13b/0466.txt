The database name is rschema_0466, and it contains four tables: Customer, Employee, Menu, and Customer_Order. The Customer table has columns for Address, Contact_Number, Customer_ID (primary key), Name, and Payment_Method. The Employee table has columns for Contact_Number, Employee_ID (primary key), Name, and Position. The Menu table has columns for Dish_Name, Menu_ID (primary key), and Price. The Customer_Order table has columns for Customer_ID (not null), Delivery_Location, Delivery_Time, Menu_ID (not null), Order_Quantity, Order_Status, Order_Time, and a composite primary key consisting of Customer_ID, Menu_ID, and Order_Time. The Customer_Order table also has foreign keys to the Customer and Menu tables, with update and delete rules of CASCADE.
