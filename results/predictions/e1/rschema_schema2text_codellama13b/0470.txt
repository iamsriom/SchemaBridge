This database schema, named "rschema_0470," is used to store information about an online food delivery service. It consists of seven tables: Enterprise_Employee, Menu, Delivery_Person, Order, Order_Dish, Delivery_Record, and Payment_Request.
The Enterprise_Employee table stores information about the employees of the delivery service, including their names, addresses, and phone numbers. The Employee_ID column is the primary key, and the Employee_Name and Employee_Address columns are required.
The Menu table contains information about the dishes available for ordering, including their names, prices, and whether they have any special offers. The Dish_ID column is the primary key, and the Dish_Name, Dish_Price, and Dish_Special_Offer_Indicator columns are required.
The Delivery_Person table stores information about the delivery people, including their names, addresses, and phone numbers. The Delivery_Person_ID column is the primary key, and the Delivery_Person_Name and Delivery_Person_Address columns are required.
The Order table contains information about the orders placed by customers, including the delivery address, delivery time, payment method, and total price. The Order_ID column is the primary key, and the Delivery_Address, Delivery_Time, Order_Payment_Method, and Order_Total_Price columns are required. The Ordering_Employee column references the Employee_ID column in the Enterprise_Employee table.
The Order_Dish table contains information about the dishes ordered by customers, including the quantity of each dish and the Order_ID. The Dish_ID and Order_ID columns are the primary key, and the Dish_Quantity column is required. The Dish_ID column references the Dish_ID column in the Menu table, and the Order_ID column references the Order_ID column in the Order table.
The Delivery_Record table contains information about the delivery records, including the delivery time, the responsible delivery person, and the delivery order ID. The Delivery_Record_ID column is the primary key, and the Delivery_Time, Responsible_Delivery_Person_ID, and Delivery_Order_ID columns are required. The Delivery_Order_ID column references the Order_ID column in the Order table, and the Responsible_Delivery_Person_ID column references the Delivery_Person_ID column in the Delivery_Person table.
The Payment_Request table contains information about the payment requests, including the paying employee, the paying order, the payment amount, and the payment status. The Payment_Request_ID column is the primary key, and the Paying_Employee_ID, Paying_Order_ID, Request_Payment_Amount, and Request_Payment_Status columns are required. The Paying_Employee_ID column references the Employee_ID column in the Enterprise_Employee table, and the Paying_Order_ID column references the Order_ID column in the Order table.
In summary, this database schema stores information about an online food delivery service, including employees, dishes, delivery people, orders, delivery records, and payment requests. Each table has its own set of columns, and there are foreign key relationships between some of the tables to ensure data consistency and integrity.
