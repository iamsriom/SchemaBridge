The rschema_0471 database contains three tables: Customer, Credit_card, and Transaction_record. The Customer table stores information about customers, including their address, application status, contact number, customer ID, and name. The Credit_card table stores information about credit cards, including the activation status, card number, credit limit, customer ID, type, validity period, and foreign key referencing the Customer table. The Transaction_record table stores information about transactions, including the card number, transaction ID, transaction amount, transaction date, transaction description, transaction status, and foreign key referencing the Credit_card table. The Customer table has a primary key on the Customer_ID column, and the Credit_card table has a primary key on the Card_number column. The Transaction_record table has a primary key on the Transaction_ID column. The Credit_card table has a foreign key referencing the Customer table, and the Transaction_record table has a foreign key referencing the Credit_card table. The foreign keys have update and delete rules of CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively.
