The rschema_0473 database contains four tables: Publication, Customer, Administrator, and Order. The Publication table contains information about books, including the author, price, publication ID, title, and type. The Customer table contains information about customers, including their customer ID, email, name, password, phone number, and registration status. The Administrator table contains information about administrators, including their administrator ID, email, name, password, and phone number. The Order table contains information about orders, including the customer ID, order status, order time, payment method, publication ID, shipping address, and primary key (customer ID, publication ID, order time). The Order table also contains foreign keys referencing the Customer and Publication tables.
