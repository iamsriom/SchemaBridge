The database name is rschema_0474, and it contains four tables: Student, Instructor, Assignment, and Assignment_Grading. The Student table has three columns: Class, Name, and Student_ID, which is the primary key. The Instructor table has three columns: Course, Instructor_ID, and Name, where Instructor_ID is the primary key. The Assignment table has three columns: Assignment_Content, Assignment_ID, and Student_ID, where Assignment_ID is the primary key and Student_ID is a foreign key referencing the Student table. The Assignment_Grading table has five columns: Assignment_ID, Evaluation, Grading_Time, Instructor_ID, and Score, where Assignment_ID and Instructor_ID are foreign keys referencing the Assignment and Instructor tables, respectively. The primary key of the Assignment_Grading table is a composite key consisting of Assignment_ID, Instructor_ID, and Grading_Time.
