This schema is for a database that stores information about an enterprise, its employees, their roles, and their attendance records. The database is called "rschema_0493" and has five tables: enterprise, role, employee, attendance_record, and to-do_item.
The enterprise table contains information about the enterprise, including its creation time, ID, and name. The role table contains information about the roles of the employees, including their daily working hours, hourly wage, lunch time, and ID. The employee table contains information about the employees, including their creation time, email, ID, identification number, name, password, role ID, update time, and foreign keys to the enterprise and role tables.
The attendance_record table contains information about the attendance records of the employees, including their check-in location, check-in time, employee ID, remarks, and primary key (employee ID and check-in time). The to-do_item table contains information about the to-do items of the employees, including their completion status, creation time, employee ID, task description, update time, and primary key (employee ID and creation time).
The foreign keys in the employee and attendance_record tables reference the enterprise and role tables, respectively, while the foreign keys in the to-do_item table reference the employee table. The foreign key constraints are set to cascade on update and restrict on delete, ensuring that the data in the child tables is consistent with the data in the parent tables.
Overall, this schema provides a comprehensive structure for storing and managing information about an enterprise, its employees, and their attendance records, as well as their to-do items.
