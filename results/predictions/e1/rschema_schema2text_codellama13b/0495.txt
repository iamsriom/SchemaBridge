The rschema_0495 database is a relational database that stores information about a hospital. It has seven tables: Ward, Medicine, Patient, Medication_Dispensing, Doctor, Department, and Consultation_Record.
The Ward table contains information about the wards in the hospital, including their location, number of beds, and ward level. The Medicine table contains information about the medicines available in the hospital, including their inventory quantity, name, remarks, and sales price. The Patient table contains information about the patients in the hospital, including their age, contact information, gender, name, patient ID, and ward ID. The Medication_Dispensing table contains information about the medications dispensed to patients, including the medication retrieval time, medicine ID, patient ID, quantity retrieved, and primary key.
The Doctor table contains information about the doctors in the hospital, including their department ID, gender, ID, name, phone number, title, years of service, and foreign key to the Department table. The Department table contains information about the departments in the hospital, including their department ID, department name, department phone number, location, person in charge ID, and total number of personnel. The Consultation_Record table contains information about the consultation records, including the consultation time, diagnosis description, doctor ID, patient ID, remarks, treatment results, and primary key.
The foreign keys in the tables ensure referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate. For example, the foreign key in the Patient table references the Ward table, ensuring that the patient's ward ID is valid. The foreign key in the Medication_Dispensing table references the Medicine and Patient tables, ensuring that the medication ID and patient ID are valid. The foreign key in the Consultation_Record table references the Doctor and Patient tables, ensuring that the doctor ID and patient ID are valid.
The primary keys in the tables ensure entity integrity, ensuring that each record in the table is unique and cannot be duplicated. For example, the primary key in the Ward table is the ward ID, ensuring that each ward is unique. The primary key in the Medicine table is the medicine ID, ensuring that each medicine is unique. The primary key in the Patient table is the patient ID, ensuring that each patient is unique. The primary key in the Medication_Dispensing table is the combination of patient ID, medicine ID, and medication retrieval time, ensuring that each medication dispensing is unique. The primary key in the Consultation_Record table is the combination of patient ID, doctor ID, and consultation time, ensuring that each consultation record is unique.
In summary, the rschema_0495 database is a relational database that stores information about a hospital, including wards, medicines, patients, medication dispensing, doctors, departments, and consultation records. The tables in the database are designed to ensure referential integrity and entity integrity, ensuring that the data is consistent and accurate.
