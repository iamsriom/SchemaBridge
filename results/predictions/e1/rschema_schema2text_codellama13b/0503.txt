This database schema, named rschema_0503, is used to store information about materials, warehouses, drivers, and vehicles. It has seven tables: Material, Warehouse, Driver, Material_Storage, Material_Dispatch, Vehicle, and Material_Transportation.
The Material table contains information about the materials being stored, including their ID, name, source location, specification, type, volume, and weight. The Warehouse table contains information about the warehouses where the materials are stored, including their ID, name, address, current inventory level, maximum capacity, remaining capacity, and type. The Driver table contains information about the drivers who transport the materials, including their ID, name, age, gender, driving license number, and phone number.
The Material_Storage table contains information about the storage of materials in the warehouses, including the material ID, notes, storage quantity, storage time, and warehouse ID. The Material_Dispatch table contains information about the dispatch of materials from the warehouses, including the dispatch quantity, dispatch time, material ID, notes, and warehouse ID. The Vehicle table contains information about the vehicles used for transportation, including their ID, current load, license plate number, maximum load, vehicle type, and assigned driver ID. The Material_Transportation table contains information about the transportation of materials from the warehouses to their destinations, including the destination, driver ID, material ID, transportation start time, and vehicle ID.
The schema enforces entity integrity by using primary keys and foreign keys to ensure that each table has a unique identifier and that the relationships between tables are consistent. For example, the Material_Storage and Material_Dispatch tables have a composite primary key that combines the material ID, warehouse ID, and storage time or dispatch time, respectively. The Material_Transportation table has a composite primary key that combines the vehicle ID, material ID, and transportation start time. The foreign keys in the Material_Storage, Material_Dispatch, and Material_Transportation tables reference the primary keys of the Material, Warehouse, and Vehicle tables, respectively, to ensure that the relationships between the tables are consistent.
In addition, the schema uses referential integrity to ensure that the relationships between the tables are consistent. For example, the Material_Storage and Material_Dispatch tables have foreign keys that reference the Material and Warehouse tables, respectively, to ensure that the materials and warehouses referenced in the tables actually exist. The Material_Transportation table has foreign keys that reference the Driver, Material, and Vehicle tables, respectively, to ensure that the drivers, materials, and vehicles referenced in the table actually exist.
Overall, this schema provides a comprehensive and consistent way to store and manage information about materials, warehouses, drivers, and vehicles, and ensures that the relationships between the tables are consistent and accurate.
