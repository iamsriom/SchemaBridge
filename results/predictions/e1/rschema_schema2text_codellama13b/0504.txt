The rschema_0504 database is used to store information about cultural relics, their exhibitions, repairs, and monitoring records. It contains five tables: Cultural_Relic, Staff, Cultural_Relic_Exhibition, Cultural_Relic_Repair_Record, and Cultural_Relic_Monitoring_Record.
The Cultural_Relic table contains information about the cultural relics themselves, including their category, ID, name, year, and excavation site. The Staff table contains information about the staff members, including their name, gender, and phone number.
The Cultural_Relic_Exhibition table contains information about the exhibitions of cultural relics, including the ID of the cultural relic, the exhibition location, and the start and end times. The Cultural_Relic_Repair_Record table contains information about the repairs of cultural relics, including the ID of the cultural relic, the repair method, the start and end times, and the notes. The Cultural_Relic_Monitoring_Record table contains information about the monitoring of cultural relics, including the air quality, humidity, light, temperature, and notes.
Each table has primary keys and foreign keys to ensure data integrity. The Cultural_Relic table has a primary key of Cultural_Relic_ID, while the Staff table has a primary key of Staff_ID. The Cultural_Relic_Exhibition table has a composite primary key of Cultural_Relic_ID and Exhibition_Start_Time, while the Cultural_Relic_Repair_Record table has a composite primary key of Staff_ID, Cultural_Relic_ID, and Repair_Start_Time. The Cultural_Relic_Monitoring_Record table has a composite primary key of Cultural_Relic_ID, Monitoring_Time, and Person_In_Charge_ID.
The Cultural_Relic_Repair_Record table has foreign keys to the Cultural_Relic and Staff tables, ensuring that the repairs are linked to the correct cultural relic and staff member. The Cultural_Relic_Monitoring_Record table has foreign keys to the Cultural_Relic and Staff tables, ensuring that the monitoring records are linked to the correct cultural relic and staff member.
In summary, the rschema_0504 database is a comprehensive system for managing information about cultural relics, their exhibitions, repairs, and monitoring records. It uses five tables to store and organize this information, with primary and foreign keys to ensure data integrity.
