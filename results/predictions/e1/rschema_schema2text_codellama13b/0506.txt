The database schema "rschema_0506" is used to manage the construction of an electrical grid infrastructure project. It consists of 11 tables, each representing a different aspect of the project. The tables are interconnected through foreign keys, which ensure referential integrity and data consistency.
The "construction_management_unit" table contains information about the construction management units involved in the project, including their names, contact numbers, responsible persons, and associated projects. The "business_project_department_progress" table tracks the progress of business project departments, including their names, responsible persons, and project progress.
The "resource_allocation" table manages the allocation of resources to projects, including the resources' types, quantities, and allocation statuses. The "collaborative_task" table tracks the progress of collaborative tasks, including their names, responsible persons, task progress, and task priorities.
The "supervision_project_department" table manages the supervision of project departments, including their names, responsible persons, resource allocations, and personnel management. The "electrical_grid_infrastructure_project" table contains information about the electrical grid infrastructure project, including its name, construction progress, resource allocation, quality control, safety management, and project department.
The "resource_table" table manages the resources used in the project, including their types, quantities, and allocation statuses. The "collaborative_work_platform" table tracks the progress of collaborative work platforms, including their names, responsible persons, task progress, task priorities, and completion statuses.
The "project_management" table manages the management of projects, including their names, unit IDs, construction progress, resource allocation, quality control, and safety management. The "project_department_management" table tracks the management of project departments, including their names, project IDs, project progress, and issue reporting.
The "supervision_department_management" table manages the supervision of department management, including their names, project IDs, resource allocation, and personnel management.
Overall, the schema is designed to ensure data consistency, referential integrity, and data management for the construction of an electrical grid infrastructure project.
