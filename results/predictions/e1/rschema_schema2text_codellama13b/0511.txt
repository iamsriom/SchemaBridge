The database named rschema_0511 is a schema that contains four tables: Owner_Table, Department_Table, Employee_Table, and Charge_Table. The Owner_Table contains information about the owners of the houses, including their contact number, employer, house area, name, owner ID, and room number. The Department_Table contains information about the departments, including the department head, department ID, department name, and department phone number. The Employee_Table contains information about the employees, including their address, contact number, date of birth, department ID, employee ID, gender, name, password, position, and foreign key references to the Department_Table. The Charge_Table contains information about the charges, including the charge amount, charge date, charge type, employee ID, owner ID, quantity, room number, and foreign key references to the Employee_Table and Owner_Table. The primary key for the Charge_Table is a composite key consisting of room number, owner ID, and charge date. The foreign keys in the Charge_Table reference the Employee_Table and Owner_Table.
