The database schema for the database "rschema_0003" consists of 6 tables. The database is used for managing a warehouse, goods, loader, transaction records, and details of goods transaction records.
The "warehouse" table represents the warehouse information. It has three columns: warehouse_number (a unique identifier for each warehouse), address (the location of the warehouse), and capacity (the maximum capacity of the warehouse). The warehouse_number is the primary key of this table.
The "goods" table represents the goods information. It has two columns: goods_id (a unique identifier for each good), and goods_name (the name of the good). The goods_id is the primary key of this table.
The "loader" table represents the loader information. It has three columns: name (the name of the loader), phone (the contact number of the loader), and loader_id (a unique identifier for each loader). The loader_id is the primary key of this table.
The "transaction_record" table represents the transaction records. It has four columns: transaction_id (a unique identifier for each transaction), time (the time of the transaction), warehouse_number (the warehouse where the transaction took place), and transaction_type (the type of the transaction). The transaction_id is the primary key of this table. The warehouse_number is a foreign key referencing the warehouse table, and it has ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE options.
The "good_transaction_record_details" table represents the details of goods transaction records. It has three columns: goods_id (the good involved in the transaction), transaction_id (the transaction that the good is involved in), and quantity (the quantity of the good involved in the transaction). The combination of goods_id and transaction_id is the primary key of this table. Both goods_id and transaction_id are foreign keys referencing the goods and transaction_record tables, respectively, and they have ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE options.
Finally, the "stevedore-transaction_record" table represents the stevedore-transaction records. It has two columns: loader_id (the loader involved in the transaction), and transaction_id (the transaction that the loader is involved in). The combination of loader_id and transaction_id is the primary key of this table. Both loader_id and transaction_id are foreign keys referencing the loader and transaction_record tables, respectively, and they have ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE options.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. Whenever a record in the parent table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the child tables will be automatically deleted or updated to maintain the consistency of the data.
