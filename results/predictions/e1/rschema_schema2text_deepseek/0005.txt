The database schema named 'rschema_0005' consists of three tables: 'Service', 'User', and 'Service_usage_record'.
The 'Service' table represents services offered by the company. It has five columns: 'Service_ID' which is the primary key, 'Service_description' which stores a brief description of the service, 'Service_name' which stores the name of the service, 'Service_price' which stores the price of the service, and 'Service_type' which stores the type of the service.
The 'User' table represents users of the company. It has two columns: 'User_ID' which is the primary key and 'User_name' which stores the name of the user.
The 'Service_usage_record' table represents the usage records of the services. It has five columns: 'Operating_user_ID', 'Service_ID', 'Usage_duration', 'Usage_time'. 'Operating_user_ID' and 'Service_ID' are foreign keys referencing 'User' and 'Service' respectively. 'Usage_duration' stores whether the service was used for a duration or not, and 'Usage_time' stores the time when the service was used. 'Operating_user_ID' and 'Service_ID' together form the primary key of this table, ensuring entity integrity as each record represents a unique usage of a service by a user. The foreign key constraints ensure referential integrity, ensuring that the 'Operating_user_ID' and 'Service_ID' values exist in the 'User' and 'Service' tables respectively.
