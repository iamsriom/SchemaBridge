The database schema named "rschema_0008" consists of three tables: Equipment, Maintenance_Personnel, and Equipment_Maintenance.
The Equipment table represents the equipment in the facility. It has an Equipment_ID as the primary key, which is an integer. The Model, Name, and Usage_Status columns are all of type VARCHAR(255), allowing for a maximum of 255 characters each.
The Maintenance_Personnel table represents the personnel involved in maintaining the equipment. It has a Maintenance_Personnel_ID as the primary key, which is an integer. The Name, Email, and Telephone columns are all of type VARCHAR(255), allowing for a maximum of 255 characters each.
The Equipment_Maintenance table represents the maintenance records of the equipment. It has a pair of foreign keys, Equipment_ID and Maintenance_Personnel_ID, both of type INTEGER. Both columns are NOT NULL, meaning they must always have a value. The Maintenance_Result and Maintenance_Time columns are of type VARCHAR(255) and DATETIME respectively, allowing for a maximum of 255 characters for the result and a date and time for the maintenance time. The Equipment_ID column references the Equipment table's Equipment_ID, and the Maintenance_Personnel_ID column references the Maintenance_Personnel table's Maintenance_Personnel_ID.
This schema ensures referential integrity, as each maintenance record in the Equipment_Maintenance table corresponds to a valid equipment and maintenance personnel in their respective tables. It also ensures entity integrity, as each equipment and maintenance personnel has a unique identifier. The schema is exhaustive, covering all necessary aspects of the data model.
