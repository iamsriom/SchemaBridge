The database schema named 'rschema_0009' contains three tables: 'candidate', 'voter', and 'voting_record'.
The 'candidate' table represents a list of candidates for election. It has five columns: 'candidate_id', 'name', 'gender', 'date_of_birth', and 'position'. 'candidate_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'voter' table represents a list of voters. It has five columns: 'voter_id', 'name', 'gender', 'date_of_birth', and 'contact_information'. 'voter_id' is the primary key of this table. The 'candidate_id' column is a foreign key referencing the 'candidate_id' in the 'candidate' table, with on delete and on update cascade rules.
The 'voting_record' table represents the voting records. It has four columns: 'voter_id', 'candidate_id', 'voting_time', and 'voting_location'. 'voter_id' and 'candidate_id' are the primary key of this table. The 'candidate_id' column is a foreign key referencing the 'candidate_id' in the 'candidate' table, with on delete and on update cascade rules. The 'voter_id' column is a foreign key referencing the 'voter_id' in the 'voter' table, with on delete and on update cascade rules.
This schema ensures referential integrity by maintaining the relationship between the 'candidate' and 'voter' tables and the 'voting_record' table. It also ensures entity integrity by ensuring that each record in the 'candidate' table has a unique 'candidate_id', each record in the 'voter' table has a unique 'voter_id', and each record in the 'voting_record' table has a unique combination of 'voter_id' and 'candidate_id' and 'voting_time'.
