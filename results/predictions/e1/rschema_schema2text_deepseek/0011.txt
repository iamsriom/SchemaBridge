The database schema named "rschema_0011" consists of four tables: Farm, Operator, Equipment, and Maintenance_Record.
The Farm table represents a farm, with columns for Address, Contact_Information, Farm_ID (as the primary key), and Name. Each record in the Farm table represents a unique farm with its corresponding address, contact information, and name.
The Operator table represents an operator, with columns for Name, Operator_ID (as the primary key), and Phone. Each record in the Operator table represents a unique operator with its corresponding name and phone number.
The Equipment table represents the equipment used in the farms, with columns for Equipment_ID (as the primary key), Farm_ID (as a foreign key referencing the Farm table), Model, Name, Purchase_Date (as a datetime), and Usage_Status (as a boolean). Each record in the Equipment table represents a unique piece of equipment with its corresponding farm, model, name, purchase date, and usage status.
The Maintenance_Record table represents the maintenance records of the equipment, with columns for Equipment_ID (as a foreign key referencing the Equipment table), Maintenance_Date (as a datetime), Maintenance_Result, Maintenance_Type, Operator_ID (as a foreign key referencing the Operator table), and Device_ID (as a combination of Equipment_ID and Operator_ID and Maintenance_Date, acting as the primary key). Each record in the Maintenance_Record table represents a unique maintenance record with its corresponding equipment, date, result, type, and operator.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary key and foreign key constraints. The primary key constraints ensure that each record in each table is unique, while the foreign key constraints ensure that the relationships between the tables are maintained.
