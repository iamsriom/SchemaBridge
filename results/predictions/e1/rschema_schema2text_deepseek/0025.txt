The database schema for this database is named 'rschema_0025'. It contains 5 tables: 'tobacco_products', 'department', 'employee', 'production_record', and 'production_record_details'.
The 'tobacco_products' table represents the products of tobacco industry. It has product_id, product_name, type, production_date, shelf_life, sales_price, and production_batch_number as columns. All these columns allow null values and have a default charset of utf8mb3.
The 'department' table represents the departments in the tobacco industry. It has department_id, department_name, total_number_of_people_in_the_department, person_in_charge_id, and department_responsibilities as columns. The primary key is department_id. The person_in_charge_id is a foreign key referencing the employee table, and it has on delete and on update cascade rules.
The 'employee' table represents the employees in the tobacco industry. It has employee_id, name, gender, phone, position, joining_date, and department_id as columns. The primary key is employee_id. The department_id is a foreign key referencing the department table, and it has on delete and on update cascade rules.
The 'production_record' table represents the production records of tobacco products. It has product_id, sales_time, salesperson_id, customer_name, sales_quantity, sales_price, customer_phone, customer_address, and sales_type as columns. The primary key is a combination of salesperson_id, product_id, and sales_time. The product_id and salesperson_id are foreign keys referencing the tobacco_products and employee tables, respectively, and they have on delete and on update cascade rules.
Finally, the 'production_record_details' table represents the details of production records. It has production_record_id and producer_id as columns. The primary key is a combination of producer_id and production_record_id. The producer_id and production_record_id are foreign keys referencing the employee and production_record tables, respectively, and they have on delete and on update cascade rules.
