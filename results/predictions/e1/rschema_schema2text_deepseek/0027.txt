The database schema named 'rschema' comprises five tables: Department, Clothing, Employee, Production_Record, and Sales_Record.
The Department table holds information about the departments in the organization, including the contact information, department ID, department name, and the person in charge.
The Clothing table represents the inventory of clothing items, including the inventory count, name, price, product ID, production date, and style.
The Employee table details information about the employees, including the department ID, employee ID, gender, name, phone number, and position. The Department_ID is a foreign key referencing the Department table.
The Production_Record table records the production activities, including the producer employee ID, product ID, production time, and quantity. Both the Producer_Employee_ID and Product_ID are foreign keys referencing the Employee and Clothing tables respectively.
Lastly, the Sales_Record table records sales activities, including the customer, product ID, sales quantity, sales time, and salesperson employee ID. Both the Product_ID and Salesperson_Employee_ID are foreign keys referencing the Clothing and Employee tables respectively. The primary key of this table is a combination of Product_ID, Salesperson_Employee_ID, and Sales_Time.
