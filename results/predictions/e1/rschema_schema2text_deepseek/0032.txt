The database schema named "rschema_0032" contains four tables: Rental_Location, Customer, Vehicle, and Rental_Task.
The Rental_Location table represents the location where vehicles are rented out. It has three columns: Rental_Location_Address, Rental_Location_ID, and Rental_Location_Name. Rental_Location_ID is the primary key.
The Customer table represents the customers who rent out vehicles. It has three columns: Customer_ID, Customer_Name, and Customer_Phone_Number. Customer_ID is the primary key.
The Vehicle table represents the vehicles that are rented out. It has four columns: Rental_Location_ID, Vehicle_ID, Vehicle_Model, and Rental_Location_ID is a foreign key referencing the Rental_Location table. Vehicle_ID is the primary key.
The Rental_Task table represents the tasks associated with renting out vehicles. It has seven columns: Customer_Feedback, Customer_ID, Rental_Cost, Rental_Date, Rental_Duration, Vehicle_Condition_After_Rental, and Vehicle_ID. The combination of Vehicle_ID and Customer_ID and Rental_Date form the primary key. Customer_ID and Vehicle_ID are foreign keys referencing the Customer and Vehicle tables respectively.
All columns allow null values except for Rental_Location_ID in the Vehicle table. The default value for Rental_Cost is not set. The primary keys and foreign keys enforce entity integrity and referential integrity, respectively.
