The database schema named 'rschema_0040' contains five tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage and track the sales and contracts of properties.
The 'Property_Information' table holds information about each property, including its address, area, unique ID, sales price, and sales status. The 'Salesperson' table contains information about the salesperson, including their email, name, and phone number. The 'Customer_Information' table contains information about the customers, including their address, unique ID, email, name, and phone number.
The 'Sales_Contract' table is a junction table that links the 'Property_Information' and 'Customer_Information' tables. It contains information about the contract, including the contract date, contract price, contract signing time, contract specific content, and the foreign keys to the 'Customer_Information' and 'Property_Information' tables.
The 'Salesperson_Sold_Property' table is another junction table that links the 'Salesperson' and 'Sales_Contract' tables. It contains information about which salesperson sold which property, and the foreign keys to the 'Sales_Contract' and 'Salesperson' tables.
All tables have been designed with entity integrity in mind, with primary keys and foreign keys ensuring that each record in the table is unique and can be linked to other records in the database. The schema also ensures referential integrity, with foreign key constraints ensuring that the data in the 'Sales_Contract' and 'Salesperson_Sold_Property' tables matches the data in the 'Customer_Information' and 'Property_Information' tables.
The schema is designed to be flexible and can be updated and deleted as needed, with cascading actions ensuring that related data is also updated or deleted.
