The Land_ID field is a foreign key referencing the Land table, ensuring referential integrity.
Finally, the Land_Usage_Status table records the usage status of the land. It has Current_Status, Land_Usage_Area, Project_ID, Record_Personnel, and Record_Time as variable character fields with a maximum length of 255 characters and a datetime field respectively.
