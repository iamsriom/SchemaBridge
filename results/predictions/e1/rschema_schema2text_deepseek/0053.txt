The database schema named 'rschema_0053' consists of four tables: 'processing_pool', 'staff', 'processing_pool_record', and 'processing_pool_maintenance'.
The 'processing_pool' table represents a pool of processing units, with columns for 'processing_pool_id', 'name', 'location', and 'responsible_person_id'. The 'responsible_person_id' column is the primary key and is also a foreign key referencing the 'responsible_person_id' in the 'processing_pool_maintenance' table.
The 'staff' table stores information about the staff members, with columns for 'name', 'phone', 'email', 'responsibilities', and 'staff_id'. 'staff_id' is the primary key.
The 'processing_pool_record' table records the processing activities in the 'processing_pool', with columns for 'processing_pool_id', 'recording_staff_id', 'record_time', 'processing_capacity', and 'processing_efficiency'. The 'processing_pool_id' and 'record_time' form a composite primary key, and 'processing_pool_id' and 'recording_staff_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'processing_pool_id' and 'staff_id' in the 'processing_pool' and 'staff' tables respectively.
Finally, the 'processing_pool_maintenance' table keeps track of the maintenance activities in the 'processing_pool', with columns for 'processing_pool_id', 'maintenance_staff_id', 'maintenance_time', 'maintenance_content', and 'maintenance_cost'. The composite primary key is formed by 'maintenance_staff_id' and 'processing_pool_id' along with 'maintenance_time'. The 'processing_pool_id' and 'maintenance_staff_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'processing_pool_id' and 'staff_id' in the 'processing_pool' and 'staff' tables respectively. The DELETE and UPDATE rules are set to CASCADE, meaning that if the referenced record in the 'processing_pool' or 'staff' tables is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'processing_pool_maintenance' table will also be deleted or updated.
