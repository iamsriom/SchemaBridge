The database schema named 'rschema_0058' consists of three tables: Equipment, Inspection_Personnel, and Inspection_Record.
The Equipment table represents the equipment that is being inspected. It has an Equipment_ID as the primary key, which is an integer. The Equipment_Model and Equipment_Name are both VARCHAR(255) fields that store the model and name of the equipment respectively. The Installation_Date and Installation_Location are DATETIME fields that store the date and location of the equipment's installation.
The Inspection_Personnel table represents the personnel involved in the inspections. It has an Inspection_Personnel_ID as the primary key, which is an integer. The Contact_Information, Department, and Name are VARCHAR(255) fields that store the personnel's contact information, department, and name respectively.
The Inspection_Record table represents the records of the inspections. It has a composite primary key made up of Equipment_ID, Inspection_Personnel_ID, and Inspection_Date. Equipment_ID is a foreign key referencing the Equipment table, and Inspection_Personnel_ID is a foreign key referencing the Inspection_Personnel table. The Inspection_Content and Inspection_Date are DATETIME and VARCHAR(255) fields that store the content and date of the inspection respectively.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing the foreign key constraints. When an entry in the Equipment table is deleted, all corresponding entries in the Inspection_Record table will also be deleted due to the CASCADE delete rule. Similarly, when an entry in the Inspection_Personnel table is deleted, all corresponding entries in the Inspection_Record table will also be deleted due to the CASCADE delete rule.
The schema is exhaustive and covers all the necessary fields and relationships between the tables.
