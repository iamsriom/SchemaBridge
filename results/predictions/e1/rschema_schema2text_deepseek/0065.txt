The database schema named 'rschema_0065' consists of three tables: 'employee', 'project', and 'employee_participation'.
The 'employee' table represents a list of employees with various details such as 'employee_id', 'name', 'phone_number', and 'position'. The 'employee_id' is the primary key for this table.
The 'project' table contains information about the projects such as 'project_id', 'project_name', 'start_time', 'end_time', 'budget', and 'actual_expenses'. The 'project_id' is the primary key for this table.
The 'employee_participation' table is a junction table that links the 'employee' and 'project' tables. It contains 'employee_id', 'project_id', and 'employee_work_hours'. The combination of 'project_id' and 'employee_id' forms the primary key for this table. The 'employee_id' and 'project_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'employee' and 'project' tables respectively. The delete and update rules are set to CASCADE, meaning if a record in the 'employee' or 'project' tables is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'employee_participation' table will also be deleted or updated.
This schema ensures referential integrity by linking the 'employee' and 'project' tables through the 'employee_participation' table. It also ensures data domain integrity by defining the data types and constraints for each column.
