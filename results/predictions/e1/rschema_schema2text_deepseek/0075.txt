The database schema named 'rschema_0075' consists of four tables: 'Analyst', 'Stock', 'Analysis_Record', and 'Stock_Historical_Price'.
The 'Analyst' table is used to store information about the analysts. It includes columns for 'Analyst_ID' (integer, primary key), 'Contact_Number' (integer), 'Date_of_Hire' (datetime), and 'Name' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters).
The 'Stock' table stores information about the stocks. It has columns for 'Current_Price' (float), 'Stock_Code' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters, primary key), and 'Stock_Name' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters).
The 'Analysis_Record' table is used to record the analysis results of the analysts on the stocks. It includes columns for 'Analysis_Reasoning' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), 'Analysis_Result' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), 'Analyst_ID' (integer, not null), 'Date_of_Analysis' (datetime), and 'Stock_Code' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters, not null). The primary key of this table is a combination of 'Analyst_ID', 'Stock_Code', and 'Date_of_Analysis'. The 'Analyst_ID' and 'Stock_Code' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Analyst' and 'Stock' tables respectively.
Finally, the 'Stock_Historical_Price' table stores historical price data for the stocks. It includes columns for 'Closing_Price' (float), 'Date' (datetime), 'Highest_Price' (float), 'Lowest_Price' (float), 'Opening_Price' (float), 'Stock_Code' (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters, not null), 'Trading_Volume' (integer), and the primary key is a combination of 'Stock_Code' and 'Date'. The 'Stock_Code' column is a foreign key referencing the 'Stock' table.
