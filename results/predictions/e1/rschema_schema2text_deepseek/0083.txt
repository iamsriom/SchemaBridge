The database schema named rschema_0083 consists of three tables: Author, Category, and Article.
The Author table has three columns: Author_ID, which is the primary key and of type INTEGER, Email, which is of type VARCHAR(255), and Name, also of type VARCHAR(255).
The Category table has two columns: Category_ID, which is the primary key and of type INTEGER, and Name, of type VARCHAR(255).
The Article table has four columns: Article_ID, which is the primary key and of type INTEGER, Author_ID, of type INTEGER, Category_ID, of type INTEGER, and Name, of type VARCHAR(255). The Author_ID and Category_ID columns are foreign keys referencing the Author and Category tables respectively.
