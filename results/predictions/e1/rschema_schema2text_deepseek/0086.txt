The database schema named 'rschema_0086' consists of 7 tables. The tables are described as follows:
1. **Material**: This table holds information about materials. It includes a description, a unique Material_ID, a name, a type, and a unit. The Material_ID is the primary key.
2. **Region**: This table represents regions. It includes a unique Region_ID and a Region_Name. The Region_ID is the primary key.
3. **Country**: This table contains information about countries. It includes a unique Country_Code, a unique Country_ID, and a Country_Name. The Country_ID is the primary key.
4. **Procurement_Application**: This table represents procurement applications. It includes an Application_Quantity, an Application_Time, a Material_ID, a unique Procurement_Application_ID, and remarks. The Material_ID is a foreign key referencing the Material table.
5. **Supplier**: This table represents suppliers. It includes an Address, a Country_ID, a Fax, a Name, a Phone, a Region_ID, a unique Supplier_ID, and a Tax_ID. The Country_ID and Region_ID are foreign keys referencing the Country and Region tables respectively.
6. **Company**: This table represents companies. It includes an Activation_Time, an Address, a unique Company_ID, a Country_ID, a Creation_Time, an ERP_System_Identifier, an Email, a Name, a Password, a Phone, a Region_ID, a Security_Answer, a Security_Question, a Status, a Tax_ID, an Update_Time, a Username, a VAT_Registration_Number. The Country_ID and Region_ID are foreign keys referencing the Country and Region tables respectively.
7. **Supplier_Production_Material**: This table represents the materials produced by suppliers. It includes a Material_ID, a Production_Quantity, a Production_Time, Remarks, and a Supplier_ID. The Material_ID and Supplier_ID are foreign keys referencing the Material and Supplier tables respectively. The combination of Material_ID and Supplier_ID, Production_Time forms the primary key.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary and foreign key constraints. It also ensures entity integrity by ensuring uniqueness of primary keys and referential integrity by ensuring that foreign keys reference valid primary keys in the referenced tables.
