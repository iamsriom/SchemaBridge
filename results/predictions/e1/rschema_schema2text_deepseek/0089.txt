The database schema named 'rschema_0089' consists of three tables: 'Block', 'Account', and 'Transaction'.
The 'Block' table represents the blockchain data, with each record containing a unique 'Block_Hash', the 'Height' of the block, the 'Merkle_Root', the 'Previous_Block_Hash', and the 'Timestamp'. The 'Previous_Block_Hash' column is a foreign key referencing the 'Block_Hash' in the 'Block' table, ensuring referential integrity.
The 'Account' table holds information about the accounts, with each record containing an 'Address' and the 'Balance'. The 'Address' is the primary key of this table.
Finally, the 'Transaction' table records all transactions. Each record contains a 'Block_Hash', the 'Fee', the 'Input_Address', the 'Output_Address', the 'Timestamp', and a unique 'Transaction_Hash'. The 'Block_Hash' column is a foreign key referencing the 'Block_Hash' in the 'Block' table, the 'Input_Address' and 'Output_Address' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Address' in the 'Account' table, ensuring referential integrity.
This schema allows for the tracking of blockchain transactions, balances, and the relationships between blocks and accounts.
