The Technical_solution_feedback table is used to store feedback about technical solutions. It includes feedback content, feedback date, satisfaction level, and suggestions from users. The User_ID and Technical_solution_ID are foreign keys referencing the User and Technical_solution tables respectively, ensuring referential integrity.
Finally, the Technical_solution_evaluation table stores evaluations of technical solutions.
