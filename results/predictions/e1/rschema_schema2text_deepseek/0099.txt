The 'speaker_id' column is the primary key, ensuring entity integrity as it cannot be null and must be unique.
The 'conference' table is used to store information about the conferences. The 'conference_id' column is the primary key, and it references the 'speaker_id' column in the 'speaker' table. The foreign key constraints on 'speaker_id' allow for cascading deletions and updates, meaning if a speaker is deleted or updated, all associated conference records will also be deleted or updated.
The 'speaker_conference' table is a junction table that links the 'speaker' and 'conference' tables. The primary key of this table is a composite of 'conference_id' and 'speaker_id', ensuring entity integrity. The foreign key constraints on 'conference_id' and 'speaker_id' allow for cascading deletions and updates, meaning if a conference is deleted or updated, all associated speaker-conference records will also be deleted or updated. Similarly, if a speaker is deleted or updated, all associated speaker-conference records will be deleted or updated.
