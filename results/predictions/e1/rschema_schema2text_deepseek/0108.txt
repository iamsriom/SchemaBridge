The database schema named 'rschema_0108' consists of two tables: 'Department' and 'Employee'.
The 'Department' table represents the departmental information in the organization. It has three columns: 'Department_ID' as the primary key, 'Department_name' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, and 'Department_responsibilities' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters.
The 'Employee' table represents the employee information in the organization. It has nine columns: 'Age' as an integer field, 'Base_salary' as a floating-point field, 'Department_ID' as an integer field that cannot be null, 'Email_address' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, 'Employee_ID' as the primary key, 'Gender' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, 'Name' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, 'Phone_number' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, 'Position' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters, and 'Social_security_number' as a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters.
The 'Department_ID' in the 'Employee' table is a foreign key that references the 'Department_ID' in the 'Department' table, establishing a relationship between the two tables. This schema ensures referential integrity by preventing deletion of a department if there are employees associated with it.
