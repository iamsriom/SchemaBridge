The database schema named 'rschema_0112' consists of 8 tables. The 'Employee' table is the main entity in the schema, holding information about the employees such as their base salary, commission rate, email, employee number, hire date, name, office phone, position, and their supervisor's ID. The 'Employee_ID' is the primary key and a foreign key in the 'Office_location' table.
The 'Product' table holds information about the products, including brand, cost price, description, inventory quantity, model, name, product ID, specification, suggested retail price, and supplier. The 'Product_ID' is the primary key in this table.
The 'Order' table contains information about the orders, including actual shipping date, notes, order ID, order date, order status, required delivery date, and special instructions. The 'Order_ID' is the primary key in this table.
The 'Office_location' table holds information about the office locations, including city, contact phone, country, fax, manager's employee ID, office location ID, postal code, and street address. The 'Office_location_ID' is the primary key and a foreign key in the 'Employee' table.
The 'Customer' table contains information about the customers, including city, contact person name, contact person phone, contact person position, country, credit limit, customer ID, customer company name, dedicated sales rep employee ID, postal code, state/province, and street. The 'Customer_ID' is the primary key and a foreign key in the 'Sales_performance_record' and 'Payment_record' tables.
The 'Sales_performance_record' table holds information about the sales performance records, including record ID, sales person ID, total sales, and year. The 'Record_ID' is the primary key and a foreign key in the 'Employee' table.
The 'Order_detail' table contains information about the order details, including line number, order ID, product ID, quantity, and unit price. The combination of 'Order_ID' and 'Product_ID' forms the primary key. Both 'Order_ID' and 'Product_ID' are foreign keys in the 'Order' and 'Product' tables respectively.
Finally, the 'Payment_record' table holds information about the payment records, including check clearing date, check number, customer accounts receivable, invoice amount, paying customer ID, payment date, record ID, and foreign key 'Paying_customer_ID' referencing the 'Customer_ID' in the 'Customer' table.
