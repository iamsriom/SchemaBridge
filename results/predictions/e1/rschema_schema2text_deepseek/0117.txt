The database schema named 'rschema_0117' consists of three tables: 'user', 'data_record', and 'operation_data'.
The 'user' table is used to store user information. It has six columns: 'id', 'user_name', 'password', 'phone', 'creation_time', and 'id' is the primary key.
The 'data_record' table is used to store data records. It has seven columns: 'data_record_id', 'name', 'data_content', 'user_id_who_created_the_record', 'creation_time', 'user_id_of_the_most_recent_operation', and 'last_operation_time'. The 'data_record_id' is the primary key, and two foreign keys: 'user_id_of_the_most_recent_operation' and 'user_id_who_created_the_record' reference the 'id' in the 'user' table.
The 'operation_data' table is used to store operation data. It has seven columns: 'user_id', 'data_record_id', 'operation_type', 'operation_time', 'remarks', and 'operation_content'. The primary key is a composite of 'user_id', 'data_record_id', and 'operation_time', and two foreign keys: 'data_record_id' references 'data_record_id' in the 'data_record' table and 'user_id' references 'id' in the 'user' table. The 'operation_data' table has referential integrity constraints: on delete and update, the related data in 'data_record' and 'user' tables will be cascaded.
