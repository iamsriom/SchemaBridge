The database schema named 'rschema_0118' consists of six tables: 'department', 'employee_attendance', 'employee', 'employee_transfer_department', 'employee_leave', and 'employee_overtime'.
The 'department' table represents the department information, including the department ID, location, name, responsibilities, and the ID of the person in charge. It also contains the total number of employees in the department.
The 'employee_attendance' table records the attendance information of employees, including the employee ID, punch time, and punch type.
The 'employee' table contains information about employees, including their age, contact information, date of birth, department ID, employee ID, gender, name, position, salary, and time of employment. The department ID is a foreign key referencing the department table.
The 'employee_transfer_department' table records the transfer of employees between departments, including the employee ID, transfer time, and the IDs of the departments involved. Both the employee ID and the department IDs are foreign keys referencing the 'employee' and 'department' tables respectively.
The 'employee_leave' table records the leave information of employees, including the employee ID, leave time, and leave type. The employee ID is a foreign key referencing the 'employee' table.
Finally, the 'employee_overtime' table records the overtime information of employees, including the employee ID, overtime time, and overtime type. The employee ID is a foreign key referencing the 'employee' table.
Each table has primary keys and foreign keys to enforce entity integrity and referential integrity. For instance, the department ID in the 'employee' table references the department_ID in the 'department' table, ensuring that the department IDs in the 'employee' table exist in the 'department' table. Similarly, the employee ID in the 'employee_transfer_department' table references the employee ID in the 'employee' table, ensuring that the employee IDs in the 'employee_transfer_department' table exist in the 'employee' table. The 'employee_leave' and 'employee_overtime' tables also have foreign keys referencing the 'employee' table to maintain referential integrity.
