The database schema named 'rschema_0135' comprises three tables: 'shelf', 'product', and 'brand'. The 'shelf' table is used to store information about the shelves in a warehouse, including the shelf's unique identifier, name, location, and the responsible person. The 'product' table is used to store information about the products in the warehouse, including the product's unique identifier, name, category, selling price, the shelf it belongs to, a specific location within the shelf, and the brand of the product. The 'brand' table is used to store information about the brands of the products in the warehouse, including the brand's unique identifier and the name of the brand. The primary keys of all tables are their unique identifiers. The 'product' table has two foreign keys, 'brand' and 'belonging_shelf', which reference the 'brand_id' and 'shelf_id' of the 'brand' and 'shelf' tables respectively. The delete and update rules for these foreign keys are set to CASCADE, meaning that if a record in the 'brand' or 'shelf' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'product' table will also be deleted or updated.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary key constraints on all tables, ensuring that each record in a table has a unique identifier. It also enforces referential integrity by using foreign key constraints to link records between tables. This ensures that every product has a valid brand and shelf, and that if a shelf or brand is deleted, all products associated with it will also be deleted to maintain data consistency.
