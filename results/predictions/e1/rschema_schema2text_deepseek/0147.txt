The database schema named 'rschema_0147' consists of 6 tables: Class, Member, Announcement, Message, Log, and Application.
The 'Class' table represents the classes in the school. It has columns for Class_ID (integer, primary key), Class_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Department_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255), and Grade (varchar with a maximum length of 255).
The 'Member' table represents the members of the school. It has columns for Age (integer), Class_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Class.Class_ID), Contact_Number (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Email (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Member_ID (integer, primary key), Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Password (varchar with a maximum length of 255), and Workplace (varchar with a maximum length of 255).
The 'Announcement' table represents the announcements made by the school. It has columns for Announcement_Content (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Announcement_ID (integer, primary key), Announcement_Time (datetime), and Class_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Class.Class_ID).
The 'Message' table represents the messages exchanged between members. It has columns for Message_Content (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Read_Flag (boolean), Receiver_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Member.Member_ID), Send_Time (datetime), and Sender_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Member.Member_ID). The primary key of this table is a composite of Sender_ID, Receiver_ID, and Send_Time.
The 'Log' table represents the mood logs of the members. It has columns for Log_Content (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Log_ID (integer, primary key), Member_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Member.Member_ID), Mood (varchar with a maximum length of 255), and Time (datetime).
Finally, the 'Application' table represents the applications made by the members. It has columns for Application_Content (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Application_ID (integer, primary key), Application_Time (datetime), Approval_Flag (boolean), and Member_ID (integer, not null, foreign key referencing Member.Member_ID).
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary keys, foreign keys, and not null constraints. It also allows for referential integrity by maintaining the relationship between tables.
