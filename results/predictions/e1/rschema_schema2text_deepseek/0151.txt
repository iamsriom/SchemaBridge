The database schema named 'rschema_0151' consists of three tables: 'Customer', 'Savings_Institution', and 'Transaction_Record'.
The 'Customer' table represents customers of a financial institution. It has columns for 'Address', 'Identity_Card_Number' (primary key), 'Name', and 'Phone_Number'.
The 'Savings_Institution' table represents the savings institutions that the financial institution interacts with. It has columns for 'Address', 'Name', 'Phone_Number', and 'Savings_Institution_ID' (primary key).
The 'Transaction_Record' table represents the transactions made by the customers. It has columns for 'Identity_Card_Number' (foreign key referencing 'Customer'), 'Interest_Rate', 'Maturity_Date', 'Password', 'Savings_Institution_ID' (foreign key referencing 'Savings_Institution'), 'Transaction_Amount', 'Transaction_Time', and 'Type'. The primary key of this table is a composite key consisting of 'Identity_Card_Number', 'Savings_Institution_ID', and 'Transaction_Time'.
The 'Identity_Card_Number' and 'Savings_Institution_ID' columns in the 'Transaction_Record' table are not allowed to have null values. The 'Transaction_Amount' column is of integer type and represents the amount of money involved in the transaction. The 'Type' column is of varchar type and represents the type of transaction (e.g., deposit, withdrawal). The 'Interest_Rate' column is of float type and represents the interest rate applicable for the transaction. The 'Maturity_Date' and 'Transaction_Time' columns are of datetime type and represent the maturity date and time of the transaction respectively.
