The database schema named 'rschema_0163' contains five tables: Sugar_cane_variety, Employee, Plot, Planting_record, and Fertilization_record.
The Sugar_cane_variety table represents the variety of sugar cane. It has columns for Maturation_cycle, Suitable_climate, Suitable_soil, Variety_ID, and Variety_name. Variety_ID is the primary key and it is an integer.
The Employee table represents the employees of the organization. It has columns for Employee_ID, Gender, Name, Phone, and Working_hours. Employee_ID is the primary key and it is an integer.
The Plot table represents the plots in the field. It has columns for Area, Location, Organic_matter_content, Person_in_charge_ID, Plot_ID, Soil_pH, and Soil_type. Person_in_charge_ID is a foreign key referencing the Employee_ID in the Employee table. Plot_ID is the primary key and it is an integer.
The Planting_record table represents the records of sugar cane planting. It has columns for Planter_ID, Planting_method, Planting_quantity, Planting_time, Plot_ID, and Variety_ID. The combination of Plot_ID and Variety_ID and Planting_time form the primary key. Both Planter_ID and Plot_ID are foreign keys referencing the Employee_ID and Plot_ID in the Employee and Plot tables respectively.
The Fertilization_record table represents the records of sugar cane fertilization. It has columns for Fertilization_amount, Fertilization_method, Fertilization_time, Fertilization_type, Person_in_charge_ID, Plot_ID, Remarks. The combination of Person_in_charge_ID and Plot_ID and Fertilization_time form the primary key. Both Person_in_charge_ID and Plot_ID are foreign keys referencing the Employee_ID and Plot_ID in the Employee and Plot tables respectively.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary and foreign key constraints. It also allows referential integrity by ensuring that the foreign key references in the tables point to the primary key in the referenced tables.
