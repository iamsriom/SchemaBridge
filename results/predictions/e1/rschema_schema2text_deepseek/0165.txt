The database schema named "rschema_0165" contains four tables: Meteorological_Station, Operator, Historical_Evolution_Information, and Audit_Information.
The Meteorological_Station table represents a meteorological station, with columns for Elevation (FLOAT), Geographic_Environment_Description (VARCHAR(255)), Latitude (FLOAT), Longitude (FLOAT), Observation_Elements_List (VARCHAR(255)), Observation_Instrument_Name_and_Model (VARCHAR(255)), Observation_Record_Medium (VARCHAR(255)), Observation_Standards (VARCHAR(255)), Observation_Time (DATETIME), Station_ID (INTEGER, PRIMARY KEY), and Station_Name and Station_Type (VARCHAR(255)).
The Operator table holds information about the operators, with columns for Email (VARCHAR(255)), Level (VARCHAR(255)), Name (VARCHAR(255)), Operator_ID (INTEGER, PRIMARY KEY), Phone (INTEGER), and Role (VARCHAR(255)).
The Historical_Evolution_Information table contains information about changes made to the meteorological station, with columns for Belonging_Station_ID (INTEGER, NOT NULL), Change_ID (INTEGER, PRIMARY KEY), Change_Type (VARCHAR(255)), End_Time (DATETIME), New_Value (VARCHAR(255)), Old_Value (VARCHAR(255)), Operation_Status (VARCHAR(255)), Operation_Time (DATETIME), Operator_ID (INTEGER), Reason_for_Change (VARCHAR(255)), Start_Time (DATETIME), and Station_ID (INTEGER). The Belonging_Station_ID is a foreign key referencing the Station_ID in the Meteorological_Station table.
Finally, the Audit_Information table records the audit information, with columns for Audit_Comments (VARCHAR(255)), Audit_Status (VARCHAR(255)), Audit_Time (DATETIME), Change_ID (INTEGER, NOT NULL), Operator_ID (INTEGER, NOT NULL), and is the PRIMARY KEY for these three columns. The Change_ID and Operator_ID are foreign keys referencing the Change_ID and Operator_ID in the Historical_Evolution_Information table, respectively.
