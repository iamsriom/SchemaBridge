The database schema named 'rschema_0170' consists of three tables: 'store', 'department', and 'employee'.
The 'store' table represents a store with various attributes such as 'store_id', 'name', 'address', and 'contact_phone_number'. It has 'store_id' as the primary key.
The 'department' table represents a department within a store. It has attributes such as 'department_id', 'department_name', 'distribution_location', 'contact_phone_number', 'manager', 'manager_tenure', and 'belonging_store'. 'department_id' is the primary key and 'manager' and 'belonging_store' are foreign keys referencing 'employee' and 'store' respectively.
The 'employee' table represents an employee within a department. It has attributes such as 'employee_id', 'name', 'position', 'phone_number', 'salary', 'emergency_contact', and 'belonging_department'. 'employee_id' is the primary key and 'emergency_contact' and 'belonging_department' are foreign keys referencing 'employee' and 'department' respectively. The 'employee' table also has two foreign key constraints that cascade on delete and update operations.
This schema ensures referential integrity by linking the 'employee', 'department', and 'store' tables together. It also ensures entity integrity by having primary keys and unique constraints on the tables. The schema is exhaustive and covers all the necessary attributes for each table.
