The database schema named 'rschema_0172' contains three tables: 'Patient', 'Medical_Record', and 'Alert_Record'. The 'Patient' table is used to store patient information, including their age, contact number, emergency contact number, and name. Each patient is uniquely identified by a 'Patient_ID' which is a primary key.
The 'Medical_Record' table stores medical records for each patient. It includes information such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, and the time of the record. The 'Patient_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Patient' table, ensuring that each medical record is associated with a valid patient.
Finally, the 'Alert_Record' table stores alert records for each medical record. It includes information such as the content of the alert and the time of the alert. The 'Abnormal_Record_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Medical_Record' table, ensuring that each alert is associated with a valid medical record. This schema ensures referential integrity by linking the 'Patient', 'Medical_Record', and 'Alert_Record' tables together.
