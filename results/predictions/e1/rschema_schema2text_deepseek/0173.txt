The database schema named 'rschema_0173' consists of four tables: 'Borrower', 'Book', 'Borrower_Loaned_Books', and 'Fines'.
The 'Borrower' table represents the borrowers of the library. It has columns for 'Borrower_ID' as the primary key, 'Fines' as the total fines owed by the borrower, 'Name' as the borrower's name, and 'Overdue_Books' as the number of overdue books.
The 'Book' table represents the books in the library. It has columns for 'Author' as the author's name, 'Book_ID' as the primary key, 'Book_Title' as the title of the book, 'Fine' as the fine amount for overdue books, 'Loan_Duration' as the loan duration in days, 'Loan_Status' as the current loan status of the book, 'Publication_Date' as the publication date of the book, 'Publisher' as the name of the publisher, and 'Stock' as the current stock of the book.
The 'Borrower_Loaned_Books' table represents the books loaned by the borrowers. It has columns for 'Book_ID' and 'Borrower_ID' as foreign keys referencing the 'Book' and 'Borrower' tables respectively. It also includes 'Expected_Return_Date' as the expected return date of the book and 'Loan_Date' as the actual loan date.
Finally, the 'Fines' table represents the fines charged for overdue books. It has columns for 'Book_ID' and 'Borrower_ID' as foreign keys referencing the 'Book' and 'Borrower' tables respectively. It also includes 'Fine_Amount' as the fine amount and 'Overdue_Days' as the number of overdue days.
All columns allow null values except for 'Book_ID' and 'Borrower_ID' in the 'Borrower_Loaned_Books' and 'Fines' tables. The primary keys are 'Borrower_ID' and 'Book_ID' in the 'Borrower_Loaned_Books' table and 'Borrower_ID' and 'Book_ID' in the 'Fines' table. The foreign keys enforce referential integrity by ensuring that the referenced records in the 'Book' and 'Borrower' tables exist. The update and delete rules are set to CASCADE or RESTRICT to maintain referential integrity.
