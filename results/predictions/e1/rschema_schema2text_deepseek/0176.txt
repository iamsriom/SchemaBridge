The database schema named 'rschema_0176' consists of four tables: 'Flight', 'Passenger', 'Discount_Information', and 'Ticket_Purchase_Record'.
The 'Flight' table represents flight information, including the airline, arrival and departure times, departure and destination cities, flight ID, and ticket price. The primary key for this table is 'Flight_ID'.
The 'Passenger' table stores information about passengers, including their date of birth, gender, ID, name, phone number, and VIP discount. The primary key for this table is 'Passenger_ID'.
The 'Discount_Information' table contains information about discount rates and their duration, with 'Flight_ID' as a foreign key referencing the 'Flight' table.
The 'Ticket_Purchase_Record' table records ticket purchases, including the flight ID, passenger ID, purchase amount, ticket order number, and travel date. Both 'Flight_ID' and 'Passenger_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Flight' and 'Passenger' tables respectively.
All columns allow null values, except for 'Flight_ID' in the 'Discount_Information' and 'Flight_ID' and 'Passenger_ID' in the 'Ticket_Purchase_Record' tables, which are not allowed to have null values. There are also cascade delete and update rules on foreign keys, meaning if a record in the 'Flight' or 'Passenger' tables is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'Discount_Information' and 'Ticket_Purchase_Record' tables will also be affected.
The primary keys and unique constraints ensure entity integrity in the database, ensuring that each record in the tables is unique and can be identified by its primary key. The foreign keys ensure referential integrity, linking the tables together in a meaningful way.
Overall, this schema is designed to manage flight information, passenger data, discount information, and ticket purchase records effectively.
