The database schema named 'rschema_0182' consists of three tables: 'market', 'agricultural_products', and 'sales_record'.
The 'market' table represents a market with various attributes such as 'market_id', 'name', 'address', and 'contact_information'. The 'market_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'agricultural_products' table represents agricultural products with attributes such as 'product_id', 'name', 'specification', 'price', and 'current_stock'. The 'product_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'sales_record' table represents sales records with attributes such as 'market_id', 'product_id', 'sales_date', 'sales_quantity', 'sales_unit_price', and 'operator'. The combination of 'product_id' and 'market_id' and 'sales_date' forms the primary key of this table.
The 'sales_record' table has two foreign keys: 'product_id' and 'market_id'. The 'product_id' references the 'agricultural_products' table and the 'market_id' references the 'market' table. The delete and update rules for these foreign keys are set to CASCADE, meaning that if a record in the 'agricultural_products' or 'market' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'sales_record' table will also be deleted or updated.
