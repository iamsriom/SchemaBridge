The database schema named 'rschema_0183' consists of three tables: 'tool', 'usage_record', and 'maintenance_record'.
The 'tool' table is used to store information about the tools in the facility. It has five columns: 'Maintenance_Count', 'Tool_ID', 'Tool_Model', 'Tool_Name', and 'Usage_Frequency'. 'Maintenance_Count' is an integer representing the number of times the tool has been maintained. 'Tool_ID' is the primary key and is an integer. 'Tool_Model' and 'Tool_Name' are both varchar(255) fields that store the model and name of the tool respectively. 'Usage_Frequency' is a float field that records the frequency of usage of the tool.
The 'usage_record' table records the usage of tools in the facility. It has five columns: 'Record_ID', 'Tool_ID', 'Usage_Location', 'Usage_Time', and 'User'. 'Record_ID' is the primary key and is an integer. 'Tool_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Tool_ID' in the 'tool' table. It is not nullable, meaning it cannot be left empty. 'Usage_Location' and 'User' are varchar(255) fields that store the location and the user who used the tool respectively. 'Usage_Time' is a datetime field that records the time of usage.
The 'maintenance_record' table records the maintenance of tools in the facility. It has five columns: 'Maintenance_Content', 'Maintenance_ID', 'Maintenance_Person', 'Maintenance_Time', and 'Tool_ID'. 'Maintenance_ID' is the primary key and is an integer. 'Tool_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Tool_ID' in the 'tool' table. It is not nullable. 'Maintenance_Content', 'Maintenance_Person', and 'Maintenance_Time' are varchar(255), varchar(255), and float fields respectively that store the content, the person who performed the maintenance, and the time of maintenance respectively.
The 'tool', 'usage_record', and 'maintenance_record' tables are interconnected through the 'Tool_ID' foreign key. This schema ensures referential integrity as it maintains the consistency of data across tables.
