The database schema named rschema_0184 consists of three tables: 'member', 'room', and 'booking'.
The 'member' table represents a member of the hotel. It has five columns: 'member_id', 'name', 'phone', 'registration_date', and 'points'. 'member_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'room' table represents the rooms in the hotel. It has three columns: 'room_number', 'room_type', and 'price'. 'room_number' is the primary key of this table.
The 'booking' table represents the bookings made by members. It has seven columns: 'member_id', 'room_number', 'booking_date', 'check-in_date', 'check-out_date', 'booking_status', and 'points_change'. The primary key of this table is a composite key consisting of 'booking_date', 'member_id', and 'room_number'.
The 'member_id' and 'room_number' columns in the 'booking' table are foreign keys referencing the 'member' and 'room' tables respectively. The DELETE and UPDATE rules for these foreign keys are set to CASCADE, meaning that if a record in the 'member' or 'room' tables is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'booking' table will also be deleted or updated.
This schema ensures the integrity of the data by enforcing referential integrity through foreign keys, maintaining the relationship between the 'member', 'room', and 'booking' tables.
