The database schema for the database named rschema_0188 consists of seven tables. The database is used for managing various aspects of a company, including product production, inventory, sales, and performance evaluations.
The 'product' table represents the products that the company produces. It has five columns: 'product_id', 'product_name', 'product_type', 'production_date', and 'price'. The 'product_id' is the primary key for this table.
The 'department' table represents the departments within the company. It has four columns: 'department_id', 'department_name', 'manager', and 'contact_information'. The 'department_id' is the primary key and the 'manager' column is a foreign key referencing the 'employee' table.
The 'employee' table represents the employees within the company. It has six columns: 'employee_id', 'employee_name', 'employee_gender', 'employee_phone', 'job_position', and 'department_id'. The 'employee_id' is the primary key and the 'department_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'department' table.
The 'production_record' table represents the production records of the products. It has four columns: 'product_id', 'producer', 'production_time', and 'production_quantity'. The combination of 'production_time' and 'product_id' and 'producer' forms the primary key and 'producer' is a foreign key referencing the 'employee' table.
The 'inventory_record' table represents the inventory records of the products. It has three columns: 'product_id', 'stock_in_time', and 'stock_quantity'. The 'product_id' is the primary key and the 'product_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'product' table.
The 'sales_record' table represents the sales records of the products. It has five columns: 'salesperson', 'customer', 'sales_time', 'product_id', and 'sales_quantity'. The combination of 'product_id', 'salesperson', and 'sales_time' forms the primary key and both 'product_id' and 'salesperson' are foreign keys referencing the 'product' and 'employee' tables respectively.
The 'performance_evaluation' table represents the performance evaluations of the departments. It has three columns: 'department_id', 'evaluation_time', and 'evaluation_score'. The combination of 'department_id' and 'evaluation_time' forms the primary key and 'department_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'department' table.
All foreign keys in this schema have on delete cascade and on update cascade rules, which means that when the referenced record in the parent table is updated or deleted, the corresponding records in the child tables will also be updated or deleted.
