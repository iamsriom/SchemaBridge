The database schema for the database named rschema_0193 consists of five tables: 'material', 'project', 'material_usage_record', 'department', and 'employee'.
The 'material' table represents the materials that are used in the projects. It has five columns: 'material_id', 'material_name', 'specifications', 'unit_price', and 'inventory'. 'material_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'project' table represents the projects. It has seven columns: 'project_id', 'project_name', 'start_date', 'expected_completion_date', 'actual_completion_date', 'project_budget', and 'project_location'. 'project_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'material_usage_record' table represents the usage records of the materials in the projects. It has six columns: 'usage_record_id', 'project_id', 'material_id', 'usage_date', 'usage_quantity', and 'usage_employee_id'. 'usage_record_id' is the primary key of this table. 'employee_id', 'material_id', and 'project_id' are foreign keys referencing 'employee', 'material', and 'project' respectively.
The 'department' table represents the departments. It has two columns: 'department_id' and 'department_name'. 'department_id' is the primary key of this table.
The 'employee' table represents the employees. It has three columns: 'employee_id', 'employee_name', and 'department_id'. 'employee_id' is the primary key of this table. 'department_id' is a foreign key referencing 'department' and has ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE options.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. When a record in the 'employee', 'material', or 'project' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'material_usage_record' table will also be deleted or updated to maintain the consistency of the data.
