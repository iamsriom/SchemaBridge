The database schema named 'rschema_0197' comprises three tables: 'Personnel', 'Project', and 'Construction_Cost_Record'.
The 'Personnel' table represents the personnel in the organization, with columns for 'Email', 'Name', 'Personnel_ID', 'Phone_Number', and 'Role'. The 'Personnel_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Project' table holds information about the projects managed by the personnel. It has columns for 'Project_ID', 'Project_Location', 'Project_Manager_ID', and 'Project_Name'. The 'Project_ID' is the primary key and 'Project_Manager_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Personnel_ID' in the 'Personnel' table.
The 'Construction_Cost_Record' table keeps track of the construction costs associated with each project. It has columns for 'Cost_Amount', 'Expense_Type', 'Project_ID', 'Record_Date', 'Record_ID', and 'Recording_Staff_ID'. The 'Record_ID' is the primary key and both 'Project_ID' and 'Recording_Staff_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Project_ID' and 'Personnel_ID' in the 'Personnel' and 'Project' tables respectively.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. When a record in the 'Project' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'Construction_Cost_Record' table will be automatically deleted or updated due to the cascade delete and update rules. Similarly, when a record in the 'Personnel' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'Construction_Cost_Record' table will be deleted or updated.
The schema is exhaustive, covering all the necessary tables and their columns, ensuring that all the data in the database is properly organized and related.
