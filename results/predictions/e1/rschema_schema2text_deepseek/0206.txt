The 'Flora_and_Fauna_Record' table is used to record information about flora and fauna. It has six columns: 'Quantity', 'Record_ID', 'Record_Time', 'Recorder', 'Region_ID', and 'Type'. 'Region_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Region_ID' in the 'Region' table. 'Record_ID' is the primary key.
The 'Environmental_Change_Record' table is used to record environmental changes. It has seven columns: 'Change_ID', 'Change_Time', 'Humidity', 'Location', 'Rainfall', 'Region_ID', and 'Temperature'. 'Region_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Region_ID' in the 'Region' table. 'Change_ID' is the primary key.
Each table has been designed with entity integrity in mind, with primary keys and foreign keys ensuring that each record in the table is unique and can be linked to other records in the database. The database schema is designed to maintain referential integrity, with foreign keys ensuring that the 'Region_ID' in the 'Flora_and_Fauna_Record' and 'Environmental_Change_Record' tables match the 'Region_ID' in the 'Region' table.
