The database schema named 'rschema_0208' contains three tables: 'Equipment', 'Operator', and 'Equipment_Operation'.
The 'Equipment' table represents the equipment in the facility. It has an integer primary key 'Equipment_ID', a varchar field 'Equipment_Model' to store the model of the equipment, a varchar field 'Equipment_Name' to store the name of the equipment, and a datetime field 'Installation_Date' to record the date of installation. The 'Installation_Location' field is also a varchar to record the location of the equipment.
The 'Operator' table represents the operators of the facility. It has an integer primary key 'Operator_ID', varchar fields 'Contact_Information', 'Department', and 'Name' to store the contact information, department, and name of the operator respectively.
The 'Equipment_Operation' table represents the operations performed on the equipment. It has datetime fields 'End_Time' and 'Start_Time' to record the end and start times of the operation. It has two integer fields 'Equipment_ID' and 'Operator_ID' as foreign keys referencing 'Equipment_ID' in the 'Equipment' table and 'Operator_ID' in the 'Operator' table respectively. Both 'Equipment_ID' and 'Operator_ID' along with 'Start_Time' form the primary key of this table. The 'Operational_Status' field is a varchar to record the operational status of the equipment.
This schema ensures referential integrity as all foreign keys reference primary keys in their respective tables. It also ensures entity integrity as each record in the 'Equipment' and 'Operator' tables has a unique 'Equipment_ID' and 'Operator_ID' respectively. The primary key constraint ensures that the combination of 'Equipment_ID', 'Operator_ID', and 'Start_Time' is unique for each record in the 'Equipment_Operation' table.
