The database schema named 'rschema_0209' contains three tables: 'laboratory', 'equipment', and 'maintenance_record'.
The 'laboratory' table represents the laboratory information, with columns for 'laboratory_number', 'laboratory_name', and 'laboratory_location'. The 'laboratory_number' is the primary key for this table.
The 'equipment' table holds information about the equipment in the laboratories. It has columns for 'equipment_number', 'equipment_name', 'equipment_model', 'purchase_date', 'equipment_status', and 'laboratory_number'. The 'equipment_number' is the primary key and 'laboratory_number' is a foreign key referencing the 'laboratory' table. The foreign key constraints are set to CASCADE on delete and update operations.
The 'maintenance_record' table records the maintenance activities performed on the equipment. It has columns for 'maintenance_number', 'equipment_number', 'maintenance_date', 'maintenance_personnel', and 'maintenance_content'. The 'maintenance_number' is the primary key and 'equipment_number' is a foreign key referencing the 'equipment' table. The foreign key constraints are also set to CASCADE on delete and update operations. This schema ensures referential integrity by maintaining the consistency of data across the tables.
