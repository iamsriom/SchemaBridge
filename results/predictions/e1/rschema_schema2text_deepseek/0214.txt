The database schema named 'rschema_0214' consists of three tables: 'Employee', 'Assessment_Record', and 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics'.
The 'Employee' table represents the employees of a company. It has five columns: 'Employee_ID', which is the primary key, 'Gender', 'Name', 'Phone_Number', and 'Position'. These columns store the employee's gender, name, phone number, and position respectively.
The 'Assessment_Record' table records the assessments taken by the employees. It has five columns: 'Assessment_Content', 'Assessment_Date', 'Assessment_ID', 'Assessment_Score', and 'Employee_ID'. 'Assessment_Content' and 'Assessment_Date' store the content and date of the assessment, 'Assessment_ID' is the primary key, 'Assessment_Score' stores the score of the assessment, and 'Employee_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Employee' table.
The 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' table keeps track of the monthly assessment statistics of the employees. It has four columns: 'Average_Score', 'Employee_ID', 'Month', and 'Month'. 'Average_Score' stores the average score of the assessments, 'Employee_ID' is the primary key and a foreign key referencing the 'Employee' table, and 'Month' stores the month of the assessment.
The primary key constraints ensure entity integrity by uniquely identifying each record in the table. The foreign key constraints ensure referential integrity by ensuring that the 'Employee_ID' in the 'Assessment_Record' and 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' tables references a valid 'Employee_ID' in the 'Employee' table.
The 'Assessment_Record' table allows null values in the 'Assessment_Content' column, but not in the 'Assessment_Date', 'Assessment_Score', and 'Employee_ID' columns. The 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' table also allows null values in the 'Average_Score' column, but not in the 'Employee_ID' and 'Month' columns.
The composite primary key in the 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' table ensures that a combination of 'Employee_ID' and 'Month' is unique, providing entity integrity.
The 'Assessment_Record' table has a foreign key 'Employee_ID' that references the 'Employee' table, and the 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' table has a foreign key 'Employee_ID' that references the 'Employee' table. The foreign key constraints are set to CASCADE, which means if a record in the 'Employee' table is deleted, all the corresponding records in the 'Assessment_Record' and 'Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics' tables will be deleted as well.
The schema is designed to efficiently store and retrieve data related to employees, their assessments, and their monthly assessment statistics.
