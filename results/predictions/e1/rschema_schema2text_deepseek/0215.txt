The database schema named 'rschema_0215' consists of four tables.
The 'material_administrator' table is used to store information about the administrators of the mine. It includes columns for 'administrator_id', 'name', 'gender', 'phone', 'position', and 'mine_id'. The 'mine_id' column is a foreign key referencing the 'mine' table, and it has cascading delete and update rules.
The 'mine' table stores information about the mines. It includes columns for 'mine_id', 'name', 'location', and 'person_in_charge'.
The 'purchase_record' table is used to record the purchase records of materials. It includes columns for 'record_id', 'purchase_date', 'material_name', 'quantity', 'supplier', and 'purchase_cost'.
Finally, the 'material_distribution_record' table is used to record the distribution records of materials. It includes columns for 'record_id', 'distribution_date', 'material_name', 'quantity', 'distributor', and 'recipient'. The 'distributor' column is a foreign key referencing the 'material_administrator' table, and it has cascading delete and update rules.
This schema ensures the integrity of data by enforcing primary keys, foreign keys, and constraints. It allows for the tracking of material purchases, their costs, and their distributions, while also ensuring the integrity of the administrative data.
