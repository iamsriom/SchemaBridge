The database schema named 'rschema_0216' consists of five tables: 'Credit_Card_Type', 'Customer', 'Credit_Card', 'Transaction', and 'Repayment'.
The 'Credit_Card_Type' table represents the type of credit cards and contains columns for 'Annual_Fee', 'Card_Type_Code', and 'Card_Type_Name'. The 'Card_Type_Code' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Customer' table holds information about customers, including 'Contact_Number', 'Customer_ID', 'ID_Number', and 'Name'. 'Customer_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Credit_Card' table represents the credit cards issued by the bank. It contains columns for 'Account_Opening_Date', 'Card_Number', 'Card_Type_Code', 'Credit_Limit', and 'Customer_ID'. 'Card_Number' is the primary key and 'Card_Type_Code' and 'Customer_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Credit_Card_Type' and 'Customer' tables respectively.
The 'Transaction' table records all transactions made by customers. It includes columns for 'Card_Number', 'Customer_ID', 'Transaction_Amount', 'Transaction_Location', 'Transaction_Time', and 'Transaction_Type'. The combination of 'Customer_ID', 'Card_Number', and 'Transaction_Time' form the primary key for this table, with 'Card_Number' and 'Customer_ID' being foreign keys referencing the 'Credit_Card' and 'Customer' tables respectively.
Finally, the 'Repayment' table keeps track of all repayments made by customers. It includes columns for 'Card_Number', 'Customer_ID', 'Repayment_Amount', and 'Repayment_Time'. The combination of 'Customer_ID' and 'Card_Number' and 'Repayment_Time' form the primary key for this table, with 'Card_Number' and 'Customer_ID' being foreign keys referencing the 'Credit_Card' and 'Customer' tables respectively.
The schema is designed to maintain referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. This ensures that all data in the 'Credit_Card' and 'Customer' tables that are referenced in the 'Credit_Card_Type', 'Transaction', and 'Repayment' tables actually exist in those tables.
