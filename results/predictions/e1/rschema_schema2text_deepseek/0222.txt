The database schema named 'rschema_0222' consists of four tables: 'Recipe_Type_Table', 'Ingredient_Table', 'Recipe_Table', and 'Recipe_and_Ingredient_Relationship_Table'.
The 'Recipe_Type_Table' represents the type of recipes and contains columns for 'Description', 'Name', and 'Type_ID'. The 'Type_ID' is the primary key.
The 'Ingredient_Table' represents the ingredients used in the recipes and contains columns for 'Description', 'Image', 'Ingredient_ID', and 'Name'. The 'Ingredient_ID' is the primary key.
The 'Recipe_Table' represents the recipes and contains columns for 'Description', 'Image', 'Name', 'Recipe_ID', 'Steps', and 'Type_ID'. The 'Recipe_ID' is the primary key and 'Type_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Recipe_Type_Table'.
Finally, the 'Recipe_and_Ingredient_Relationship_Table' represents the relationship between recipes and ingredients. It contains columns for 'Ingredient_ID', 'Quantity', and 'Recipe_ID'. The combination of 'Recipe_ID' and 'Ingredient_ID' forms the primary key. Both 'Ingredient_ID' and 'Recipe_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Ingredient_Table' and 'Recipe_Table' respectively.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary key and foreign key constraints, and it allows for the efficient storage and retrieval of recipe and ingredient data.
