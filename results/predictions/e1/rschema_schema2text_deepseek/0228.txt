The database schema for the database named 'rschema_0228' consists of four tables: 'customer', 'magazine', 'pricing', and 'customer_subscribes_to_magazine'.
The 'customer' table represents a customer entity with attributes such as 'customer_id', 'customer_name', and 'customer_address'. The 'customer_id' attribute is the primary key for this table.
The 'magazine' table represents a magazine entity with attributes such as 'magazine_name', 'publisher', 'theme', 'publication_frequency', and 'website'. The combination of 'publisher' and 'magazine_name' form the primary key.
The 'pricing' table represents a pricing entity for magazines. It has attributes such as 'pricing_id', 'magazine_name', 'publisher', 'subscription_package', 'subscription_period', and 'price'. The 'pricing_id' is the primary key and 'magazine_name' and 'publisher' form a foreign key referencing the 'magazine' table.
The 'customer_subscribes_to_magazine' table represents the relationship between customers and magazines. It has attributes such as 'customer_id', 'pricing_id', 'creation_time', and 'subscription_start_date'. The combination of 'pricing_id', 'customer_id', and 'creation_time' form the primary key. The 'pricing_id' and 'customer_id' form foreign keys referencing the 'pricing' and 'customer' tables respectively. The delete and update rules are set to cascade, meaning if a record in the referenced table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'customer_subscribes_to_magazine' table will also be deleted or updated.
