The database schema named 'rschema_0231' consists of two tables: 'User' and 'Experience'.
The 'User' table is used to store user information. It includes five columns: 'Email', 'Password', 'Phone_Number', 'Registration_Time', and 'User_ID'. 'Email' and 'Password' are of type VARCHAR(255) and are not nullable. 'Phone_Number' and 'Registration_Time' are of type VARCHAR(255) and 'User_ID' is of type INTEGER and is the primary key.
The 'Experience' table is used to store user experiences. It includes eight columns: 'Code_Content', 'Creation_Time', 'Creator_ID', 'Experience_Description', 'Experience_ID', 'Language', 'Modification_Time', and 'Status'. 'Code_Content' and 'Experience_Description' are of type VARCHAR(255), 'Creation_Time' and 'Modification_Time' are of type DATETIME, 'Creator_ID' is of type INTEGER and is not nullable, 'Experience_ID' is of type INTEGER and is the primary key, and 'Language' is of type VARCHAR(255).
The 'Creator_ID' column in the 'Experience' table is a foreign key referencing the 'User_ID' column in the 'User' table. This schema ensures referential integrity as it links the 'User' and 'Experience' tables together.
