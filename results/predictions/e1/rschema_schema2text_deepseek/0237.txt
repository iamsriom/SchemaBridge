The Area table lists different areas, each identified by an Area_ID and an Area_Name.
The House_Type table contains different types of houses, each identified by a Type_ID and a Type_Name.
The Street table represents different streets, each identified by a Street_ID and a Street_Name. The Belongs_to_Area_ID column is a foreign key referencing the Area_ID in the Area table, establishing a relationship between the Street and Area tables.
The House table contains information about different houses, including their descriptions, prices, and the Type_ID of the house type. The House_ID is the primary key for this table, and the Type_ID and Belongs_to_Street_ID are foreign keys referencing the Type_ID and Street_ID in the House_Type and Street tables, respectively.
Finally, the Lease table contains information about house leases, including the description, price, and the User_ID and House_ID of the user and house leased. The combination of User_ID, House_ID, and Lease_Date forms the primary key for this table, with User_ID and House_ID being foreign keys referencing the User_ID and House_ID in the User and House tables, respectively. This schema ensures referential integrity by maintaining relationships between the tables.
