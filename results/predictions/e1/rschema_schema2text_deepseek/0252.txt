The database schema named "rschema_0252" contains five tables.
The "Player" table is used to store player-related information. It includes columns for 'Game', 'Password', 'Phone_Number', 'Player_ID', 'Registration_Time', and 'Username'. The 'Player_ID' column is the primary key, and it represents the unique identifier for each player.
The "Game_Record" table is used to store game-related information. It includes columns for 'Game_Content', 'Game_ID', 'Game_Rules', and 'Maximum_Number_of_Players'. The 'Game_ID' column is the primary key, and it represents the unique identifier for each game.
The "Player_Communication" table is used to store communication-related information between players. It includes columns for 'Communication_Content', 'Communication_End_Time', 'Communication_Start_Time', 'First_Player_ID', and 'Second_Player_ID'. The combination of 'First_Player_ID', 'Second_Player_ID', and 'Communication_Start_Time' form the primary key. The 'First_Player_ID' and 'Second_Player_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Player_ID' in the 'Player' table.
The "Single_Player_Game" table is used to store single-player game-related information. It includes columns for 'Game_End_Time', 'Game_ID', 'Game_Start_Time', 'Game_Status', and 'Player_ID'. The combination of 'Player_ID', 'Game_ID', and 'Game_Start_Time' form the primary key. The 'Game_ID' and 'Player_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Game_ID' and 'Player_ID' in the 'Game_Record' and 'Player' tables, respectively.
The "Two-Player_Battle_Game" table is used to store two-player battle game-related information. It includes columns for 'Battle_End_Time', 'Battle_Result', 'Battle_Start_Time', 'First_Player_ID', 'Game_ID', and 'Second_Player_ID'. The combination of 'First_Player_ID', 'Second_Player_ID', 'Game_ID', and 'Battle_Start_Time' form the primary key. The 'First_Player_ID', 'Game_ID', and 'Second_Player_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Player_ID', 'Game_ID', and 'Player_ID' in the 'Player', 'Game_Record', and 'Player' tables, respectively. This schema ensures referential integrity by linking the tables together through the foreign keys.
