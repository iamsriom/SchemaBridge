The database schema named 'rschema_0260' contains four tables: 'Student', 'Course', 'Degree_Examination_Result', and 'Registration'.
The 'Student' table represents a collection of students with various attributes such as 'Date_of_Birth', 'Home_Address', 'Name', 'Phone_Number', and 'Registration_Eligibility'. The 'Student_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Course' table contains information about the courses offered, including 'Class_Location', 'Class_Time', 'Course_ID', 'Course_Name', 'Credits', and 'Instructor'. The 'Course_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Degree_Examination_Result' table holds the results of degree examinations. It includes 'Examination_Date', 'Examination_Name', 'Grade', and 'Result_ID'. The 'Student_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Student' table, ensuring entity integrity.
Finally, the 'Registration' table is used to track the registrations of students for courses. It includes 'Course_ID' and 'Registration_Time' as well as 'Status'. Both 'Course_ID' and 'Student_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Course' and 'Student' tables respectively, ensuring referential integrity.
The 'Student_ID' and 'Course_ID' form a composite primary key in the 'Registration' table, ensuring entity integrity.
