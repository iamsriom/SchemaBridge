The database schema named "rschema_0261" consists of five tables: Address, City, District, Voter, and Town.
The Address table has an Address_ID as the primary key and two columns: Detailed_address and Type. It represents the detailed address information and the type of the address.
The City table has a City_ID as the primary key and two columns: Name and Region. It represents the city name and region information.
The District table has a District_ID as the primary key and two columns: Name and Type. The City_ID column is a foreign key referencing the City table. It represents the district name and type information.
The Voter table has a Voter_ID as the primary key and four columns: Name, Registration_district_ID, Registration_status, and Address_ID. The Address_ID and Registration_district_ID columns are foreign keys referencing the Address and District tables respectively. It represents the voter name, registration district ID, registration status, and address information.
Finally, the Town table has a Town_ID as the primary key and three columns: Name, Region, and District_ID. The District_ID column is a foreign key referencing the District table. It represents the town name, region, and district information.
