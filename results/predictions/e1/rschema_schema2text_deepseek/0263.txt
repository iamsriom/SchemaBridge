The 'assignment_id' is the primary key, and 'student_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'student' table.

The 'correction_id' is the primary key, and 'homework_id' and 'lecturer_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'assignment' and 'lecturer' tables respectively.

The 'sampling_id' is the primary key, and 'correction_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'correction_record' table.
