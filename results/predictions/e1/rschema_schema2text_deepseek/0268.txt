The database schema named 'rschema_0268' contains five tables: 'Restaurant', 'Dish', 'Customer', 'Restaurant_Dish', and 'Customer_Reservation'.
The 'Restaurant' table represents a restaurant with attributes such as its location, name, operating hours, and a unique identifier. The 'Dish' table contains information about dishes, including their category, name, and price. The 'Customer' table holds customer data, including their name and phone number.
The 'Restaurant_Dish' table acts as a junction table that links the 'Restaurant' and 'Dish' tables. It contains foreign keys referencing the 'Dish_ID' and 'Restaurant_ID' in the 'Dish' and 'Restaurant' tables respectively.
The 'Customer_Reservation' table records reservations made by customers. It includes attributes such as the number of diners, reservation time, and a unique reservation identifier. The 'Customer_ID' and 'Restaurant_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Customer' and 'Restaurant' tables respectively.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. When a record in the 'Dish' or 'Restaurant' table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the 'Restaurant_Dish' and 'Customer_Reservation' tables will be automatically updated or deleted to maintain data integrity.
