The database schema named 'rschema_0271' contains three tables: 'Service', 'Client', and 'Service_Subscription'. The 'Service' table represents the services offered by the company, with columns for 'Price', 'Service_ID', 'Service_Name', 'Transmission_Rate', and 'Transmission_Type'. The 'Client' table holds information about the clients, including 'Client_Address', 'Client_Email', 'Client_ID', 'Client_Name', and 'Client_Phone'. The 'Service_Subscription' table records the subscriptions of clients to services. It includes columns for 'Client_ID', 'Operator', 'Service_ID', 'Subscription_End_Time', and 'Subscription_Start_Time'. The 'Client_ID' and 'Service_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Client' and 'Service' tables respectively. The primary key of this table is a combination of 'Service_ID', 'Client_ID', and 'Subscription_Start_Time'.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary key constraints on each table, ensuring uniqueness of 'Client_ID' and 'Service_ID' in the 'Client' and 'Service' tables respectively. It also enforces foreign key constraints, ensuring that 'Client_ID' and 'Service_ID' in the 'Service_Subscription' table reference valid 'Client_ID' and 'Service_ID' in the 'Client' and 'Service' tables respectively. The 'Subscription_End_Time' and 'Subscription_Start_Time' columns in the 'Service_Subscription' table allow for tracking of the duration of the subscription.
The schema is designed with referential integrity in mind, ensuring that the relationships between tables are maintained. Changes to the 'Client_ID' or 'Service_ID' in the 'Service_Subscription' table will cascade to the 'Client' and 'Service' tables, maintaining the integrity of the data.
