The database schema named 'rschema_0273' consists of two tables: 'Customer' and 'Feedback'.
The 'Customer' table represents a customer base with columns: 'Customer_ID' (an integer primary key), 'Name' (a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters), and 'Phone_Number' (also a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters).
The 'Feedback' table, on the other hand, represents customer feedback. It has columns: 'Customer_ID' (an integer field that cannot be null, which is a foreign key referencing the 'Customer_ID' in the 'Customer' table), 'Feedback_Content' (a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters), 'Feedback_ID' (an integer primary key), 'Feedback_Time' (a date and time field), and 'Processing_Status' (a variable character field with a maximum length of 255 characters). This schema ensures that each feedback record is associated with a valid customer, and also allows for tracking of the time and status of feedback processing.
