The 'Broadcast_Record' table represents the records of when and where the advertisements are broadcasted. The 'Advertisement_Push' table represents the records of which advertisements are pushed to which users. It has two columns: 'Advertisement_ID' which is a foreign key referencing the 'Advertisement' table, 'User_ID' which is a foreign key referencing the 'User' table, and both 'Advertisement_ID' and 'User_ID' form the primary key of the table.
