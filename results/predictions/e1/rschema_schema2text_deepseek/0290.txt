The database schema named 'rschema_0290' contains two tables: 'Project' and 'Fund_Flow_Record'.
The 'Project' table represents a project with columns for 'Budget', 'Name', and 'Project_ID'. The 'Project_ID' is the primary key and is an integer.
The 'Fund_Flow_Record' table represents a record of funds flowing into or out of a project. It has columns for 'Amount', 'Date', 'Flow_ID', 'Fund_Type', 'Project_ID', 'Remarks'. The 'Project_ID' column is not null and is a foreign key referencing the 'Project_ID' in the 'Project' table. This schema ensures referential integrity as it maintains the relationship between the 'Project' and 'Fund_Flow_Record' tables.
