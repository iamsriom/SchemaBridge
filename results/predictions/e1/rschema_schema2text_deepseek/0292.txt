The 'Facility_Maintenance_Table' represents the maintenance records for each facility. The primary key of this table is a combination of 'maintenance_personnel_ID', 'facility_ID', and 'maintenance_time'.
The 'Facility_ID' and 'maintenance_personnel_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'Facility_Table' and 'Maintenance_Personnel_Table' respectively.
