The database schema named 'rschema_0293' consists of four tables. The 'Mine_Table' represents the information about the mines, including the location, mine ID, mine name, and the person in charge. The 'Maintenance_Personnel_Table' holds data about the maintenance personnel, including their contact number, personnel ID, and name. The 'Equipment_Table' contains information about the equipment, including the equipment ID, mine ID, model, name, and purchase date. The 'Maintenance_Record_Table' records the maintenance records, including the equipment ID, maintenance content, cost, date, and personnel ID. The primary keys and foreign keys ensure the integrity of the data. The foreign keys in the 'Equipment_Table' reference the 'Mine_Table' and the 'Maintenance_Record_Table' references the 'Maintenance_Personnel_Table'. The database schema is designed to maintain a comprehensive record of the mines, equipment, and maintenance personnel, ensuring data integrity and consistency.
