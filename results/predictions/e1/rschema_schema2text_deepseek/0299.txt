The database schema named 'rschema_0299' contains three tables: 'City', 'Weather_Description', and 'Weather_Forecast'.
The 'City' table represents a list of cities, with each city identified by a unique 'city_ID' (integer, primary key). The 'city_name', 'country', 'latitude', 'longitude', 'population', and 'timezone' columns store information about the city's name, country, geographical location, population, and timezone respectively.
The 'Weather_Description' table provides detailed descriptions of weather conditions, with each weather type identified by a unique 'weather_type_ID' (integer, primary key). The 'detailed_description', 'icon', and 'main_description' columns store the detailed description, icon, and main description of the weather condition respectively.
The 'Weather_Forecast' table holds weather forecast data for various cities. Each forecast is identified by a unique 'forecast_ID' (integer, primary key). The 'morning_temperature', 'afternoon_temperature', 'noon_temperature', 'humidity', 'atmospheric_pressure', 'cloud_cover', 'date', 'sunrise_time', 'sunset_time', 'wind_direction', and 'wind_speed' columns store the morning, afternoon, noon, humidity, atmospheric pressure, cloud cover, date, sunrise time, sunset time, wind direction, and wind speed respectively. The 'city_ID' and 'weather_type_ID' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'city_ID' and 'weather_type_ID' in the 'City' and 'Weather_Description' tables respectively. This schema ensures referential integrity, as each weather forecast is tied to a specific city and weather condition.
