The database schema named 'rschema_0307' contains six tables: Companies_Table, API_Keys_Table, Employees_Table, Sites_Table, Devices_Table, and Users_Table.
The Companies_Table represents the company details. It includes columns for Company_ID (integer, primary key), Company_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Company_Website (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Creation_Date (datetime), and Update_Date (datetime).
The API_Keys_Table stores API keys. It includes columns for Application_Reason (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Creation_Date (datetime), Key_ID (integer, primary key), Key_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Private_Key (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), URI (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), and Update_Date (datetime).
The Employees_Table contains employee details. It includes columns for Company_ID (integer), Creation_Date (datetime), Employee_ID (integer, primary key), Employee_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Position (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Salary (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), and Update_Date (datetime).
The Sites_Table contains site details. It includes columns for Creation_Date (datetime), Database_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Hostname (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Is_Deprecated (boolean), Site_Abbreviation (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Site_ID (integer, primary key), Site_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), and Update_Date (datetime).
The Devices_Table contains device details. It includes columns for Associated_Employee_ID (integer), Creation_Date (datetime), Device_ID (integer, primary key), Device_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), and Update_Date (datetime).
Finally, the Users_Table contains user details. It includes columns for Company_ID (integer), Creation_Date (datetime), Display_Name_Usage (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Email (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Is_Admin (boolean), Is_Developer (boolean), Last_Login_Time (datetime), Password (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Password_Salt (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Password_Type (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Real_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), Site_ID (integer, not null), Update_Date (datetime), User_ID (integer, primary key), Username (varchar with a maximum length of 255 characters), and a foreign key constraint on Site_ID referencing the Sites_Table.
