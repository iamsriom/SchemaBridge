The database schema named 'rschema_0338' contains five tables: College, Student, Teacher, Course, and Course_Enrollment_and_Grade.
The College table represents the main entity in the system, holding information about each college, including its unique ID, location, name, number of staff, and number of students. The College_ID is the primary key for this table.
The Student table is a more detailed entity, storing information about each student, including their age, associated college ID, date of birth, gender, name, registration time, student ID, and telephone number. The Associated_College_ID is a foreign key referencing the College_ID in the College table, ensuring data integrity and referential integrity.
The Teacher table also holds information about each teacher, including their age, associated college ID, contact information, gender, name, teacher ID, and title. The Associated_College_ID is a foreign key referencing the College_ID in the College table.
The Course table represents the courses offered by the colleges. It holds information about each course, including class hours, classroom, course ID, course summary, credit number, name, and the ID of the teacher teaching the course. The Teaching_Teacher_ID is a foreign key referencing the Teacher_ID in the Teacher table.
Finally, the Course_Enrollment_and_Grade table represents the enrollment and grades of students in different courses. It holds information about each enrollment, including course grade, course ID, enrollment time, and student ID. The Course_ID and Student_ID are foreign keys referencing the Course_ID and Student_ID in their respective tables respectively, ensuring data integrity and referential integrity. The combination of Student_ID, Course_ID, and Enrollment_Time forms the primary key of this table, ensuring entity integrity.
