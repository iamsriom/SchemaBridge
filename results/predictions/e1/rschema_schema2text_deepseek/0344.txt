The database schema named 'rschema_0344' consists of 8 tables. The 'User' table is used to store user information, including the user's email, unique identifier, and username. The 'Application' table stores information about the applications, including the application's unique identifier, code, name, and type. The 'Web_Service' table contains information about web services, including the unique identifier of the application they belong to, creation date, description, name, and the unique identifier of the service itself. The 'Permissions' table stores information about the permissions of users on different applications, including the unique identifiers of the application and user, and the role. The 'Error_Information' table stores information about errors, including the unique identifier of the error, error code, description, occurrence time, return status, and the unique identifier of the service it belongs to. The 'Service_Monitoring' table stores information about service monitoring, including the call time, delay time, unique identifier of the monitoring, return status, and the unique identifier of the service. The 'Statistics' table stores statistics about services, including the call count, success rate, time period, and the unique identifier of the service. Finally, the 'Error_and_Monitoring_Association' table is used to associate errors with their corresponding monitoring records, using the unique identifiers of the error and monitoring records. All these tables have foreign keys that reference the primary keys of other tables, ensuring referential integrity.
