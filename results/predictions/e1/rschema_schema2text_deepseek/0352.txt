The database schema named 'rschema_0352' consists of five tables: 'Supplier', 'Customer', 'Product', 'Invoice', and 'Invoice_Product'.
The 'Supplier' table represents a supplier entity with attributes such as 'Supplier_Address', 'Supplier_ID', 'Supplier_Name', and 'Supplier_Phone'. The 'Supplier_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Customer' table represents a customer entity with attributes such as 'Address', 'Customer_ID', 'Email', 'First_Name', 'Last_Name', and 'Phone'. 'Customer_ID' is the primary key for this table.
The 'Product' table represents a product entity with attributes such as 'Description', 'Minimum_Stock_Quantity', 'Name', 'Product_ID', 'Purchase_Price', 'Selling_Price', 'Stock_Quantity', and 'Supplier_ID'. 'Product_ID' is the primary key and 'Supplier_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Supplier' table.
The 'Invoice' table represents an invoice entity with attributes such as 'Customer_ID', 'Date', 'Invoice_ID', 'Tax_Amount', and 'Total_Value'. 'Invoice_ID' is the primary key and 'Customer_ID' is a foreign key referencing the 'Customer' table.
Finally, the 'Invoice_Product' table represents a many-to-many relationship between invoices and products. It has attributes such as 'Invoice_ID', 'Product_ID', and 'Product_Quantity'. 'Invoice_ID' and 'Product_ID' are the primary key and 'Invoice_ID' and 'Product_ID' are foreign keys respectively referencing the 'Invoice' and 'Product' tables.
