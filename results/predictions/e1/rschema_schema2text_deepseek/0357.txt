The database schema named 'rschema_0357' consists of 7 tables.
The 'rental_location' table represents the locations where vehicles can be rented. It has columns for 'location_id', 'location_name', 'location_address', 'location_phone', and 'business_hours'. The 'location_id' is the primary key.
The 'vehicle_category' table represents the categories of vehicles that can be rented. It has columns for 'category_id', 'category_name', 'weekday_rental_price', and 'weekend_rental_price'. The 'category_id' is the primary key.
The 'vehicle' table represents the vehicles that can be rented. It has columns for 'license_plate_number', 'brand', 'model', 'photo_path', 'location_id', and 'category_id'. The 'license_plate_number' is the primary key and 'location_id' and 'category_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'rental_location' and 'vehicle_category' tables respectively.
The 'user' table represents the users of the rental service. It has columns for 'user_id', 'email', 'name', 'address', 'phone', 'postal_code', 'city', and 'credit_card_information'. The 'user_id' is the primary key.
The 'user_vehicle_reservation' table represents the reservations made by users. It has columns for 'user_id', 'vehicle_id', 'reservation_date', and 'return_date'. The combination of 'user_id' and 'vehicle_id' and 'reservation_date' is the primary key and 'user_id' and 'vehicle_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'user' and 'vehicle' tables respectively.
The 'news_and_featured_content' table represents the news and featured content on the website. It has columns for 'content_id', 'title', 'content', and 'release_date'. The 'content_id' is the primary key.
The 'vehicle_current_location' table represents the current location of the vehicles. It has columns for 'location_id', 'license_plate_number', and 'record_time'. The combination of 'location_id', 'license_plate_number', and 'record_time' is the primary key and 'location_id' and 'license_plate_number' are foreign keys referencing the 'rental_location' and 'vehicle' tables respectively. The 'vehicle_current_location' table is used to track the movement of the vehicles.
