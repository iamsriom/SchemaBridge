The database schema named 'rschema_0361' contains four tables: 'Restaurant', 'User_review', 'Dish', and 'Review_reply'.
The 'Restaurant' table represents a collection of restaurants. It contains columns for the restaurant's address, average cost, business hours, name, phone number, and a unique identifier (Restaurant_ID).
The 'User_review' table holds reviews made by users. It includes columns for the review's actual photos (in BLOB format), dining time (in DATETIME format), rating (in FLOAT format), a unique identifier (Review_ID), and the content of the review (in VARCHAR format).
The 'Dish' table represents dishes served by the restaurants. It includes columns for the category of the dish, description, a unique identifier (Dish_ID), the URL of the dish image, monthly sales (in FLOAT format), name, price (in FLOAT format), the identifier of the restaurant serving the dish (Restaurant_ID), and the satisfaction rate (in FLOAT format). The Restaurant_ID column is a foreign key referencing the Restaurant table.
Finally, the 'Review_reply' table holds replies to user reviews. It includes columns for a unique identifier (Reply_ID), the content of the reply (in VARCHAR format), the time of the reply (in DATETIME format), and the identifier of the review being replied to (Review_ID_being_replied_to). The Review_ID_being_replied_to column is a foreign key referencing the User_review table.
All columns allow null values except for the Restaurant_ID and Review_ID_being_replied_to in the 'Dish' and 'Review_reply' tables respectively. The primary keys are the unique identifiers in their respective tables. Referential integrity is maintained through foreign key references.
