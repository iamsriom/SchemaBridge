The Course_ID is the primary key of this table and Teaching_Professor_ID is a foreign key referencing the Professor_ID in the Professor table.
Finally, the Course_Registration table is used to track the registrations of students for courses. It contains Course_ID, Grade, Registration_Time, and Student_ID. The combination of Student_ID, Course_ID, and Registration_Time form the primary key of this table. The Course_ID and Student_ID are foreign keys referencing the Course_ID and Student_ID in the Course and Student tables respectively.
