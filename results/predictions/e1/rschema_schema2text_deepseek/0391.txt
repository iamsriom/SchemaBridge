The database schema named 'rschema_0391' consists of three tables: Student, Post, and Comment.
The Student table represents a collection of students. It has five columns: Admission_Date (representing the date of admission), Gender (indicating the gender of the student), Major (describing the major of the student), Name (representing the name of the student), and Password (used for authentication). The Student_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Post table is used to store information about posts. It has five columns: Author (representing the ID of the student who created the post), Content (describing the content of the post), Post_ID (representing the unique identifier of the post), Publication_Date (indicating the date of publication), and Topic (describing the topic of the post). The Author column is a foreign key referencing the Student_ID in the Student table.
The Comment table is used to store comments made by students on posts. It has four columns: Comment_Content (describing the content of the comment), Comment_Time (indicating the time of comment), Commented_Post (representing the ID of the post the comment is made on), and Commenter (representing the ID of the student who made the comment). The Commenter and Commented_Post columns form a composite primary key. Both Commented_Post and Commenter are foreign keys referencing the Post_ID and Student_ID in their respective tables respectively.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. When a record in the Student table is deleted, any associated records in the Post and Comment tables will also be deleted due to the CASCADE delete rule. Similarly, when a record in the Post table is deleted, any associated records in the Comment table will also be deleted.
The schema is exhaustive and covers all the necessary entities and their relationships. It also includes constraints to maintain data integrity, such as not allowing null values in the Author column of the Post table and ensuring unique values in the Student_ID column of the Student table.
