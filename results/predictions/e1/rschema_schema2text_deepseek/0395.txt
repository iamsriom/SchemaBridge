The database schema named "rschema_0395" consists of three tables: Student, Meal_card, and Consumption_record.
The Student table represents the information of students. It has three columns: Contact_information (a VARCHAR of maximum length 255), Department (another VARCHAR of maximum length 255), and Name (another VARCHAR of maximum length 255). The Student_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Meal_card table represents the meal cards of students. It has three columns: Balance (a FLOAT), Card_ID (an INTEGER), and Student_ID (an INTEGER). The Student_ID column is a foreign key referencing the Student table's Student_ID.
The Consumption_record table represents the consumption records of meal cards. It has five columns: Amount (an INTEGER), Card_ID (an INTEGER), Location (a VARCHAR of maximum length 255), Record_ID (an INTEGER), and Time (a DATETIME). The Card_ID column is a foreign key referencing the Meal_card table's Card_ID.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing the constraints of foreign keys. When a record in the Student table is deleted, any corresponding records in the Meal_card and Consumption_record tables will also be deleted due to the CASCADE delete rule.
