The database schema named 'rschema_0402' consists of 9 tables. The 'Department' table represents the departmental information, including the department address, ID, name, and phone number. The 'Patient' table holds patient-related data, including age, contact information, diagnosis description, gender, name, patient ID, and visit date. The 'Ward' table contains information about the ward, including the number of beds and ward level. The 'Registration_Category' table contains registration category-related data, including the category ID and category name. The 'Medicine' table contains information about the medicines, including the barcode, medicine ID, name, price, and quantity. The 'Prescription' table holds prescription-related data, including the medicine information and prescription ID. The 'Doctor' table contains doctor-related data, including the date of birth, doctor ID, gender, name, title, work certificate, and years of employment. Finally, the 'Treatment' and 'Prescription_Medicine' tables reference the 'Patient' table, providing treatment content and prescription details respectively. All tables have primary keys and foreign keys to maintain entity integrity and referential integrity.
This schema is designed to manage various aspects of a hospital, including patient information, doctor details, medicine prescriptions, treatment plans, and departmental information. It allows for easy tracking and management of various aspects of the hospital's operations.
