The database schema named 'rschema_0403' comprises six tables: Student, Administrator, Book, Student_Borrow_Book, Student_Return_Book, and Administrator_Audit.
The Student table represents students, with columns for Contact_Number (VARCHAR(255)), Email (VARCHAR(255)), Grade (INTEGER), Major (VARCHAR(255)), Name (VARCHAR(255)), and Student_ID (INTEGER) as primary keys.
The Administrator table holds information about administrators, including Administrator_ID (INTEGER), Contact_Number (VARCHAR(255)), Email (VARCHAR(255)), Job_Number (INTEGER), Name (VARCHAR(255)), and Position (VARCHAR(255)).
The Book table contains information about books, including Author (VARCHAR(255)), Book_ID (INTEGER), Borrowed_Quantity (INTEGER), Category (VARCHAR(255)), Publication_Date (DATETIME), Publisher (VARCHAR(255)), Stock_Quantity (INTEGER), and Title (VARCHAR(255)).
The Student_Borrow_Book table records the borrowing of books by students, with columns for Borrow_Date (DATETIME), Borrowed_Book_ID (INTEGER), Return_Date (DATETIME), Review_Status (VARCHAR(255)), and Student_ID (INTEGER). The primary key is a combination of Student_ID and Borrowed_Book_ID. The foreign keys are Borrowed_Book_ID referencing Book(Book_ID) and Student_ID referencing Student(Student_ID).
The Student_Return_Book table records the returning of books by students, with columns for Book_ID (INTEGER), Return_Date (DATETIME), Returned_Book_ID (INTEGER), Review_Status (VARCHAR(255)), and Student_ID (INTEGER). The primary key is a combination of Student_ID and Returned_Book_ID. The foreign keys are Returned_Book_ID referencing Book(Book_ID) and Student_ID referencing Student(Student_ID).
Finally, the Administrator_Audit table records administrative actions, including Administrator_ID (INTEGER), Audit_Date (DATETIME), Audit_Result (VARCHAR(255)), Book_ID (INTEGER), Borrowing_Status (VARCHAR(255)). The primary key is a combination of Administrator_ID and Book_ID. The foreign keys are Administrator_ID referencing Administrator(Administrator_ID) and Book_ID referencing Book(Book_ID).
