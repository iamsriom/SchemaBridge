The database schema named 'rschema_0404' consists of five tables: Employee, Supplier, Product, Employee_Sales, and Inventory.
The Employee table represents the employees of the organization. It has columns for Contact_Number, Employee_ID, Password, Position, and Username. The Employee_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Supplier table holds information about the suppliers. It includes Supplier_ID, Supplier_Name, Supplier_Number, and Supplier_Phone. The Supplier_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Product table contains information about the products. It includes Product_ID, Product_Name, Product_Number, Purchase_Price, Sales_Price, Stock_Quantity, and Supplier_ID. The Product_ID is the primary key and Supplier_ID is a foreign key referencing the Supplier table.
The Employee_Sales table records sales data. It includes Employee_ID, Product_ID, Sales_Date, Sales_Price, and Sales_Quantity. Employee_ID and Product_ID are the primary key of this table, and both are foreign keys referencing the Employee and Product tables respectively.
Lastly, the Inventory table keeps track of the inventory. It includes Inventory_ID, Product_ID, Sales_Price, and Stock_Quantity. The Product_ID is the foreign key referencing the Product table. The Inventory_ID is the primary key of this table.
This schema ensures entity integrity by having primary and foreign keys, and it also maintains referential integrity by ensuring that the foreign keys in the tables reference the primary keys of the related tables.
