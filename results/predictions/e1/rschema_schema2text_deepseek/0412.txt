The database schema named rschema_0412 consists of four tables: student, teacher, course, and student_grade.
The student table represents a collection of students with attributes such as student_id, name, contact_information, college, and major. The student_id is the primary key for this table.
The teacher table holds information about the teachers, including employee_id, name, contact_information, and identification. The employee_id is the primary key for this table.
The course table contains information about the courses, including course_id, course_name, course_credit, and teacher_id. The course_id is the primary key and the teacher_id is a foreign key referencing the employee_id in the teacher table.
Lastly, the student_grade table records the grades of students in different courses. It includes student_id, course_id, enrollment_time, and grade. The combination of enrollment_time, course_id, and student_id forms the primary key for this table. The student_id and course_id are foreign keys referencing the student_id and course_id in the student and course tables respectively. The DELETE and UPDATE rules are set to CASCADE, meaning if a record in the referenced table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the referencing table will also be deleted or updated.
