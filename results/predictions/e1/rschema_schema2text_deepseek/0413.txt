The database schema for the database named 'rschema_0413' consists of four tables: 'employee', 'department', 'training_course', and 'course_participation'.
The 'employee' table represents the employees of the organization. It has columns such as 'employee_id', 'name', 'gender', 'position', 'department', 'contact_number', and 'hire_date'. The 'employee_id' column is the primary key of this table. The 'department' column is a foreign key referencing the 'department_id' in the 'department' table, with on delete and on update cascade rules.
The 'department' table represents the departments within the organization. It has columns such as 'department_id', 'department_name', 'department_manager', and 'department_phone'. The 'department_id' column is the primary key of this table. The 'department_manager' column is a foreign key referencing the 'employee_id' in the 'employee' table, with on delete and on update cascade rules.
The 'training_course' table represents the training courses offered by the organization. It has columns such as 'course_id', 'course_name', 'course_duration', 'instructor', and 'course_description'. The 'course_id' column is the primary key of this table.
The 'course_participation' table represents the participation of employees in training courses. It has columns such as 'employee_id', 'course_id', 'training_date', 'training_score', and 'pass_status'. The combination of 'training_date' and 'course_id' and 'employee_id' form the primary key of this table. The 'employee_id' and 'course_id' columns are foreign keys referencing the 'employee_id' and 'course_id' in the 'employee' and 'training_course' tables, respectively, with on delete and on update cascade rules.
This schema ensures the integrity of data by enforcing referential integrity constraints, maintaining the relationship between the tables and ensuring data consistency.
