The database schema for the database named 'rschema_0420' consists of 12 tables. The database is used for storing and managing user information, news, videos, categories, instructors, and their associated courses.
The 'user' table stores information about the users, including their unique user_id, username, password, phone number, WeChat open ID, name, QQ number, email, registration time, and account status. The primary key for this table is the user_id.
The 'news' table stores information about the news articles, including their unique news_id, title, content, publication time, read count, news status, and category_id. The category_id is a foreign key referencing the 'news_category' table, and it has cascade delete and update rules.
The 'video_category_assignment' table is a junction table that links videos and categories, with video_id and category_id as primary keys. Both video_id and category_id are foreign keys referencing the 'video' and 'video_category' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
The 'news_category_assignment' table is a junction table that links news and categories, with news_id and category_id as primary keys. Both news_id and category_id are foreign keys referencing the 'news' and 'news_category' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
The 'news_category' table stores information about the news categories, including their unique category_id, category name, and sort order. The category_id is the primary key.
The 'video' table stores information about the videos, including their unique video_id, title, description, cover image URL, market, directory structure, course count, total duration, price, video status, and category_id. The category_id is a foreign key referencing the 'video_category' table, and it has cascade delete and update rules.
The 'instructor' table stores information about the instructors, including their unique instructor_id, name, avatar, title, introduction, teaching experience, professional field, and teaching achievements. The instructor_id is the primary key.
The 'video_category' table stores information about the video categories, including their unique category_id, category name, and parent category_id. The category_id is the primary key, and parent_category_id is a foreign key referencing the 'video_category' table, with cascade delete and update rules.
The 'instructor_course' table is a junction table that links instructors and videos, with instructor_id and video_id as primary keys. Both instructor_id and video_id are foreign keys referencing the 'instructor' and 'video' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
The 'user_collection' table stores information about user collections, including the user_id, video_id, collection time, and valid status. Both user_id and video_id are foreign keys referencing the 'user' and 'video' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
The 'user_video_order' table stores information about user video orders, including the user_id, video_id, order time, payment amount, payment method, payment status, payment time, and order status. Both user_id and video_id are foreign keys referencing the 'user' and 'video' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
Finally, the 'user_learning_record' table stores information about user learning records, including the user_id, video_id, purchase time, expiration time, viewing duration, last viewing time, and completion status. Both user_id and video_id are foreign keys referencing the 'user' and 'video' tables respectively, with cascade delete and update rules.
