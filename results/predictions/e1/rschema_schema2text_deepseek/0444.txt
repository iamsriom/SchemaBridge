The database schema named 'rschema_0444' contains five tables: Student, Instructor, Course, Attendance_Record, and Leave_Record.
The Student table represents a collection of students, with each student having an Age, Game_Preference, Gender, Name, Phone, and Student_ID. The Student_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Instructor table contains information about instructors, including Gender, Instructor_ID, Name, Title, and Work_Duration. The Instructor_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Course table contains details about the courses, including Course_ID, Course_Name, Credits, Instructor_ID, and Location. The Instructor_ID is a foreign key referencing the Instructor table, and Course_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Attendance_Record table records attendance information, including Attendance_Status, Check-in_Time, Class_Time, Course_ID, Remarks, and Student_ID. The combination of Student_ID and Course_ID, along with Check-in_Time, is the primary key of this table. Course_ID and Student_ID are foreign keys referencing the Course and Student tables respectively.
Finally, the Leave_Record table records information about student absences, including Class_Time, Course_ID, Leave_Reason, Leave_Status, Leave_Time, and Student_ID. The combination of Student_ID and Course_ID, along with Leave_Time, is the primary key of this table. Course_ID and Student_ID are foreign keys referencing the Course and Student tables respectively.
This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary and foreign key constraints, and it allows for referential integrity by maintaining the relationships between tables.
