The 'Order' table contains information about orders including 'Member_ID', 'Order_Date', 'Order_ID' (primary key), and 'Shipping_Date'. The 'Member_ID' column in the 'Order' table is a foreign key referencing the 'Member_ID' in the 'Member' table.
Finally, the 'Order_Detail' table contains details about the orders made, including 'Book_ID', 'Order_ID', 'Quantity'.
