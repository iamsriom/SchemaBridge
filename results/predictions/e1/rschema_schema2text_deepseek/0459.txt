The database schema for the database named 'rschema_0459' consists of four tables: 'employee', 'department', 'project', and 'employee_project_participation'.

The 'employee_id' is the primary key of this table. The 'department_id' is a foreign key referencing the 'department' table, and it has on delete cascade and on update cascade rules.
The 'department' table represents the departments within the organization. It has columns for 'department_id', 'department_name', and 'manager'. The 'department_id' is the primary key of this table. The 'manager' is a foreign key referencing the 'employee' table, and it has on delete cascade and on update cascade rules.
The 'project' table represents the projects within the organization. It has columns for 'project_id', 'project_name', 'department_id', and 'responsible_person'. The 'project_id' is the primary key of this table. The 'department_id' and 'responsible_person' are foreign keys referencing the 'department' and 'employee' tables respectively, and they have on delete cascade and on update cascade rules.
The 'employee_project_participation' table represents the participation of employees in projects. It has columns for 'employee_id', 'project_id', and 'project_participation_time'. The combination of 'project_id' and 'employee_id' and 'project_participation_time' forms the primary key of this table. The 'employee_id' and 'project_id' are foreign keys referencing the 'employee' and 'project' tables respectively, and they have on delete cascade and on update cascade rules.
This schema ensures referential integrity as all foreign keys reference primary keys, and the on delete cascade and on update cascade rules ensure that when the referenced data is updated or deleted, the corresponding data in the referencing tables will also be updated or deleted.
