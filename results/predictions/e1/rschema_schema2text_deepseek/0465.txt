The database schema named "rschema_0465" consists of three tables: User, Attachment_Information, and Email.
The User table represents a user of the system and has the following columns: Account_Creation_Time (datetime), Email_Address (varchar with a maximum length of 255), and Password (varchar with a maximum length of 255). The Email_Address column is the primary key of this table.
The Attachment_Information table contains information about attachments. It has the following columns: Attachment_ID (integer), File_Name (varchar with a maximum length of 255), File_Path (varchar with a maximum length of 255), File_Size (varchar with a maximum length of 255), and Upload_Time (datetime). Attachment_ID is the primary key of this table.
The Email table is used to store emails. It has the following columns: Attachment_ID (integer), Delivery_Time (datetime), Email_Content (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Email_ID (integer), Email_Subject (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Recipient_Email_Address (varchar with a maximum length of 255), Send_Time (datetime), and Sender_Email_Address (varchar with a maximum length of 255). Email_ID is the primary key of this table. The Attachment_ID, Recipient_Email_Address, and Sender_Email_Address columns are foreign keys referencing the Attachment_Information and User tables respectively.
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints. When a record in the User table is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the Attachment_Information and Email tables will also be updated or deleted based on the specified rules (CASCADE or RESTRICT).
