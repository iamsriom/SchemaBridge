The 'Responsibility' table represents the responsibility of an agent for a property, with columns for 'Agent_ID', 'Property_ID', 'Responsibility_end_time', and 'Responsibility_start_time'. Both 'Agent_ID' and 'Property_ID' are foreign keys referencing the 'Agent' and 'Property' tables respectively. The combination of 'Agent_ID' and 'Property_ID' forms the primary key of this table.
