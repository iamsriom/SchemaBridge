The Customer table represents a customer entity, with columns for Address, Contact_Information, Customer_ID (as the primary key), and Customer_Name. The data type for Address and Contact_Information is VARCHAR(255), and Customer_ID is an INTEGER.
The Product table represents a product entity, with columns for Product_ID (as the primary key), Product_Name, and Unit_Price. Product_ID is an INTEGER, and Unit_Price is a FLOAT.
