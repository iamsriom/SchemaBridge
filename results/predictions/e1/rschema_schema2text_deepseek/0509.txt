The database schema named "rschema_0509" comprises seven tables: Patient, Department, Doctor, Ward, Hospitalization_Record, Registration_Record, and Nurse.
The Patient table contains information about patients, including their age, emergency contact phone number, gender, identity card number, medical record number, name, patient ID, and phone number. The Department table represents departments within the hospital, with columns for contact phone number, department head ID, department ID, department name, and location. The Doctor table holds information about doctors, including contact phone number, department ID, doctor ID, gender, name, and title. The Ward table contains information about wards in the hospital, including bed count, head ID, location, remaining bed count, and ward ID. The Hospitalization_Record table records hospitalization details, including attending physician ID, bed number, discharge time, hospitalization start time, patient ID, and ward ID. The Registration_Record table holds consultation records, including consultation time, diagnosis result, doctor ID, and patient ID. Finally, the Nurse table contains information about nurses, including department ID, duties, gender, name, nurse ID, phone number, and title. All tables have primary keys and foreign keys to ensure data integrity and referential integrity.
