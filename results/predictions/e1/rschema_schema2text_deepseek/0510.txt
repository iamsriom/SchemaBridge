The database schema named "rschema_0510" consists of two tables - "Vehicle_information_table" and "Parking_space_table".
The "Vehicle_information_table" is used to store information about vehicles. It includes columns for 'Contact_information', 'License_plate_number', and 'Owner_name'. The 'License_plate_number' is the primary key for this table.
The "Parking_space_table" is used to store information about parking spaces. It includes columns for 'Owner', 'Parking_space_number', 'Status'. The 'Owner' column is not null and is a foreign key referencing the 'License_plate_number' in the 'Vehicle_information_table'. The 'Owner' and 'Parking_space_number' are the primary keys for this table.
The 'Status' column in the 'Parking_space_table' indicates whether the parking space is occupied or not. The foreign key constraint ensures that the 'Owner' in the 'Parking_space_table' matches the 'License_plate_number' in the 'Vehicle_information_table', maintaining referential integrity.
