The database schema named "rschema_0517" consists of 9 tables. The tables are defined as follows:
1. **Product**: This table represents the product details. The columns include Model, Product_ID (primary key), Product_name, Quantity, and Unit_price.
2. **Supermarket**: This table holds information about the supermarkets. The columns include Address, Manager_employee_ID (not null), Phone, Supermarket_ID (primary key), and Supermarket_name. The Manager_employee_ID is a foreign key referencing the Employee table.
3. **Department**: This table contains information about the departments. The columns include Department_ID (primary key), Department_name, Department_responsibility, Employee_count, Establishment_time, and Manager_employee_ID (not null). The Manager_employee_ID and Supermarket_ID are foreign keys referencing the Employee and Supermarket tables respectively.
4. **Employee**: This table contains details about the employees. The columns include Contact_info, Department_ID (not null), Employee_ID (primary key), Name, Position, and Salary. The Department_ID is a foreign key referencing the Department table.
5. **Allocation**: This table keeps track of product allocations. The columns include Allocation_quantity, Allocation_time, Product_ID (not null), and Sales_employee_ID (not null). The Product_ID and Sales_employee_ID are foreign keys referencing the Product and Employee tables respectively.
6. **Establish**: This table establishes the relationship between departments and supermarkets. The columns include Department_ID (not null) and Supermarket_ID (not null). Both are foreign keys referencing the Department and Supermarket tables respectively.
7. **Manage_supermarket**: This table manages the relationship between managers and supermarkets. The columns include Manager_employee_ID (not null) and Supermarket_ID (not null). Both are foreign keys referencing the Employee and Supermarket tables respectively.
8. **Manage_department**: This table manages the relationship between managers and departments. The columns include Department_ID (not null) and Manager_employee_ID (not null). Both are foreign keys referencing the Department and Employee tables respectively.
9. **Work**: This table represents the work relationship between employees and departments. The columns include Department_ID (not null) and Employee_ID (not null). Both are foreign keys referencing the Department and Employee tables respectively.
The schema is designed to maintain data integrity by ensuring that all tables have primary keys, foreign keys, and not null constraints. It also includes indexes to optimize data access and manipulation.
