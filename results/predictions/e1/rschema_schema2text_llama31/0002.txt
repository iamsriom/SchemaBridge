The rschema_0002 database is designed to manage information about multimodal transportation tasks, which involve multiple modes of transportation and carriers. The database consists of six tables: transportation_mode, carrier, multimodal_transportation_task, task_detail, task_transportation_mode, and task_carrier. The transportation_mode table stores information about different modes of transportation, with each mode identified by a unique mode_id, and includes a schema_name to provide context. The carrier table holds details about the carriers involved in these transportation tasks, with each carrier identified by a unique carrier_id and including their name and contact phone number. The multimodal_transportation_task table contains information about each transportation task, including the task number, origin, destination, cargo name, weight, transportation time, start time, and end time. The task_detail table provides a more detailed breakdown of each task, including the mode of transportation used, the carrier involved, and the start and end times for each leg of the journey, with foreign keys linking it to the transportation_mode, carrier, and multimodal_transportation_task tables. The task_transportation_mode and task_carrier tables are used to establish many-to-many relationships between tasks and modes of transportation, and tasks and carriers, respectively, with foreign keys referencing the multimodal_transportation_task table. These relationships are set up with cascade delete and update rules, meaning that if a task is deleted or updated, all associated details and relationships will be automatically updated or deleted accordingly.
