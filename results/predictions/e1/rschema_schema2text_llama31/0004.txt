The rschema_0004 database is designed to manage customer information and their subscriptions to various service plans. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, email, phone number, and address, with a unique Customer_Number serving as the primary key. The Service_Plan table contains information about the different service plans available, with a unique Plan_Number as the primary key and a description of the service content. The Subscription_Record table tracks the subscription history of each customer, including the customer's Customer_Number and the Plan_Number they are subscribed to, along with the start and end dates of the subscription, and the operating personnel involved. The Subscription_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the Customer_Number, Plan_Number, and Subscription_Time, ensuring that each subscription is uniquely identified. The Customer_Number in the Subscription_Record table is a foreign key referencing the Customer table, and the Plan_Number is a foreign key referencing the Service_Plan table, establishing relationships between customers, their subscriptions, and the service plans they are subscribed to. The Customer_Number in the Subscription_Record table is not nullable, indicating that every subscription is associated with a specific customer, and the Plan_Number is also not nullable, signifying that every subscription is tied to a specific service plan.
