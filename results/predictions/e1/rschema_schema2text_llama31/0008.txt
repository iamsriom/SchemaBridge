The rschema_0008 database is designed to manage information about equipment maintenance. The Equipment table stores details about the equipment, including a unique identifier, model, name, and current usage status. Each piece of equipment is assigned a unique identifier, which is the primary key, and the model and name are stored as strings up to 255 characters long. The Usage_Status field is also a string, allowing the current status of the equipment to be recorded. The Maintenance_Personnel table contains information about the individuals responsible for performing maintenance, with a unique identifier, email address, name, and telephone number. The Equipment_Maintenance table links equipment with the personnel who perform maintenance on it, including the maintenance result, the date and time of the maintenance, and the identifiers of the equipment and personnel involved. The Equipment_ID and Maintenance_Personnel_ID fields in the Equipment_Maintenance table are both primary keys, ensuring that each maintenance record is uniquely associated with a piece of equipment and a maintenance personnel. The Equipment_ID field in the Equipment_Maintenance table references the Equipment table's Equipment_ID field, and the Maintenance_Personnel_ID field references the Maintenance_Personnel table's Maintenance_Personnel_ID field, establishing relationships between the tables.
