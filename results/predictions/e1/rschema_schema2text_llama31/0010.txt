The rschema_0010 database is designed to store information about members, meetings, and their participation. The 'member' table contains details about each member, including their unique identifier, name, gender, date of birth, and contact information, with all fields being required and stored as strings. The 'meeting' table holds information about each meeting, such as its type, time, location, content, and topic, with all fields being required and stored as strings. The 'meeting_participation' table tracks which members attend each meeting, including the member's identifier, meeting identifier, any remarks, and the time of attendance, with all fields being required and stored as strings. The 'meeting_participation' table has a composite primary key consisting of the member's identifier, meeting identifier, and attendance time, ensuring each combination is unique. The 'meeting_id' field in the 'meeting_participation' table references the 'meeting_id' field in the 'meeting' table, enforcing referential integrity by ensuring that a meeting must exist before a member can be recorded as attending it. If a meeting is deleted, all corresponding records in the 'meeting_participation' table will also be deleted, and if a meeting is updated, the corresponding records in the 'meeting_participation' table will also be updated.
