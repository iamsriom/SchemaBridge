The rschema_0011 database is designed to manage information about farms, their operators, equipment, and maintenance records. The Farm table contains details about each farm, including its address, contact information, a unique identifier (Farm_ID), and its name. The Operator table stores information about the individuals who operate the farms, with a unique identifier (Operator_ID) and their name, as well as their phone number. The Equipment table holds data about the equipment used on each farm, including its model, name, purchase date, usage status, and a foreign key referencing the Farm_ID to establish the relationship between the equipment and the farm it belongs to. The Maintenance_Record table tracks maintenance activities performed on the equipment, including the equipment's identifier, the date of maintenance, the type of maintenance, the result of the maintenance, and the operator who performed it, with foreign keys referencing the Equipment_ID and Operator_ID to establish relationships with the Equipment and Operator tables, and a composite primary key consisting of the Equipment_ID, Operator_ID, and Maintenance_Date to uniquely identify each maintenance record, ensuring that the same maintenance activity cannot be recorded multiple times for the same equipment and operator on the same date.
