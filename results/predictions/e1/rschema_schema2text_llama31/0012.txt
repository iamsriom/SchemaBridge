The rschema_0012 database is designed to manage inventory and circulation records for a warehouse. The Warehouse table stores information about each warehouse, including its address, capacity, current inventory level, a unique identifier, and a name. Each warehouse is uniquely identified by its Warehouse_ID, which is a primary key. The Product table contains details about each product, such as its inventory level, name, and specification, and is identified by a unique Product_ID, which is also a primary key. The Product_Circulation_Record table tracks changes to the inventory levels of products within the warehouse, including the date of the change, the operator who made the change, the quantity changed, and a unique Record_ID. This table also references the Warehouse table through the Warehouse_ID, which is a foreign key that links each record to the specific warehouse where the change occurred, and the Product table through the Product_ID, which links each record to the product that was affected by the change. The Warehouse_ID in the Product_Circulation_Record table is not nullable, meaning that every record must be associated with a warehouse, and the foreign key references the Warehouse_ID in the Warehouse table, ensuring that only valid warehouse IDs can be used.
