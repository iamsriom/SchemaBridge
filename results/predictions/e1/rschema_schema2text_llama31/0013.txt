The rschema_0013 database is designed to store and manage data related to weather stations and their associated meteorological data, as well as subscription information for farms. The Weather_Station table contains information about each individual weather station, including its contact details, location, name, and the person in charge, which is identified by a unique Weather_Station_ID. The Meteorological_Data table stores the actual weather readings collected from each station, including humidity, precipitation, temperature, wind direction, and wind speed, with a corresponding recording date and a foreign key referencing the Weather_Station_ID to link each reading to its source station. The Subscription_Data table tracks which farms are subscribed to which weather stations, with a unique combination of farm name and weather station ID serving as the primary key, and a foreign key referencing the Weather_Station_ID to ensure that only valid stations are associated with subscriptions.
