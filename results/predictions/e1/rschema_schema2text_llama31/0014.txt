The rschema_0014 database is used to manage information about irrigation systems and their usage. The Irrigation_System table stores details about each irrigation system, including its installation date, maintenance records, name, a unique system ID, and type. Each system is identified by a unique System_ID, which is the primary key of the table. The Plot table contains information about individual plots of land, such as their area, current irrigation status, and soil type, with each plot having a unique Plot_ID that serves as its primary key. The Irrigation_Record table tracks the irrigation activities for each plot, including the amount of water used, the date and duration of the irrigation, and the ID of the plot and irrigation system involved. The Irrigation_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the Irrigation_Date, System_ID, and Plot_ID, ensuring that each irrigation event is uniquely identified. The Plot_ID and System_ID columns in the Irrigation_Record table are foreign keys that reference the Plot and Irrigation_System tables, respectively, establishing relationships between the tables and enforcing referential integrity. The Plot_ID column in Irrigation_Record is not allowed to be null, indicating that every irrigation record must be associated with a specific plot, while the System_ID column is also not allowed to be null, meaning every irrigation record must be linked to a particular irrigation system.
