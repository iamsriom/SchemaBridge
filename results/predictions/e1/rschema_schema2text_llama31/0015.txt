The rschema_0015 database is designed to manage information related to agricultural plots, pests and diseases, and monitoring records. The database contains four tables: plot, pest_and_disease, employee, and monitoring_record. The plot table stores information about individual plots, including their unique plot number, area, soil type, current pest and disease status, and geographic location. Each plot is uniquely identified by its plot number, which serves as the primary key. The pest_and_disease table holds details about different pests and diseases, such as their unique number, name, degree of harm, prevention and control methods, and season of occurrence, with pest_and_disease_number being the primary key. The employee table stores information about the staff involved in monitoring, including their unique employee number, name, gender, age, and contact information. The monitoring_record table captures data from monitoring activities, including the plot number, pest number, monitoring time, monitoring results, and the employee number of the staff member who conducted the monitoring. This table has a composite primary key consisting of plot number, pest number, and monitoring time, and it references the pest_and_disease and plot tables through foreign keys, which are set up to cascade delete and update operations.
