The rschema_0016 database is designed to manage information about farms, their plots, and planting plans. The Farm table stores details about each farm, including its address, contact information, a unique identifier (Farm_ID), and its name. The Plot table contains information about each plot, such as its area, current status, a unique identifier (Plot_ID), and the type of soil it has. The Planting_Plan table is used to record planting plans for each farm and plot, including the type of crop, expected harvest time, the operator responsible, the quantity of the crop to be planted, the time of planting, and the farm and plot it is associated with. The Planting_Plan table has a composite primary key consisting of the Farm_ID, Plot_ID, and Planting_Time, ensuring that each planting plan is unique to a specific farm, plot, and time. The Farm_ID and Plot_ID in the Planting_Plan table are foreign keys referencing the Farm and Plot tables, respectively, establishing relationships between the tables and enforcing referential integrity. The Farm_ID column in the Planting_Plan table is not nullable, indicating that every planting plan must be associated with a specific farm, and the Plot_ID column is also not nullable, indicating that every planting plan must be associated with a specific plot.
