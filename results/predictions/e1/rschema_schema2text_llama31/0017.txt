The rschema_0017 database is designed to manage information about workers, workshops, raw materials, and products involved in a production process. The Worker table stores details about individual workers, including their contact number and name, with a unique identifier for each worker. The Workshop table contains information about the workshops where production takes place, such as the workshop's address and name, with a unique identifier for each workshop. The Raw_material table lists the different types of raw materials used in the production process, identified by a unique raw material ID. The Product table stores details about the products being produced, including their names. The Workshop_raw_material_management table tracks the quantity of each raw material stored in each workshop, linking raw materials to workshops through a composite primary key. The Product_processing_task table records the processing tasks for each product, including the processing time, product quality rating, and production quantity, with a foreign key linking to the Product table. The Worker_processing_product table links workers to specific processing tasks, ensuring that each task is assigned to a worker, and the Raw_materials_involved_in_product_processing table tracks which raw materials are used in each processing task.
