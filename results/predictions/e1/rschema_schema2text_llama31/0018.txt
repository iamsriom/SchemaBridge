The rschema_0018 database is designed to track and manage equipment usage and maintenance records. At its core, the Equipment table stores information about the equipment itself, including a unique identifier, model, and name. The Equipment_ID, which is the primary key, ensures that each piece of equipment is uniquely identified. The Usage_Record table captures details about when and where equipment is used, including the equipment's ID, usage location, time of usage, and the user responsible for the usage. The Equipment_ID in this table is a foreign key referencing the Equipment table, establishing a relationship between the usage records and the equipment they belong to. The primary key of the Usage_Record table is a composite key consisting of the Equipment_ID and Usage_Time, meaning that each equipment can have multiple usage records, but each usage record is tied to a specific time and equipment. The Maintenance_Record table is similar to the Usage_Record table, but it stores information about maintenance activities performed on the equipment, including the maintenance content, a unique maintenance record ID, and the time of maintenance. The Equipment_ID in this table is also a foreign key referencing the Equipment table, linking maintenance records to the equipment they pertain to. The primary key of the Maintenance_Record table is also a composite key, consisting of the Equipment_ID and Maintenance_Time, indicating that each equipment can have multiple maintenance records, each tied to a specific time and equipment.
