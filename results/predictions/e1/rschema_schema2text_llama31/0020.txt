The rschema_0020 database is designed to manage information about suppliers and their purchases of ingredients. The Supplier table contains details about each supplier, including their name, contact information, and a unique identifier, Supplier_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Ingredient table stores information about the ingredients purchased, with each ingredient having a unique identifier, Ingredient_ID, which is the primary key, as well as a name and specification, and a price. The Purchase_Record table tracks the purchases made by suppliers, including the ingredient purchased, the quantity purchased, the purchase amount, the date of the purchase, and a unique purchase identifier, Purchase_ID. The Supplier_ID and Ingredient_ID in the Purchase_Record table form a composite primary key, ensuring that each purchase is uniquely identified. The Supplier_ID in the Purchase_Record table references the Supplier_ID in the Supplier table, establishing a relationship between the supplier and the purchase, and the Ingredient_ID references the Ingredient_ID in the Ingredient table, linking the ingredient to the purchase. The Supplier_ID in the Purchase_Record table is not nullable, indicating that a supplier must be specified for each purchase, and the Ingredient_ID is also not nullable, meaning that an ingredient must be specified for each purchase.
