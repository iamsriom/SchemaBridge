The rschema_0022 database is designed to manage documents and their associated personnel and activities. The database consists of five tables: document, document_management_personnel, approval_record, archiving_record, and query_record. The document table stores information about each document, including its unique identifier, name, category, creation time, and status. The document_management_personnel table contains details about the personnel involved in managing documents, such as their employee ID, name, gender, position, and contact information. The approval_record table tracks the approval history of each document, including the approver's employee ID, approval time, and comments, and is linked to both the document and document_management_personnel tables through foreign keys that enforce referential integrity. Similarly, the archiving_record table stores information about the archiving of documents, including the archivist's employee ID, archiving time, and location, and is also linked to the document and document_management_personnel tables. The query_record table tracks the queries made on documents, including the query personnel's employee ID, query time, and result, and is linked to the document and document_management_personnel tables. Each table has a primary key that uniquely identifies each record, and some tables have composite primary keys to ensure that multiple columns are used to uniquely identify each record. Additionally, the database uses foreign keys to establish relationships between tables, with some foreign keys enforcing cascade deletion and update rules to maintain data consistency.
