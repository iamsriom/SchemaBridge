The rschema_0023 database is designed to manage information about donors, recipients, donated items, and donation records. The database contains five tables: donor, recipient, donated_item, donation_record, and donation_record_details. The donor table stores information about individuals who donate items, including their donor number, name, gender, date of birth, and contact information, with a primary key of donor number. The recipient table stores information about individuals who receive donations, with the same structure as the donor table. The donated_item table stores information about the items being donated, including the item number, name, quantity, and description, with a primary key of item number. The donation_record table stores information about each donation, including a unique donation record ID, the recipient number, the time of donation, and the item number, with a primary key of donation record ID and foreign keys referencing the donor and recipient tables with cascade delete and update rules. The donation_record_details table stores additional details about each donation, including the donation record ID and item number, with a composite primary key of donation record ID and item number, and foreign keys referencing the donation_record and donated_item tables with cascade delete and update rules.
