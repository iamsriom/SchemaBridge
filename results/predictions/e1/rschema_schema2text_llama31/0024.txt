The rschema_0024 database is designed to manage the operations of an agricultural and sideline products business, encompassing employee information, product details, and transaction records. The database contains six tables: agricultural_and_sideline_products, employee, department, purchase_record, processing_record, and sales_record. The agricultural_and_sideline_products table stores information about the products, including their unique product ID, name, and origin. The employee table holds details about the staff, such as their employee ID, name, gender, telephone number, position, department ID, and joining time. Each employee is assigned to a department, which is recorded in the department table along with its ID, name, person in charge ID, responsibilities, and total number of people in the department. The department table also includes a foreign key referencing the employee table, indicating that the person in charge of a department is an employee. The purchase_record table tracks the products purchased, including the product ID, purchase time, quantity, person in charge ID, purchase price, shipper name, shipping address, shipping time, and shipper phone number. The purchase_record table has composite primary keys consisting of the person in charge ID, product ID, and purchase time, and includes foreign keys referencing the employee and agricultural_and_sideline_products tables. The processing_record table records the processing of products, including the product ID, processing time, quantity, person in charge ID, and processing cost, with a composite primary key of person in charge ID, product ID, and processing time, and foreign keys referencing the employee and agricultural_and_sideline_products tables. The sales_record table tracks the sales of products, including the product ID, sales time, quantity, salesperson ID, sales price, sales location, customer name, customer phone number, and customer address, with a composite primary key of salesperson ID, product ID, and sales time, and foreign keys referencing the employee and agricultural_and_sideline_products tables.
