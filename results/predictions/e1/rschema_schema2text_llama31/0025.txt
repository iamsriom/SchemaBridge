The rschema_0025 database is designed to manage information about tobacco products, employees, and sales records. The database contains five tables: tobacco_products, department, employee, production_record, and production_record_details. The tobacco_products table stores information about each type of tobacco product, including its product ID, name, type, production date, shelf life, sales price, and production batch number. The department table holds details about each department within the organization, including its ID, name, total number of employees, person in charge, and department responsibilities. The employee table stores information about each employee, including their ID, name, gender, phone number, position, joining date, and department ID. The production_record table tracks sales records, including the product ID, sales time, salesperson ID, customer name, sales quantity, sales price, customer phone number, customer address, and sales type. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the salesperson ID, product ID, and sales time. The production_record_details table provides additional details about each sales record, including the producer ID and production record ID. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that data in one table is linked to corresponding data in another table, such as linking employees to departments and sales records to tobacco products.
