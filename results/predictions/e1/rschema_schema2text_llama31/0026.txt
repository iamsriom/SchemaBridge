The rschema_0026 database is designed to manage the production, inventory, and sales of textiles, as well as the performance evaluation of employees within a company. The Textile table stores information about each type of textile, including its name, price, production date, unique ID, and type. The Department table contains details about each department within the company, such as its name, manager, and contact information, identified by a unique department ID. The Inventory table tracks the quantity of each textile in stock, including the time it was stocked, and links each item to its corresponding textile ID in the Textile table. The Employee table stores information about each employee, including their department ID, name, phone number, position, and gender, and is linked to the Department table through the department ID. The Production_Relationship table records the production history of each textile, including the producer's ID, production time, and quantity produced, and links each entry to the Textile and Employee tables. The Sales_Relationship table tracks the sales of each textile, including the customer, sales personnel ID, sales time, and quantity sold, and links each entry to the Textile and Employee tables. The Performance_Evaluation table stores the performance evaluation of each employee, including their ID, evaluation date, score, and a unique performance ID, and is linked to the Employee table through the employee ID.
