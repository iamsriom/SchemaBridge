The rschema_0027 database is designed to manage information about a company's departments, employees, clothing products, production records, and sales transactions. The Department table stores details about each department, including its contact information, unique department ID, name, and person in charge. The Clothing table contains information about the products sold, such as inventory levels, name, price, product ID, production date, and style. The Employee table holds data about each staff member, including their department ID, unique employee ID, gender, name, phone number, and position, with a foreign key referencing the Department table to establish the relationship between employees and departments. The Production_Record table tracks production details, linking each product to the employee who produced it and the time of production, with a composite primary key combining product ID, producer employee ID, and production time, and foreign keys referencing the Employee and Clothing tables. The Sales_Record table records sales transactions, including the customer, product ID, sales quantity, sales time, and salesperson employee ID, with a composite primary key combining product ID, salesperson employee ID, and sales time, and foreign keys referencing the Clothing and Employee tables to link sales to products and sales staff.
