The rschema_0028 database is used to manage information about timber, employees, and their activities. The timber table stores details about different types of timber, including their names, types, procurement prices, processing costs, and sales prices, with each timber item uniquely identified by a timber ID. The department table contains information about the various departments within the organization, including their names, supervisors, and contact information, with each department identified by a unique department ID. The employee table holds details about individual employees, including their names, genders, phone numbers, positions, and departments, with each employee uniquely identified by an employee ID. The employee ID is also used to link employees to their respective departments. The timber procurement table tracks the procurement of timber, including the timber ID, employee ID, procurement quantity, supplier, and procurement time, with a composite primary key consisting of the procurement time, employee ID, and timber ID. The timber processing table records the processing of timber, including the timber ID, employee ID, processing quantity, and processing time, with a composite primary key consisting of the employee ID, processing time, and timber ID. The department and employee tables are linked through a foreign key constraint, where the supervisor in the department table references the employee ID in the employee table, and the employee ID in the employee table references the department ID in the department table, both with cascade delete and update rules. Similarly, the timber procurement and processing tables are linked to the timber and employee tables through foreign key constraints, with cascade delete and update rules, ensuring that when a timber item or employee is deleted, all related procurement and processing records are also deleted.
