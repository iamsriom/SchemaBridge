The rschema_0029 database is designed to manage information about furniture, employees, departments, and sales. The furniture table contains details about each type of furniture, including its unique identifier, name, type, production date, and price. The department table stores information about each department, including its unique identifier, supervisor's employee ID, name, and contact information, with a foreign key referencing the employee table to establish the supervisor-employee relationship. The employee table holds details about each employee, including their unique identifier, gender, name, phone number, position, and department, with a foreign key referencing the department table to establish the department-employee relationship. The production table tracks the production of furniture, including the furniture ID, producer's employee ID, production time, and production quantity, with composite primary keys referencing the furniture and employee tables to establish relationships between furniture, producers, and production times. The sales table records sales transactions, including the furniture ID, sales representative's employee ID, sales time, sales quantity, and customer, with composite primary keys referencing the furniture and employee tables to establish relationships between furniture, sales representatives, and sales times.
