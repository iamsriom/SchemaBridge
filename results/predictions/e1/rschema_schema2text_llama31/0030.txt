The rschema_0030 database is designed to manage the inventory and sales of various products, as well as the employees responsible for their production and sale. The database consists of six tables: raw_material_table, product_table, production_record_table, inventory_record_table, sales_record_table, and employee_department_table. The raw_material_table stores information about the raw materials used in product production, with each raw material identified by a unique raw_material_id, and includes details such as name, production date, and shelf life. The product_table contains information about the products themselves, including product_id, name, composition, production date, shelf life, and price, and is linked to the raw_material_table through a foreign key referencing the raw_material_id. The production_record_table tracks the production of products, with each record including the product_id, quantity produced, producer's employee_id, and production time, and is linked to both the product_table and employee_department_table through foreign keys. The inventory_record_table and sales_record_table store records of product stock and sales, respectively, with each record including the product_id, quantity, and the employee_id of the person responsible for stocking or selling the product, and are also linked to the product_table and employee_department_table. The employee_department_table stores information about the employees, including their employee_id, name, gender, phone number, department_id, and position, and is linked to the production_record_table, inventory_record_table, and sales_record_table through foreign keys.
