The rschema_0031 database is designed to manage information about moving teams and their tasks. The Moving_Team table stores details about the teams, with each team assigned a unique identifier, Team_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Team_Name column allows for a description of up to 255 characters. The Mover table contains information about individual movers, including their age, gender, name, phone number, and a unique Worker_ID, which is the primary key. Each mover is assigned to a team, as indicated by the Assigned_Team_ID, which references the Team_ID in the Moving_Team table and is not nullable. The Moving_Task table tracks the tasks assigned to each team, including the team they are assigned to, as indicated by the Assigned_Team_ID, which also references the Team_ID in the Moving_Team table and is not nullable. Other details about each task include the customer's feedback, name, phone number, destination and starting addresses, moving cost, date, and quantity of items moved. The Task_ID serves as the primary key for the Moving_Task table.
