The rschema_0032 database is designed to manage information about rental locations, customers, vehicles, and rental tasks. The Rental_Location table stores details about each rental location, including its address and name, with a unique identifier for each location. The Customer table holds information about each customer, such as their name and phone number, with a unique identifier for each customer. The Vehicle table contains details about each vehicle, including its model and the location where it is based, with a foreign key referencing the Rental_Location table to establish the relationship between vehicles and their locations. The Rental_Task table stores information about each rental task, including the customer's feedback, the rental cost, the rental date and duration, and the vehicle's condition after the rental, with foreign keys referencing the Customer and Vehicle tables to establish relationships between rentals and their corresponding customers and vehicles. The Rental_Task table also has a composite primary key consisting of the vehicle ID, customer ID, and rental date, ensuring that each rental task is uniquely identified. Additionally, the Rental_Task table has a unique constraint on the combination of vehicle ID and customer ID, and a check constraint is implied by the foreign key relationships to ensure data consistency.
