The rschema_0033 database is designed to manage information about electronic products, sales teams, customers, sales tasks, and sales personnel. The Electronic_Product table stores details about the products, including their model, name, price, and a unique identifier called Product_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Sales_Team table contains information about the sales teams, such as their contact information, manager, team ID, and team name, with the Team_ID being the primary key. The Customer table holds customer data, including their ID, name, and phone number, with Customer_ID being the primary key. The Sales_Task table stores details about sales tasks, including the sales cost, date, quantity, and a unique identifier called Sales_Task_ID, which is the primary key. The Sales_Personnel table contains information about the sales personnel, including their gender, name, phone number, position, and a unique identifier called Sales_Personnel_ID, which is the primary key, and also links each sales personnel to a specific sales team through the Team_ID foreign key, which references the Team_ID in the Sales_Team table. The Sales_Task_Customer table stores feedback and ratings from customers about sales tasks, linking each customer to a specific sales task through the Customer_ID and Sales_Task_ID foreign keys, which reference the Customer_ID and Sales_Task_ID in the Customer and Sales_Task tables, respectively. The Sales_Task_Product table links electronic products to sales tasks, with the Product_ID and Sales_Task_ID foreign keys referencing the Product_ID in the Electronic_Product table and the Sales_Task_ID in the Sales_Task table, respectively.
