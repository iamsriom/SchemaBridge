The rschema_0035 database is designed to manage information about customers and their relationships with various projects. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their address, email, name, and phone number, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Project table contains information about each project, such as its actual and estimated completion dates, budget, name, and start date, with a unique identifier, Project_ID, serving as the primary key. The Customer_Project_Relationship table connects customers to the projects they are involved in, storing feedback and satisfaction ratings for each relationship, with a composite primary key consisting of the Customer_ID and Project_ID. The Customer_ID in this table references the Customer_ID in the Customer table, and the Project_ID references the Project_ID in the Project table, establishing a many-to-many relationship between customers and projects. The Customer_ID column in the Customer_Project_Relationship table does not allow null values, and the Project_ID column also does not allow null values, ensuring that every customer is associated with at least one project and every project is associated with at least one customer.
