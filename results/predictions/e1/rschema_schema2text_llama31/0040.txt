The rschema_0040 database is designed to manage information about properties, salespeople, customers, and sales contracts. The Property_Information table stores details about properties, including their address, area, unique identifier, sales price, and sales status. The Salesperson table contains information about salespeople, such as their email, name, phone number, and unique identifier. The Customer_Information table holds details about customers, including their address, unique identifier, email, name, and phone number. The Sales_Contract table links customers and properties together, storing the contract date, price, signing time, specific content, and unique identifiers for the customer and property involved, with the customer and property identifiers referencing the Customer_Information and Property_Information tables respectively. The Salesperson_Sold_Property table tracks which salespeople are involved in each sales contract, with the contract and salesperson identifiers referencing the Sales_Contract and Salesperson tables respectively, and a composite primary key ensuring each salesperson can only be associated with a sales contract once.
