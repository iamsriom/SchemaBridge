The rschema_0041 database is designed to manage properties and their associated residents, staff, and maintenance activities. The Property_Manager table stores information about property managers, including their email, name, phone number, and a unique identifier. The Repair_Staff table contains details about repair staff, such as their name and phone number, with a unique identifier for each staff member. The Community table represents individual communities within the properties, with a unique identifier, location, name, and a foreign key referencing the Property_Manager table to establish the relationship between the community and its property manager. The Resident table stores information about residents, including their email, name, phone number, and a unique identifier, with a foreign key referencing the Community table to link residents to their respective communities. The Management table maintains a record of which property managers are responsible for which communities, with a composite primary key combining the Property_Manager_ID and Community_ID. The Repair_Order table tracks maintenance requests, including the community where the request was made, a description of the issue, the cost of the repair, the date of the request, a unique identifier, and foreign keys referencing the Community, Repair_Staff, and Resident tables to establish relationships between the repair order and the relevant parties. The Residence table represents the relationship between residents and their respective communities, with a composite primary key combining the Resident_ID and Community_ID. The Execution table tracks which repair staff members are assigned to which repair orders, with a composite primary key combining the Repair_Order_ID and Repair_Staff_ID, and foreign keys referencing the Repair_Order and Repair_Staff tables to establish the relationships between the execution and the relevant parties.
