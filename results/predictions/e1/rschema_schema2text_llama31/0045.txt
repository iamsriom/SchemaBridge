The rschema_0045 database is designed to manage inventory and warehouse operations. It consists of five tables: warehouse, good, warehouse_good_record, inventory, and outgoing_record. The warehouse table stores information about individual warehouses, including their number, name, address, inventory accuracy, and outbound efficiency. Each warehouse is uniquely identified by its warehouse number. The good table contains details about the products stored in the warehouses, such as their name, model, barcode, and price, with each product identified by its unique barcode. The warehouse_good_record table tracks the quantity of each product stored in each warehouse, linking the warehouse number and product barcode to the inventory quantity. The inventory table records the time, person responsible, and quantity of inventory taken at each warehouse, referencing the warehouse number and product barcode. The outgoing_record table logs outgoing transactions, including the warehouse number, product barcode, time, quantity, person responsible, and receiving unit, linking the warehouse number and product barcode to the outgoing quantity. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign keys, ensuring that warehouse numbers and product barcodes are consistent across tables, and that when a warehouse or product is deleted, its associated records in other tables are automatically updated or deleted accordingly.
