The rschema_0046 database is designed to manage the points system of a loyalty program. The database consists of three tables: Member, Shopping_Record, and Point_Redemption_Record. The Member table stores information about each member, including their current points balance, a unique member ID, their name, and phone number. The Member_ID is the primary key, meaning it uniquely identifies each member and cannot be null. The Shopping_Record table tracks every shopping transaction, recording the member's ID, the change in their points balance, the time of the transaction, and the total price of the goods purchased. The Member_ID is a foreign key referencing the Member table, ensuring that only valid member IDs can be used, and the primary key is a composite of the Member_ID and Shopping_Time, meaning each shopping transaction is uniquely identified by the member and the time of the transaction. The Point_Redemption_Record table records every time a member redeems their points, storing the member's ID, the points consumed, the name of the redeemed product, the quantity redeemed, and the redemption time. Like the Shopping_Record table, the Member_ID is a foreign key referencing the Member table, and the primary key is a composite of the Member_ID and Redemption_Time, ensuring each redemption is uniquely identified by the member and the time of redemption.
