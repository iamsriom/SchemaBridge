The rschema_0047 database is designed to manage information about news articles, their reporters, editors, and publishers. The Reporter table stores details about individual reporters, including their email, phone number, unique Reporter ID, and name. The News_Article table contains information about each article, such as its content, title, publication time, review time, and submission time, with a unique Article ID serving as its primary key. The Editor table holds data about editors, including their email, phone number, and unique Editor ID. Similarly, the Publisher table stores information about publishers, including their email, phone number, and unique Publisher ID. The Article_Reporter_Table, Article_Editor_Table, and Article_Publisher_Table are bridge tables that establish relationships between news articles and the entities involved in their creation and publication, with each table having a composite primary key consisting of the Article ID and the ID of the respective entity. The Article_Reporter_Table links articles to their respective reporters, the Article_Editor_Table links articles to their editors, and the Article_Publisher_Table links articles to their publishers, with foreign keys referencing the corresponding tables to ensure data consistency and integrity.
