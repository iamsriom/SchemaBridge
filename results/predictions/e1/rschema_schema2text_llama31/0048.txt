The rschema_0048 database is designed to track the publication process of manuscripts, involving reporters, editors, and publishers. The Reporter table stores information about the individuals who submit manuscripts, including their email, name, phone number, and a unique Reporter ID. The Editor table contains details about the editors, such as their email, name, and phone number, identified by a unique Editor ID. The Publisher table holds information about the publishers, including their email, name, phone number, a unique Publisher ID, and the section they are responsible for. The Manuscript table stores the submitted manuscripts, with a unique Manuscript ID, content, status, and title. The Writing_Record table tracks the writing process, linking a manuscript to its reporter, submission time, responsible part, and manuscript ID, with a composite primary key ensuring each combination is unique. The Review_Record table records the review process, linking an editor to a manuscript, review time, review part, and editor ID, with a composite primary key ensuring each combination is unique. The Publication_Record table tracks the publication process, linking a manuscript to its publication time, publisher ID, and publisher, with a composite primary key ensuring each combination is unique. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, such as the Manuscript ID linking manuscripts to writing and review records, and the Editor ID and Publisher ID linking editors and publishers to their respective records.
