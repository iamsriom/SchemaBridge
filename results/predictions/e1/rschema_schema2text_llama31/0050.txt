The rschema_0050 database is designed to manage movie licensing information. It consists of three tables: Movie, Copyright_Holder, and License_Movie. The Movie table stores details about individual movies, including a unique identifier, name, type, and release date. The Copyright_Holder table contains information about the copyright holders, such as their email, a unique identifier, name, and phone number. The License_Movie table represents the licensing agreements between copyright holders and movies, with each license having a start and end time, a type, and referencing the holder and movie involved. The License_Movie table has a composite primary key consisting of the Movie_ID and Holder_ID, ensuring that each movie can only be licensed to one holder at a time. The Holder_ID in License_Movie is not nullable, indicating that a license must be associated with a copyright holder. The Holder_ID and Movie_ID in License_Movie are foreign keys referencing the Holder_ID and Movie_ID in the Copyright_Holder and Movie tables, respectively, with the update and delete rules set to RESTRICT, meaning that any attempt to update or delete a holder or movie that is referenced by a license will be prevented.
