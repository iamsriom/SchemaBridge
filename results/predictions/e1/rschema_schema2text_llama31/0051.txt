The rschema_0051 database is designed to manage information about reservoirs and rivers, the personnel responsible for monitoring and maintaining them, and the data collected during these activities. The Reservoir_and_River table stores details about each location, including its name, type, and a unique identifier known as the Resource_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Monitoring_Personnel and Maintenance_Personnel tables contain information about the individuals involved in monitoring and maintenance, respectively, with each table having a unique identifier, name, phone number, and position. The Resource_Monitoring table links monitoring personnel to specific locations and times, storing data on pollution levels, water quality indicators, and water volume, with the Resource_ID and Monitoring_Personnel_ID forming a composite primary key along with the Monitoring_Time. The Resource_Monitoring table also references the Reservoir_and_River table through the Resource_ID, ensuring that only valid locations are associated with monitoring data. Similarly, the Resource_Maintenance table links maintenance personnel to specific locations and times, storing details on maintenance content, cost, and time, with the Resource_ID and Maintenance_Personnel_ID forming a composite primary key along with the Maintenance_Time, and referencing the Reservoir_and_River table through the Resource_ID.
