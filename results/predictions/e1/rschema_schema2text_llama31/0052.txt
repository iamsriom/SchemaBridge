The rschema_0052 database is designed to manage information about land usage and projects. The Land table stores details about individual land parcels, including their location and total area. Each land parcel is uniquely identified by a Land_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Project table contains information about various projects, such as their location, name, purpose, and the person responsible for them. The Land_Change table tracks changes made to land parcels over time, including the reason for the change, the cost involved, and the date and time of the change. This table is linked to the Land table through the Land_ID, ensuring that each change is associated with a specific land parcel. The Land_Usage_Status table provides a record of how land is being used, including the current status, the area being used, and the project it's associated with. This table is also linked to both the Land and Project tables through their respective IDs, and it has a composite primary key consisting of the Land_ID, Project_ID, and Record_Time, ensuring that each record is unique. Additionally, the Land_Usage_Status table has a unique constraint on the combination of Land_ID and Project_ID, preventing duplicate records for the same land parcel and project.
