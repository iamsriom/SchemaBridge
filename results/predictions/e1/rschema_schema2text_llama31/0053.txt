The rschema_0053 database is used to manage information about processing pools, staff, and maintenance activities. The database consists of four tables: processing_pool, staff, processing_pool_record, and processing_pool_maintenance. The processing_pool table stores information about each processing pool, including its unique identifier, name, location, and the staff member responsible for it. The staff table contains details about individual staff members, such as their name, phone number, email, responsibilities, and unique staff ID. The processing_pool_record table tracks the recording of processing pool information, including the pool's ID, the staff member who recorded it, the time of recording, the pool's processing capacity, and its efficiency. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the processing pool ID, recording time, and recording staff ID. The processing_pool_maintenance table stores maintenance records for each processing pool, including the pool's ID, the staff member who performed the maintenance, the time of maintenance, the maintenance content, and the cost. This table also has a composite primary key consisting of the processing pool ID, maintenance staff ID, and maintenance time. The responsible_person_id in the processing_pool table references the staff table, meaning that the staff member ID in the processing_pool table must match a staff ID in the staff table, and when a staff member is deleted, all processing pools assigned to them are automatically deleted. Similarly, the recording_staff_id and maintenance_staff_id in the processing_pool_record and processing_pool_maintenance tables also reference the staff table, ensuring that only valid staff IDs are used.
