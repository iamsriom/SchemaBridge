The rschema_0057 database is designed to manage information about equipment, the people responsible for maintaining it, and the maintenance records associated with each piece of equipment. The Equipment table stores details about each item of equipment, including its unique identifier, the date and location it was installed, its model and name. The Maintainer table contains information about the individuals or teams responsible for maintaining the equipment, with each maintainer having a unique identifier, contact information, department, and name. The Maintenance_Record table links equipment and maintainers together, recording the maintenance activities performed on each piece of equipment, including the date of the maintenance, the content of the maintenance, and the identifier of the equipment and the maintainer involved. The Maintenance_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the Equipment_ID and Maintainer_ID, ensuring that each maintenance record is unique to a specific piece of equipment and maintainer. The Equipment_ID and Maintainer_ID columns in the Maintenance_Record table are foreign keys that reference the ID columns in the Equipment and Maintainer tables, respectively, establishing relationships between the tables and enforcing referential integrity. The Equipment_ID column in the Maintenance_Record table is not nullable, indicating that every maintenance record must be associated with a specific piece of equipment, while the Maintainer_ID column is also not nullable, ensuring that every maintenance record is linked to a specific maintainer.
