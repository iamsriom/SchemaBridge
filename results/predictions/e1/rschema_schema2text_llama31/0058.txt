The rschema_0058 database is designed to manage and track equipment inspections. The Equipment table stores information about the equipment itself, including its model, name, installation date, and location. Each piece of equipment is assigned a unique identifier, the Equipment_ID, which serves as the primary key, ensuring that each record is distinct. The Inspection_Personnel table contains details about the individuals responsible for conducting inspections, including their name, department, and a unique identifier, Inspection_Personnel_ID, which is the primary key. The Inspection_Record table stores the results of equipment inspections, including the equipment being inspected, the inspection content, date, and the personnel conducting the inspection. The Inspection_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the Equipment_ID, Inspection_Personnel_ID, and Inspection_Date, ensuring that each inspection is uniquely identified. The Equipment_ID in the Inspection_Record table references the Equipment table, linking the inspection to the specific equipment being inspected, while the Inspection_Personnel_ID references the Inspection_Personnel table, linking the inspection to the personnel conducting it. The Equipment_ID and Inspection_Personnel_ID columns in the Inspection_Record table are not nullable, indicating that every inspection must be associated with a piece of equipment and a personnel member.
