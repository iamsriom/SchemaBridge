The rschema_0059 database is designed to manage information about handlers and their interactions with faults. The Handler table stores details about each handler, including their contact information, department, name, and a unique identifier, Handler_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Fault_Handling table, on the other hand, tracks the handling of faults, with columns for the affected user's ID, a description of the fault, a unique identifier, Fault_ID, which is the primary key, the date the fault occurred, and the date it was resolved. The Handler_ID in the Fault_Handling table is a foreign key referencing the Handler_ID in the Handler table, establishing a relationship between the two tables, and it is not allowed to be null, indicating that every fault must be handled by a specific handler.
