The rschema_0060 database is designed to manage information about research projects, researchers, research results, and fund usage. The project table stores details about each project, including its unique identifier, name, start and end dates, leader, budget, and a primary key to uniquely identify each project. The researcher table contains information about individual researchers, such as their employee number, name, date of birth, gender, phone number, position, and the project they are associated with, which is linked to the project table through a foreign key that enforces a cascade delete and update. The research_result table stores details about the results of research, including a unique identifier, name, publication date, and the researcher who conducted the research, which is linked to the researcher table through a foreign key that also enforces a cascade delete and update. The fund_usage table tracks the usage of funds for each project, including a unique identifier, project identifier, purpose, amount, and usage date, and is linked to the project table through a foreign key that enforces a cascade delete and update.
