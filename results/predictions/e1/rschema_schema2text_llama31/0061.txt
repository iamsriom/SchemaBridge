The rschema_0061 database is used to store information about patents, their inventors, and the application records for each patent. The patent table contains details about each patent, including its unique patent number, name, application date, and status, with all fields being required and having a maximum length of 255 characters. The inventor table holds information about the individuals behind the patents, with each inventor having a unique inventor number, name, date of birth, gender, phone number, and position, all of which are also required and have a maximum length of 255 characters. The patent application record table serves as a bridge between patents and inventors, linking each patent to its corresponding inventor and application date, with the combination of inventor number, application date, and patent number forming the primary key. The patent application record table also references the inventor and patent tables, with the inventor number referencing the inventor table and the patent number referencing the patent table, both with a cascade delete and update rule, meaning that if an inventor or patent is deleted or updated, all corresponding patent application records will be automatically deleted or updated as well.
