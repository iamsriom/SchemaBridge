The rschema_0062 database is designed to manage documents and their modifications, with three main tables: Document, Author, and Modification_Record. The Document table stores information about each document, including its creation date, a unique identifier, title, and version number. The Author table contains details about the individuals who create and modify documents, such as their date of birth, gender, name, phone number, and position. The Modification_Record table tracks changes made to documents over time, including the document's ID, the modification content, the date of the modification, the ID of the author who made the modification, and the version number of the document at the time of the modification. The Modification_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the document ID, modification date, and modifying author ID, ensuring that each modification is uniquely identified. The Document table is referenced by the Modification_Record table through a foreign key constraint, which links each modification to the corresponding document. The Author table is also referenced by the Modification_Record table through a foreign key constraint, which links each modification to the author who made it. The Modification_Date column in the Modification_Record table is not nullable, and the Document_ID and Modifying_Author_ID columns are also not nullable, indicating that every modification must be associated with a document and an author.
