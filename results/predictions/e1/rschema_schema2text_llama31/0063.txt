The rschema_0063 database is designed to track scientific and technological achievements and their transformations into products. The Scientific_and_Technological_Achievement table stores information about each achievement, including a unique Achievement Number, its name, the date it was researched and developed, and the technical field it belongs to. The Product table contains details about each product, such as its barcode, model, name, price, and a unique Product Number, which serves as the primary key. The Achievement_Transformation table links achievements to products, recording the date of transformation, and it has a composite primary key consisting of the Achievement Number and Product Number. The Achievement Number in this table references the Achievement Number in the Scientific_and_Technological_Achievement table, and the Product Number references the Product Number in the Product table, establishing a relationship between the two tables. The Achievement_Transformation table also includes a foreign key constraint that ensures data integrity by preventing the deletion of an achievement or product if it has associated transformations.
