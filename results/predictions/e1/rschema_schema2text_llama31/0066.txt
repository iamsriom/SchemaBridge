The rschema_0066 database is designed to manage information about communities, their owners, and repair applications. The community table stores details about each community, including its unique identifier, name, address, and manager, with all fields being required. The owner table contains information about individual owners, such as their name, phone number, house number, and the community they belong to, with the community ID referencing the community table and allowing for cascading updates and deletions. The house table holds details about each house, including its unique identifier and location, with all fields being mandatory. The repair application record table tracks repair applications made by owners, including the application time, repair content, and cost, with the owner ID and house ID referencing the owner and house tables, respectively, and also allowing for cascading updates and deletions. The primary keys in the community and house tables uniquely identify each community and house, while the primary key in the repair application record table is a composite key consisting of the owner ID and house ID, ensuring that each repair application is tied to a specific owner and house.
