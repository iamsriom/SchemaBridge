The rschema_0067 database is designed to store information about customers and their rental records. The Customer table contains details about each customer, including their credit score, a unique customer ID, name, and phone number. The Customer_ID is the primary key, meaning it uniquely identifies each customer and cannot be null. The Rental_record table stores information about each rental made by a customer, including the customer's ID, a unique record ID, the rental fee, the time the item was rented and returned, and a foreign key referencing the Customer table to link each rental to its corresponding customer. The Rental_record table also has a primary key to uniquely identify each rental record. The Credit_change_record table tracks changes to a customer's credit score over time, including the reason for the change, the time of the change, the customer's ID, the new and old credit values, and a unique record ID. This table also has a primary key and a foreign key referencing the Customer table to link each credit change to its corresponding customer. The Customer_ID column in both the Rental_record and Credit_change_record tables is a non-nullable foreign key that references the Customer_ID column in the Customer table, ensuring that each rental and credit change is linked to an existing customer.
