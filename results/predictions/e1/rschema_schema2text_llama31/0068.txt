The rschema_0068 database is designed to manage customer information and services provided to them. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including a unique identifier (Customer_ID), their email address, name, and phone number. The Service table holds information about the services offered to customers, such as a description, fee, and the date of service, with a unique identifier (Service_ID) and a foreign key referencing the Customer_ID in the Customer table to establish a link between the two. The Feedback table captures customer feedback on the services they have received, including comments and a rating, and is linked to both the Customer and Service tables through foreign keys referencing their respective IDs, with a composite primary key combining Customer_ID and Service_ID to ensure each feedback entry is unique to a specific customer and service.
