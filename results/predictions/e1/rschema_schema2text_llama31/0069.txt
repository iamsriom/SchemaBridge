The rschema_0069 database is designed to manage customer information and rental records for items. The customer table stores essential details about each customer, including their unique customer ID, name, phone number, age, gender, address, and email. The rental_item table contains information about the items available for rent, such as the item's name, category, single rental fee, and total and remaining inventory. The rental_record table tracks the rental history of each item, including the customer ID, item ID, rental start and end times, number of items rented, and rental payment fee. The primary key for the rental_record table is a composite key consisting of the customer ID, item ID, and rental start time, ensuring that each rental record is unique. The customer ID in the rental_record table references the customer ID in the customer table, and the item ID references the item ID in the rental_item table, with both relationships enforcing a cascade delete and update rule, meaning that if a customer or item is deleted or updated, all related rental records will be automatically updated or deleted accordingly.
