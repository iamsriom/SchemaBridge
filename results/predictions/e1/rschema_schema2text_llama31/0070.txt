The rschema_0070 database is designed to manage information about various aspects of a mining operation. At its core, the database contains information about different types of ore, which are stored in the Ore table, characterized by their name and unique ID. The Working_Employees table holds details about the employees involved in the operation, including their age, email, employee ID, gender, name, and phone number. Each mine in the operation is represented by the Mine table, which includes its location, name, and the ID of the employee in charge, which is a foreign key referencing the Employee_ID in the Working_Employees table. Similarly, the Processing_Plant table stores information about the processing plants, including their location, name, and the ID of the employee in charge, which is also a foreign key referencing the Employee_ID in the Working_Employees table. The Output_Ore table tracks the output of ore from each mine, with each entry including the mine ID, ore ID, output quantity, and output time, and the primary key is a composite of these three fields, referencing the Mine_ID and Ore_ID in the Mine and Ore tables, respectively. The Processing_Plant_Processing_Ore table records the processing of ore at each plant, with each entry including the ore ID, processing cost, processing date, processing plant ID, and processing quantity, and the primary key is a composite of these four fields, referencing the Ore_ID and Processing_Plant_ID in the Ore and Processing_Plant tables, respectively.
