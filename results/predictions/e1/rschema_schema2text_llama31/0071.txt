The rschema_0071 database is designed to manage information about mines and their safety inspections. The mine table stores details about each mine, including its unique number, name, location, and the person in charge, which is linked to the safety officer table through a foreign key. The safety officer table contains information about each safety officer, such as their number, name, gender, telephone number, position, and the mine they are in charge of, with the mine number referencing the mine table. The safety inspection record table stores records of safety inspections, including the inspection date, content, results, and the inspector, with the mine number referencing the mine table and forming a composite primary key with the inspection date.
