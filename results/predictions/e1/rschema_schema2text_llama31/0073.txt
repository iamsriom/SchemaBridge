The rschema_0073 database is designed to manage information about insurance agents, customers, policies, payments, and claims. At its core, the database contains a table for Insurance Agents, which stores details such as the agent's name, contact phone number, and hire date, with each agent uniquely identified by their Agent ID. The Customer table holds information about policyholders, including their name, contact phone number, and ID card number, with each customer identified by their Customer ID. The Payment Record table tracks payments made by customers, with each payment recorded by its payment method, amount, and time, and linked to a specific policy through the Policy ID. However, there is an issue with the Payment Record table, as it references itself, which is likely an error and should be corrected. The Insurance Policy table stores details about the policies themselves, including the agent who sold the policy, the customer who purchased it, the insurance term, type, and premium, with each policy uniquely identified by its Policy ID. The Claim Record table tracks claims made by customers, with each claim recorded by its amount, time, and linked to a specific policy through the Policy ID. The database enforces several constraints to maintain data integrity, including primary keys to uniquely identify each record, composite primary keys to ensure that payments and claims are tied to specific policies, and foreign keys to link records between tables, such as the agent who sold the policy and the customer who purchased it, with update and delete rules set to restrict changes to these relationships.
