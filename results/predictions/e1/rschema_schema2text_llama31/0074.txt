The rschema_0074 database is designed to manage information related to insurance policies and their associated records. The Insurance_Agent table stores details about insurance agents, including their Agent ID, contact phone number, hire date, and name. The Customer table contains information about policyholders, with fields for their contact phone number, customer ID, ID card number, and name. The Insurance_Policy table links agents and customers to their respective policies, storing policy details such as the insurance term, type, insured amount, premium, and a unique policy ID. The Agent ID and Customer ID in this table reference the corresponding IDs in the Insurance_Agent and Customer tables, ensuring that each policy is associated with a valid agent and customer. The Medical_Record table tracks medical visits for customers, storing the customer ID, visit cost, and visit time, with the customer ID and visit time forming a composite primary key to uniquely identify each record. The Payment_Record table records payments made towards policies, including the payment amount, date, method, and a unique policy ID that references the Insurance_Policy table. The Claim_Record table stores information about claims made on policies, with fields for the claim amount, date, and a unique policy ID that references the Payment_Record table, which in turn references the Insurance_Policy table, allowing for a chain of relationships between policies, payments, and claims.
