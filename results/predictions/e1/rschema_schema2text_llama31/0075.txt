The rschema_0075 database is designed to store information about stock analysis and historical stock prices. The Analyst table contains details about the individuals who perform the analysis, including their unique identifier, contact number, date of hire, and name. The Stock table holds information about the stocks being analyzed, with each stock having a unique code and name. The Analysis_Record table stores the results of the analysis, including the reasoning behind the analysis, the outcome, the analyst who performed it, the date of analysis, and the stock code being analyzed. The primary key for this table is a composite of the analyst's ID, the stock code, and the date of analysis, ensuring that each analysis is unique. The Analyst_ID and Stock_Code columns in this table reference the Analyst and Stock tables, respectively, enforcing referential integrity by linking each analysis to the relevant analyst and stock. The Stock_Historical_Price table stores historical data about the stock prices, including the closing price, date, highest price, lowest price, opening price, and trading volume for each stock. The primary key for this table is a composite of the stock code and date, ensuring that each historical price record is unique. The Stock_Code column in this table references the Stock table, linking each historical price record to the relevant stock.
