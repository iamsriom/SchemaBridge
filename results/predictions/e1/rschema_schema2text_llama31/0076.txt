The rschema_0076 database is designed to manage information about beer manufacturers, bars, drinkers, beers, and sales records. The Beer_Manufacturer table stores details about the countries and names of manufacturers, with each manufacturer assigned a unique identifier, the Manufacturer_Number. The Bar table contains information about individual bars, including their addresses, names, phone numbers, and licenses, with each bar also having a unique identifier, the Bar_Number. The Drinker table holds data about individual drinkers, such as their addresses, ages, genders, names, and phone numbers, with each drinker assigned a unique identifier, the Drinker_Number. The Beer table stores information about different types of beers, including their names, prices, and the manufacturer numbers they belong to, with each beer also having a unique identifier, the Beer_Number, and a foreign key referencing the Manufacturer_Number in the Beer_Manufacturer table to establish the relationship between the two. The Sales_Record table tracks sales data for each beer at each bar, including the bar number, beer number, price, and date of sale, with a composite primary key consisting of the Bar_Number and Beer_Number to uniquely identify each sale, and foreign keys referencing the Bar_Number and Beer_Number in the Bar and Beer tables, respectively, to establish relationships between the sales data and the corresponding bars and beers.
