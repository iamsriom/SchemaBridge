The rschema_0078 database is designed to store information about authors and their articles. The database contains four tables: Author, Article, Author_creates_article, and Author_modifies_article. The Author table stores details about each author, including their ID, email, gender, introduction, name, and phone number. The Article table stores information about each article, including its ID, collection volume, comment volume, content, like volume, reading volume, title, creation time, last modification and save author ID, last modification and save time, and total number of words in the article. The Last_modification_and_save_author_ID column in the Article table references the Author table, establishing a relationship between authors and the articles they last modified and saved. The Author_creates_article table stores information about which authors are responsible for creating articles, including the article ID, author ID, author order, author responsible for part, and author role, with the primary key being a composite of the article ID and author ID. The Author_modifies_article table stores information about which authors have modified articles, including the article ID, author ID, modify notes, modify save time, and modify start time, with the primary key being a composite of the article ID, author ID, and modify save time. The Author_creates_article and Author_modifies_article tables both reference the Article and Author tables, establishing relationships between authors and the articles they create and modify.
