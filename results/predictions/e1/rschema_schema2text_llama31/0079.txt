The rschema_0079 database is designed to track and manage stock investments, orders, and their relationships. The stock table stores information about individual stocks, including their unique stock code, type, market, owner, total investment amount, number of holding shares, last transaction time, and transaction status. Each stock is uniquely identified by its stock code, which serves as the primary key. The order table contains details about each transaction, such as the order ID, type, creation time, transaction date, amount, quantity, service fee, and status. Orders are also uniquely identified by their order ID, which is the primary key. The stock_order table bridges the relationship between stocks and orders, linking each order to the specific stock it involves and vice versa. This is achieved through a composite primary key consisting of the order ID and stock code, as well as two foreign key constraints referencing the stock and order tables, respectively. When an order is deleted, all related stock orders are automatically deleted, and when a stock is deleted, all related stock orders are also deleted, ensuring data consistency and integrity throughout the database.
