The rschema_0080 database is designed to manage information about customers, repair workers, parts, and repair tasks. At its core, the database contains six tables: Customer, Repair_Worker, Part, Repair_Task, Repair_Worker_Task, and Part_Usage. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their ID, name, and phone number, with the Customer_ID serving as the primary key. The Repair_Worker table holds information about repair workers, such as their name and address, with the Repair_Worker_ID being the primary key. The Part table contains details about the parts used in repairs, including their description, inventory quantity, serial number, price, and supplier, with the Part_Serial_Number serving as the primary key. The Repair_Task table stores information about each repair task, including the customer ID, task ID, completion and start dates, status, and vehicle model, with the Customer_ID referencing the Customer table and the Repair_Task_ID serving as the primary key. The Repair_Worker_Task table links repair workers to specific tasks, with the Repair_Task_ID and Repair_Worker_ID serving as composite primary keys and referencing the Repair_Task and Repair_Worker tables, respectively. The Part_Usage table tracks the usage of specific parts in each repair task, with the Part_Serial_Number and Repair_Task_ID serving as composite primary keys and referencing the Part and Repair_Task tables, respectively.
