The rschema_0082 database is designed to store information about political parties, candidates, and voters. The political_party table contains details about each party, including a unique party ID, party name, and a brief description. The candidate table stores information about individual candidates, including a unique candidate ID, name, the ID of the party they belong to, and any industry connections they may have. The candidate table has a foreign key referencing the party ID in the political_party table, meaning that a candidate must be associated with an existing party, and if that party is deleted, all associated candidates will also be deleted. The voter table stores information about individual voters, including a unique voter ID, name, email, the time they were created, the ID of the candidate they voted for, and the time of their vote. The voter table also has a foreign key referencing the candidate ID in the candidate table, meaning that a voter must have voted for an existing candidate, and if that candidate is deleted, all associated voters will also be deleted.
