The rschema_0084 database is used to track and manage land usage records for various projects. The database consists of three main tables: Land, Project, and Land_Usage_Record. The Land table stores information about individual parcels of land, including their location and total area. Each land parcel is uniquely identified by a Land_ID, which is a unique integer that serves as the primary key. The Project table contains details about each project, such as the person in charge, project location, name, and purpose, with a unique Project_ID serving as the primary key. The Land_Usage_Record table tracks the usage history of each land parcel, including the current status, the land parcel's ID, the project ID, the date and time of recording, the personnel who recorded the usage, the duration of usage, and the area of land used. The Land_Usage_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the Land_ID, Project_ID, and Record_Time, ensuring that each usage record is unique. The Land_ID and Project_ID columns in the Land_Usage_Record table are foreign keys that reference the Land and Project tables, respectively, establishing relationships between land parcels and projects. The Land_ID column in the Land_Usage_Record table is not nullable, indicating that a land parcel must be associated with a record, while the Project_ID column is also not nullable, signifying that each record must be linked to a specific project.
