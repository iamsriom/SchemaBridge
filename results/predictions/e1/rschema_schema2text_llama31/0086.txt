The rschema_0086 database is designed to manage procurement and supplier information. It consists of seven tables: Material, Region, Country, Procurement_Application, Supplier, Company, and Supplier_Production_Material. The Material table stores details about different materials, including a unique identifier, description, name, type, and unit. Each material is associated with a procurement application in the Procurement_Application table, which also tracks the quantity applied for, the time of application, and any remarks. The Supplier table holds information about suppliers, such as their address, name, phone number, fax, tax ID, and region, with the country and region being linked to their respective tables. Companies are stored in the Company table, which includes details like activation time, address, creation time, ERP system identifier, email, name, password, phone number, region, security question and answer, status, tax ID, update time, username, and VAT registration number, with the country and region also linked to their respective tables. The Supplier_Production_Material table tracks the production of materials by suppliers, including the material ID, production quantity, production time, remarks, and supplier ID, with the material and supplier IDs referencing their respective tables. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys, such as Material_ID in the Material table and Procurement_Application_ID in the Procurement_Application table, and composite primary keys like the combination of Material_ID, Supplier_ID, and Production_Time in the Supplier_Production_Material table. Unique constraints are also enforced, such as the combination of Material_ID, Supplier_ID, and Production_Time in the Supplier_Production_Material table. Foreign keys like Material_ID in the Procurement_Application table and Supplier_ID in the Supplier_Production_Material table ensure that related data is consistent across tables, with update and delete rules like CASCADE and RESTRICT applied to maintain data integrity.
