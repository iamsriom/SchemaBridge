The rschema_0091 database is designed to manage employee information and training records within an organization. The database contains five tables: department, training, employee, attendance, and employee_training. The department table stores information about each department, including a unique department ID, department name, person in charge, and phone number. The training table holds details about training sessions, such as the training content, ID, name, and time. The employee table stores information about each employee, including their date of birth, department ID, ID, gender, name, position, and salary, with a foreign key referencing the department ID to establish the relationship between employees and their departments. The attendance table tracks employee attendance, recording the attendance date, ID, check-in and check-out times, and whether the employee was late or departed early, with a foreign key referencing the employee ID to link attendance records to specific employees. The employee_training table maintains a record of which employees have undergone which training sessions, with a composite primary key combining the employee ID and training ID, and foreign keys referencing both the employee ID and training ID to ensure data consistency.
