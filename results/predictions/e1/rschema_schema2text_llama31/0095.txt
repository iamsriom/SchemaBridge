The rschema_0095 database is designed to manage customer interactions and employee activities within a company. The Customer table stores information about each customer, including their address, company name, email, gender, name, phone number, and a unique customer ID. The Employee table contains details about each employee, such as their department, email, gender, level, name, phone number, and position. The Sales_Opportunity table tracks sales opportunities, including the actual transaction period, creation time, estimated amount, estimated transaction date, opportunity name, stage, and status. It links customers and employees through their IDs, and the primary key is a composite of the customer ID, employee ID, and creation time. The Communication_Record table records communication between customers and employees, including the communication content, method, result, stage, and creation time, with a composite primary key of customer ID, employee ID, and creation time. The Service_Request table logs service requests from customers, including the creation time, request description, type, solution, and status, linked to customers and employees through their IDs, with a composite primary key of customer ID, employee ID, and creation time.
