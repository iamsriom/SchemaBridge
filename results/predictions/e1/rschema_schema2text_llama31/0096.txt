The rschema_0096 database is designed to manage information about customers, hairstylists, and the services they provide. The Customer table stores details about each individual customer, including a unique identifier, their hair color, and their name, with the Customer ID serving as the primary key. The Hairstylist table contains information about each hairstylist, such as their area of expertise and name, with the Hairstylist ID being the primary key. The Service table captures the specifics of each service provided, including the appointment time, the customer and hairstylist involved, the service content, and the service time, with a composite primary key consisting of the Customer ID, Hairstylist ID, and appointment time to ensure each service is uniquely identified. The Customer ID and Hairstylist ID in the Service table are foreign keys referencing the Customer and Hairstylist tables, respectively, with the Customer ID being not null and the Hairstylist ID also being not null, indicating that a service must be associated with a customer and a hairstylist.
