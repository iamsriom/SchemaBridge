The rschema_0097 database is designed to store information about companies, categories, and dealers. The company table contains a list of companies, each identified by a unique company ID and name, which cannot be left blank. The category table stores categories, each with a unique category ID and name, which also cannot be left blank, and is associated with a specific company, which is also required. The relationship between a category and its company is such that if the company is deleted, all categories associated with it will also be deleted. The dealer table lists dealers, each identified by a unique dealer ID and name, which cannot be left blank, and is also associated with a specific company, which is required. Similar to the category table, if a company is deleted, all dealers associated with it will also be deleted. The company ID is used as a foreign key in both the category and dealer tables to establish these relationships, ensuring that only valid company IDs can be referenced.
