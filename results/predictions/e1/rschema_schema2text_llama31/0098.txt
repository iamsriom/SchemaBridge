The rschema_0098 database is designed to manage information about users, employees, technical solutions, and their evaluations and feedback. At the core of this database is the User table, which stores details about individual users, including their email, gender, name, phone number, and a unique User ID. The Employee table contains similar information about employees, such as their contact number, email, name, and gender, with a unique Employee ID. The Technical_solution table stores details about the technical solutions implemented, including the implementation start and end dates, a description, name, and status, with a unique Technical_solution ID. The Technical_solution_feedback table captures feedback from users about the technical solutions, including the feedback content, date, satisfaction level, and any user suggestions, with a composite primary key consisting of the User ID, Technical_solution ID, and feedback date. This table also includes foreign keys referencing the User and Technical_solution tables to establish relationships between users and the solutions they provide feedback on. The Technical_solution_evaluation table stores evaluations of the technical solutions by employees, including the evaluation content, date, indicators, and results, with a composite primary key consisting of the Technical_solution ID, Employee ID, and evaluation date. This table also includes foreign keys referencing the Employee and Technical_solution tables to establish relationships between employees and the solutions they evaluate.
