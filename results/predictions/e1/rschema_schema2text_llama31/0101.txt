The rschema_0101 database is designed to manage employee information and their interactions within a company. The database consists of five tables: employee, department, employee_department_transfer, employee_attendance, and performance_evaluation. The employee table stores information about each employee, including their unique employee ID, name, gender, date of birth, phone number, ID number, place of origin, age, department ID, position, level, and contract type. The department table contains details about each department, such as its ID, name, responsibilities, head ID, total number of people in the department, and location. The employee_department_transfer table tracks employee transfers between departments, including the employee ID, transfer-in department ID, transfer-out department ID, transfer time, and post-transfer position. The employee_attendance table records employee attendance, with columns for employee ID, punch-in time, and punch-in type. The performance_evaluation table stores performance evaluations, including employee ID, evaluation time, evaluation content, evaluation result, and evaluator ID. The primary keys in each table ensure data integrity by uniquely identifying each record, while foreign keys establish relationships between tables, such as the department head ID referencing the employee ID in the employee table, and the transfer-in and transfer-out department IDs referencing the department ID in the department table. The database uses the InnoDB engine and UTF-8mb3 character set, and all columns are defined as varchar(255), which means they can store strings of up to 255 characters.
