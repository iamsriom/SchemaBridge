The rschema_0103 database is designed to manage employee information and their associated departments and salary standards. The Department table stores details about each department, including a unique identifier, a department code, the department's name, and the person in charge. The Salary_standard table contains information about the different salary standards, with each standard having a unique identifier and a description along with its corresponding value. The Employee table holds data about each employee, including their date of birth, department they belong to, a unique employee ID, gender, hire date, name, position, and the salary standard they adhere to. The Department ID and salary standard ID in the Employee table are not allowed to be null, and they reference the Department and Salary_standard tables respectively, ensuring that each employee is assigned to a valid department and salary standard. The Employee_position_change table tracks changes in an employee's department and position over time, including the current department and position, the time they left the previous department, the employee's ID, the time they entered the new department, their original department and position, and the time they left the original department. The primary key in this table is a composite key consisting of the employee ID, department ID, and departure time, ensuring that each employee can only have one entry for each department they have left. The Department ID and employee ID in the Employee_position_change table are not allowed to be null and reference the Department and Employee tables respectively, maintaining referential integrity between the tables.
