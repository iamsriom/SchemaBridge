The rschema_0104 database is designed to manage email marketing campaigns and their associated subscribers. The User table stores information about the administrators of the system, with each user assigned a unique User_ID, which is the primary key. The Email_campaign table contains details about each email campaign, including its subject, content, scheduled sending time, and status. The Mailing_list table represents the email lists that users can create, with each list having a unique Mailing_list_ID and being associated with a specific user through the User_ID foreign key, which references the User table. The Subscriber table stores information about the individuals who are subscribed to these email lists, including their email address, name, subscription status, and the User_ID of the user who created their subscription. The Campaign_mailing_list_association table links email campaigns to the mailing lists they are associated with, using both the Campaign_ID and Mailing_list_ID as a composite primary key. Finally, the Message table tracks the messages sent to subscribers, with each message having a unique combination of Subscriber_ID, Campaign_ID, and Creation_time as its primary key, and referencing the Email_campaign and Subscriber tables through foreign keys.
