The rschema_0108 database is designed to store information about departments and employees within an organization. The Department table contains details about each department, including a unique identifier, Department_ID, which is the primary key and cannot be null, along with the department's name and responsibilities. The Employee table holds information about each employee, such as their age, base salary, email address, gender, name, phone number, position, and social security number, with Employee_ID serving as the primary key and Department_ID referencing the Department table to establish the department each employee belongs to. The Department_ID in the Employee table is not allowed to be null, indicating that every employee must be assigned to a department.
