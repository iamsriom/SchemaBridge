The rschema_0111 database is designed to manage user information, company details, employee data, and credit records. At its core, the User table stores information about registered users, including their email, password, phone number, registration and update times, role, and unique username. Each user is assigned a unique identifier, the User_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Company table contains details about various companies, such as their names, addresses, creation times, and unique identifiers, Company_ID. The Employee table links employees to their affiliated companies, with each employee having a unique identifier, Employee_ID, and a position within the company. The Employee table is linked to the Company table through the Affiliated_Company_ID foreign key, which references the Company_ID in the Company table. The Credit_Record table stores information about credit applications, including the application date, creation and deletion times, credit number, status, type, monthly factor, number of applications, and user and employee identifiers. The Credit_Record table is linked to both the User and Employee tables through foreign keys, referencing their respective User_ID and Employee_ID, ensuring that each credit record is associated with a valid user and employee.
