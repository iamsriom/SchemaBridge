The rschema_0112 database is used to manage the operations of a company, encompassing various aspects of its business, including employee information, product details, customer data, sales performance, and financial transactions. The Employee table stores information about each employee, including their base salary, commission rate, email, employee ID, employee number, hire date, name, office phone, position, and supervisor ID, which references the Employee ID of their supervisor. The Product table contains details about each product, such as its brand, cost price, description, inventory quantity, model, name, product ID, specification, suggested retail price, and supplier. The Order table tracks all orders, including the actual shipping date, notes, order ID, order date, order status, required delivery date, and special instructions. The Office_location table stores information about each office location, including the city, contact phone, country, fax, manager employee ID, which references the Employee ID of the manager, and office location ID. The Customer table contains customer information, including their city, contact person name, contact person phone, contact person position, country, credit limit, customer ID, customer company name, dedicated sales representative employee ID, which references the Employee ID of the sales representative, postal code, state or province, and street address. The Sales_performance_record table tracks the sales performance of each employee, including the record ID, sales person ID, total sales, and year. The Order_detail table links orders to the products they contain, with each entry including the line number, order ID, product ID, quantity, and unit price. The Payment_record table stores payment information, including the check clearing date, check number, customer accounts receivable, invoice amount, paying customer ID, which references the Customer ID of the customer making the payment, payment date, and record ID.
