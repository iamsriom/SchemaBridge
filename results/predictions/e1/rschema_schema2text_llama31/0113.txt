The rschema_0113 database is designed to manage a retail system, storing information about products, customers, orders, and order details. The product table contains a list of products, each identified by a unique product ID, with details such as the product name, price, inventory quantity, and supplier. The customer table holds customer information, including a unique customer ID, name, address, and contact information. The order table tracks customer transactions, with each order identified by a unique order ID, including the customer who placed the order, the transaction status, the date of the order, and the time it was last updated. The order detail table links orders to the products they contain, with each entry identified by a unique combination of order ID and product ID, including the quantity of the product purchased. The order detail table also references the product table to ensure that only valid products can be included in an order, and the order table to ensure that only valid orders can have details recorded. The database uses InnoDB as its storage engine and UTF-8mb3 character encoding, and it enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, with the order detail table using a composite primary key to uniquely identify each entry, and the order table using a foreign key to link orders to their corresponding customers, with the option to cascade delete or update if the referenced customer is deleted or updated.
