The rschema_0116 database is designed to manage customer information and airline reviews. The Customers_Table stores details about each customer, including a unique CustomerID, their name, and phone number. The Airlines_Table contains information about different airlines, such as their AirlineID, name, route, and the time of their flights. The CustomerReviews_Table links customers with airlines, allowing them to leave reviews with a ReviewID, content, grade, and referencing the AirlineID and CustomerID from the other tables. The CustomerBankAccounts_Table stores financial information about customers, including their AccountID, account number, balance, and the CustomerID that the account belongs to. The CustomerID in the CustomerBankAccounts_Table references the CustomerID in the Customers_Table, establishing a relationship between a customer and their bank account. The CustomerReviews_Table also references the AirlineID from the Airlines_Table, linking a review to the airline it is about. The CustomerID in the CustomerReviews_Table references the CustomerID in the Customers_Table, showing which customer left the review. The primary keys in each table uniquely identify each record, with the CustomerID in the Customers_Table and ReviewID in the CustomerReviews_Table being the primary keys, and the AirlineID in the Airlines_Table and AccountID in the CustomerBankAccounts_Table being primary keys as well. The CustomerID in the CustomerBankAccounts_Table is nullable, as not all customers may have a bank account, and the same goes for the AccountNumber, Balance, and AccountID. The CustomerReviews_Table has a composite primary key of ReviewID, AirlineID, and CustomerID, ensuring that each review is unique and belongs to a specific airline and customer.
