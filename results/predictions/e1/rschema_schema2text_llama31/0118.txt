The rschema_0118 database is designed to manage employee information and attendance records for a company. The database contains six tables: department, employee_attendance, employee, employee_transfer_department, employee_leave, and employee_overtime. The department table stores information about each department within the company, including its ID, location, name, responsibilities, the ID of the person in charge, and the total number of employees. The employee_attendance table tracks the attendance of employees, recording the employee's ID, the time they clocked in or out, and the type of punch. The employee table contains detailed information about each employee, including their age, contact information, date of birth, department ID, ID, gender, name, position, salary, and time of employment. The department ID in the employee table is a foreign key referencing the department ID in the department table, establishing a relationship between employees and their respective departments. The employee_transfer_department table records instances where an employee has transferred between departments, including the employee's ID, the transfer time, the ID of the department they transferred into, and the ID of the department they transferred out of. The employee_leave table tracks instances of employee leave, recording the employee's ID, the leave time, and the type of leave. The employee_overtime table tracks instances of employee overtime, recording the employee's ID, the overtime time, and the type of overtime. The primary keys in these tables ensure that each record is unique, and the foreign keys establish relationships between tables to maintain data consistency.
