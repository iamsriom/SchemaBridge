The rschema_0122 database is designed to manage conversations, customer interactions, and organization information. At its core, the database consists of six tables: conversation, message, inquiry, organization, security_base_record, and customer. The conversation table stores unique conversation IDs, customer IDs, creation and last update times, and a base ID that references the security_base_record table, which stores encrypted information and timestamps for each record. Each conversation is associated with a customer through the customer ID, and messages within a conversation are stored in the message table, which references the conversation ID. The inquiry table records customer inquiries, including the customer ID, inquiry category, content, status, creation, and end times, with a composite primary key of customer ID and creation time. The organization table contains information about organizations, including their IDs, names, phone numbers, avatars, locations, emails, descriptions, and homepage and image links. The customer table stores customer information, including IDs, names, contact details, and the organization they belong to, which is referenced by the organization ID. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that conversations are tied to customers and security base records, messages are tied to conversations, inquiries are tied to customers, and customers are tied to organizations.
