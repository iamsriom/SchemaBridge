The rschema_0123 database is a comprehensive system for managing a business's operations, encompassing customer, supplier, and product information, as well as staff and sales data. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact information, gender, and address, with a unique identifier for each customer. The Supplier table contains similar information for suppliers, including their name and address. The Product table holds details about the products sold, such as their name, description, and inventory quantity. The Staff table stores information about the business's employees, including their name, phone number, and position. The Purchase_from_Supplier table records transactions between the business and its suppliers, including the product purchased, quantity, unit price, and time of purchase, with foreign keys referencing the Supplier, Product, and Staff tables to establish relationships between these entities. The Sales_Product table tracks sales transactions between the business and its customers, including the product sold, quantity, unit price, and time of sale, with foreign keys referencing the Customer, Staff, and Product tables to establish relationships between these entities. The primary keys in both tables are composite, consisting of multiple columns to uniquely identify each transaction, and the database enforces referential integrity by restricting updates and deletions that would leave orphaned records or violate relationships between tables.
