The rschema_0126 database is designed to manage death records and the inspectors who verify them. At the core of this database is the Inspector table, which stores information about each inspector with a unique identifier, the Inspector_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Death_Record table holds details about each death record, including the Death_Record_ID, which is the primary key, the Inspector_ID of the inspector who verified the record, the date and time of the last modification, the name of the deceased, and a Unique_ID. The Inspector_ID in the Death_Record table is a foreign key referencing the Inspector_ID in the Inspector table, ensuring that only valid inspectors can be associated with a death record. The Confirmed_Death_Record table is used to track confirmed death records, with a composite primary key consisting of the Inspector_ID and Death_Record_ID, indicating that a specific inspector has confirmed a specific death record. The Death_Record_ID in the Confirmed_Death_Record table is a foreign key referencing the Death_Record_ID in the Death_Record table, and the Inspector_ID is also a foreign key referencing the Inspector_ID in the Inspector table, ensuring that only valid death records and inspectors are associated with confirmed records.
