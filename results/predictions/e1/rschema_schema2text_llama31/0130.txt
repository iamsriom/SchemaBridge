The rschema_0130 database is designed to manage information about customers, projects, and the relationships between them. It contains six tables: Customer, Project, Member, Corporate_Customer, Individual_Customer, and Project_Member_Relationship. The Customer table holds information about each customer, with a unique identifier (Customer_ID) and a type of customer. The Project table stores details about each project, including its end and start dates, whether it's a digital project, its status, value, service type, and a unique identifier (Project_ID). The Member table contains details about each team member, including their name, position, domain, date of hire, and date of departure. The Corporate_Customer and Individual_Customer tables store information about corporate and individual customers, respectively, with unique identifiers (Corporate_Tax_ID and Personal_Tax_ID) and a foreign key referencing the Customer table to establish their relationship with the Customer table. The Project_Member_Relationship table links team members to projects, with a composite primary key of Project_ID and Member_ID, and foreign keys referencing the Member and Project tables to ensure data consistency.
