The rschema_0133 database is designed to manage information about books and their borrowers. The Book_Category table stores details about different types of books, including a unique identifier for each type, a description of the category, and the name of the category. The Reader_Category table contains information about different types of readers, including a unique identifier for each type, a description of the type, and the name of the type. The Book table stores details about individual books, such as the author, a unique identifier for the book, the type of book, a description of the book's content, its price, the date of publication, the publisher, and the title. Each book must be assigned to a specific book type, which is stored in the Book_Type_ID column and references the Book_Category table. The Reader table stores information about individual readers, including their contact number, credit score, a description of the reader, a unique identifier for the reader, and the type of reader, which must be assigned from the Reader_Category table. The Borrowing_Information table tracks borrowing details, including the book identifier, the date the book was borrowed, any fine incurred, the reader identifier, the number of renewals, and the return date. Each borrowing is uniquely identified by a combination of the book identifier, reader identifier, and borrowing date. The Borrowing_Information table also references the Book and Reader tables through foreign keys, ensuring that each borrowing is associated with a valid book and reader.
