The rschema_0135 database is designed to manage information about products and their storage on shelves. It consists of three tables: 'shelf', 'product', and 'brand'. The 'shelf' table stores details about the physical locations where products are stored, including the unique shelf ID, name, location, and the person responsible for maintaining it. Each shelf is uniquely identified by its shelf ID, which cannot be null. The 'product' table contains information about the products themselves, such as the product ID, name, category, selling price, the shelf it belongs to, its specific location on that shelf, and the brand it belongs to. The product ID is the primary key, and it cannot be null. The 'product' table also references the 'brand' table, which stores information about the brands of products, including the unique brand ID and brand name. The brand ID is the primary key and cannot be null. The 'product' table has foreign keys referencing the 'shelf' and 'brand' tables, meaning that when a shelf or brand is deleted, all products belonging to it will also be deleted, and when a product is updated or deleted, the changes will be cascaded to the 'shelf' and 'brand' tables.
