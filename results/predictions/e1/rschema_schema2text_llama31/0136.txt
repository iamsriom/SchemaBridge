The rschema_0136 database is designed to store information about students, teachers, and courses. The student table contains details about each student, including their unique student ID, name, gender, birthdate, and class. The teacher table holds information about each instructor, with a unique teacher ID and name. The course table stores details about each course, including its unique course ID, name, and instructor, with the instructor being linked to the teacher table through a foreign key that ensures the instructor's details are updated or deleted if the course is updated or deleted. The course_selection table tracks which students are enrolled in which courses, with each student and course combination having a unique grade. This table is linked to both the student and course tables through foreign keys that ensure the student and course details are updated or deleted if the course selection is updated or deleted.
