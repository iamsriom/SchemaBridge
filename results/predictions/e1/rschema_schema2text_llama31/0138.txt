The rschema_0138 database is designed to manage information about employees, stores, products, and sales. It consists of five tables: employee, store, product_table, employment, and sales. The employee table stores details about each employee, including their unique employee number, name, gender, and performance. The store table contains information about each store, such as its unique store number, name, and address. The product_table holds details about each product, including its unique product number, name, specifications, and unit price. The employment table links employees to the stores they work at, with each entry including the employee's number, the store's number, the period of employment, and the employee's monthly salary. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the employee and store numbers, and it references the employee and store tables with foreign keys that enforce referential integrity by cascading any changes made to the referenced tables. The sales table records the sales volume of each product at each store, with each entry including the product number, store number, and monthly sales volume. This table also has a composite primary key consisting of the product and store numbers, and it references the product_table and store tables with foreign keys that enforce referential integrity by cascading any changes made to the referenced tables.
