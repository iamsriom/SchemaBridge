The rschema_0139 database is designed to manage information related to a healthcare system, specifically focusing on departments, doctors, patients, medicines, and their interactions. The Department table stores details about the various departments within the healthcare system, with each department having a unique name and a floor number. The Doctor table contains information about the medical professionals, including their ID, gender, name, and title. The Patient table holds data about the individuals receiving medical care, with each patient having a unique name and age. The Medicine table lists the different types of medicines available, with each medicine having a unique name and a price. The Prescription table stores unique prescription information, which is used to link prescriptions to other tables. The Diagnosis_Management table tracks the diagnosis and management of patients, linking a doctor to a patient and storing diagnosis information, while the Prescription_Management table keeps track of prescriptions, linking a doctor to a patient and storing prescription information. The Medicine_List_Management table manages the list of medicines prescribed to patients, linking a medicine to a prescription and storing the quantity of the medicine. The primary keys in these tables ensure that each department, doctor, patient, medicine, and prescription is unique, while foreign keys establish relationships between these entities, such as a doctor being associated with a patient or a medicine being prescribed to a patient. Additionally, the database enforces referential integrity by ensuring that when a doctor or patient is deleted, their associated records in other tables are also affected, and that a medicine can only be prescribed to a patient if the patient exists in the system.
