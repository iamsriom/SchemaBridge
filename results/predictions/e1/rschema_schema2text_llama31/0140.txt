The rschema_0140 database is designed to manage information about products, suppliers, staff, customers, and their relationships. The Product table stores details about each product, including its inventory quantity, price, name, and the supplier it comes from. The Supplier table contains information about each supplier, such as their name and address. The Staff table holds details about each employee, including their department, employee ID, employment status, name, and position. The Customer table stores customer information, specifically their ID and name. The Supplier_product table links suppliers to the products they supply, with each entry referencing a unique product and supplier combination, and it also references the Product and Supplier tables to establish these relationships. The Product table has a primary key of Product_ID, which uniquely identifies each product, and the Supplier_product table has a composite primary key of Supplier_ID and Product_ID to ensure each supplier-product combination is unique. The Supplier_product table also enforces referential integrity by restricting the Product_ID and Supplier_ID columns to only reference existing products and suppliers, respectively, and it uses the CASCADE rule to update or delete these references when the corresponding product or supplier is updated or deleted. The Supplier_product table has an index on the Supplier_ID and Product_ID columns to improve query performance.
