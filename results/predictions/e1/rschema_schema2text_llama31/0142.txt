The rschema_0142 database is designed to manage information about sports teams, their coaches, and the players on those teams. At the heart of this database are three main entities: the Coach, Player, and Team tables. The Coach table stores information about each coach, including their age, a unique Coach ID, and their name. The Player table contains details about each player, such as their height, name, and weight, along with a unique Player ID. The Team table holds information about each team, including its establishment date, representative region, a unique Team ID, and team name. The Coach_Team table represents the many-to-many relationship between coaches and teams, linking a coach to a team they are associated with, along with the start date of their coaching tenure. This relationship is established through a composite primary key consisting of the Coach ID, Team ID, and Coaching Start Date, ensuring each coach can only be associated with a team once. The Player_Team table serves a similar purpose, connecting players to the teams they are part of, with a unique combination of Player ID, Team ID, and Joining Date. Each of these relationships is established through foreign keys referencing the respective primary keys in the Coach, Player, and Team tables, with the Coach ID and Team ID in Coach_Team referencing the Coach and Team tables respectively, and the Player ID and Team ID in Player_Team referencing the Player and Team tables. The database also includes constraints to ensure data integrity, such as not allowing null values in the Coach ID and Team ID columns in Coach_Team and Player_Team, and enforcing that a coach can only be associated with a team once and a player can only be part of a team once, with the use of unique constraints and primary keys.
