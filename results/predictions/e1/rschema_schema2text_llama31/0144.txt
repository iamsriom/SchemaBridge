The rschema_0144 database is designed to manage information about a school's courses, classes, students, and teachers. The Course table stores details about each course, including its name, the classroom where it's held, the number of credits it's worth, and the time it meets. The Class table contains information about each class offered by the school, such as its name and specialized direction. The School table holds data about the school itself, including its address, contact number, and name. The Student table stores student information, including their age, class, gender, major, and name, with a foreign key referencing the Class table to establish the student's enrollment. The Teacher table contains details about each teacher, including their age, gender, major, name, professional title, and school ID, with a foreign key referencing the School table to establish their affiliation. The Student_course table links students to the courses they're enrolled in, storing their scores for each course, with foreign keys referencing both the Course and Student tables to ensure data consistency. The Teacher_course table does the same for teachers, linking them to the courses they teach, with foreign keys referencing both the Course and Teacher tables.
