The rschema_0145 database is designed to manage the inventory and sales of solid-state disks, as well as the employees and customers involved in the process. The database consists of ten tables, each representing a different entity. The Department table stores information about the different departments within the organization, with a unique Department ID and a department name. The Solid_State_Disk table contains details about the disks themselves, including their brand, price, and type. The Customer table holds information about the customers who purchase the disks, with a unique customer ID and name. The Employee table stores data about the employees who work in the organization, including their department, name, and telephone number. The Sales_Order table tracks the sales orders made by customers, with a unique order ID, order time, and the customer who placed the order. The Purchase_Record table records the purchases made by employees, including the purchase time, spending amount, and the employee who made the purchase. The Warehouse table stores information about the warehouses where the disks are stored, including the administrator, location, and warehouse name. The Sales_Order_Details and Purchase_Order_Details tables link the sales and purchase records to the disks they involve, with a unique combination of order ID and disk ID or purchase order ID and disk ID, respectively. The Hard_Disk_Storage table tracks the storage of disks in the warehouses, with a unique combination of disk ID and warehouse ID.
