The rschema_0148 database is designed to manage information about colleges, their majors, teachers, students, courses, and student course selections. The College table stores information about each college, with a unique College_ID and a College_Name that can be up to 255 characters long. The Major table links colleges to their respective majors, with a Major_ID and Major_Name, and a foreign key referencing the College_ID to establish the relationship. The Teacher table stores details about each teacher, including their College_ID, contact information, gender, hire date, position, and a unique Teacher_ID, all of which are linked to the College_ID to show their affiliation. The Student table contains information about each student, including their gender, Major_ID, name, and a unique Student_ID, with the Major_ID referencing the Major table to show their enrolled major. The Course table stores details about each course, including its class hours, Course_ID, name, credit, Major_ID, and Teacher_ID, all of which are linked to the Major and Teacher tables to show the course's affiliation. The Student_Course_Selection table tracks each student's course selections, including the Course_ID, selection time, grade, and Student_ID, with primary keys on both Student_ID and Course_ID to uniquely identify each selection, and foreign keys referencing the Course and Student tables to establish the relationships.
