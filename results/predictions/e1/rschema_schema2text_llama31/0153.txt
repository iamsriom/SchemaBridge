The rschema_0153 database is used to manage information about teachers, students, courses, and their enrollments. The Teacher table contains details about each instructor, including their name, income, and a unique identifier, Teacher_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Student table stores information about each student, such as their age, whether they have a balance, and a unique identifier, Student_ID, which is the primary key. The Course table holds details about each course, including its category, name, description, and a unique identifier, Course_ID, which is the primary key. The Instructor_Teacher_ID in the Course table references the Teacher_ID in the Teacher table, establishing a relationship between the instructor and the course they teach. The Student_Course_Selection table tracks the enrollment of students in courses, with each record containing the Course_ID, the time of selection, and the Student_ID, which references the Student table. The Student_ID and Course_ID in the Student_Course_Selection table form a composite primary key, ensuring each student can only be enrolled in a course once. The Course_ID in the Student_Course_Selection table also references the Course table, and the Student_ID references the Student table, enforcing referential integrity by preventing the deletion of a course or student if they have active enrollments.
