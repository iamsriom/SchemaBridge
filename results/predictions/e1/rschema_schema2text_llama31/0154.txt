The rschema_0154 database is designed to manage user information, products, and their supply and demand relationships. The 'user' table stores details about each user, including their username, password, gender, phone number, ID number, email, and a unique user ID, which serves as the primary key. The 'product' table contains information about each product, such as its title, category, price, images, content, validity period, contact person, and phone number, with the product ID being the primary key. The 'supply_and_demand_relationship' table links users and products together, with each relationship having a unique supply and demand ID, a type, and referencing the user ID from the 'user' table and the product ID from the 'product' table, both of which are foreign keys with ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE rules, meaning that if a user or product is deleted, all related supply and demand relationships will also be deleted.
