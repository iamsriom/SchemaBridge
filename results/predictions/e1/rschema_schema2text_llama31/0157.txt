The rschema_0157 database is designed to manage information about a school, its departments, classes, teachers, courses, dormitories, and students. The School table stores details about the school itself, including its facilities, introduction, and name, with a unique identifier for each school. The Department table contains information about the various departments within the school, such as their introduction and name, each identified by a unique department ID. The Class table lists the different classes offered, including their introduction and name, with a unique class ID. The Teacher table stores details about the school's teachers, including their introduction, name, and title, with a unique teacher ID. The Course table contains information about the courses offered, including their credits, description, name, and a unique course ID. The Dormitory table stores details about the school's dormitories, including the bed count, building, room number, and a unique dormitory ID. The Course_Schedule table links teachers to the courses they teach, specifying the class location, time, and a composite primary key of teacher and course IDs, referencing the Teacher and Course tables. The Student table stores information about the students, including their class, department, dormitory, gender, name, and a unique student ID, with foreign keys referencing the Class, Department, and Dormitory tables to establish relationships between students and their respective departments, classes, and dormitories.
