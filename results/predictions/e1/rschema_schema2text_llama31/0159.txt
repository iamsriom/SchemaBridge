The rschema_0159 database is designed to manage the operations of a transportation company, encompassing various aspects of its activities. At the core of this system are the Bus_Route and Bus tables, which store information about the routes and vehicles used for transportation. Each bus route has a unique identifier, a destination, passing stations, and a starting point, while buses are identified by a unique vehicle ID and categorized by type and status. The Employee table contains details about the staff, including their ID, name, position, and contact information. The Announcement table is used to record important messages, such as announcements, with a unique identifier, title, and release time. Passengers are registered with their name, contact information, and registration details, and are assigned a unique user ID. The Dispatch_Route_Arrangement, Dispatch_Vehicle_Arrangement, and Dispatch_Employee_Arrangement tables manage the assignments of routes, vehicles, and employees to specific tasks, with each task having a unique identifier and referencing the corresponding bus route, vehicle, and employee through foreign keys. The primary keys of these tables ensure that each record is unique, while the foreign keys enforce referential integrity by linking related records across tables. The database also includes indexes and constraints to ensure data consistency and efficient querying, such as the NOT NULL constraint on the Route_ID column in Dispatch_Route_Arrangement, which requires a valid bus route to be assigned to a task, and the CASCADE rule on the foreign key referencing the Employee table in Dispatch_Employee_Arrangement, which automatically updates the task when an employee's details are modified.
