The rschema_0161 database is designed to manage user accounts, products, and transactions. The User table stores information about each registered user, including their email, password, unique user ID, and username. The Product table contains details about the items available for purchase, such as a brief description, inventory levels, name, and price, with each product assigned a unique ID. The Shopping_Cart table represents the temporary collections of items a user has added to their cart, with each cart linked to a specific user through their unique ID, and includes the creation time of the cart. The Order table tracks completed transactions, including the order ID, status, time of order, and the user who placed the order. The Shopping_Cart_Details table links products to the shopping carts they belong to, with each product and cart combination having a quantity, while the Order_Details table does the same for completed orders, including the unit price of each item. The relationships between these tables are enforced through foreign keys, such as the Belonging_User_ID in Shopping_Cart referencing the User_ID in User, and the Product_ID in Shopping_Cart_Details and Order_Details referencing the Product_ID in Product, ensuring data consistency and integrity. Additionally, primary keys and composite keys, like the combination of Shopping_Cart_ID and Product_ID in Shopping_Cart_Details, and Order_ID and Product_ID in Order_Details, uniquely identify each record in their respective tables, while indexes and constraints like NOT NULL and UNIQUE ensure that data is accurate and complete.
