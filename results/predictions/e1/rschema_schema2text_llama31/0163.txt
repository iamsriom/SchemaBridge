The rschema_0163 database is designed to manage information related to sugar cane cultivation, employee management, and farm operations. The Sugar_cane_variety table stores details about different sugar cane varieties, including their maturation cycle, suitable climate, suitable soil type, a unique identifier (Variety_ID), and the variety name. The Employee table contains information about the farm's staff, such as their ID, gender, name, phone number, and working hours. Each employee is associated with a plot of land in the Plot table, which includes details about the plot's area, location, organic matter content, soil pH, and type, as well as the ID of the employee in charge. The Planting_record table tracks planting activities, including the planter's ID, planting method, quantity, time, and the plot and variety involved, with a composite primary key combining plot, variety, and planting time to ensure uniqueness. The Fertilization_record table logs fertilization events, including the amount, method, time, type, and any remarks, with a composite primary key combining the person in charge, plot, and fertilization time. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: the Plot table references the Employee table for the person in charge, while the Planting_record and Fertilization_record tables reference both the Employee and Plot tables for the planter and plot involved, and the Sugar_cane_variety table for the variety planted.
