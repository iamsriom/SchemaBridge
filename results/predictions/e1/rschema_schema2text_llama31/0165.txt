The rschema_0165 database is designed to store information about meteorological stations, their operators, and the historical evolution of the stations' data. The Meteorological_Station table contains details about each station, including its elevation, geographic environment, latitude, longitude, observation elements, instruments, and standards, as well as the time of observation and the station's name and type. Each station is uniquely identified by its Station_ID, which is the primary key. The Operator table stores information about the individuals responsible for managing the stations, including their name, email, phone number, role, and level, with the Operator_ID serving as the primary key. The Historical_Evolution_Information table tracks changes made to the stations' data over time, including the type of change, the new and old values, the reason for the change, and the time of the change, with the Belonging_Station_ID referencing the Meteorological_Station table and the Change_ID serving as the primary key. The Audit_Information table provides a record of the audits performed on the changes, including comments, status, and time, with the Change_ID referencing the Historical_Evolution_Information table and the Operator_ID referencing the Operator table, and the primary key being a composite of Change_ID, Operator_ID, and Audit_Time.
