The rschema_0166 database is designed to manage medical records and images, with a focus on the interactions between patients, doctors, and medical images. The Patient table stores information about each patient, including their age, contact details, medical records, name, and a unique identifier. The Doctor table contains details about each doctor, such as their contact information, department, name, and a unique identifier. The Medical_Image table stores medical images, including the image data, enhancement information, resolution, type, and a unique identifier, which is linked to the patient's identifier in the Patient table. The Doctor_Operation_on_Image table tracks the operations performed on each image by a doctor, including the operation remarks, time, and type, with foreign keys referencing the Doctor and Medical_Image tables. The Doctor_Upload_Image table records when a doctor uploads an image to the system, including the server path and upload time, with foreign keys referencing the Doctor and Medical_Image tables. The Doctor_Share_Image table tracks when a doctor shares an image with another doctor, including the target institution and sharing time, with foreign keys referencing the Medical_Image and Doctor tables.
