The rschema_0167 database is designed to store and manage information related to natural disasters and the departments responsible for disaster management. The natural_disaster_data table contains detailed information about the data itself, including its type, source, upload time, content, format, and layer type, with each entry uniquely identified by a data_id. Each piece of data is associated with a specific region, which is stored in the region_information table, where regions are identified by a unique region_id and contain information such as the region's name, administrative division, latitude and longitude, area, and disaster risk level. The department table stores information about the departments responsible for disaster management, including their department_id, name, person-in-charge, contact information, and functional scope. Users who interact with the system are stored in the user table, where each user is identified by a unique user_id and has a username, role, contact information, permissions, and a department_id that links them to a specific department. The region_and_department table serves as a bridge between regions and departments, linking a department to one or more regions and specifying the division scope for each link. The relationships between these tables are enforced through foreign keys: the natural_disaster_data table references the region_information table, and the user and region_and_department tables reference the department table, with all relationships set to cascade on delete and update, ensuring that when a department is deleted or updated, all associated users and region links are automatically updated accordingly.
