The rschema_0168 database is designed to manage and track information about electrical equipment, its maintenance history, and log records. The Electrical_Equipment table stores details about each piece of equipment, including its unique identifier, name, type, installation date, and manufacturer. The Equipment_ID is the primary key, ensuring that each piece of equipment is uniquely identified. The Log_Record table captures a record of the state of each piece of equipment over time, with each entry including the equipment's ID, the time the record was made, and a description of the state parameters. The Equipment_ID in Log_Record is a foreign key referencing the Equipment_ID in Electrical_Equipment, establishing a relationship between the two tables. Similarly, the Maintenance_Record table stores details about maintenance activities performed on each piece of equipment, including the cost, personnel involved, time of maintenance, and type of maintenance. The Equipment_ID in Maintenance_Record is also a foreign key referencing the Equipment_ID in Electrical_Equipment, linking maintenance records to the corresponding equipment. Both Log_Record and Maintenance_Record tables have composite primary keys, consisting of the Equipment_ID and the respective time of the record or maintenance, ensuring that each piece of equipment has a unique log record and maintenance record for each time point.
