The rschema_0169 database is designed to manage information about library members and their interactions with books. The Member table stores details about each member, including their address, expenditure, phone number, points balance, and login credentials such as username and password. The Book table contains information about the library's book collection, including the author, title, ISBN, publication date, publisher, and type. The Order table tracks transactions between members and the library, recording the date of each order, the points earned, sales amount, and shipping date, with a foreign key referencing the Member table to link each order to the corresponding member. The Order_Detail table provides a detailed breakdown of each order, listing the book ID, order ID, and sales quantity, with foreign keys referencing both the Book and Order tables to establish relationships between these entities.
