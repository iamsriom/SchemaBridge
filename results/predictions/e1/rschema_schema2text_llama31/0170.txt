The rschema_0170 database is designed to manage information about various stores, their departments, and employees. The store table contains details about each store, including its unique identifier, name, address, and contact phone number. Each store is uniquely identified by its store_id, which cannot be null. The department table stores information about each department within a store, including its unique identifier, name, distribution location, contact phone number, manager's name, manager's tenure, and the store it belongs to. The department_id is the primary key, and it references the employee table to establish a relationship with the manager, with the constraint that if the manager is deleted, the department will also be deleted. The department also references the store table to establish a relationship with the store it belongs to, with the same constraint. The employee table contains details about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, position, phone number, salary, emergency contact, and the department they belong to. The employee_id is the primary key, and it references the employee table to establish a relationship with the emergency contact, with the constraint that if the emergency contact is deleted, the employee will also be deleted. The employee also references the department table to establish a relationship with the department they belong to, with the same constraint.
