The rschema_0171 database is designed to manage parking records for a facility, tracking the entry and exit of vehicles into and out of designated parking spaces. The database consists of five tables: Parking, Entrance, Exit, Vehicle, and Parking_Record. The Parking table stores information about each parking space, with a unique identifier (Parking_Space_ID) and a status (Parking_Space_Status). The Entrance and Exit tables contain details about the locations and numbers of entrances and exits, with each table having a unique identifier (Entrance_Number and Exit_Number, respectively). The Vehicle table holds information about the vehicles, including their license plate numbers and models, with a unique identifier (Vehicle_ID). The Parking_Record table is the core of the database, storing the entry and exit times, duration, and fee for each vehicle, as well as the parking space and vehicle identifiers. This table also includes foreign keys referencing the Entrance, Exit, Parking, and Vehicle tables, ensuring that each parking record is linked to a valid entrance, exit, parking space, and vehicle. The primary key of the Parking_Record table is a composite key consisting of the Parking_Space_ID and Vehicle_ID, ensuring that each parking record is unique. The foreign keys in the Parking_Record table are set up with ON DELETE RESTRICT rules, meaning that if a parking space, entrance, exit, or vehicle is deleted, any associated parking records will not be deleted, but instead, the deletion will be restricted.
