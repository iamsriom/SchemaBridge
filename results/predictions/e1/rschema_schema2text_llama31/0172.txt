The rschema_0172 database is designed to store patient information and medical records. The Patient table contains details about each individual, including their age, contact number, emergency contact number, and name, which is uniquely identified by a Patient ID. The Medical Record table stores medical data for each patient, such as blood pressure, heart rate, and temperature, along with the time the record was taken, and is linked to the Patient table through the Patient ID, which is a foreign key that ensures each record is associated with a valid patient. The Medical Record table also has a unique Record ID that serves as its primary key. The Alert Record table captures instances where a patient's medical record indicates an abnormal reading, and is linked to the Medical Record table through the Abnormal Record ID, which is also a foreign key that ensures each alert is associated with a valid medical record. The Alert Record table has its own unique Alert ID as its primary key, and the Alert Time is also recorded.
