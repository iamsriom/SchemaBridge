The rschema_0174 database is designed to manage patient information and their vital signs. The Patient table stores details about each individual, including their name, gender, phone number, ID card number, and medical record number, which serves as the primary key to uniquely identify each patient. The Address and Admission_time fields are also included to provide additional context about the patient's information. The Vital_signs table contains information about the normal ranges for various vital signs, such as blood pressure and heart rate, with each feature having a unique name and corresponding lower and upper limits. The Monitoring table tracks the actual vital sign readings for each patient, including the feature name, its value, and the time of recording, with the medical record number and feature name serving as a composite primary key to ensure data integrity. The Medical_record_number field in the Monitoring table references the Medical_record_number field in the Patient table, establishing a relationship between the two, and the Feature_name field references the Feature_name field in the Vital_signs table, indicating that the recorded vital sign values must fall within the normal ranges defined in the Vital_signs table.
