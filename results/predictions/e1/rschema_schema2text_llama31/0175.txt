The rschema_0175 database is designed to track donations and projects within an organization. The alumni table stores information about individual alumni, including their name, employer, job title, department, class, enrollment year, and ID number, with each record uniquely identified by a unique alumni ID. The employer field references the unit table, which contains information about different units within the organization, identified by a unique unit ID. The donation record table captures details of donations made by alumni, including the donating alumni's ID, donation time, amount, type, and usage, with each record uniquely identified by a unique donation ID. The project table stores information about individual projects, including the project ID, name, start and end times, and total donation amount. The benefit detail table links projects to the units they benefit, specifying the percentage of the donation allocated to each unit, with each record uniquely identified by a combination of the beneficiary unit and project ID. The donation project table serves as a bridge between the donation record and project tables, linking each donation to the corresponding project.
