The rschema_0176 database is designed to manage information about flights, passengers, and their interactions. The Flight table stores details about each flight, including the airline, arrival and departure times, departure city, destination, a unique flight ID, and the ticket price. The Passenger table contains information about each passenger, such as their name, date of birth, gender, phone number, and a VIP discount rate. The Discount_Information table links specific discounts to flights, with each discount having a unique ID, start and end dates, a discount rate, and a reference to the Flight table's Flight_ID to associate it with a particular flight. The Ticket_Purchase_Record table tracks the purchase of tickets, including the flight ID, passenger ID, purchase amount, ticket order number, and travel date, with foreign keys referencing the Flight and Passenger tables to ensure that only valid flights and passengers can be associated with a ticket purchase.
