The rschema_0178 database is designed to manage customer information and their interactions with medication and payment systems. The Customer table stores essential customer details, including their name, email, password, and contact information, with a unique identifier for each customer. The Medication table holds information about available medications, such as their name, price, and stock quantity. The Payment_Method table links customers to their preferred payment methods, which are identified by a unique ID and include details about the payment type and method. The Prescription table stores information about prescriptions, including the customer who requested it, the doctor who prescribed it, and the prescription status, with a unique identifier for each prescription. The Prescription_Medication_Association table acts as a bridge between prescriptions and medications, detailing the quantity of each medication prescribed. The Order table tracks customer orders, including the order status, total price, and the prescription associated with the order. The Verification_Log table records verification results for prescriptions, including the verification time and outcome. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys, which uniquely identify each customer, medication, payment method, prescription, and order, as well as composite keys in the Payment_Method and Prescription_Medication_Association tables, which ensure that each payment method and prescription-medication association is unique. Foreign keys, such as the Customer_ID in the Payment_Method and Prescription tables, establish relationships between tables, and referential integrity rules, like CASCADE and RESTRICT, govern how these relationships are updated and deleted. Additionally, indexes and constraints are used to optimize data retrieval and enforce data consistency, ensuring that the database remains accurate and reliable.
