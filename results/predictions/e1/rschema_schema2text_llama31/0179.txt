The rschema_0179 database is used to manage information about supermarkets, their employees, departments, products, and distribution of products. The supermarket table stores details about each supermarket, including its unique identifier, name, address, and telephone number, with a primary key to uniquely identify each supermarket. The supermarket table also references the department table through a foreign key, indicating the manager of each supermarket. The department table contains information about each department, including its unique identifier, name, manager, and contact telephone number, with a primary key to uniquely identify each department and a foreign key referencing the employee table to identify the manager of each department. The employee table stores details about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, contact information, position, salary, and department, with a primary key to uniquely identify each employee and foreign keys referencing the department table to link employees to their departments. The product table stores details about each product, including its unique identifier, name, model, unit price, and quantity, with a primary key to uniquely identify each product. The distribution table tracks the distribution of products to employees, including the employee's unique identifier, product identifier, and distribution time, with a composite primary key to uniquely identify each distribution event and foreign keys referencing the employee and product tables to link each distribution event to the employee and product involved.
