The rschema_0180 database is designed to manage the operations of a pizza delivery service, storing information about customers, orders, pizzas, and the quantities of pizzas ordered. The Pizza table contains details of the available pizzas, including a unique identifier, name, price, and stock quantity. The Customer table holds information about each customer, such as their address, ID, name, and phone number. The Order table stores details of each order, including the customer's ID, expected delivery date, order date, status, and a unique order ID. The Ordered_Pizza table tracks the specific pizzas ordered by each customer, linking orders to pizzas through their respective IDs, and also referencing the Order and Pizza tables to ensure data consistency. The Customer table's Customer_ID is the primary key, uniquely identifying each customer, while the Order table's Order_ID serves the same purpose for orders. The primary key of the Ordered_Pizza table is a composite of the Order_ID and Pizza_ID, ensuring that each pizza is uniquely linked to an order. The Customer_ID in the Order table references the Customer table, establishing the relationship between customers and their orders, and the Order_ID in the Ordered_Pizza table references the Order table, linking pizzas to their respective orders. The Pizza_ID in the Ordered_Pizza table references the Pizza table, ensuring that each pizza is correctly associated with its ordered quantity.
