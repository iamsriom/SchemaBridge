The rschema_0181 database is designed to manage information related to freight trucks, employees, and transportation tasks. The Freight_Truck table contains details about each truck, including its License Plate Number, Load Capacity, and Model, with the License Plate Number serving as the primary identifier. The Employee table stores information about the individuals involved in transportation tasks, with each employee assigned a unique Employee ID, Name, Phone Number, and Role. The Transportation_Task table brings together the various components of a transportation task, including the Assigned License Plate Number of the truck, the IDs of the Assistant and Driver, the Cargo Name, Destination, and Origin, as well as the Start and End times, Transportation Time, and Weight of the cargo. The Assigned License Plate Number in the Transportation_Task table references the License Plate Number in the Freight_Truck table, establishing a link between the truck and the task it is assigned to, while the Assistant_ID and Driver_ID in the Transportation_Task table reference the Employee_ID in the Employee table, indicating which employees are involved in each task. The Task_Number in the Transportation_Task table serves as its primary identifier, and the Assigned License Plate Number, Assistant_ID, and Driver_ID are not nullable, ensuring that each task is associated with a specific truck and two employees.
