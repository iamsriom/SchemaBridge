The rschema_0182 database is designed to manage sales data for agricultural products across various markets. It consists of three tables: market, agricultural_products, and sales_record. The market table stores information about each market, including a unique market identifier, name, address, and contact details. The agricultural_products table holds details about the products sold, such as a unique product identifier, name, specification, price, and current stock level. The sales_record table tracks sales transactions, including the market and product involved, the date of sale, quantity sold, unit price, and the operator who made the sale. The sales_record table has a composite primary key consisting of the product identifier, market identifier, and sales date, ensuring each sale is uniquely identified. The sales_record table also references the agricultural_products table through a foreign key, which means that a product must exist in the agricultural_products table before it can be recorded as sold. This relationship is set up with an ON DELETE CASCADE rule, meaning that if a product is deleted from the agricultural_products table, all sales records for that product will also be deleted. However, there is an inconsistency in the foreign key referencing the market table, as it references a non-existent table named `schema_0` instead of the market table itself.
