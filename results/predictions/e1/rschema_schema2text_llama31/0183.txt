The rschema_0183 database is designed to track and manage the maintenance and usage of various tools within an organization. At its core, the database contains three main tables: tool, usage_record, and maintenance_record. The tool table stores information about each tool, including its model, name, usage frequency, and a unique identifier, Tool_ID, which serves as the primary key. The usage_record table captures details about each instance of tool usage, such as the location, time, and user, with a foreign key referencing the Tool_ID in the tool table to link each usage record to its corresponding tool. The maintenance_record table documents maintenance activities performed on each tool, including the content of the maintenance, the person responsible, the time taken, and the tool's ID, with a foreign key referencing the Tool_ID in the tool table to establish the relationship between maintenance records and their respective tools.
