The rschema_0184 database is designed to manage bookings for a hotel or similar establishment. It contains three main tables: member, room, and booking. The member table stores information about each registered member, including their unique member ID, name, phone number, registration date, and points balance. The room table holds details about each available room, such as its room number, type, and price. The booking table is where bookings are recorded, with each entry consisting of a member ID, room number, booking date, check-in and check-out dates, booking status, and points change. The primary key for the booking table is a composite key made up of the member ID, booking date, and room number, ensuring that each booking is uniquely identified. The member ID in the booking table references the member ID in the member table, with a cascade delete and update rule, meaning that if a member is deleted or updated, all their bookings will be automatically deleted or updated accordingly. Similarly, the room number in the booking table references the room number in the room table, with the same cascade delete and update rules.
