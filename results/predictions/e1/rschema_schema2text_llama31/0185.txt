The rschema_0185 database is designed to manage proposals and their associated handling and submission processes. At its core, the database contains a Proposal table, which stores information about each proposal, including its content, a unique identifier, the time it was submitted, and its current status. Each proposal is uniquely identified by its Proposal_ID, which is an integer that serves as the primary key. The Member table stores details about the individuals involved in the proposal process, including their date of birth, gender, a unique identifier, name, party affiliation, and position. The Proposal_Submission table links proposals to the members who submitted them, with each submission being uniquely identified by a combination of the Proposal_ID and Member_ID. The Proposal_Handling table tracks the handling of proposals, including the opinion of the handling member, the time of handling, and the member and proposal involved, with each handling being uniquely identified by a combination of the Proposal_ID and Member_ID. The Member_ID in both the Proposal_Submission and Proposal_Handling tables references the Member table, ensuring that only valid member IDs are used, and the Proposal_ID in both tables references the Proposal table, linking each proposal to its associated submissions and handling.
