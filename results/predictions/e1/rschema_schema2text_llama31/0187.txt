The rschema_0187 database is designed to manage information about farms, crops, and their planting and harvesting activities. The Farm table stores details about individual farms, including their address, contact information, farm ID, owner's name, and the type of owner. Each farm is uniquely identified by its Farm_ID, which is the primary key. The Crop table contains information about different types of crops, such as their name, growth cycle, suitable climate conditions, and suitable soil type, with Crop_ID serving as the primary key. The Plot table represents individual plots of land within a farm, with each plot having a unique ID, area, current status, soil type, and a foreign key referencing the Farm_ID of the farm it belongs to. The Planting_record table stores records of when and how crops are planted, including the crop ID, expected harvest time, operator, planting quantity, and planting time, with a composite primary key consisting of plot ID, crop ID, and planting time. The Planting_record table also references the Crop and Plot tables through foreign keys. Similarly, the Harvest_record table stores records of when and how crops are harvested, including the crop ID, harvest quantity, harvest time, operator, and plot ID, with a composite primary key consisting of plot ID, crop ID, and harvest time, and referencing the Crop and Plot tables through foreign keys.
