The rschema_0188 database is designed to manage the operations of a company, encompassing various aspects such as product management, employee information, production records, inventory tracking, sales transactions, and performance evaluations. The database consists of seven tables: product, department, employee, production_record, inventory_record, sales_record, and performance_evaluation. The product table stores information about each product, including its unique identifier, name, type, production date, and price. The department table contains details about each department, including its identifier, name, manager's employee ID, and contact information, with the manager's employee ID referencing the employee table for cascading updates and deletions. The employee table holds information about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, gender, phone number, job position, and department ID, with the department ID referencing the department table for cascading updates and deletions. The production_record table tracks the production of products, including the product ID, producer's employee ID, production time, and quantity, with the producer's employee ID referencing the employee table for cascading updates and deletions. The inventory_record table keeps track of the stock levels, including the product ID, stock-in time, and quantity, with the product ID referencing the product table for cascading updates and deletions. The sales_record table records sales transactions, including the salesperson's employee ID, customer, sales time, product ID, and sales quantity, with the salesperson's employee ID and product ID referencing the employee and product tables, respectively, for cascading updates and deletions. The performance_evaluation table evaluates the performance of departments, including the department ID, evaluation time, and evaluation score, with the department ID referencing the department table for cascading updates and deletions.
