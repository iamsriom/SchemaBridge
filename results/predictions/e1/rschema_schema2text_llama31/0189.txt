The rschema_0189 database is designed to manage information about vehicle repairs and the technicians who perform them. The database consists of five tables: station, user, repair_technician, vehicle, and repair_record. The station table holds information about the repair stations where technicians work, with each station identified by a unique Station ID and named with a Station Name. The user table stores details about the vehicle owners, including their contact number and name, and assigns a unique User ID to each individual. The repair_technician table contains information about the technicians who work at the stations, including their contact number, skill level, station ID, technician ID, and name, with the station ID referencing the station where they work. The vehicle table stores details about the vehicles, including their license plate number, owner ID, and model, with the owner ID referencing the user who owns the vehicle. The repair_record table holds records of the repairs performed on the vehicles, including the license plate number, owner's evaluation of the repair, repair cost, repair date and duration, repair effect, and repair type, with the license plate number referencing the vehicle being repaired and the technician ID referencing the technician who performed the repair.
