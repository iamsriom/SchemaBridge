The rschema_0190 database is designed to manage information about workstations, products, repair technicians, and repair records. The Workstation table stores details about each workstation, including a unique identifier (Workstation_ID) and its name. The Product table contains information about the products that are repaired, with each product having a unique identifier (Product_ID), model, and name. The Repair_Technician table stores details about the technicians who perform the repairs, including their unique identifier (Repair_Technician_ID), name, phone number, and the workstation they are assigned to, which is a required field referencing the Workstation table. The Repair_Record table holds records of each repair, including the customer's name, phone number, feedback, and description of the fault, as well as the product being repaired, the duration and time of the repair, and the type of repair, with the unique identifier for the repair record being a composite of the repair technician's ID, product ID, and repair time. The Repair_Record table also references the Product and Repair_Technician tables through foreign keys, ensuring that each repair record is linked to a valid product and technician.
