The rschema_0191 database is designed to track sales team performance and customer interactions. The database consists of five tables: Sales_Team, Salesperson, Sales_Task, Performance_Evaluation, and Task_Record. The Sales_Team table stores information about each sales team, with a unique identifier Team_ID and a team name. The Salesperson table contains details about individual salespeople, including their age, sex, telephone number, and a unique identifier Salesperson_ID, which is linked to the team they belong to through the Belongs_to_Team_ID field. The Sales_Task table holds records of sales transactions, including the customer's name and telephone number, the product model and name, price, and sales cost, as well as the date and quantity of the sale, all linked to the team they were assigned to through the Belongs_to_Team_ID field. The Performance_Evaluation table tracks the performance of each salesperson, including their customer satisfaction rating, a unique evaluation ID, and the quantity of tasks they completed, linked to the salesperson through their Salesperson_ID. The Task_Record table stores additional information about each sales task, including a customer evaluation and a unique record ID, linked to the task through the Task_ID field.
