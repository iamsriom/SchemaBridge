The rschema_0193 database is designed to manage information about materials, projects, and employees within an organization. The material table stores details about various materials, including their unique identifier, name, specifications, unit price, and inventory levels, with each material having a unique identifier. The project table holds information about ongoing and completed projects, such as their unique identifier, name, start and expected completion dates, actual completion date, budget, and location. The material_usage_record table tracks the usage of materials across different projects, including the unique identifier for each usage record, the project it belongs to, the material used, the date of usage, the quantity used, and the employee responsible for the usage. This table also references the employee and material tables, meaning that each usage record is linked to a specific employee and material, and any changes to these entities will be reflected in the usage records. The employee table stores details about individual employees, including their unique identifier, name, and department, with each employee belonging to a specific department. The department table contains a list of all departments within the organization, with each department having a unique identifier and name.
