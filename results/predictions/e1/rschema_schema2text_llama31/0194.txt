The rschema_0194 database is designed to manage information about suppliers, projects, materials, supplier credit assessments, and procurement records. The Supplier table stores details about each supplier, including their address, email, name, a unique identifier, and telephone number. The Project table contains information about each project, such as its actual and expected completion dates, budget, location, name, and start date. The Material table holds details about each material, including its description and name. The Supplier_Credit_Assessment table tracks assessments of each supplier's creditworthiness, including the assessment content, date, score, and a foreign key referencing the supplier's unique identifier in the Supplier table. The Procurement_Record table records procurement activities, including the material's identifier, quantity, unit price, procurement record identifier, project identifier, purchase date, supplier identifier, and total price, with foreign keys referencing the Project and Supplier tables to establish relationships between procurement records and the projects and suppliers involved. The Supplier table's Supplier_ID is the primary key, while the Project table's Project_ID and Material table's Material_ID are also primary keys. The Supplier_Credit_Assessment table's Assessment_ID is the primary key, and the Procurement_Record table's Procurement_Record_ID is the primary key. The Supplier table's Supplier_ID is a foreign key in the Supplier_Credit_Assessment table, and the Procurement_Record table's Project_ID and Supplier_ID are foreign keys referencing the Project and Supplier tables, respectively, with both set to NOT NULL to ensure that each procurement record is associated with a project and a supplier.
