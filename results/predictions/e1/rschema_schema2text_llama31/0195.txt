The rschema_0195 database is designed to manage real estate sales data, storing information about properties, sales personnel, clients, and sales records. The Property table contains details about each property, including its address, area, unique identifier, sale price, and sale status. The Sales_Personnel table holds information about the sales staff, such as their commission rate, contact phone number, and unique employee ID. The Client table stores client details, including their address, contact phone number, and unique client ID. The Sales_Record table is the core of the database, linking properties, clients, and sales personnel through a composite primary key consisting of the property ID, employee ID, and client ID. This table also includes the sale date and sale price, as well as a foreign key referencing the Client table to ensure data consistency. The Sales_Record table further references the Property table through a foreign key, linking each sale to the corresponding property, and the Sales_Personnel table through another foreign key, which ties each sale to the employee who made it. The Client, Property, and Sales_Personnel tables have their own unique identifiers as primary keys, preventing duplicate entries and ensuring data integrity.
