The rschema_0196 database is designed to manage loan applications and their associated approvals. It consists of four tables: Applicant, Approver, Loan_Application, and Loan_Approval. The Applicant table stores information about individuals who submit loan applications, including their identification number, name, phone number, and email address, with a unique identifier for each applicant. The Approver table contains details about the individuals who review and approve loan applications, such as their name, phone number, and email address, with a unique identifier for each approver. The Loan_Application table holds information about each loan application, including the applicant's identifier, a unique application number, the loan amount, loan term, and the current approval status, with a foreign key referencing the Applicant table to link each application to its corresponding applicant. The Loan_Approval table tracks the approval process for each loan application, including the application number, the date of approval, a boolean indicating whether the application was approved, the identifier of the approver, and a composite primary key consisting of the approver's identifier, application number, and approval date, with foreign keys referencing both the Loan_Application and Approver tables to ensure data consistency and enforce referential integrity.
