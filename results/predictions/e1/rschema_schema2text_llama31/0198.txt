The rschema_0198 database is designed to manage information about communities, their properties, maintenance staff, and maintenance tasks. At the core of this database is the Community_information table, which stores details about each community, including a unique identifier, name, location, and property manager. The Property_information table is closely related to Community_information, as it contains details about each property within a community, such as its address, area, and maintenance status, with a foreign key referencing the Community_ID in Community_information to establish this relationship. The Maintenance_staff_information table stores information about the maintenance staff, including their contact details, ID, name, and position. The Maintenance_task table is where maintenance tasks are recorded, including the task content, cost, time, and the staff member and property involved, with foreign keys referencing the Maintenance_staff_ID in Maintenance_staff_information and Property_ID in Property_information to link these tasks to the relevant staff and properties. The Maintenance_task table has a composite primary key consisting of Property_ID, Maintenance_staff_ID, and Maintenance_time, ensuring that each task is uniquely identified by the property, staff member, and time it was performed.
