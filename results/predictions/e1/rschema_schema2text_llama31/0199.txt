The rschema_0199 database is designed to manage data related to stores and their sales performance. The database contains four main tables: store, product, sales_record, and store_performance. The store table stores information about each store, including its unique store number, name, address, and manager. The product table contains details about each product, such as its unique product number, name, model, barcode, and price. The sales_record table tracks sales data, including the store number, product number, sales time, quantity sold, total sales amount, and the sales personnel involved. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the product number, store number, and sales time, ensuring that each sales record is unique. The sales_record table also has foreign keys referencing the product and store tables, indicating which product was sold at which store, and these relationships are set up to cascade in both directions, meaning that if a product or store is deleted, all associated sales records will be automatically deleted or updated. The store_performance table stores performance metrics for each store, including the total sales amount and customer satisfaction level, and is linked to the store table through a foreign key that also cascades in both directions, ensuring that store performance data is updated or deleted when a store is modified or deleted.
