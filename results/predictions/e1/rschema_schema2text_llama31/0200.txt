The rschema_0200 database is designed to manage customer information and their orders. It consists of four tables: Customer, Order, Customer_Credit_Rating, and Order_Detail. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact person, phone number, and address, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Order table tracks each order made by a customer, containing the order ID, order time, total price, and a foreign key referencing the Customer_ID to link each order to its corresponding customer. The Customer_Credit_Rating table stores the credit rating of each customer, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key and referencing the Customer_ID in the Customer table to link the credit rating to its corresponding customer. The Order_Detail table contains information about the products ordered, including the order detail ID, order ID, product ID, and quantity, with a foreign key referencing the Order_ID in the Order table to link each order detail to its corresponding order.
