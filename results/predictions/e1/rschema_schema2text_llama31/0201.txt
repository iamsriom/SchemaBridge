The rschema_0201 database is designed to manage customer loyalty programs and shopping records. It consists of four tables: Level, Product, Member, and Shopping_Record. The Level table stores information about the different levels of customer loyalty, with each level having a unique ID, a points range, and a shopping frequency range. The Product table contains details about the products sold, with each product having a unique ID and a name. The Member table represents individual customers, with each member having a unique ID, a name, a phone number, and a points balance, and is linked to a specific level through the Level_ID foreign key. The Shopping_Record table stores records of each purchase, including the member ID, product ID, quantity purchased, shopping time, store ID, and total price, with the combination of member ID, product ID, and shopping time serving as the primary key to uniquely identify each purchase. The Member table is linked to the Shopping_Record table through the Member_ID foreign key, and the Product table is linked to the Shopping_Record table through the Product_ID foreign key, ensuring that each purchase is associated with a specific member and product.
