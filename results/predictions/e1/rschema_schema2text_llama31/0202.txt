The rschema_0202 database is designed to manage information about products and their procurement from suppliers. At its core, the database consists of three tables: supplier, product, and procured_product. The supplier table stores details about each supplier, including their name, address, historical procurement quantity and amount, credit rating, contact person, phone number, email, fax number, and company website. The product table contains information about each product, such as its name, model, price, category, shelf life, and current stock quantity. The procured_product table tracks the procurement of specific products from suppliers, with each record including the product ID, supplier ID, procurement time, quantity, total procurement price, payment method, payment status, delivery address, and recipient name and phone number. The procured_product table has a composite primary key consisting of the supplier ID, product ID, and procurement time, ensuring that each procurement is uniquely identified. Additionally, the procured_product table has foreign key constraints referencing the supplier and product tables, which means that a supplier can only be associated with a product if they exist in the supplier and product tables, respectively. The foreign key constraints are set to cascade delete and update, meaning that if a supplier or product is deleted or updated, the corresponding procurement records will also be deleted or updated accordingly.
