The rschema_0203 database is designed to manage and track promotional activities across various stores and products. The database consists of seven tables: promotion_activity, store, product, promotion_effect_record, promotion_effect_evaluation, promotion_activity_participating_store, and promotion_activity_participating_product. The promotion_activity table stores information about each promotional activity, including its unique identifier, name, time, and discount offered. The store table contains details about each store, such as its unique identifier and address. The product table holds information about each product, including its unique identifier, name, model, barcode, and price. The promotion_effect_record table tracks the effects of each promotional activity, including the sales amount, quantity, and number of participating members. The promotion_effect_evaluation table evaluates the success of each promotional activity by measuring its sales growth rate and customer engagement. The promotion_activity_participating_store table links each promotional activity to the stores that participate in it, while the promotion_activity_participating_product table links each promotional activity to the products that are discounted during the activity. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that each promotional activity can only be associated with existing stores and products, and that each store and product can only participate in existing promotional activities. Additionally, the database uses primary keys to uniquely identify each record in each table, and unique constraints to prevent duplicate records from being inserted.
