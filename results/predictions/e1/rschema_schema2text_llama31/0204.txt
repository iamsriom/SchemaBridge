The rschema_0204 database is designed to track information about participating teams, referees, and events in a competitive setting. The participating_teams table stores details about each team, including its unique identifier, name, size, members, team leader's name and phone number, and head coach's name and phone number. The referees table contains information about each referee, such as their unique identifier, name, gender, email, and phone number. The match_results table stores the results of each match, including the event identifier, team identifier, team ranking, and referee evaluation result, with foreign keys referencing the events and referees tables, indicating that the match results are associated with specific events and referees. However, there seems to be a mistake in the match_results table, as it references the referees table by team_id, which should be event_id, and the events table has a foreign key referencing itself, which is likely incorrect. The events table stores information about each event, including its unique identifier, name, type, start and end dates, the identifier of the referee assigned to the event, and the winning team's identifier.
