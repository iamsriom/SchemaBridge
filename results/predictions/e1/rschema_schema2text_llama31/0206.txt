The rschema_0206 database is a comprehensive system for tracking and analyzing environmental data, specifically focusing on geographical regions and their associated flora and fauna, as well as records of environmental changes. At the core of this database is the Region table, which stores information about each geographical area, including its administrator, geographical location, name, and a unique identifier, Region_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Flora_and_Fauna_Record table is used to document observations of plants and animals within these regions, with each record containing details such as the quantity of the observed species, the time of the observation, the person making the observation, the type of species, and a reference to the region in which it was observed, linked through the Region_ID foreign key, which is not nullable and must match a valid Region_ID in the Region table. The Environmental_Change_Record table is used to track changes in environmental conditions, such as humidity, rainfall, and temperature, at specific locations within these regions, with each record containing the time of the change, the location, and a reference to the region in which the change occurred, also linked through the Region_ID foreign key, which is not nullable and must match a valid Region_ID in the Region table.
