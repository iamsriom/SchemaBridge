The rschema_0208 database is designed to manage information about equipment and its operation. The Equipment table stores details about each piece of equipment, including its model, name, installation date, and location. Each equipment item is assigned a unique identifier, which serves as the primary key. The Operator table contains information about the individuals responsible for operating the equipment, with a unique identifier for each operator and their department and contact details. The Equipment_Operation table tracks the operational history of each piece of equipment, including the start and end times of each operation, the status of the operation, and the operator responsible. This table is a composite key, meaning that each combination of equipment ID, operator ID, and start time is unique. The Equipment_Operation table references the Equipment table through the Equipment_ID column, which is a foreign key that links each operation to the corresponding equipment item. Similarly, the Operator_ID column in Equipment_Operation is a foreign key that links each operation to the operator responsible. The Equipment_ID and Operator_ID columns in Equipment_Operation are not nullable, indicating that each operation must be associated with a specific piece of equipment and operator.
