The rschema_0209 database is designed to manage information about laboratories, their equipment, and maintenance records. The database contains three tables: laboratory, equipment, and maintenance_record. The laboratory table stores information about individual laboratories, including a unique laboratory number, name, and location. Each laboratory is identified by its laboratory number, which serves as the primary key. The equipment table stores details about the equipment used in each laboratory, such as the equipment number, name, model, purchase date, and status. Each piece of equipment is associated with a specific laboratory, as indicated by the laboratory number, which is a foreign key referencing the laboratory table. The equipment table also has a primary key of equipment number, and when a laboratory is deleted, all associated equipment will also be deleted. The maintenance_record table stores records of maintenance performed on the equipment, including a unique maintenance number, equipment number, date, personnel involved, and a description of the maintenance. Each maintenance record is linked to a specific piece of equipment, as indicated by the equipment number, which is a foreign key referencing the equipment table. When an equipment is deleted, all associated maintenance records will also be deleted.
