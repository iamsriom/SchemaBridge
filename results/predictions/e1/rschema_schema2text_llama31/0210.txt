The rschema_0210 database is designed to manage information about conferences, attendees, and their interactions. The Conference table stores details about each conference, including its unique identifier, date, location, name, and theme. The Attendee_Information table contains personal details about each attendee, such as their gender, name, organization, and position. The Attendance table links attendees to specific conferences, with each attendee allowed to attend multiple conferences, but a conference can only be attended by one attendee at a time. The Speaker_Presentation table tracks presentations given by attendees at conferences, including the presentation address, time, topic, and the attendee's unique identifier, with each attendee allowed to give multiple presentations at a single conference, but a presentation can only be given by one attendee at a given time and conference. The Conference, Attendee_Information, and Speaker_Presentation tables are linked through foreign keys, which ensure that an attendee can only be associated with a conference or presentation that actually exists, and that a presentation can only be given by an attendee who is registered in the Attendee_Information table.
