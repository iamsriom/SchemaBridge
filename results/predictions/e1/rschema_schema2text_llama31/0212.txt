The rschema_0212 database is designed to manage information about customers, branches, vehicles, and their rentals. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their unique identifier, email address, name, and phone number. The Branch table contains information about each branch, such as its address, identifier, income, manager's name, and name. The Vehicle table holds data about each vehicle, including its branch identifier, license plate number, model, rental rate, unique identifier, and year of manufacture. The Customer_vehicle_rental table tracks the rentals of vehicles by customers, with columns for the customer's identifier, rental fee, rental and return times, and vehicle identifier. The primary key of this table is a composite key consisting of the customer and vehicle identifiers, ensuring that each rental is uniquely associated with a customer and a vehicle. The Branch_ID in the Vehicle table references the Branch table, indicating which branch each vehicle is assigned to. The Customer_ID and Vehicle_ID in the Customer_vehicle_rental table reference the Customer and Vehicle tables, respectively, establishing the relationships between customers, vehicles, and rentals. The Customer_ID in the Customer_vehicle_rental table is not nullable, meaning that a rental must be associated with a valid customer, and the Vehicle_ID is also not nullable, indicating that a rental must be associated with a valid vehicle.
