The rschema_0213 database is designed to manage customer information and their contracts, as well as track payment records for these contracts. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including a unique identifier (Customer_ID), their email address, name, and phone number. The Contract table represents the agreements between customers and the service provider, with each contract having a unique identifier (Contract_ID), an amount, start and end times, and a description of the service content. The Contract table is linked to the Customer table through the Customer_ID, which is a foreign key that references the Customer_ID in the Customer table, and this relationship is set up with an ON DELETE RESTRICT rule to prevent the deletion of a customer if they have active contracts. The Payment_Record table tracks payments made by customers for their contracts, with each payment having a unique identifier (Payment_ID), a contract identifier (Contract_ID) that references the Contract table, a payment amount, and a payment date. The Contract_ID in the Payment_Record table is also a foreign key that references the Contract_ID in the Contract table, and this relationship is set up with an ON DELETE CASCADE rule to automatically delete any payment records when a contract is deleted.
