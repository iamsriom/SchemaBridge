The rschema_0214 database is designed to store information about employees and their performance assessments. The Employee table contains details about each employee, including their unique identifier, gender, name, phone number, and position. The Assessment_Record table holds records of each assessment taken by an employee, with columns for the assessment content, date, score, and a foreign key referencing the Employee table to link the assessment to the relevant employee. The Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics table stores statistics about each employee's average score over time, with columns for the average score, employee identifier, and date, and a composite primary key combining the employee identifier and date to ensure each employee's monthly statistics are unique. The Employee_Monthly_Assessment_Statistics table also references the Employee table through a foreign key, enforcing the relationship between employee identifiers in both tables.
