The rschema_0215 database is designed to manage information related to material administration and distribution in mines. The database consists of four tables: material_administrator, mine, purchase_record, and material_distribution_record. The material_administrator table stores details about the administrators responsible for managing materials, including their administrator ID, name, gender, phone number, position, and the mine they are associated with, which is linked to the mine table through a foreign key. Each mine is uniquely identified by its mine ID and contains information such as its name, location, and person in charge. The purchase_record table tracks all material purchases, including the purchase date, material name, quantity, supplier, and purchase cost. The material_distribution_record table records the distribution of materials, including the distribution date, material name, quantity, distributor, and recipient, with the distributor linked to the material_administrator table through a foreign key. The database ensures data integrity through primary keys, which uniquely identify each record in each table, and foreign keys, which establish relationships between tables and enforce referential integrity by cascading updates and deletions.
