The rschema_0216 database is designed to manage customer credit card information and transactions. It contains five tables: Credit_Card_Type, Customer, Credit_Card, Transaction, and Repayment. The Credit_Card_Type table stores information about different types of credit cards, with each type identified by a unique code and name. The Customer table holds details about each customer, including their ID, name, and contact number. The Credit_Card table links customers to their respective credit cards, specifying the card type, opening date, credit limit, and card number, which is the primary key. The Transaction table records all transactions made by customers, including the card number, customer ID, transaction amount, location, time, and type, with a composite primary key of customer ID, card number, and transaction time. The Repayment table tracks repayments made by customers, with a similar composite primary key of customer ID, card number, and repayment time. Foreign keys in the Credit_Card, Transaction, and Repayment tables reference the Customer and Credit_Card tables to establish relationships between these entities, ensuring data consistency and integrity.
