The rschema_0219 database is designed to manage information about trading cards, their collections, and user interactions. The Card table stores details about individual cards, including their artist, effect, endurance, intelligence, power, and type, with a unique identifier (Card_ID) serving as the primary key. The Card_Collection table contains information about card collections, with a unique identifier (Collection_ID) serving as the primary key. The Card_Collection_Association table links cards to their respective collections, with each association having a unique combination of Card_ID and Collection_ID, and referencing the Card and Card_Collection tables through foreign keys. The User table stores user information, including their email, password, phone number, and username, with a unique identifier (User_ID) serving as the primary key. The User_Card_Exchange table tracks card exchanges between users, with each exchange having a unique combination of User_1_ID, User_2_ID, and Exchange_Time, and referencing the Card, User, and User tables through foreign keys.
