The rschema_0221 database is designed to manage information about customers, suppliers, products, and sales transactions. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, phone number, and address, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Supplier table contains information about each supplier, such as their name, address, responsible person's name and phone number, and a unique identifier, Supplier_ID, which is the primary key. The Product table holds details about each product, including its name, stock level, unit price, and the supplier ID it belongs to, with Product_ID being the primary key. The Product_Purchase table tracks purchases made by suppliers, including the product ID, purchase price, quantity, purchase time, and total purchase price, with a composite primary key consisting of the supplier ID, product ID, and purchase time, referencing the Product and Supplier tables for data integrity. The Sales_Record table stores sales data, including the customer ID, product ID, sale price, quantity, sale time, and total sale price, with a composite primary key consisting of the product ID, customer ID, and sale time, referencing the Customer and Product tables to ensure data consistency.
