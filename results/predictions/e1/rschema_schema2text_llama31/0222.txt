The rschema_0222 database is designed to manage and store information about recipes, their types, and the ingredients used in them. The Recipe_Type_Table contains a list of different types of recipes, each identified by a unique Type_ID, along with a description and name. The Ingredient_Table stores information about individual ingredients, including a description, image, and name, with each ingredient assigned a unique Ingredient_ID. The Recipe_Table holds details about each recipe, such as its description, image, name, and steps, and is linked to a specific type of recipe through the Type_ID, which references the Type_ID in the Recipe_Type_Table. The Recipe_and_Ingredient_Relationship_Table establishes the relationships between recipes and ingredients, specifying the quantity of each ingredient used in a particular recipe, and is linked to both the Ingredient_Table and Recipe_Table through their respective IDs, with the combination of Recipe_ID and Ingredient_ID serving as the primary key to uniquely identify each relationship.
