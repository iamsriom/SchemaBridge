The rschema_0226 database is designed to manage a bookstore's operations, storing information about its branches, customers, categories of books, the books themselves, and the orders placed by customers. The Branch table contains details about each store location, with a unique identifier called Branch_ID and a Location field to store the physical address. The Customer table holds information about each customer, including their Address, Email, First_Name, Last_Name, and Phone number, with a unique identifier called Customer_ID. The Category table lists the different categories of books available, with a unique identifier called Category_ID and a Category_Name field to describe each category. The Book table stores information about each book, including its Book_Name, Category_ID to link it to the corresponding category, Price, and Stock_Quantity, with a unique identifier called Book_ID. The Order table tracks each purchase made by a customer, including the Book_ID of the book purchased, the Branch_ID of the store where the purchase was made, the Customer_ID of the customer who made the purchase, the Order_Date, and the Purchase_Quantity, with a unique identifier called Order_ID. The relationships between these tables are enforced by foreign keys, such as the Category_ID in the Book table referencing the Category_ID in the Category table, and the Book_ID, Branch_ID, and Customer_ID in the Order table referencing the corresponding IDs in the Book, Branch, and Customer tables, ensuring that only valid and consistent data can be entered.
