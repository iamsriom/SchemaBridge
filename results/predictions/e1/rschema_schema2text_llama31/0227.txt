The rschema_0227 database is designed to manage information about countries, products, suppliers, customers, and orders. The Country table stores details about different countries, including a unique identifier, a description, and a name. The Product table contains information about various products, such as their brand, manufacturer, name, price, size, and type. The Region table links countries to their respective regions, with each region having a unique identifier, description, name, and a foreign key referencing the Country table. The Supplier table holds details about suppliers, including their account balance, address, phone number, region, and a unique identifier, with a foreign key referencing the Region table. The Customer table stores customer information, such as their account balance, address, name, market segment, phone number, and region, also with a foreign key referencing the Region table. The Supplier_Supplies_Product table tracks the products supplied by each supplier, with a composite primary key consisting of the product and supplier identifiers, and foreign keys referencing the Product and Supplier tables. The Order table contains details about customer orders, including the customer identifier, handler, date, status, priority, total price, and a foreign key referencing the Customer table. The Order_Composed_of_Products table breaks down each order into individual products, including the discount, extended price, order identifier, product identifier, quantity, receipt date, return indicator, shipment date, shipping instructions, shipping method, status, and tax, with a composite primary key consisting of the order, product, and shipment date identifiers, and foreign keys referencing the Order and Product tables.
