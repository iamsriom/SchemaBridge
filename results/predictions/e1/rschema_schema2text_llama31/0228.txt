The rschema_0228 database is designed to manage customer subscriptions to magazines. The customer table stores information about each individual customer, including their unique customer ID, name, and address. The magazine table contains details about each magazine, such as its name, publisher, theme, publication frequency, and website, with a composite primary key combining the magazine name and publisher to ensure uniqueness. The pricing table stores the various subscription packages available for each magazine, including the package name, period, and price, with a foreign key referencing the magazine table to link each package to its corresponding magazine. The customer_subscribes_to_magazine table tracks which customers have subscribed to which magazines, including the customer's ID, the subscription package ID, the time the subscription was created, and the start date of the subscription, with composite primary keys combining the customer ID, creation time, and pricing ID to ensure uniqueness, and foreign keys referencing the customer and pricing tables to enforce referential integrity.
