The rschema_0229 database is designed to manage customer information and their orders. The Customer_Information table stores details about each customer, including their name, gender, phone number, and address, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. This table also enforces a self-referential integrity constraint, where the Customer_ID is referenced back to itself to ensure data consistency. The Order table contains information about each order, such as the order content, creation time, and a unique identifier, Order_ID, which is the primary key. The Order_ID is also referenced back to itself, but this is likely an error, as it creates a circular reference and does not serve any meaningful purpose. The Customer_ID in the Order table references the Customer_Information table to link each order to its corresponding customer. The Customer_Payment_Order table stores payment details for each order, including the payment amount, method, status, and time, with a composite primary key consisting of the Customer_ID, Order_ID, and Payment_Time. The Customer_ID and Order_ID in this table reference the Customer_Information and Order tables, respectively, to establish relationships between payments and their corresponding customers and orders.
