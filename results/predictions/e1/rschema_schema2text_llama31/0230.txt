The rschema_0230 database is a comprehensive system for managing vehicle maintenance and inventory. It consists of seven tables: vehicle_model, mechanic, customer, maintenance_record, part, order, and order_detail. The vehicle_model table stores information about different vehicle models, with each model identified by a unique vehicle_model_id and including a name. The mechanic table holds details about the mechanics who perform the maintenance tasks, with each mechanic identified by a unique employee_id and including their name and contact information. The customer table contains information about the customers who bring their vehicles in for maintenance, with each customer identified by a unique customer_id and including their name, phone number, and contact address. The maintenance_record table tracks the maintenance tasks performed on each vehicle, with each record identified by a unique record_id and including the associated customer, vehicle model, task status, start and end dates, and the mechanic who performed the task. The part table stores information about the spare parts used in the maintenance tasks, with each part identified by a unique serial number and including its price, stock quantity, and supplier. The order table tracks the orders placed for spare parts, with each order identified by a unique order_id and including the dispatch date and order status. The order_detail table links the orders to the specific parts ordered, with each detail identified by a unique combination of order_id and part_serial_number and including the purchase quantity. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that maintenance records are linked to valid customers, mechanics, and vehicle models, and that orders and order details are linked to valid orders and parts.
