The rschema_0233 database is designed to manage customer information and their interactions with products. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their birthday, creation and deletion times, email, first and last names, and update time. The Product table contains information about each product, such as its code, creation and deletion times, description, name, price, and update time. The Address table links customers to their addresses, storing address lines, cities, countries, creation and deletion times, customer IDs, postal codes, states, and types, with a foreign key referencing the Customer table to ensure that each address belongs to a valid customer. The Order table tracks customer orders, storing creation and deletion times, customer IDs, and update times, with a foreign key referencing the Customer table to link each order to its corresponding customer. The Order_and_Product table connects orders to the products they contain, storing order and product IDs, quantities, and serving as a composite primary key to uniquely identify each product in an order, with foreign keys referencing both the Order and Product tables to ensure data consistency.
