The rschema_0235 database is designed to manage user interactions with products and their associated transactions. At its core, the database contains information about users, who are identified by a unique User_ID and can be reached through their Email, Phone_Number, and Username, with their registration and update times recorded. Each user can interact with products, which are cataloged in the Product table, where each product has a unique Product_ID and is associated with a name, description, price, and creation and update times. The Receipt table stores information about completed transactions, including a unique Receipt_ID and a hash value for verification. Users can purchase and subscribe to products, which is tracked in the User_Subscribed_Products table, where each subscription is tied to a specific product and user, with start and end dates, and a subscription status. Users can also refund products, which is recorded in the User_Refunded_Products table, where each refund is tied to a specific product and user, with a refund amount, date, and status. Finally, the User_Purchased_Products table tracks all purchases made by users, including the product ID, purchase price, receipt ID, and subscription status, with each purchase tied to a specific user and product.
