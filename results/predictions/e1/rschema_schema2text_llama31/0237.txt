The rschema_0237 database is designed to manage information about users, houses, and their associated properties. The User table stores details about individuals, including their email, password, unique user ID, and username. Each user is uniquely identified by their User_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Area table contains information about geographic regions, with each area having a unique Area_ID and name. The House_Type table lists different types of houses, with each type identified by a unique Type_ID and name. The Street table stores details about streets, including the street name and the area it belongs to, with the Belongs_to_Area_ID referencing the Area table. The House table contains information about individual houses, including their description, price, type, and the street they are located on, with the Belongs_to_Street_ID referencing the Street table and the Type_ID referencing the House_Type table. The Lease table stores details about leases, including the description, house ID, lease date, and price, with the primary key being a composite of the User_ID, House_ID, and Lease_Date. The User_ID and House_ID in the Lease table reference the User and House tables, respectively, ensuring that a user can only lease a house they have not already leased.
