The rschema_0239 database is designed to manage user interactions with articles and comments. The User table contains information about each registered user, including their contact details, a unique identifier, their username, and the permissions they have been granted. The User_ID field serves as the primary key, ensuring that each user is uniquely identified. The Article table stores details about published articles, including the number of likes and reads they have received, the time of publication, the user who published the article, and its current status. The Article_ID field is the primary key, and the Publishing_User field references the User_ID in the User table, establishing a relationship between the article and its author. The Comment table records user comments, with a unique identifier, the time of publication, the user who made the comment, and the comment's status. The Comment_ID field is the primary key, and the Publishing_User field references the User_ID in the User table, linking each comment to its author.
