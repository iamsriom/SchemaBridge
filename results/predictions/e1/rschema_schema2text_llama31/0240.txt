The rschema_0240 database is designed to manage information about ships, their crews, and their missions. At the core of this database is the Fleet table, which stores details about each fleet, including its name, base location, and commander's ID, which references a specific crew member. Each fleet is associated with a unique ID, which is also the primary key for this table. The Ship table stores information about individual ships, such as their construction date, name, service status, type, and ID, which is the primary key for this table. The Ship table also references the Fleet table through a foreign key, indicating which fleet each ship belongs to. The Crew_Member table holds details about each crew member, including their ID, name, gender, length of service, phone number, and position, with a foreign key referencing the Fleet table to indicate which fleet they belong to. The Fleet_Mission_Execution table tracks the execution of missions by crew members, including the crew member's ID, execution time, fleet ID, and whether they were the primary responsible crew member, with a composite primary key consisting of the crew member's ID, fleet ID, and execution time. This table also references both the Crew_Member and Fleet tables through foreign keys. The Ship_Supply table stores information about the supply of items to ships, including the responsible supply crew member's ID, ship ID, supply items, quantity, and supply time, with a composite primary key consisting of the responsible supply crew member's ID, ship ID, and supply time, and foreign keys referencing both the Crew_Member and Ship tables.
