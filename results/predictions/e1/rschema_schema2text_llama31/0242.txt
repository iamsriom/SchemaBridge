The rschema_0242 database is designed to manage user accounts and orders within a business. It consists of four tables: Employee, Member, Administrator, and Order. The Employee table stores information about employees, including a unique identifier, password, and username. The Member table contains details about customers, such as their balance, birth month and year, gender, unique member ID, password, phone number, and username. The Administrator table holds information about administrators, including a unique identifier, password, and username. The Order table represents transactions between employees and customers, with each order having a unique identifier, a timestamp, and referencing the employee and member involved in the transaction through their respective IDs. The Employee_ID and Member_ID in the Order table are not nullable, indicating that every order must be associated with an employee and a member. The Employee_ID in the Order table references the Employee table's Employee_ID, and the Member_ID references the Member table's Member_ID, establishing relationships between the tables and ensuring data consistency.
