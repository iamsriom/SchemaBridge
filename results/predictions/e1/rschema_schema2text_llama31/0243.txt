The rschema_0243 database is designed to manage a library's collection of books and their borrowing history. The Reader table stores information about each library patron, including their email address, unique ID, name, phone number, registration date, and the last time their information was updated. The Book table contains details about each book in the library's collection, such as the author, ISBN number, publication date, stock level, title, type, and the last time the book's information was updated. The Borrowing table tracks the borrowing history of each book, including the date it was borrowed, any overdue fines, the reader's ID, the number of times the book has been renewed, and the date it was returned. The Borrowing table is a composite primary key, meaning that each borrowing is uniquely identified by a combination of the reader's ID, the book's ID, and the borrowing date. The Borrowing table also includes foreign keys referencing the Reader and Book tables, indicating which reader borrowed which book, and these relationships are enforced with cascade update and delete rules, meaning that if a reader's ID or a book's ID is deleted, all associated borrowing records will also be deleted.
