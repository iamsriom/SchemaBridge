The rschema_0251 database is designed to manage information about pet grooming services. The Groomer table stores details about individual groomers, including their unique identifier, name, rating, specialization, and years of experience. The Pet table contains information about the pets being groomed, such as their age, breed, fur characteristics, and weight. The Grooming_service table tracks the services provided to each pet, including the customer's rating of the service, the groomer's identifier, the pet's identifier, the price of the service, the items included in the service, the market in which the service was provided, and the time taken for the service. The Groomer_ID and Pet_ID columns in the Grooming_service table are foreign keys referencing the Groomer and Pet tables respectively, ensuring that each service is linked to a valid groomer and pet. The Grooming_service table has a composite primary key consisting of the Groomer_ID, Pet_ID, and Service_time, meaning each service is uniquely identified by these three factors. The Groomer_ID column in the Grooming_service table is not nullable, indicating that every service must be associated with a groomer. The Pet_ID column in the Grooming_service table is also not nullable, meaning every service must be associated with a pet. The Groomer_ID and Pet_ID columns in the Grooming_service table have update and delete rules set to CASCADE, meaning if a groomer or pet is deleted, all services associated with them will also be deleted.
