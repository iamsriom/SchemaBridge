The rschema_0256 database is designed to manage information about shopping malls, their departments, and the employees working within those departments. At the core of the database is the Mall table, which stores details about each mall, including its address, contact number, unique identifier, and name. The Department table is linked to the Mall table, as each department is located within a specific mall and is managed by a designated employee. This table also includes the department's name, location, and the employee ID of its manager. The Employee table stores information about each employee, including their department ID, unique identifier, name, phone number, position, and salary. Notably, the Employee table has a composite primary key consisting of the employee's ID and department ID, indicating that an employee can only belong to one department. The Department table references the Employee table through the manager's employee ID, and the Employee table references the Department table through the department ID, establishing relationships between employees and their departments.
