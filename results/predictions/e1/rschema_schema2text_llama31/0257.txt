The rschema_0257 database is designed to manage information about customers, flights, and their bookings. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their contact information, gender, name, and ID number, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Flight table contains information about each flight, such as its name, departure and arrival times, and destination, with Flight_ID being the primary key. The Flight_Booking_Record table links customers to the flights they have booked, referencing the Flight_ID from the Flight table, and includes details like the booking record ID, customer ID, discount rate, and ticket order number, with Booking_Record_ID serving as the primary key. The Order table represents the actual orders made by customers, referencing both the Customer_ID from the Customer table and the Flight_ID from the Flight table, and includes details like the discount rate and ticket order number, with Order_ID being the primary key. The Customer_ID in the Order table is not nullable, indicating that a customer must be associated with each order, and the Order_Flight_ID is also not nullable, meaning each order must be linked to a specific flight.
