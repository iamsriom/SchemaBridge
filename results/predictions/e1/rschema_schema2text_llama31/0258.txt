The rschema_0258 database is designed to manage information about donors, fundraising events, and the donations received during these events. The Donor table stores details about each individual who donates, including their name, date of birth, gender, identity card number, phone number, address, and type of donor. The Donor_ID field, a unique integer identifier, serves as the primary key for this table. The Fundraising_Event table contains information about each fundraising event, such as its name, start and end times, address, and current and total amounts raised. The Event_ID field, another unique integer identifier, is the primary key for this table. The Donation_Record table tracks the donations made during each event, including the amount donated, the method of donation, and a message from the donor. This table also includes foreign keys referencing the Donor_ID and Event_ID fields in the Donor and Fundraising_Event tables, respectively, to establish relationships between donations and the individuals who made them and the events they were made at. The Donation_ID field, a composite key consisting of the Donor_ID, Event_ID, and Donation_Time, uniquely identifies each donation.
