The rschema_0260 database is designed to manage student information and their academic records. It consists of four main tables: Student, Course, Degree_Examination_Result, and Registration. The Student table stores information about each student, including their date of birth, home address, name, phone number, registration eligibility, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Course table contains details about each course, such as its location, time, name, credits, and instructor, with the course ID being the primary key. The Degree_Examination_Result table holds records of students' examination results, including the examination date, name, grade, and a unique result ID, which is the primary key. The Student ID in this table is a foreign key referencing the Student table, indicating the student who took the examination. The Registration table represents the enrollment of students in courses, with columns for the course ID, registration time, and status, as well as a composite primary key consisting of the student ID and course ID. The Course ID and Student ID in this table are foreign keys referencing the Course and Student tables, respectively, with the Student ID also referencing the Student table, establishing relationships between students, courses, and their registrations.
