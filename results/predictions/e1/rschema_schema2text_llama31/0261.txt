The rschema_0261 database is designed to store information about geographic locations and voter registration. It consists of five tables: Address, City, District, Voter, and Town. The Address table holds details about individual addresses, with a unique identifier (Address_ID), a detailed address description, and an address type. Each city is represented in the City table, with a unique identifier (City_ID), a name, and a region. The District table contains information about districts within cities, including a city identifier (City_ID), a district identifier (District_ID), a name, and a type, with a foreign key referencing the City table to establish the relationship. The Voter table stores voter registration information, including an address identifier (Address_ID), a name, a registration district identifier (Registration_district_ID), a registration status, and a unique voter identifier (Voter_ID), with foreign keys referencing the Address and District tables to link the voter to their address and registration district. Finally, the Town table lists towns within districts, with a district identifier (District_ID), a name, a region, and a unique town identifier (Town_ID), and a foreign key referencing the District table to establish the relationship between the town and district.
