The rschema_0262 database is used to manage information about companies, products, salespeople, supermarkets, departments, and employees. The Company table stores details about each company, including its address, name, and telephone number, with a unique identifier called Company_ID. The Product table contains information about each product, such as its model, name, and unit price, with a unique identifier called Product_ID. The Salesperson table holds details about each salesperson, including their name and a unique identifier called Salesperson_ID. A Supermarket table stores information about each supermarket, including its address, manager, and telephone number, with a unique identifier called Supermarket_ID, and it is linked to the Company table through the Company_ID foreign key, which means that each supermarket is associated with a specific company. The Allocation_Record table tracks the allocation of products to salespeople, with a unique identifier called Allocation_ID, and it includes the quantity allocated, the time of allocation, the Product_ID, and the Salesperson_ID, which are foreign keys referencing the Product and Salesperson tables, respectively, and these fields cannot be null. The Department table stores information about each department, including its contact telephone number, name, manager, and a unique identifier called Department_ID, and it is linked to the Supermarket table through the Supermarket_ID foreign key, which means that each department is associated with a specific supermarket. The Product_Inventory table tracks the quantity of each product in each supermarket, with a unique identifier called Inventory_ID, and it includes the Product_ID and Supermarket_ID, which are foreign keys referencing the Product and Supermarket tables, respectively, and these fields cannot be null. The Employee table stores information about each employee, including their contact information, department, name, position, and salary, with a unique identifier called Employee_ID, and it is linked to the Department table through the Department_ID foreign key, which means that each employee is associated with a specific department.
