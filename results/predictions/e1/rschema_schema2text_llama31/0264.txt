The rschema_0264 database is designed to manage the operations of a hotel, encompassing various aspects of its management, including departments, employees, positions, rooms, customers, bookings, and arrangements. The database consists of seven tables: department, employee, position, room, customer, booking, and arrangement. The department table stores information about the different departments within the hotel, including their unique IDs, names, manager IDs, and phone numbers, with the manager ID referencing the employee table to establish a hierarchical structure. The employee table contains details about each staff member, such as their unique IDs, names, position IDs, department IDs, phone numbers, and salaries, with the position ID referencing the position table to define their roles and the department ID referencing the department table to determine their departmental affiliation. The position table stores information about the various positions within the hotel, including their unique IDs, names, and authorities. The room table contains details about each room, including its unique ID, type, standard charge, and check-in status. The customer table stores information about each customer, including their unique ID, company name, contact person, contact number, and contact address. The booking table records each booking made by a customer, including the booking ID, customer ID, check-in time, number of days stayed, room type, and number of rooms, with the customer ID referencing the customer table to establish the customer's identity. The arrangement table links bookings to specific rooms and employees, including the booking ID, room ID, and employee ID, with the booking ID referencing the booking table, the room ID referencing the room table, and the employee ID referencing the employee table to manage the allocation of rooms and staff to bookings.
