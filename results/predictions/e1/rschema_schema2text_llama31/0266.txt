The rschema_0266 database is designed to track and record maintenance activities for pipelines. The Pipeline table stores information about each pipeline, including its diameter, the starting and ending points, and a unique identifier for each pipeline. The Maintenance_Personnel table contains details about the individuals responsible for maintenance, with a unique identifier for each person and their name and phone number. The Maintenance_Activity_Record table logs maintenance activities, recording the maintenance details, the personnel involved, the time of maintenance, and the pipeline being worked on. The Maintenance_Activity_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of the pipeline identifier, the maintenance personnel identifier, and the maintenance time, ensuring that each maintenance activity is uniquely identified. The Maintenance_Personnel_ID and Pipeline_ID columns in the Maintenance_Activity_Record table are not nullable, indicating that every maintenance activity must be associated with a specific personnel and pipeline. The Maintenance_Personnel_ID column in Maintenance_Activity_Record references the Maintenance_Personnel table, and the Pipeline_ID column references the Pipeline table, enforcing referential integrity by ensuring that only valid personnel and pipelines are associated with maintenance activities.
