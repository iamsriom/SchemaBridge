The rschema_0267 database is designed to manage room reservations for a hotel. It consists of three tables: room_table, customer_table, and reservation_record_table. The customer_table holds details about each customer, such as their unique customer ID, name, and phone number, with the customer ID being the primary key. The reservation_record_table tracks all room reservations, containing the customer's ID, the room number, the date of the reservation, the check-in and check-out dates, and the reservation status. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the customer ID, reservation date, and room number, ensuring each reservation is unique.
