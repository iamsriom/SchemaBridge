The rschema_0268 database is designed to manage information about restaurants, their dishes, customers, and customer reservations. At its core, the database contains a table for Restaurants, which stores details such as the location, name, operating hours, and a unique identifier for each restaurant. The Dish table holds information about the various dishes offered by restaurants, including their category, a unique identifier, name, and price. The Customer table stores customer data, including their name and phone number, with a unique identifier for each customer. The Restaurant_Dish table serves as a bridge between restaurants and dishes, linking each dish to the restaurants that offer it, with a composite primary key consisting of the restaurant and dish identifiers. The Customer_Reservation table tracks customer reservations, including the number of diners, reservation time, and a unique identifier, and links each reservation to the customer and the restaurant where the reservation was made. Each restaurant can have multiple dishes, and each dish can be offered by multiple restaurants, while each customer can make multiple reservations, and each reservation is associated with one restaurant.
