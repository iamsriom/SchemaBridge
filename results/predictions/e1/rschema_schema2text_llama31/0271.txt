The rschema_0271 database is designed to manage information about services and their subscriptions to clients. The Service table stores details about the services offered, including their price, unique service ID, name, transmission rate, and type. Each service is uniquely identified by its Service_ID, which is an integer and the primary key of the table. The Client table contains information about the individuals or organizations that subscribe to these services, including their address, email, phone number, and a unique client ID, also an integer and the primary key of the table. The Service_Subscription table links services to their respective clients, storing the client's ID, the operator providing the service, the service ID, and the start and end times of the subscription. The Client_ID and Service_ID in the Service_Subscription table are both foreign keys referencing the Client and Service tables respectively, ensuring that a client can only subscribe to a service that exists in the database, and a service can only be subscribed to by a client that exists in the database. The primary key of the Service_Subscription table is a composite key consisting of the Service_ID, Client_ID, and Subscription_Start_Time, meaning that a client can only subscribe to a service at a single point in time.
