The rschema_0272 database is designed to track and manage information about ranches, their livestock, and the various activities and events related to these animals. At the core of this database is the Ranch table, which stores details about each ranch, including its name and address, and assigns a unique identifier, Ranch_ID, to each one. The Livestock table is linked to the Ranch table through the Ranch_ID, indicating which ranch each animal belongs to, and contains information such as the animal's name, species, and a unique identifier, Livestock_ID. The Health_Status, Vaccination_Status, Feed_Consumption, and Entry/Exit_Records tables all reference the Livestock table through the Livestock_ID, allowing the database to track the health status, vaccination history, feed consumption, and entry and exit records of each animal. The Health_Status table records the health status of each animal at a specific time, with the combination of Livestock_ID and Recording_Time serving as the primary key. Similarly, the Vaccination_Status table tracks the vaccination measures administered to each animal at a specific time, while the Feed_Consumption table logs the amount of feed consumed by each animal at a specific time, and the Entry/Exit_Records table records the entry and exit times of each animal. Each of these tables has a composite primary key consisting of the Livestock_ID and the relevant time, ensuring that each record is unique and linked to a specific animal and time.
