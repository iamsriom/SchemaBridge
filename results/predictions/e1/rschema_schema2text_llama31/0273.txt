The rschema_0273 database is designed to store customer information and their feedback. The Customer table holds details about each customer, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, which is a primary key that cannot be null, and is used to uniquely identify each customer. The Name and Phone_Number columns store the customer's name and phone number respectively, both of which can be up to 255 characters long. The Feedback table stores feedback provided by customers, with a unique identifier, Feedback_ID, which is the primary key and cannot be null. The Customer_ID column in the Feedback table references the Customer_ID in the Customer table, establishing a relationship between a customer and their feedback, and is not nullable. The Feedback_Content column stores the actual feedback provided by the customer, which can be up to 255 characters long, and the Feedback_Time column records the date and time when the feedback was given. The Processing_Status column tracks the status of the feedback, which can also be up to 255 characters long.
