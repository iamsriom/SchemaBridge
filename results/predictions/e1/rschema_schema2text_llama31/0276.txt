The rschema_0276 database is designed to manage information related to maintenance services provided to customers. It consists of five tables: maintenance_technician, maintenance_team, customer, maintenance_task, and maintenance_feedback. The maintenance_technician table stores details about the technicians, including their unique identifier, name, gender, phone number, position, employment date, and the team they belong to, with the team_id referencing the customer table. The maintenance_team table contains information about the teams, such as the team's unique identifier, name, leader's identifier, responsibilities, and total number of members, with the team_leader_id referencing the maintenance_technician table. The customer table holds details about the customers, including their unique identifier, name, phone number, and address. The maintenance_task table stores records of maintenance tasks, including the task's unique identifier, customer identifier, maintenance cost, duration, result, time, and the technician who performed the task, with the customer_id referencing the customer table and the maintenance_technician referencing the maintenance_technician table. The maintenance_feedback table contains feedback from customers regarding the maintenance tasks, including the task's unique identifier, feedback content, resolution result, and customer satisfaction, with the maintenance_task_number referencing the maintenance_task table. The database uses InnoDB as its storage engine and UTF-8mb3 as its character set.
