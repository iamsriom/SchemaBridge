The rschema_0277 database is designed to manage information about services provided to customers, the staff members who perform these services, and the teams they belong to. The Service_team table contains details about the different teams, with each team identified by a unique Team_ID and having a corresponding Team_name. The Customer table stores information about the individuals or businesses that receive these services, including their Customer_ID, Customer_name, and Customer_phone. The Service_evaluation table tracks the effectiveness of the services provided, with each evaluation assigned a unique Evaluation_ID and rated on a scale of service effectiveness and a service fee. The Housekeeper table holds data about the staff members who perform these services, including their contact information, professional skills, service type, years of experience, and the team they belong to, which is linked to the Service_team table through the Team_ID. The Service_task table records the details of each service task, including the evaluation it belongs to, service address, date, duration, and type, while the Staff_task and Customer_task tables link staff members and customers to specific tasks, respectively, through their IDs and task IDs, ensuring that each task is assigned to one staff member and one customer.
