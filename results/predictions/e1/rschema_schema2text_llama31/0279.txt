The rschema_0279 database is designed to store information about properties and analysis reports related to these properties. The Property_Table is the core table in this database, containing details about each property, such as its address, area, and sale date, along with its unique identifier, Property_ID, which serves as the primary key. This table also includes the sale price of each property, recorded as a floating-point number. The Analysis_Report_Table is used to store the results of analysis reports related to these properties, with each report having a unique identifier, Report_ID, serving as its primary key. This table also includes the date the report was generated, the type of report, and the property to which the report pertains, which is identified by the Property_ID foreign key referencing the Property_ID in the Property_Table. The Property_ID in the Analysis_Report_Table is not allowed to be null, ensuring that every report is associated with a specific property.
