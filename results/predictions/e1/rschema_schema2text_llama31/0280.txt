The rschema_0280 database is designed to manage information about movie projects, including their budgets, filming times, and types. The Movie_Project table stores this information, with each project assigned a unique identifier, a name, and a type, along with its budget and filming time. The Director, Actor, and Producer tables store information about the individuals involved in the projects, including their names, email addresses, and phone numbers. The Director, Actor, and Producer tables each have a unique identifier for each individual, which is used as the primary key to ensure that each person is uniquely identified. The Director_Participate_Project, Actor_Participate_Project, and Producer_Participate_Project tables store the relationships between these individuals and the projects they are involved in, with each table containing the unique identifier of the individual and the project they are associated with. These relationships are established through foreign keys that reference the primary keys in the Director, Actor, and Producer tables, and the primary keys in the Movie_Project table. The primary keys in the Director_Participate_Project, Actor_Participate_Project, and Producer_Participate_Project tables are composite keys, combining the unique identifiers of the individual and the project to ensure that each person can only be associated with a project once. The database also includes constraints to ensure data integrity, such as not allowing null values in the Director_ID, Actor_ID, and Producer_ID columns in the Director_Participate_Project, Actor_Participate_Project, and Producer_Participate_Project tables, and enforcing referential integrity by restricting the deletion of projects or individuals that are associated with other projects or individuals.
