The rschema_0281 database is designed to manage information about exhibitions, curators, and exhibits. The Exhibition table stores details about each exhibition, including its name, start and end times, and location, with a unique identifier for each exhibition. The Curator table contains information about the individuals responsible for managing exhibitions, such as their name, email, and phone number, with a unique identifier for each curator. The Exhibit table holds details about the individual exhibits, including their name, description, image path, and source, with a unique identifier for each exhibit. The Exhibition_Management table links curators to exhibitions, indicating which curator is responsible for which exhibition, with a composite primary key combining the curator and exhibition identifiers. The Exhibit_Association table also links exhibits to exhibitions, but in a many-to-many relationship, allowing multiple exhibits to be associated with a single exhibition, and vice versa, with a composite primary key combining the exhibit and exhibition identifiers. The relationships between these tables are further defined by foreign keys, which ensure that a curator can only be associated with an exhibition they exist in the Curators table, and an exhibit can only be associated with an exhibition it exists in the Exhibitions table, and that these associations are updated and deleted in accordance with the rules specified in the foreign key constraints.
