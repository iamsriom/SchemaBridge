The rschema_0282 database is designed to store information about books, their authors, and editors. The Book table holds details about each book, including its title, category, ISBN number, and publication date. The ISBN number serves as the primary key for this table, meaning each book must have a unique identifier. The Author table contains information about the individuals who wrote the books, with fields for their address, email, name, and a unique identifier known as Author_ID, which is the primary key for this table. The Editor table stores details about the individuals who edited the books, including their email, name, and office location, with Editor_ID serving as the primary key. The Book_author table establishes relationships between books and their authors, linking each book to its corresponding author through the ISBN and Author_ID fields, with both fields being non-null to ensure a book can only be associated with one author and an author can only write one book. The Book_editor table does the same for editors and books, also linking each book to its corresponding editor through the ISBN and Editor_ID fields, with both fields being non-null to prevent a book from being associated with multiple editors or an editor from editing multiple books.
