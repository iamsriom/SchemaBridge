The rschema_0283 database is designed to store information about books, their authors, and editors. The database contains three tables: Book, Author, and Editor. The Book table holds details about each book, including its unique identifier (BookID), the ISBN number, category, title, publication date, and the IDs of the author and editor involved. The Author table stores information about each author, such as their unique identifier (AuthorID), email address, name, and phone number. The Editor table contains details about each editor, including their unique identifier (EditorID), email address, name, and phone number. The Book table's primary key is the BookID, and it also references the AuthorID and EditorID from the Author and Editor tables, respectively, to establish relationships between books and their creators. The Author and Editor tables have their own unique identifiers as primary keys. The AuthorID in the Book table is a foreign key referencing the Author table, and it has an ON DELETE RESTRICT rule to prevent the deletion of an author if they have associated books. The EditorID in the Book table is also a foreign key referencing the Editor table, with an ON DELETE RESTRICT rule for the same reason. There are no indexes or additional constraints specified in the schema.
