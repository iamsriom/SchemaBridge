The rschema_0285 database is designed to manage information about advertisements and their interactions with clients. The Advertisement table stores details about each advertisement, including its content, duration, and name, with a unique identifier for each advertisement. The Client table holds information about each client, such as their email, name, and phone number, with a unique identifier for each client. The Play_Advertisement table tracks the playing of advertisements by clients, including the start and end times, duration, and viewership rating, with a composite primary key combining the advertisement and client identifiers to ensure each client can only play each advertisement once. The Advertisement_ID in the Play_Advertisement table references the Advertisement table to link the playing of an advertisement to its details, and the Client_ID references the Client table to link the playing of an advertisement to the client who played it. The Advertisement_ID in the Play_Advertisement table is not nullable, and neither is the Client_ID, ensuring that every advertisement played by a client is recorded.
