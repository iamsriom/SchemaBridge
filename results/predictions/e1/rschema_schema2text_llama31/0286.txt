The rschema_0286 database is designed to manage information about venues, actors, and their performances. The Venue table stores details about each venue, including its address, name, seating capacity, and a unique identifier, Venue_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Actor table contains information about individual actors, such as their email, name, and phone number, with Actor_ID being the primary key. The Performance table holds details about each performance, including the start and end times, a unique identifier, Performance_ID, and the Venue_ID it takes place in, which is a foreign key referencing the Venue table and is required to be non-null. The Actor_Participation_in_Performance table tracks the actors involved in each performance, with a composite primary key consisting of the Performance_ID and Actor_ID, and foreign keys referencing the Actor and Performance tables, ensuring that an actor can only participate in a performance that exists and vice versa, with the Actor_ID and Performance_ID being non-null to prevent orphaned records.
