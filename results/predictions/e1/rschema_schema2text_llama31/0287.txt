The rschema_0287 database is designed to manage information about parks and their ecological records, as well as the plants that grow within them. The Park table stores details about each park, including its area, location, name, and the person responsible for its maintenance. Each park is uniquely identified by its Park_ID, which is a unique integer that serves as the primary key. The Park_Maintenance table tracks maintenance activities for each park, including the content of the maintenance, the cost, the personnel involved, and the time of the maintenance, with the Park_ID referencing the Park table to link the maintenance to the relevant park. The Park_Ecological_Record table stores ecological records for each park, with a unique Record_Number serving as the primary key, and the Park_ID referencing the Park table to associate the record with the relevant park. The Plant table contains information about the different types of plants, including their category and name, with each plant identified by a unique Plant_ID. The Plant_Growth_Record table tracks the growth status and quantity of each plant, with the Plant_ID referencing the Plant table to identify the plant, the Record_Number referencing the Park_Ecological_Record table to link the growth record to the relevant ecological record, and the Record_Number and Plant_ID forming a composite primary key to uniquely identify each growth record.
