The rschema_0288 database is designed to manage data related to monitoring and maintenance activities at various sites. The database consists of five tables: monitoring_site, monitoring_personnel, maintenance_personnel, site_monitoring_data, and site_maintenance_data. The monitoring_site table stores information about each site, including its unique site ID, name, location, and the person responsible for it. The monitoring_personnel and maintenance_personnel tables store details about the individuals involved in monitoring and maintenance activities, respectively, with each person having a unique ID, name, contact information, and scope of work. The site_monitoring_data table contains records of monitoring activities at each site, including the site ID, the personnel ID of the person conducting the monitoring, the time of the record, air quality, water quality, noise level, and the result of the monitoring. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the site ID, record time, and monitoring personnel ID, and it references the monitoring_personnel and monitoring_site tables with ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE rules, meaning that if a monitoring personnel or site is deleted, all associated monitoring records will also be deleted. The site_maintenance_data table stores information about maintenance activities at each site, including the site ID, the personnel ID of the person conducting the maintenance, the time of the maintenance, the content of the maintenance, and the cost of the maintenance. This table also has a composite primary key consisting of the site ID, maintenance personnel ID, and maintenance time, and it references the maintenance_personnel and monitoring_site tables with ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE rules, ensuring that if a maintenance personnel or site is deleted, all associated maintenance records will also be deleted.
