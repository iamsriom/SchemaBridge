The rschema_0291 database is designed to manage information about reagents used in a laboratory setting. The Reagent table stores details about each type of reagent, including its name, specifications, inventory quantity, and expiration date. The Laboratory table contains information about the laboratories where these reagents are used, including their names, locations, and the personnel in charge. The Personnel table holds data about the individuals working in these laboratories, such as their names, ages, genders, and affiliated laboratory IDs. The Reagent_Usage_Record table tracks the usage of reagents by recording the duration of usage, the quantity used, and the start time of each usage event, along with the personnel ID of the person who used the reagent. The Reagent table has a primary key of Reagent_ID, while the Laboratory and Personnel tables have a primary key of Laboratory_ID and Personnel_ID, respectively. The Reagent_Usage_Record table has a composite primary key consisting of Reagent_ID, Usage_Personnel_ID, and Usage_Start_Time, ensuring that each usage event is uniquely identified. The Reagent_ID in the Reagent_Usage_Record table references the Reagent_ID in the Reagent table, and the Usage_Personnel_ID references the Personnel_ID in the Personnel table, establishing relationships between reagent usage and the personnel involved. The Affiliated_Laboratory_ID in the Personnel table references the Laboratory_ID in the Laboratory table, linking personnel to their affiliated laboratories.
