The rschema_0292 database is designed to manage information about facilities and their maintenance activities. The Facility_Table stores details about each facility, including a unique facility ID, its location, and its name. The Maintenance_Personnel_Table contains information about the individuals responsible for maintenance, with a unique personnel ID, their name, and a contact number. The Facility_Maintenance_Table links facilities with their maintenance activities, listing the facility ID, a description of the maintenance work, the cost involved, the time it was performed, and the personnel ID of the individual who carried out the work. The facility ID in this table is not nullable, ensuring that each maintenance activity is associated with a specific facility. The maintenance personnel ID is also not nullable, meaning that each maintenance activity must be attributed to a specific individual. The primary key for the Facility_Maintenance_Table is a composite key consisting of the maintenance personnel ID, facility ID, and maintenance time, which uniquely identifies each maintenance activity. The facility ID in the Facility_Maintenance_Table references the facility ID in the Facility_Table, establishing a relationship between the two tables, and the maintenance personnel ID references the personnel ID in the Maintenance_Personnel_Table, linking the maintenance activity to the individual responsible.
