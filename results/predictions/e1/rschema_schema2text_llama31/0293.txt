The rschema_0293 database is designed to manage information about mines and their maintenance activities. The Mine_Table stores details about individual mines, including their location, a unique mine ID, name, and the person in charge. The Maintenance_Personnel_Table contains information about the personnel responsible for maintenance, with a unique ID and name. The Equipment_Table holds details about the equipment used in the mines, including a unique equipment ID, the mine it belongs to, model, name, and the date it was purchased. The mine ID in the Equipment_Table is a foreign key referencing the mine ID in the Mine_Table, establishing a relationship between the equipment and the mine it belongs to. The Maintenance_Record_Table stores records of maintenance activities, including the equipment ID, a description of the maintenance, the cost, the date of the maintenance, and the ID of the maintenance personnel involved. The equipment ID and maintenance date in the Maintenance_Record_Table form a composite primary key, ensuring each maintenance record is unique to a specific piece of equipment on a specific date. The equipment ID in the Maintenance_Record_Table references the equipment ID in the Equipment_Table, and the maintenance personnel ID references the maintenance personnel ID in the Maintenance_Personnel_Table, establishing relationships between maintenance records and the equipment and personnel involved.
