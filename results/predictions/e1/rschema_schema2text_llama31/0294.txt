The rschema_0294 database is designed to manage information about banking operations, specifically focusing on branch offices, customers, their accounts, and transaction records. The Branch Office table stores details about each branch, including its address and name, with a unique identifier called Branch Office ID that serves as the primary key. The Customer table contains information about each customer, such as their contact phone number, email address, ID number, and name, with a unique identifier called Customer ID that serves as the primary key. The Account table links customers to their respective branch offices and accounts, storing the account number, type, balance, opening date, and branch office ID, which is a foreign key referencing the Branch Office table, ensuring that each account is associated with a valid branch office. The Customer ID in the Account table is also a foreign key referencing the Customer table, establishing the relationship between customers and their accounts. The Transaction Record table tracks all transactions made on accounts, including the account number, transaction amount, location, time, and type, with the account number serving as a foreign key referencing the Account table to ensure that each transaction is linked to a valid account.
