The rschema_0296 database is designed to manage the relationships between financial advisors, clients, and financial products. The Financial_Advisor table stores information about each advisor, including their unique identifier, contact number, hire date, and name. The Client table contains details about each client, such as their identifier, contact number, ID number, name, risk preference, and total assets. The Financial_Product table lists the available financial products, with each product having a unique code, name, type, and value. The Client_Inquiry_Advisor table tracks interactions between advisors and clients, recording the advisor's ID, client's ID, inquiry content, and time of inquiry, with a composite primary key ensuring each interaction is unique. The Client_Holds_Financial_Products table stores information about the financial products held by each client, including the client's ID, product code, and quantity held, with a composite primary key ensuring each client-product combination is unique. The Client_Transactions_Products table records all transactions involving financial products, including the client's ID, product code, transaction price, quantity, time, and type, with a composite primary key ensuring each transaction is unique. Foreign keys in the Client_Inquiry_Advisor, Client_Holds_Financial_Products, and Client_Transactions_Products tables reference the Financial_Advisor and Client tables to establish relationships between advisors, clients, and their interactions, while the Client_Holds_Financial_Products and Client_Transactions_Products tables also reference the Financial_Product table to link clients with the products they hold or transact with.
