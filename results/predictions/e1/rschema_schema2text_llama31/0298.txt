The rschema_0298 database is designed to manage employee information and department details within an organization. The Salary_Level table stores the minimum and maximum salaries for different levels within the company, with each level uniquely identified by a Salary_Level_ID. The Department table contains information about the various departments, including their locations and names, and each department is uniquely identified by a Department_ID. The Employee table holds details about each employee, such as their name, phone number, position, and department, which is linked to the Department table through the Department_ID. The Employee table also includes a Salary_Level_ID to indicate the employee's salary level, and a Supervisor_ID to identify the employee's supervisor, which is another employee in the same organization. The Department_ID, Salary_Level_ID, and Supervisor_ID in the Employee table are all non-nullable, meaning they must be provided for each employee, and they reference the corresponding IDs in the Department, Salary_Level, and Employee tables, respectively, ensuring data consistency and preventing invalid references. The Department_ID in the Employee table has a foreign key constraint that references the Department_ID in the Department table, and the Salary_Level_ID and Supervisor_ID have similar constraints referencing the Salary_Level_ID and Employee_ID in their respective tables, with the Supervisor_ID also having an update and delete rule of CASCADE, meaning if the supervisor's employee record is deleted, the supervisor's ID in the Employee table will also be deleted.
