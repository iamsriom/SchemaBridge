The rschema_0299 database is designed to store information about cities and their weather forecasts. The City table contains details about each city, including a unique identifier, city name, country, latitude and longitude coordinates, population, and timezone. The Weather_Description table stores descriptions of different types of weather, with each description having a unique identifier, a detailed description, an icon, and a main description. The Weather_Forecast table links cities to their corresponding weather forecasts, which include the afternoon, morning, and noon temperatures, atmospheric pressure, cloud cover, humidity, precipitation probability, sunrise and sunset times, wind direction and speed, and the date of the forecast. The city_ID in the Weather_Forecast table references the unique identifier in the City table, establishing a relationship between cities and their forecasts, and the weather_type_ID references the unique identifier in the Weather_Description table, linking each forecast to a specific type of weather. The city_ID in the Weather_Forecast table is not allowed to be null, ensuring that every forecast is associated with a city, and the weather_type_ID is also not allowed to be null, ensuring that every forecast is associated with a specific type of weather.
