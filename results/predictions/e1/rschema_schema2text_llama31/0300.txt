The rschema_0300 database is designed to manage and track information about materials and projects, as well as the bills and bill details associated with these projects. The Material_Table stores information about individual materials, with each material assigned a unique Material_ID, a name, and a unit price. The Project_Table contains details about each project, including its creation and close dates, and is identified by a unique Project_ID. The Bill_Table stores information about each bill, including its creation and modification times, the project it is associated with, the total sale amount, and the unit of measurement, with the Project_ID being a required field referencing the Project_Table. The Bill_Details_Table stores the details of each bill item, including the bill it belongs to, the material it is for, the quantity, and the selling price, with both the Bill_ID and Material_ID being required fields referencing the Bill_Table and Material_Table respectively, and the combination of Bill_ID and Material_ID serving as the primary key to uniquely identify each bill item.
