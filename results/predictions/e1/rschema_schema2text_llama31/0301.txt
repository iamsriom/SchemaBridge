The rschema_0301 database is designed to manage information about seats in a venue, their templates, and the users who reserve them. The Seat_Template table stores details about different seat templates, including a description, a unique template ID, and a name. The User table holds information about the individuals who reserve seats, with fields for their contact number, email, name, and a unique user ID. The Seat table contains details about each individual seat, such as its column number, creation and update times, location coordinates, price, and type, as well as the user ID of the person who reserved it. The Seat_Template_Layout table links seat templates to specific seats and time periods, with fields for the occasion, seat ID, template ID, and time period. The Seat table's reservedPurchasedUserID field references the User table's user_ID field, establishing a relationship between reserved seats and their owners. The Seat_Template_Layout table has a composite primary key consisting of the seat ID and template ID, ensuring each seat can only be associated with one template at a time, and its foreign keys reference the Seat and Seat_Template tables to enforce referential integrity. The Seat table's reservedPurchasedUserID field is not nullable, indicating that every seat must be reserved by a user, and the Seat_Template_Layout table's seat_ID and template_ID fields are also not nullable, meaning every layout must be associated with a specific seat and template.
