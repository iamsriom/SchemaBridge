The rschema_0302 database is a comprehensive system for managing patient information, doctor details, and medical records. It consists of five tables: Patient, Doctor, Medicine, Consultation_Record, and Patient_Medication_Record. The Patient table stores essential information about each patient, including their address, age, date of birth, emergency contact number, gender, name, and phone number, with a unique Patient_ID serving as the primary key. The Doctor table contains details about each doctor, such as their age, date of birth, department affiliation, gender, level, name, and phone number, with a unique Doctor_ID as the primary key. The Medicine table holds information about various medicines, including their function, ID, inventory, name, and unit price. The Consultation_Record table stores records of consultations between doctors and patients, with each record linked to a specific patient and doctor through their respective IDs, and also includes the consultation time, diagnosis results, and treatment recommendations. The Patient_Medication_Record table tracks the medication prescribed to each patient, including the dosage, frequency, medicine ID, patient ID, precautions, and prescription time, with a unique combination of patient ID, medicine ID, and prescription time serving as the primary key. The relationships between these tables are maintained through foreign keys, with the Doctor_ID in Consultation_Record referencing the Doctor table, the Patient_ID in Consultation_Record and Patient_Medication_Record referencing the Patient table, and the Medicine_ID in Patient_Medication_Record referencing the Medicine table, ensuring that each record is linked to the correct patient, doctor, and medicine.
