The rschema_0304 database is designed to manage roles and permissions within a system, allowing for the assignment of specific permissions to various roles. The Role table stores information about each role, including its creation and update times, a unique identifier (Role_ID), a descriptive name, and a brief description. The Permission table contains details about each permission, such as its creation and update times, a unique identifier (Permission_ID), a descriptive name, and a brief description. The Role_Permission_Assignment table serves as a bridge between roles and permissions, linking a role to one or more permissions, and vice versa, with each assignment recorded along with its creation and update times. The Role_ID and Permission_ID columns in the Role_Permission_Assignment table are both non-nullable and serve as the primary key, ensuring that each assignment is unique. The Role_ID and Permission_ID columns in the Role_Permission_Assignment table also reference the Role_ID and Permission_ID columns in the Role and Permission tables, respectively, establishing a many-to-many relationship between roles and permissions, with the update rule set to CASCADE, meaning that if a role or permission is deleted, all assignments involving it will also be deleted.
