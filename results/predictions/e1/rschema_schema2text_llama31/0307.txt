The rschema_0307 database is designed to store information about companies, their employees, and various related entities. The Companies_Table stores details about each company, including its unique identifier, name, website, creation and update dates. The API_Keys_Table holds information about API keys, such as the reason for their creation, creation and update dates, a unique identifier, name, private key, and URI. The Employees_Table contains data about employees, including their company affiliation, creation and update dates, a unique identifier, name, position, and salary. The Sites_Table stores details about various sites, including their creation and update dates, database name, hostname, whether they are deprecated, abbreviation, name, and a unique identifier. The Devices_Table holds information about devices, such as their associated employee, creation and update dates, and a unique identifier. The Users_Table stores user account information, including their company affiliation, creation and update dates, display name usage, email, whether they are an administrator or developer, last login time, password, password salt, password type, real name, a unique identifier, username, and a foreign key referencing the Sites_Table to establish the site they belong to, with the Site_ID column being a non-nullable foreign key.
