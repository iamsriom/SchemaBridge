The rschema_0309 database is designed to store information about customers, their associated income groups, insurance policies they purchase, and the insurance companies offering these policies. The customer table contains essential details about each customer, including their unique identifier, name, email, phone number, gender, date of birth, marital status, region, and income group ID, which references the income group table. The income group table stores information about different income groups, with each group having a unique identifier, name, and income range. The insurance table holds details about various insurance policies, including their unique identifier, name, type, premium, and vehicle segment. The customer_purchases_insurance table tracks the insurance policies purchased by each customer, with each entry containing the customer's unique identifier, the insurance policy's unique identifier, the purchase date, and the expiration date. The customer_associated_income_group table links customers to their respective income groups, with each entry containing the customer's unique identifier and the income group's unique identifier. The database enforces referential integrity by ensuring that customers can only be associated with existing income groups and insurance policies, and that insurance policies can only be purchased by existing customers.
