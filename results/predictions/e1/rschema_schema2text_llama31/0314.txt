The rschema_0314 database is designed to manage organizational information, specifically focusing on departments, roles, and employees. The Department table contains details about each department, including a unique identifier, a description, and a name, with a foreign key referencing the superior department to establish a hierarchical structure. The Role table stores information about different roles within the organization, such as a unique identifier, role name, and salary. The Employee table holds data about individual employees, including their actual salary, department, role, and name, with foreign keys referencing the Department and Role tables to establish relationships. The Department_and_Role table, which has a composite primary key of department ID and role ID, is used to map specific roles to departments, providing a many-to-many relationship between these two entities. This table also includes foreign keys referencing the Department and Role tables to ensure data consistency.
