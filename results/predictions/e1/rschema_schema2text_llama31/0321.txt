The rschema_0321 database is designed to store information about users, teams, and matches in a sports-related application. The General_User_Information table holds details about each user, including their account name, age, email, gender, name, password, phone number, and a unique user ID, which serves as the primary key. The Member_Information table stores information about team members, with each member having a unique ID, a level, a status, and start and end dates for their membership validity. The Team_Information table contains details about each team, including the team's creation time, a unique team ID, the ID of the team leader, the team's name, and the total number of team members. The Team_Leader_ID in the Team_Information table references the User_ID in the General_User_Information table, establishing a relationship between team leaders and users. The Football_Player_Information table links football players to the teams they are affiliated with, with each player having a unique ID and being associated with a team ID and a user ID, which references the General_User_Information table. The Match_Information table stores details about each match, including the IDs of the away and home teams, a unique match ID, the match location, the match result, and the match time. The Away_Team_ID and Home_Team_ID in the Match_Information table reference the Team_Information table, establishing relationships between teams and matches.
