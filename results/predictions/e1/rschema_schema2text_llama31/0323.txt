The rschema_0323 database is designed to track sales data and customer information. It contains three main tables: Salesperson, Customer, and Sales_record. The Salesperson table stores information about the salespeople, with each salesperson having a unique identifier, Salesperson_ID, which is a primary key. The Customer table holds details about the customers, with each customer also having a unique identifier, Customer_ID, which is the primary key. The Sales_record table captures sales data, including the customer's identifier, the amount of the sale, the date of the sale, the salesperson's identifier, the quantity of the sale, and the number of visits made to the customer. The Sales_record table has a composite primary key consisting of the salesperson's identifier, the customer's identifier, and the date of the sale, ensuring that each sale is uniquely identified. The Sales_record table also references the Customer and Salesperson tables through foreign keys, which link each sale to the corresponding customer and salesperson. The Customer_ID and Salesperson_ID columns in the Sales_record table cannot be null, as they must be associated with a valid customer and salesperson, respectively.
