The rschema_0327 database is designed to manage information about customers, their addresses, and orders for various resources. The Address table stores details about a customer's physical location, including their city, country, district, postal code, and street address, with each address assigned a unique identifier. The Resource table contains information about the resources being ordered, such as their name and type. The Customer table links customers to their addresses and resources, with each customer having a unique identifier, first and last name, phone number, and associated address and resource. The Order table tracks the orders placed by customers, including the amount, currency, start time, and type of order, with each order linked to a specific customer and resource. The relationships between these tables are enforced through foreign keys, ensuring that a customer can only be associated with one address and one resource, and an order can only be placed by a customer and for a specific resource. The database also includes primary keys to uniquely identify each address, customer, and order, and unique constraints to prevent duplicate addresses and resources.
