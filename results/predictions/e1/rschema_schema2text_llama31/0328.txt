The rschema_0328 database is designed to manage and store data related to online surveys. The Administrator table contains information about the administrators of the system, including their account names, unique IDs, names, passwords, and phone numbers. Each administrator is uniquely identified by their Administrator_ID, which is the primary key. The Survey table stores details about the surveys themselves, including their titles, descriptions, start and end times, access restrictions, and logos. Each survey has a unique ID, which is the primary key. The Question table contains the questions within each survey, along with their titles, descriptions, display styles, evaluation scores, and validation types. Questions are linked to their respective surveys through the Survey_ID foreign key, which references the Survey table. The Option table stores the possible choices for multiple-choice questions, with each option having a unique ID, display order, and content. Options are linked to their parent questions through the Question_ID foreign key, which references the Question table. The Text_answer_record table stores the text-based answers provided by respondents, including the answer content, question ID, respondent information, and submission time. The Choice_answer_record table stores the multiple-choice answers provided by respondents, including the answer type, option ID, respondent information, and submission time. Both the Text_answer_record and Choice_answer_record tables reference the Question table through the Question_ID foreign key, which ensures that each answer is linked to its corresponding question.
