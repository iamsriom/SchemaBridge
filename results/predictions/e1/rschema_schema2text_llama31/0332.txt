The rschema_0332 database is designed to manage information about music albums, artists, users, and playlists. At its core, the database contains two main entities: Artist and User, each with their own unique identifier, represented by the Artist_ID and User_ID respectively. The Artist table stores the names of musicians, while the User table stores the names of individuals who can create and manage playlists. The Album table links artists to their respective albums, containing details such as the album name, release date, and the artist responsible for it. Each album is uniquely identified by its Album_ID. The Playlist table represents a collection of songs, with a Creator_ID referencing the User who created the playlist, a name for the playlist, and a Play_Count to track how many times it has been played. The Playlist_Creation table stores the time a playlist was created, the playlist's ID, and the user's ID who created it, ensuring that each creation event is uniquely identified by a combination of these three fields. The Song table contains information about individual tracks, including the album they belong to, their name, popularity, video duration, and video ID. The Songs_In_Album table establishes a many-to-many relationship between albums and songs, allowing multiple songs to be included in one album and vice versa. This table is essential for managing the composition of each album.
