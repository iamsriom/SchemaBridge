The rschema_0337 database is designed to manage and track student performance in exams. The Exam table stores information about each exam, including its unique identifier (Exam_ID), name, and the time it was held. The Student table holds details about each student, with a unique identifier (Student_ID) and their name. The Practice_Question table contains questions from the exams, each with a unique identifier (Question_ID), a description of the question, and a score. Each question is linked to a specific exam through the Exam_ID_it_belongs_to field, which references the Exam table's Exam_ID. The Submission_Record table tracks each student's submissions, including the exam they took (Exam_ID), the student's ID (Student_ID), and a unique submission identifier (Submission_ID), with the submission status. The Submission_Record table also references the Exam and Student tables through the Exam_ID and Student_ID fields, ensuring that each submission is linked to a valid exam and student. The Answer_Record table stores the answers given by students to practice questions, including the answer content, a unique identifier (Answer_Record_ID), and links to the practice question they answered (Practice_Question_ID_it_belongs_to) and the submission they were part of (Submission_ID_it_belongs_to), referencing the Practice_Question and Submission_Record tables respectively.
