The rschema_0338 database is designed to manage information about colleges, their staff, and students, as well as the courses they offer and the grades students achieve in those courses. The College table stores details about each college, including its location, name, total number of staff, and total number of students. Each college is uniquely identified by its College_ID, which is the primary key. The Student table contains information about individual students, such as their age, date of birth, gender, name, registration time, and telephone number, with the Student_ID serving as the primary key. A student is associated with a specific college, as indicated by the Associated_College_ID foreign key, which references the College_ID in the College table. The Teacher table stores details about the staff, including their age, contact information, gender, name, and title, with the Teacher_ID serving as the primary key. Like students, teachers are also associated with a specific college, as indicated by the Associated_College_ID foreign key, which references the College_ID in the College table. The Course table contains information about the courses offered by the colleges, including the class hours, classroom, course summary, credit number, and name, with the Course_ID serving as the primary key. Each course is taught by a specific teacher, as indicated by the Teaching_Teacher_ID foreign key, which references the Teacher_ID in the Teacher table. The Course_Enrollment_and_Grade table stores the grades students achieve in each course, including the course grade, enrollment time, and student ID, with the Student_ID and Course_ID serving as composite primary keys. This table also references the Course_ID in the Course table and the Student_ID in the Student table, indicating that a student can only enroll in a course once and receive a grade for it.
