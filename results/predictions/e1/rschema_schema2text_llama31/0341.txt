The rschema_0341 database is designed to manage user-generated comments, with a focus on storing and organizing information about both anonymous and registered users. At its core, the database contains three main tables: Anonymous_Comment, User_Information, and Normal_Comment. The Anonymous_Comment table stores comments made by users who choose to remain anonymous, with each comment assigned a unique Comment_ID, which serves as the primary key. This table also captures the content of the comment, the URL associated with the user, the time of publication, and the storage format used. In contrast, the User_Information table holds details about registered users, including their name, contact information, and address, with the User_ID serving as the primary key. The Normal_Comment table represents comments made by registered users, with each comment also assigned a unique Comment_ID, which is the primary key. This table includes the comment content, publication time, storage format, and the User_ID of the registered user who made the comment, which is a foreign key referencing the User_ID in the User_Information table. The relationship between Normal_Comment and User_Information is established through this foreign key, ensuring that each comment is linked to a registered user.
