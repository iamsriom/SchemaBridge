The rschema_0343 database is designed to manage information about personnel, vehicles, and insurance policies. The Personnel table contains details about individuals, including their city, name, street address, and a unique personnel ID, which serves as the primary key. The Insurance_Type table stores information about different types of insurance, such as the insurance company name, content, cost, and a unique insurance type ID, also serving as the primary key. The Vehicle table holds data about vehicles, including the driver's ID, manufacturer, model, Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), and year, with the VIN being the primary key and the driver's ID referencing the Personnel table. The User_Phone_Table stores phone numbers associated with personnel, with a composite primary key consisting of the user ID and phone number, and the user ID referencing the Personnel table. The Vehicle_Purchase_Insurance table tracks insurance policies purchased for vehicles, including the insurance type ID, purchase time, validity time, and VIN, with a composite primary key consisting of the VIN, insurance type ID, and purchase time, and foreign keys referencing the Insurance_Type and Vehicle tables.
