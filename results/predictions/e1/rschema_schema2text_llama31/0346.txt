The rschema_0346 database is designed to manage financial information related to expenses. It consists of two main tables: Account and Expense. The Account table stores information about individual accounts, with each account represented by a unique identifier, known as the Account, which is a string of up to 255 characters and serves as the primary key. Each account is affiliated with a specific unit, also represented as a string of up to 255 characters, and is associated with a user, also a string of up to 255 characters. The Expense table, on the other hand, stores details about individual expenses, including the amount spent, which is an integer value. Each expense is linked to a specific account, denoted by the Associated_Account field, which is a string of up to 255 characters and is a foreign key referencing the Account table, meaning it must match an existing account identifier. The Expense table also tracks the creation time, description, due time, payment time, and status of each expense, with the Record_ID serving as the primary key, a unique integer identifier. The Associated_Account field in the Expense table cannot be null, and the creation time, due time, and payment time are all represented as datetime values.
