The rschema_0350 database is designed to manage information about events, tickets, and participants. The Ticket_Type table stores details about different types of tickets, including a unique identifier, description, whether it includes a workshop, name, price, and a primary key to uniquely identify each ticket type. The Discount_Code table contains information about discount codes, with a unique identifier, name, type, and the amount of the discount. The Meeting table holds details about meetings, including the date, description, start and end times, room, and a unique identifier. The Workshop table stores information about workshops, including capacity, date, description, start and end times, location, requirements, and a unique identifier. The Speaker table contains details about speakers, including their bio, company, name, and a unique identifier. The Participant table links participants to their tickets and discount codes, with columns for company, email, name, and a unique identifier, as well as foreign keys referencing the Discount_Code and Ticket_Type tables to ensure data consistency. The Meeting_Speaker table connects meetings to their speakers, with a composite primary key consisting of the meeting and speaker IDs, and foreign keys referencing the Meeting and Speaker tables to maintain referential integrity. The Workshop_Speaker table does the same for workshops and speakers, with a composite primary key and foreign keys referencing the Workshop and Speaker tables.
