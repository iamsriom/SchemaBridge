The rschema_0352 database is designed to manage information about suppliers, customers, products, invoices, and the products included in those invoices. The Supplier table stores details about each supplier, including their address, ID, name, and phone number, with the Supplier_ID serving as the primary key to uniquely identify each supplier. The Customer table holds information about each customer, such as their address, ID, email, first name, last name, and phone number, with the Customer_ID being the primary key. The Product table contains details about each product, including its description, minimum stock quantity, name, purchase price, selling price, stock quantity, and the ID of the supplier from which it was sourced, with the Product_ID being the primary key and the Supplier_ID referencing the Supplier table to establish the relationship between suppliers and products. The Invoice table stores information about each invoice, including the customer's ID, date, invoice ID, tax amount, and total value, with the Invoice_ID serving as the primary key and the Customer_ID referencing the Customer table to link each invoice to its corresponding customer. The Invoice_Product table links each product to the invoices it appears on, with the Invoice_ID and Product_ID forming a composite primary key and referencing the Invoice and Product tables, respectively, to establish the relationships between products and invoices.
