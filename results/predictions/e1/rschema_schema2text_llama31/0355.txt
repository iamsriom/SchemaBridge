The rschema_0355 database is designed to track information about horse racing events. At its core, the database contains three main entities: Races, Horses, and Bettors. Each Race is identified by a unique ID and has a name, result, start and end time, which are all recorded in the Race table. The Horse table stores information about individual horses, including their unique IDs and names. Meanwhile, the Bettor table holds details about individuals who place bets, such as their IDs and identity information. The Horse_Participation table links horses to specific races, recording their rankings and the time they participated, with each combination of horse and race being unique. The Bettor_Betting_Race table tracks the betting activity of individuals, including the actual and expected rankings of the horses they bet on, the amount they wagered, and whether they won or lost. This table also links bettors to specific horses and races, with each combination of bettor, horse, and race being unique. The relationships between these entities are enforced by foreign keys, which ensure that a horse can only participate in a race that exists in the database, and a bettor can only place bets on a horse that exists in the database and a race that exists in the database.
