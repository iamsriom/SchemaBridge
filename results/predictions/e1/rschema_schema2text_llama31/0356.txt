The rschema_0356 database is designed to manage and track work items and their associated attributes. The Work_Item table stores information about each work item, including its creation and update dates, a description, a unique identifier (workItem_ID), and a name. The workItem_ID is the primary key, ensuring each work item is uniquely identified. The Work_Item_Attributes table, on the other hand, stores additional attributes of each work item, such as a unique identifier (Attributes_ID), a name, a value, and a link status. The Attributes_ID and workItem_ID form a composite primary key, meaning each attribute is uniquely tied to a specific work item. The workItem_ID in this table is also a foreign key referencing the workItem_ID in the Work_Item table, establishing a relationship between the two tables. This foreign key is not nullable, indicating that each attribute must be associated with a work item.
