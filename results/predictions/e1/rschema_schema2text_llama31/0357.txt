The rschema_0357 database is designed to manage the operations of a car rental service, encompassing various aspects of the business. The database consists of seven tables: rental_location, vehicle_category, vehicle, user, user_vehicle_reservation, news_and_featured_content, and vehicle_current_location. The rental_location table stores information about the physical locations where vehicles are rented, including the location ID, name, address, phone number, and business hours. The vehicle_category table contains details about the different types of vehicles available for rent, such as category ID, name, weekday and weekend rental prices. The vehicle table holds information about each vehicle, including its license plate number, brand, model, photo path, and the location and category it belongs to. The user table stores customer information, including user ID, email, name, address, phone number, postal code, city, and credit card information. The user_vehicle_reservation table tracks vehicle reservations made by users, including the user ID, vehicle ID, reservation date, and return date. The news_and_featured_content table stores promotional content, such as news and featured articles, with a unique content ID, title, content, and release date. The vehicle_current_location table logs the current location of each vehicle, including the license plate number, record time, and the location ID of the rental location where it is currently situated. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, which ensure data consistency and integrity, such as the cascade deletion of related records when a location or vehicle is removed, and the referencing of user and vehicle IDs in the user_vehicle_reservation table.
