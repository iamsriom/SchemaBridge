The rschema_0361 database is designed to manage information about restaurants and their associated reviews, dishes, and replies. At the heart of the database is the Restaurant table, which stores details about each restaurant, including its address, average cost, business hours, name, phone number, and a unique identifier known as the Restaurant ID. The User_review table holds reviews written by users, containing the actual photos, dining time, rating, a unique review ID, and the content of the review. The Dish table is used to store information about the dishes served at each restaurant, including their category, description, image URL, monthly sales, name, price, and satisfaction rate, with each dish linked to its parent restaurant through the Restaurant ID. The Review_reply table allows users to respond to existing reviews, storing the reply content, reply time, and the ID of the review being replied to. The Restaurant ID in the Dish table is a foreign key referencing the Restaurant ID in the Restaurant table, ensuring data consistency between the two tables, while the Review_ID_being_replied_to in the Review_reply table is a foreign key referencing the Review_ID in the User_review table, linking replies to their parent reviews.
