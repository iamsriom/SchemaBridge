The rschema_0367 database is designed to manage user information and interactions with medical institutions. The database contains three main tables: 'user', 'medical_institution', and 'operator'. The 'user' table stores information about individual users, including their unique identifier, name, email, phone number, birth date, gender, password, address, and insurance status. Each user is uniquely identified by their 'user_id', which is a non-null, 255-character string. The 'medical_institution' table represents medical institutions, with fields for their unique identifier, name, address, opening and closing times, and the identifier of the operator responsible for the institution. The 'operator' table stores information about the operators, including their unique identifier, username, password, and the identifier of the medical institution they are affiliated with. The 'operator' table references the 'medical_institution' table through the 'affiliated_institution_id' field, with a cascade delete and update rule, meaning that if a medical institution is deleted or updated, all associated operators will be automatically deleted or updated. Similarly, the 'medical_institution' table references the 'operator' table through the 'operatorid' field, with a cascade delete and update rule. The 'user' table does not reference any other tables. The database uses InnoDB as its storage engine and UTF8mb3 character encoding.
