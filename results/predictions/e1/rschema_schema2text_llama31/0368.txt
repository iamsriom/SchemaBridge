The rschema_0368 database is designed to manage information about students, teachers, subjects, and exams within an educational institution. It consists of eight tables: Student, Teacher, Subject, Teacher_and_Student_Guidance, Exam, Student_and_Subject, Teacher_and_Subject, and Student_and_Exam. The Student table stores details about each student, including their name, age, phone number, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Teacher table contains information about each teacher, such as their name and phone number, with a unique teacher ID as the primary key. The Subject table lists the various subjects offered, with a subject ID as the primary key and the number of hours allocated to each subject. The Teacher_and_Student_Guidance table links students with their assigned teachers, with both student and teacher IDs referencing their respective tables. The Exam table records exam details, including the corresponding subject ID and date, with an exam ID as the primary key. The Student_and_Subject table tracks students' grades in each subject, linking students with their subjects through both student and subject IDs. The Teacher_and_Subject table assigns teachers to specific subjects, referencing both teacher and subject IDs. Finally, the Student_and_Exam table records students' grades in each exam, linking students with their exam IDs. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that relationships between tables are maintained, and update and delete rules like CASCADE and RESTRICT are applied to prevent data inconsistencies.
