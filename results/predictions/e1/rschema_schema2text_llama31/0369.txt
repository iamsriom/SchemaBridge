The rschema_0369 database is designed to track and manage content, users, and their interactions within the system. At its core, the Content table stores information about the content itself, including a unique identifier, description, type, creation and update times, and a title. The User table holds details about the individuals who interact with the content, such as their email, role, unique identifier, and username. The Operation_Log table records every action taken on the content, including the content's ID, a unique log ID, a comment about the operation, the time it occurred, the type of operation, and the user responsible. The User_Content_Modification table tracks changes made to the content, with a composite primary key consisting of the content ID, modification time, and user ID, ensuring each modification is uniquely identified. The Content_ID in the Operation_Log table references the Content table, establishing a relationship between the log entries and the content they relate to. Similarly, the Content_ID in the User_Content_Modification table references the Content table, linking modifications to the content they affect. The User_ID in the User_Content_Modification table references the User table, connecting the modifications to the users who made them.
