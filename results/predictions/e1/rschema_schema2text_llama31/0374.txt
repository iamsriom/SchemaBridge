The rschema_0374 database is designed to manage various aspects of an organization's operations, including user roles, departments, resources, and work units. The database contains eight tables: Role, Supervisory_department, Resource, Approval_document, User, Work_unit, Operation, and Resource_transaction_record. The Role table stores information about different roles within the organization, including the role name and a unique identifier, Role_ID. The Supervisory_department table contains details about the various departments, such as their names and responsibility descriptions, with each department having a unique identifier, Department_ID. The Resource table lists the available resources, identified by a unique code, Resource_code. The Approval_document table stores documents, including their content and names, with each document having a unique identifier, Document_ID. The User table represents the organization's employees, with each user having a unique identifier, User_ID, an email address, password, and a role assigned to them, linked to the Role table through the Role_ID foreign key. The Work_unit table contains information about the organization's work units, including their names, responsibility descriptions, and the department they belong to, linked to the Supervisory_department table through the Supervisory_department_ID foreign key. The Operation table tracks the operations performed by users, including the operation content, time, and the user who performed it, linked to the User table through the Operating_user_ID foreign key. The Resource_transaction_record table records transactions involving resources, including the budget year, the user who received the resource, the resource code, storage location, and the work unit it belongs to, linked to the User, Resource, and Work_unit tables through foreign keys.
