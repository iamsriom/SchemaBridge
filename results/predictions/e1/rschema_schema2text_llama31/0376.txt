The rschema_0376 database is designed to manage a repair service's operations, encompassing customer information, technician details, and inventory management. The customer table stores customer IDs, names, and phone numbers, serving as the primary key. The technician table contains technician IDs, names, and addresses, also with a primary key. The order_parts table links orders to specific parts, with order and part IDs as the primary key, referencing the order and part tables respectively, and enforcing a cascade delete and update rule. The task_parts table connects repair tasks to parts, with a composite primary key of task and part IDs, referencing the repair_task and part tables, and enforcing a cascade delete and update rule. The car_model table stores car model IDs and names, with a primary key. The repair_task table contains task IDs, statuses, customer IDs, creation and completion dates, with a primary key and a foreign key referencing the customer table, enforcing a cascade delete and update rule. The task_technician table associates tasks with technicians, with a composite primary key of task and technician IDs. The task_car_model table links tasks to car models, with a composite primary key of task and car model IDs, referencing the repair_task and car_model tables, and enforcing a cascade delete and update rule. The order table stores order IDs, creation dates, and delivery statuses, with a primary key. The order_task table connects orders to tasks, with a composite primary key of order and task IDs, referencing the order and repair_task tables, and enforcing a cascade delete and update rule. The supplier table contains supplier IDs and names, with a primary key. The part table stores part IDs, serial numbers, descriptions, prices, supplier IDs, and stock quantities, with a primary key and a foreign key referencing the supplier table, enforcing a cascade delete and update rule.
