The rschema_0377 database is designed to manage articles and their associated tags. The database contains three tables: article, tag, and article_tag_association. The article table stores information about individual articles, with each article identified by a unique post_id and having a title. The tag table stores information about different categories of tags, with each tag identified by a unique tag_id and categorized under a specific tag_category. The article_tag_association table links articles to their corresponding tags, with each association having a post_id referencing the article, a tag_id referencing the tag, a created_at timestamp, and two foreign keys, fk_post_id and fk_tag_id, which reference the post_id in the article table and the tag_id in the tag table respectively. The article table has a composite primary key consisting of post_id and article_title, while the tag table has a composite primary key consisting of tag_id and tag_category. The article_tag_association table has a composite primary key consisting of post_id and tag_id, and its foreign keys are set to cascade delete and update, meaning that when an article or tag is deleted or updated, all associated entries in the article_tag_association table will be automatically updated or deleted.
