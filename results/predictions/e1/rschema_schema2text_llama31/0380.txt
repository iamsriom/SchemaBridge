The rschema_0380 database is designed to manage information about customers, airlines, flights, and flight reservations. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their unique identifier, email address, name, and phone number. The Airline table contains information about each airline, such as its address, name, and phone number, with the Airline_ID serving as the primary key. The Flight table holds details about each flight, including the airline it belongs to, its arrival and departure times, departure location, destination, and a unique identifier, with the Airline_ID referencing the Airline table to establish the relationship between flights and airlines. The Flight_Reservation table stores information about each customer's flight reservations, including the customer's identifier, the flight's identifier, the number of tickets purchased, and the time of reservation, with the combination of Customer_ID, Flight_ID, and Reservation_Time serving as the primary key to uniquely identify each reservation, and the Customer_ID and Flight_ID referencing the Customer and Flight tables, respectively, to establish relationships between reservations and their corresponding customers and flights.
