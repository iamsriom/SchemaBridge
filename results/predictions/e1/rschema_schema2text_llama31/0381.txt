The rschema_0381 database is designed to manage user subscriptions to news articles. The User table stores information about each registered user, with a unique identifier, User_ID, which serves as the primary key. The News table contains details about each news article, including a unique identifier, News_ID, which is also the primary key. The Subscription table represents the relationship between users and news articles, storing the creation and modification times of each subscription, the IP address of the user, and the subscription status. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the User_ID, News_ID, and Creation_Time, ensuring each subscription is uniquely identifiable. The News_ID in the Subscription table references the News_ID in the News table, and the User_ID references the User_ID in the User table, establishing relationships between the tables. The News_ID in the Subscription table is not nullable, indicating that a subscription must be associated with a specific news article, and the User_ID is also not nullable, meaning a subscription must be linked to a registered user.
