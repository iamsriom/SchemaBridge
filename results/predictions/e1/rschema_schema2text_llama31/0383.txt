The rschema_0383 database is designed to manage student and course information for an educational institution. The Student table stores details about each student, including their name, date of birth, department ID, enrollment year, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Professor table contains information about each faculty member, such as their name, office duration, office number, and start time, with a unique professor ID as the primary key. The Course table holds details about each course, including its name, duration, semester, year, and the professor responsible for teaching it, identified by their professor ID, which is a foreign key referencing the Professor table. The Course_Registration table tracks student enrollment in courses, with columns for the course ID, grade, registration time, and student ID, with a composite primary key consisting of the student ID, course ID, and registration time. The Course_Registration table also references the Course and Student tables through foreign keys, ensuring data consistency by linking each registration to the corresponding course and student.
