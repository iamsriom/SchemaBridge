The rschema_0385 database is designed to manage financial transactions and account information for a banking system. It consists of three main tables: Currency_Type, Bank_Account, and Account_Transfer. The Currency_Type table stores information about different currencies, including a unique code, name, and symbol. The Bank_Account table holds details about individual bank accounts, such as the account balance, number, currency type, frozen amount, user information, and user number. The Currency_Code field in the Bank_Account table is a foreign key referencing the Currency_Type table, ensuring that each account is associated with a valid currency. The Account_Transfer table tracks transactions between accounts, including the initiating and receiving account numbers, initiation time, transaction amount, currency, and status. The primary key for this table is a composite of the initiating account number, receiving account number, and initiation time, preventing duplicate transactions. The Initiating_Account_Number and Receiving_Account_Number fields in the Account_Transfer table are foreign keys referencing the Account_Number field in the Bank_Account table, enforcing referential integrity by linking transactions to their respective accounts. Additionally, the Transaction_Currency field is a foreign key referencing the Currency_Type table, ensuring that transactions are associated with a valid currency.
