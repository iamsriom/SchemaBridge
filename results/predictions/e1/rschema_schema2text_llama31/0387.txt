The rschema_0387 database is designed to manage information about customers, airlines, flights, reservations, and payment records. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact information, and a unique customer ID. The Airline table holds information about each airline, such as its name and contact details, with a unique airline ID serving as its primary key. The Flight table contains details about each flight, including its airline ID, departure and arrival times, destination, and flight number, with a unique flight ID as its primary key and a foreign key referencing the Airline table to establish the relationship between flights and airlines. The Reservation table stores information about each reservation made by a customer, including the customer's ID, flight ID, number of tickets, reservation date and time, and a unique reservation ID, with foreign keys referencing the Customer and Flight tables to establish relationships between reservations and the entities they relate to. The Payment_Record table tracks payment details for each reservation, including the payment amount, date, method, and a unique payment ID, with a foreign key referencing the Reservation table to link payments to the corresponding reservation.
