The rschema_0389 database is designed to manage information about students, teachers, courses, and their interactions. The Student table stores details about individual students, including their age, gender, name, phone number, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Teacher table contains information about instructors, such as their gender, name, phone number, title, and a unique teacher ID, also serving as the primary key. The Course table holds details about classes, including the number of class hours, location, credits, the number of students enrolled, and the teacher responsible for teaching the course, which is linked to the Teacher table through the Teaching_Teacher_ID foreign key. The Student_Course_Selection table tracks students' enrollment in courses, with each entry including the course ID, grade received, selection time, and student ID, with the combination of student ID, course ID, and selection time forming the primary key. The Student_ID and Course_ID in this table are also foreign keys referencing the Student and Course tables, respectively, ensuring data consistency and enforcing referential integrity through the use of NOT NULL constraints and ON DELETE and ON UPDATE rules, which are not explicitly specified but are implied by the foreign key definitions.
