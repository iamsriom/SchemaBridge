The rschema_0390 database is designed to manage information about consumers, courier companies, and their cooperation. At its core, the Consumer table stores details about individuals, including their address information and phone number, with each consumer uniquely identified by a Consumer ID. The Courier Company table contains essential data about courier companies, such as their arrival times, charging prices, and company names, with the company name serving as the primary key. The Courier Tracking table tracks the status of deliveries, including a unique tracking number, recipient ID, recipient name, and recipient phone number. The Administrator table stores information about the administrators of the system, including their IDs, usernames, passwords, and names, with each administrator uniquely identified by their ID. The Cooperation table represents the partnership between administrators and courier companies, linking administrators to the companies they manage, with the company name and administrator ID forming a composite primary key. The Administrator ID in the Cooperation table references the Administrator table, ensuring that only valid administrators can be associated with companies, and the Company Name references the Courier Company table, linking each company to its corresponding administrator.
