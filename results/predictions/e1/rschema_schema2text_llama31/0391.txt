The rschema_0391 database is designed to store information about students, their posts, and comments on those posts. The Student table contains details about each student, including their admission date, gender, major, name, password, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Post table stores information about each post, including the author's student ID, the content of the post, a unique post ID, the date of publication, and the topic of the post. The author's student ID in the Post table references the Student ID in the Student table, establishing a relationship between the two tables. The Comment table stores comments made on posts, including the content of the comment, the time it was made, the post it was made on, and the student who made the comment. The Commented Post and Commenter columns in the Comment table form a composite primary key, ensuring that each comment is unique to a specific post and student. The Commented Post column references the Post ID in the Post table, while the Commenter column references the Student ID in the Student table, establishing relationships between the Comment table and the other two tables. Additionally, the Commented Post and Commenter columns in the Comment table are not nullable, indicating that a comment must always be associated with a specific post and student.
