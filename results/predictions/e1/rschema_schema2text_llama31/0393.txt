The rschema_0393 database is designed to manage patient information and their medical treatments. It consists of five tables: patient, ward, registration_category, drug, and prescription. The patient table stores details about each patient, including their age, contact information, gender, name, and unique patient ID, which serves as the primary key. The ward table contains information about the different wards in the hospital, identified by a unique ward ID, which is also the primary key. The registration_category table holds details about the different registration categories, with a unique registration category ID serving as the primary key. The drug table stores information about the various medications available, with a unique drug ID as the primary key. The prescription table links patients with the medications they are prescribed, referencing the patient ID from the patient table and the drug ID from the drug table, both of which are non-nullable foreign keys. The prescription table also has a composite primary key consisting of the patient ID and drug ID, ensuring that each patient can only be prescribed each medication once.
