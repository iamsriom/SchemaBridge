The rschema_0394 database is designed to manage user accounts, menu items, and orders for a restaurant. At its core, the User table stores information about each registered user, including their address, phone number, username, and password, with a unique User_ID serving as the primary key. The Administrator table, on the other hand, contains details about the restaurant's administrators, such as their names, login times, IP addresses, and passwords, with Administrator_ID being the primary key. The Menu table holds information about the dishes available, including their descriptions, names, images, and prices, with Menu_ID serving as the primary key. The Order table, which has a composite primary key consisting of User_ID, Menu_ID, and Creation_Time, stores details about each order, including the creation time, amount, modification time, status, and any remarks, referencing the User and Menu tables through foreign keys that ensure data consistency.
