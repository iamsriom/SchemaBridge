The rschema_0395 database is designed to track student meal card information and consumption records. The Student table contains essential details about each student, including their contact information, department, name, and a unique student ID, which serves as the primary key. The Meal_card table stores information about each meal card, such as its balance and a unique card ID, which is the primary key. The Student_ID column in the Meal_card table references the Student_ID in the Student table, establishing a relationship between a student and their meal card, and this relationship is enforced by a foreign key constraint that ensures a meal card is always associated with a valid student. The Consumption_record table logs each time a student uses their meal card, recording the amount spent, the location, and the time of the transaction, with a unique record ID serving as the primary key. The Card_ID column in the Consumption_record table references the Card_ID in the Meal_card table, linking each consumption record to the corresponding meal card, and this relationship is also enforced by a foreign key constraint that ensures a consumption record is always associated with a valid meal card.
