The rschema_0396 database is designed to store information about various travel-related entities, including users, guides, restaurants, hotels, scenic spots, and routes. The User_Table stores information about registered users, with each user having a unique Username that serves as the primary key, along with a Nickname, Password, and Profile Picture. The Guide_Table contains details about travel guides, including a Category, Content, Guide_Title, and Tags, with the Guide_Title being the primary key. The Restaurant_Table and Hotel_Table store information about restaurants and hotels, respectively, with each having a unique Restaurant_Name and Hotel_Name as primary keys, along with an Image, Introduction, and Location. The Scenic_Spot_Table holds details about scenic spots, including an Address, Days of operation, Image, Introduction, Opening Hours, Scenic_Spot_Name as the primary key, and Tips. The Route_Table stores information about routes, with a Content, Introduction, Route_Name as the primary key, and Tags. The Write_Guide_Table is a many-to-many relationship table that links users to guides, with a composite primary key of Username and Guide_Title, and foreign keys referencing the User_Table and Guide_Table, ensuring that a user can write multiple guides and a guide can be written by multiple users.
