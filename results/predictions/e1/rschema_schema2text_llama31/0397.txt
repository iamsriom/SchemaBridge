The rschema_0397 database is designed to manage employee information and related data within a company. It consists of four main tables: department, employee, attendance_records, and recruitment. The department table stores information about each department within the company, including a unique department ID, department name, and the ID of the employee who manages that department. The employee table contains details about each employee, such as their unique employee ID, name, department ID, position, and salary. The attendance_records table tracks the attendance of each employee, with a unique combination of date and employee ID serving as the primary key, and the employee ID referencing the employee table to link attendance records to the corresponding employee. The recruitment table stores information about job openings, including a unique recruitment ID, position, name of the job, work experience required, contact information, and the ID of the employee who is conducting the interview, which references the employee table to link the recruitment process to the employee responsible.
