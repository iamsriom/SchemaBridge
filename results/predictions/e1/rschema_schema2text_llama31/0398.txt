The rschema_0398 database is designed to manage the operations of a restaurant, encompassing various aspects of its business. The Menu table contains information about the dishes served, including the category, unique identifier, name, and price. The Table table stores details about the seating arrangements, with a unique identifier for each table. The Customer table holds customer information, such as their name, gender, and phone number, while the Employee table stores details about the staff, including their age, name, gender, and salary. The Order table tracks the dining experiences, with a unique identifier for each order, the employee responsible for the service, and the date and time of the dining experience. The Dish_Table_Order table links dishes to specific orders and tables, ensuring that each dish is served at the correct table and order. The Customer_Order table connects customers to their respective orders, allowing for customer reviews of their dining experience. The primary keys in each table uniquely identify individual records, while foreign keys establish relationships between tables, such as the Service_Employee_ID in the Order table referencing the Employee_ID in the Employee table, ensuring that each order is linked to a valid employee. The composite primary keys in the Dish_Table_Order and Customer_Order tables prevent duplicate entries and maintain data consistency.
