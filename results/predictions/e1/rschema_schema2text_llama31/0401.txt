The rschema_0401 database is designed to manage student and staff information within an educational institution. The database consists of four main tables: students, classes, departments, and teachers. The students table stores information about each student, including their unique student ID, name, gender, grade level, department, home address, date of birth, and enrollment time, with all fields being required. The classes table contains details about each class, such as its unique class ID, name, grade level, major, head teacher, department ID, and class monitor, with all fields being mandatory. The departments table holds information about each department, including its unique department ID, name, size, and subordinate majors. The teachers table stores details about each teacher, including their unique teacher ID, name, gender, grade level, department, home address, date of birth, and entry time, with all fields being required. The students table has a foreign key referencing the departments table, meaning that a student's department is linked to a specific department ID, and when a department is deleted, all students in that department will also be deleted. The classes table has foreign keys referencing the teachers, students, and departments tables, indicating that a class has a head teacher, a class monitor, and a department, and when any of these entities are deleted, the corresponding class will also be deleted. The departments table has a primary key on the department ID, ensuring that each department is unique.
