The rschema_0402 database is designed to manage patient information and medical data within a healthcare facility. It consists of nine tables, each representing a distinct entity within the system. The Department table stores information about the various departments within the facility, including their addresses, names, phone numbers, and staff members. The Patient table contains details about each patient, such as their age, contact information, diagnosis descriptions, gender, names, and visit dates. The Ward table holds information about the different wards, including their numbers and levels. The Registration_Category table lists various categories for patient registration. The Medicine table stores details about medications, including their barcodes, names, prices, and quantities. The Prescription table contains information about prescriptions, with each prescription having a unique identifier. The Doctor table stores details about the medical staff, including their dates of birth, names, titles, work certificates, and years of employment. The Treatment table links patients with their respective treatments, with each treatment having a unique case number and referencing the patient's ID. The Prescription_Medicine table connects prescriptions with the medications they contain, with each entry referencing the prescription and medicine IDs, as well as the quantity of each medication. The Department table has a primary key of Department_ID, while the Patient, Ward, Registration_Category, Medicine, Doctor, and Treatment tables have primary keys of Patient_ID, Ward_Number, Category_ID, Medicine_ID, Doctor_ID, and Case_Number respectively. The Patient_ID in the Treatment table is a foreign key referencing the Patient table, ensuring that each treatment is linked to a valid patient. The Prescription_ID in the Prescription_Medicine table is a composite primary key, referencing the Prescription table, and the Medicine_ID is a foreign key referencing the Medicine table, while the Quantity column allows null values. The Medicine_ID in the Prescription_Medicine table is a foreign key referencing the Medicine table, and the Prescription_ID is also a foreign key referencing the Prescription table, with both referencing rules set to CASCADE, meaning that if a prescription or medicine is deleted, all related entries in the Prescription_Medicine table will also be deleted.
