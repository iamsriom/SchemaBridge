The rschema_0404 database is designed to manage the inventory and sales of products from various suppliers, as well as the employees responsible for these transactions. The Employee table stores information about each employee, including their unique Employee ID, contact number, password, position, and username. The Supplier table contains details about each supplier, such as their ID, name, supplier number, and phone number. The Product table holds information about each product, including its ID, name, product number, purchase and sales prices, stock quantity, and the ID of the supplier from which it is sourced. The Employee_Sales table tracks sales made by each employee, including the date of sale, sales price, and quantity sold, and is linked to both the Employee and Product tables through foreign keys. The Inventory table keeps track of the current stock quantity and sales price of each product, and is also linked to the Product table through a foreign key. The primary keys in the database are Employee_ID in the Employee table, Supplier_ID in the Supplier table, Product_ID in the Product table, and a composite key of Employee_ID and Product_ID in the Employee_Sales table, ensuring that each employee can only make sales for a product once and that each product can only be sold by one employee. The Supplier_ID in the Product table and the Product_ID in the Employee_Sales and Inventory tables are also foreign keys that reference the Supplier_ID in the Supplier table and the Product_ID in the Product table, respectively, to establish relationships between these tables.
