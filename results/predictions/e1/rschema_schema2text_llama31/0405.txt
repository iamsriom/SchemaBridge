The rschema_0405 database is designed to manage a healthcare system, storing information about patients, doctors, prescriptions, and medical consultations. The database consists of eight tables: drug, doctor, patient, prescription, outpatient_management, patient_outpatient_consultation, patient_payment_prescription, and prescription_contains_drug. The drug table stores details about medications, including their unique identifier, name, specification, and usage. The doctor table contains information about medical professionals, such as their unique identifier, name, address, and phone number. The patient table stores patient data, including their unique identifier, name, address, and phone number. The prescription table holds records of medical orders, including the prescription's unique identifier, medical orders, date, category, the doctor who issued it, and any remarks. Each prescription is linked to the doctor who issued it, and when a doctor is deleted, all their prescriptions are automatically deleted as well. The outpatient_management table manages outpatient services, including the unique identifier, doctor's identifier, outpatient type, and opening time. When a doctor is deleted, all their outpatient services are also deleted. The patient_outpatient_consultation table stores records of patient consultations, including the patient's identifier, outpatient service identifier, consultation time, and consultation content. This table has a composite primary key consisting of the patient's identifier, outpatient service identifier, and consultation time. The patient_payment_prescription table tracks payments made by patients for prescriptions, including the patient's identifier, prescription's unique identifier, payment amount, and discount. When a patient is deleted, all their payments are also deleted. The prescription_contains_drug table links prescriptions to the medications they contain, including the prescription's unique identifier, medication's unique identifier, and quantity. When a prescription or medication is deleted, all their associated links are also deleted.
