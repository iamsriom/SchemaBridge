The rschema_0406 database is a comprehensive system for managing various aspects of a business, including financial transactions, customer and supplier information, inventory management, production planning, and employee performance evaluation. At its core, the database contains 18 tables that store information about general ledger entries, accounts receivable and payable, customer and supplier details, material inventory, and employee information. The general ledger table tracks financial transactions with columns for the transaction ID, account name, amount, and date. Accounts receivable and payable tables store information about outstanding invoices and payments due to and from customers and suppliers, respectively, with columns for the transaction ID, customer or supplier ID, amount, and due date. Customer and supplier information is stored in separate tables with columns for the customer or supplier ID, name, contact information, and address. The material table stores information about different materials, including their ID, name, description, and type. The warehouse information table stores details about the warehouse, including its ID, name, and address. The project plan table tracks project details, including the project ID, name, planned start and end dates, execution status, and project progress. The supplier material supply table links suppliers to materials, and the material inventory table tracks the quantity of materials stored in the warehouse. The performance table evaluates employee performance, with columns for the employee ID, evaluation date, and performance score. The production plan table outlines production plans, including the plan ID, product name, production quantity, start and end dates. The material requirement plan table outlines the materials required for each production plan, and the production scheduling table schedules production, including the scheduling ID, production plan ID, production date, and scheduling status. The sales order table tracks sales orders, including the order ID, customer ID, order date, and total amount.
