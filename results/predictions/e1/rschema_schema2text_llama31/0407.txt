The rschema_0407 database is designed to store information about stores and their customers, along with records of the items they consume. The Store table contains details about each store, including its address, phone number, a unique identifier (Store_ID), and its name. The Customer table stores information about each customer, such as their address, age, gender, and name, with a unique identifier (Customer_ID) serving as the primary key. The Consumption_record table tracks the items consumed by each customer at each store, including the consumption amount, date, and a composite primary key consisting of the customer's ID, the store's ID, and the consumption date. The Customer_ID and Store_ID in the Consumption_record table are foreign keys referencing the Customer and Store tables respectively, ensuring that only valid customer and store IDs can be used, and that when a customer or store is deleted, their associated consumption records are also deleted.
