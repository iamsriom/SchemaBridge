The rschema_0410 database is designed to store information about users, vehicles, repair shops, announcements, and vehicle maintenance activities. The User_Information table contains details about the users, including their username, password, and role, with a unique identifier for each user. The Vehicle_Information table stores information about the vehicles, such as their category, name, photo, and status, with a unique identifier for each vehicle. The Repair_Shop_Information table holds details about the repair shops, including their name, contact person, and contact number, with a unique identifier for each shop. The Announcement_Information table stores announcements, including their title, content, and creation time. The Token_Information table stores information about tokens, including their creation and expiration times, and a unique identifier for each token. The Vehicle_Maintenance table stores maintenance records for each vehicle, including the maintenance details, time, and review reply, with a foreign key referencing the Vehicle_Information table to link each maintenance record to its corresponding vehicle. The Vehicle_Trip table stores trip records for each vehicle, including the trip remarks, time, and a foreign key referencing the Vehicle_Information table to link each trip record to its corresponding vehicle. The Vehicle_Maintenance_Application table stores applications for vehicle maintenance, including the application reason, time, and review reply, with a foreign key referencing the Vehicle_Information table to link each application to its corresponding vehicle. The Vehicle_ID column in the Vehicle_Maintenance, Vehicle_Trip, and Vehicle_Maintenance_Application tables cannot be null, indicating that each of these records must be associated with a vehicle. The database also enforces referential integrity by ensuring that the Vehicle_ID in the Vehicle_Maintenance, Vehicle_Trip, and Vehicle_Maintenance_Application tables matches a valid Vehicle_ID in the Vehicle_Information table, and that any changes to the Vehicle_Information table are reflected in the related records in the other three tables.
