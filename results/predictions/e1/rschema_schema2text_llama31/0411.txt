The rschema_0411 database is designed to manage customer information and their purchases of various products. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, type, contact person, address, phone number, email, and credit rating. The Customer_ID field serves as the primary key, uniquely identifying each customer. The Product table contains information about the products being sold, such as their name, category, brand, supplier, production date, specifications, and unit price. The Product_ID field is the primary key for this table. The Customer_product_purchase table tracks the purchases made by customers, including the order status, order time, payment method, payment status, and payment time. This table also stores the quantity of each product purchased and the total amount paid. The Customer_ID and Product_ID fields in the Customer_product_purchase table are foreign keys referencing the Customer and Product tables, respectively, establishing relationships between customers and the products they purchase. The primary key for the Customer_product_purchase table is a composite key consisting of the Customer_ID, Product_ID, and Order_time fields, ensuring that each purchase is uniquely identified.
