The rschema_0412 database is designed to manage information about students, teachers, courses, and their grades. The student table contains details about each student, including their unique student ID, name, contact information, college, and major, with all fields being required. The teacher table stores information about each teacher, including their unique employee ID, name, contact information, and identification, also with all fields being mandatory. The course table holds data about each course, such as its unique course ID, name, credit, and the ID of the teacher responsible for it, with the teacher ID referencing the employee ID in the teacher table, and any deletion or update of the course will automatically affect the associated teacher. The student_grade table tracks the grades of students in each course, including the student's ID, course ID, enrollment time, and grade, with all fields being required, and the student ID and course ID referencing the student and course tables respectively, with any deletion or update of a student or course automatically affecting the associated grades.
