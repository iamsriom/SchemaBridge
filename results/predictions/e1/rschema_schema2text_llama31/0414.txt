The rschema_0414 database is designed to manage information about cities, companies, venues, sales points, sales plans, and devices. The City table stores details about cities, with each city identified by a unique City_ID, and having a name and a province associated with it. The Company table contains information about companies, including their address, a unique Company_ID, and a name. The Venue table holds data about venues, which are identified by a Venue_ID, have a name, and a description. The Sales_Point table represents sales points, which are associated with a city, a company, and a venue, and are identified by a unique Sales_Point_ID. Each sales point has a name and an address. The Sales_Plan table stores details about sales plans, which are linked to a company and a sales point, and are identified by a unique Sales_Plan_ID. Each sales plan has a name, a description, a start and end date, and is associated with a sales point. The Device table contains information about devices, which are linked to a sales point and identified by a unique Device_ID, and have a model and a name. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: the City_ID in the Sales_Point table references the City_ID in the City table, the Company_ID in the Sales_Point and Sales_Plan tables reference the Company_ID in the Company table, the Venue_ID in the Sales_Point table references the Venue_ID in the Venue table, and the Sales_Point_ID in the Sales_Plan and Device tables reference the Sales_Point_ID in the Sales_Point table.
