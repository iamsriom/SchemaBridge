The rschema_0416 database is designed to manage user interactions and job information. The User table stores information about each user, including their avatar, email, nickname, password, registration time, role, and username, with a unique identifier for each user. The Company table contains details about each company, such as their address, name, profile, and contact information, with a unique identifier for each company. The Conversation table represents a conversation between two users, storing the conversation ID, message content, send time, and the IDs of the sender and receiver, with foreign keys referencing the User table to establish relationships between users. The Message_Board table stores messages posted by users, including the message content, send time, and the user who posted it, with a foreign key referencing the User table. The Job_Info table contains information about job openings, including the company ID, job category, education level, requirements, salary, title, and work location, with a unique identifier for each job. The User_and_Conversation table is a composite key that represents a conversation between two users, with foreign keys referencing the Conversation and User tables to establish relationships between users and conversations. The User_and_Message table stores messages posted by users, with a composite key referencing the Message_Board and User tables to establish relationships between users and messages. The Resume_Submission and Resume_Save tables store information about users who have submitted or saved job applications, with foreign keys referencing the Job_Info and User tables to establish relationships between users and job openings. The Company_and_Job_Info table contains duplicate information about job openings, with a composite key referencing the Job_Info and Company tables to establish relationships between companies and job openings.
