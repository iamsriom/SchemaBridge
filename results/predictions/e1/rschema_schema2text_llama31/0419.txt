The rschema_0419 database is designed to manage information about a restaurant, its dishes, users, orders, and shopping carts. The database contains five tables: Dish, User, Order, Shopping_cart, and Order_detail. The Dish table stores details about each dish, including its type, name, ingredients, price, recommendation index, recommended status, special features, and taste. Each dish is uniquely identified by a unique ID, which is the primary key of the table. The User table holds information about the restaurant's customers, including their name, address, phone number, password, registration time, and account status, with a unique ID serving as the primary key. The Order table stores details about each order, such as the delivery address, time, and status, with a unique order ID as its primary key. The Shopping_cart table links dishes to users, indicating the quantity of each dish added to a user's cart, with a composite primary key consisting of the dish ID, user ID, and add time, and foreign keys referencing the Dish and User tables to ensure data consistency. The Order_detail table stores the details of each order, including the dish ID, quantity, order ID, total amount, and unit price, with a composite primary key of the dish ID and order ID, and foreign keys referencing the Dish and Order tables to maintain referential integrity.
