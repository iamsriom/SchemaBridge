The rschema_0423 database is designed to manage an online learning platform, where users can enroll in courses, view chapters, and interact with instructors. The Course table stores information about each course, including its ID, category, description, name, difficulty level, number of lessons, whether it's recommended, target audience, and total duration. The Chapter table represents individual chapters within a course, with each chapter having a unique ID, description, name, duration, and references to the next, parent, and previous chapters. The Instructor table contains details about each instructor, such as their ID, name, personal profile, professional field, and teaching experience. The Student table stores information about each registered user, including their ID, account status, age, contact information, gender, membership level, points earned, registration time, and avatar. The Course_material table links courses to their respective materials, which are stored in the database with their IDs, names, and URLs. The Instructor_teaching table tracks the courses taught by each instructor, along with their ratings and student feedback. Finally, the Viewing_record table records each student's viewing history, including the chapter ID, completion status, student ID, view count, and viewing duration, with foreign keys referencing the Chapter and Student tables to establish relationships between these entities.
