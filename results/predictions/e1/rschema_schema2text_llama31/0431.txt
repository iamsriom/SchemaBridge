The rschema_0431 database is designed to manage information about users, doctors, appointments, and medical history. At its core, the User table stores contact information, passwords, unique user IDs, and usernames for all registered users. Each user is associated with a unique appointment through the Appointment table, which contains details about the appointment time, status, and the doctor and user involved. The Doctor table holds information about medical professionals, including their department affiliation, title, and ID, with each doctor belonging to a specific department as defined in the Department table. The Schedule table keeps track of a doctor's availability, listing their schedule, time slots, and remaining appointments for each date. The History table stores medical history for each user, including the appointment ID, operation type, and operation time, with each history entry linked to a specific appointment and user. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys, such as the unique User_ID, Doctor_ID, and History_ID, as well as foreign keys that ensure consistency between related tables, like the relationships between users and appointments, doctors and departments, and appointments and doctors. Additionally, some columns, like Contact_Information and Password in the User table, and Department_ID in the Doctor table, do not allow null values, while others, like Appointment_Time and Operation_Time, are restricted to specific data types to ensure accurate date and time entries.
