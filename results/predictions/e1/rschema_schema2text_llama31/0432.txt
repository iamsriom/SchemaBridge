The rschema_0432 database is designed to manage information about students, professors, courses, and departments within an educational institution. The database consists of five tables: student, professor, course, course_registration, and department. The student table stores information about each student, including their unique student ID, name, address, enrollment year, date of birth, and the department they belong to, which is linked to the department table through a foreign key that allows for cascading updates and deletions. The professor table contains details about each professor, such as their unique professor ID, name, office number, office start time, and the department they belong to, also linked to the department table. The course table holds information about each course, including its unique course ID, name, semester, year, teaching professor ID, class day, course duration, and the department it belongs to, with foreign keys linking it to both the professor and department tables. The course_registration table tracks the registration of students for specific courses, with a composite primary key consisting of the student ID, registration time, and course ID, and foreign keys linking it to both the student and course tables. The department table stores basic information about each department, including its unique department ID and name.
