The rschema_0435 database is used to manage data related to space exploration missions, specifically the probes, scientific instruments, celestial bodies, and observation records. The Probe table stores information about each probe, including its launch date, name, operational status, unique identifier, remaining fuel, service life, and weight. The Scientific_instrument table contains details about the instruments used on the probes, such as their accuracy, calibration data, name, operating parameters, power consumption, and weight. The Celestial_body table holds data about the celestial bodies being studied, including their identifier, name, orbital parameters, physical characteristics, research value, and type. The Probe_instrument_configuration table links probes to the scientific instruments they carry, with each configuration uniquely identified by the probe and instrument IDs. The Observation_record table stores records of observations made of celestial bodies, including the celestial body being observed, observation accuracy, observation data, results, and the time of observation. The Exploration_mission table tracks the details of each mission, including the celestial body being explored, the execution status, mission priority, resource allocation, the probe used, its flight trajectory, and the status of scientific data acquisition. Each probe is uniquely identified by its ID, and each mission is linked to a specific probe and celestial body, with the primary keys of the Probe and Celestial_body tables referenced in the Exploration_mission table. The Celestial_body table is also referenced in the Observation_record table, and the Scientific_instrument table is referenced in the Probe_instrument_configuration table, ensuring data consistency and relationships between these entities.
