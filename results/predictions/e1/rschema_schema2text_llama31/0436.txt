The rschema_0436 database is designed to manage a website's content and user interactions. It consists of nine tables, each representing a distinct entity within the website's structure. The administrator table stores information about the website's administrators, including their unique identifiers, usernames, passwords, emails, creation and update times, and serves as the primary key for the database. The news_article table contains details about published articles, such as their titles, content, authors, publication times, categories, tags, creation and update times, and is identified by a unique article_id. The website_information table holds information about the website itself, including its name, logo, copyright details, creation and update times, and is identified by a unique website_id. The member table stores user account information, including unique identifiers, usernames, passwords, emails, registration times, and update times, and serves as the primary key for the database. The advertisement table contains details about advertisements, including their titles, content, publication times, creation and update times, and is identified by a unique advertisement_id. The message table stores user comments and replies to articles, including the user's unique identifier, the article being commented on, the comment content, the time of posting, and the reply content, and is identified by a unique message_id. The administrator_publish_news table tracks which administrators are responsible for publishing which articles, linking administrators to articles through their unique identifiers. The administrator_manage_advertisement table tracks which administrators are responsible for managing which advertisements, linking administrators to advertisements through their unique identifiers. Finally, the user_message table tracks which users have commented on which messages, linking users to messages through their unique identifiers.
