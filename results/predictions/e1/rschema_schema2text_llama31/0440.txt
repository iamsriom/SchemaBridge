The rschema_0440 database is designed to manage and track the status, maintenance, and inventory of devices within an organization. The Device table stores information about each device, including its creation and update times, department, model, group, IP address, person in charge, product name, type, and usage location. The User table contains details about the individuals involved in the device management process, such as their name, role, and contact information. The Device_Status table tracks the current status of each device, including the last update time and any remarks, and is linked to the Device table through a foreign key referencing the device's unique identifier. The Device_Maintenance table records maintenance activities performed on devices, including the content of the maintenance, the person responsible, the result, and the user's satisfaction, and is linked to both the Device and User tables through foreign keys. The Device_Repair_Request table tracks requests for device repairs, including the handling personnel, status, and time, as well as the requester's ID, and is linked to both the Device and User tables through foreign keys. The Device_Inventory table tracks the inventory of devices, including their location and the person responsible for the inventory, and is linked to both the Device and User tables through foreign keys. Each of these tables has a unique identifier or set of identifiers that serve as primary keys, ensuring data integrity and preventing duplicate entries, and some tables also have composite primary keys to account for multiple relationships between entities.
