The rschema_0442 database is designed to manage data related to medical waste management. It consists of seven tables: medical_institution, department, medical_waste, traceability_code, medical_waste_inbound, medical_waste_temporary_storage_outbound, and transport_vehicle. The medical_institution table stores information about medical institutions, including their unique identifier, name, address, and contact information. Each medical institution is associated with one or more departments, which are stored in the department table, along with their unique identifier, the identifier of the medical institution they belong to, name, and description. The medical_waste table contains information about medical waste, including its unique identifier, the identifier of the medical institution and department it belongs to, type, weight, generation time, collector's identifier, collection time, traceability code identifier, and current status. The traceability_code table stores information about traceability codes, including their unique identifier, number, generation time, and the identifier of the medical waste they are associated with. The medical_waste_inbound table tracks the inbound medical waste, including the identifier of the medical institution and medical waste, batch number, expected and actual quantity, expected and actual weight, inbound time, collector's identifier, and confirmer's identifier. The medical_waste_temporary_storage_outbound table tracks the outbound medical waste, including the identifier of the medical institution and medical waste, batch number, outbound quantity and weight, outbound time, transporter's identifier, and confirmer's identifier. The transport_vehicle table stores information about transport vehicles, including the identifier of the medical waste and vehicle, transport start and end time, and receiver. Each of these tables has a primary key, and some tables have foreign keys that reference other tables to establish relationships between them, such as the department being associated with a medical institution, medical waste being associated with a department and medical institution, and traceability codes being associated with medical waste. The database uses InnoDB as its storage engine and UTF-8mb3 as its character set.
