The rschema_0443 database is designed to manage the tracking and monitoring of assets and user interactions within an organization. The database consists of seven tables: user_information, asset_information, status_log_information, account_dynamic_log_information, user_asset_relationship, device_number_log_id, and asset_account_dynamic_log_relationship. The user_information table stores information about users, including their unique user ID, username, login password, registered phone number, and role permissions. The asset_information table contains details about the assets, such as device ID, name, number, model, status, calibration date, and responsible person. The status_log_information table tracks the borrowing and return of assets, including the device number, borrower, borrowing time, and return time, with each log entry having a unique log ID. The account_dynamic_log_information table records the dynamic changes to asset ownership, including the device number, borrowing and returning accounts, and remarks. The user_asset_relationship table establishes the connection between users and assets, linking a user ID to a device number, while the device_number_log_id table links a device number to a log ID, and the asset_account_dynamic_log_relationship table connects a device number to a dynamic log ID. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, ensuring data integrity and consistency, with most relationships set to cascade on delete and update, meaning that when a related record is deleted or updated, the dependent records will also be updated or deleted accordingly.
