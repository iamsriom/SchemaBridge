The rschema_0445 database is designed to manage personnel, units, and various records related to their activities. The database consists of 13 tables, each representing a distinct entity. The personnel table stores information about individuals, including their ID, name, type, contact phone, email, health status, entry permission, and the unit they belong to. The unit table contains details about the units, such as their ID, name, type, and the administrator in charge. The administrator table stores information about the administrators, including their ID, name, and management role. The health record entry table links personnel to their health records, while the access personnel table links personnel to access records. The access point entry table links access records to access control points. The appointment table stores information about scheduled appointments, including the ID, scheduled entry and exit times, status, visitor name, and the personnel being visited. The health record table stores health records for personnel, including the appointment ID, scheduled entry and exit times, status, and the personnel ID. The access record table stores access records for personnel, including the record ID, entry and exit times, personnel ID, and access control ID. The access control table stores information about access control points, including their ID, name, location, and status. The affiliation table links personnel to the units they belong to, while the management table links administrators to the units they manage. The visit appointment table links appointments to the personnel being visited. Each table has a primary key, and some tables have composite primary keys to ensure data integrity. Foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables, with cascade delete and update rules applied to maintain consistency.
