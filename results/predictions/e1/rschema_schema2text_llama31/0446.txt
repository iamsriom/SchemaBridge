The rschema_0446 database is designed to manage data related to crop growth and monitoring. At its core, it contains information about users, who are identified by a unique user ID and have a username, password, phone number, registration time, last login time, and role. Each user is associated with a sensor, which is a device that collects data on soil moisture, temperature, rainfall, light intensity, and other environmental factors. Sensors are linked to specific locations and are also associated with meteorological data, which includes temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed, and light intensity. This meteorological data is used to inform the growth of crops, which are represented by a unique crop ID, name, type, and model ID. Crop growth models are defined in the growth_model table, which contains information about the model's schema, parameters, and creation time. Users can perform operations on crops, such as watering and fertilizing, which are tracked in the user_operation table. Additionally, the system evaluates the performance of both crops and growth models, with the system_performance_evaluation and model_performance_evaluation tables storing data on evaluation metrics such as crop yields and model accuracy. The user_water_and_fertilizer_supply table tracks the supply of water and fertilizers to crops, while linking it to the associated sensor and user who performed the supply.
