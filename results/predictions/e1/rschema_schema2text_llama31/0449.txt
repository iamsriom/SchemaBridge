The rschema_0449 database is designed to manage employee information and their associated data. It consists of six tables: employee, position, training, department, employee_position_change, and training_personnel_assignment. The employee table stores details about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, gender, date of birth, join date, leave date, position, contact information, email, status, and department. The department table contains information about each department, including its unique identifier, name, function, and plan. The position table lists the available positions within the organization, including their unique identifier, name, responsibilities, and requirements. The training table stores details about each training session, including its unique identifier, date, location, content, and trainer. The employee_position_change table tracks changes to an employee's position over time, including the employee's unique identifier, the new position's unique identifier, the date of the change, and the reason for the change. The training_personnel_assignment table links employees to the training sessions they have attended, with each entry containing the employee's unique identifier and the training session's unique identifier. The employee table has a foreign key referencing the department table, meaning that each employee is assigned to a specific department, and any changes to the department will automatically be reflected in the employee's record. Similarly, the employee_position_change and training_personnel_assignment tables have foreign keys referencing the employee and training tables, respectively, ensuring that any changes to these tables will be reflected in the corresponding records.
