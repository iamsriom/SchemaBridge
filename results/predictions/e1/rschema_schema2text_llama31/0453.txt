The rschema_0453 database is designed to manage information about stores, their departments, and the employees working within those departments. The store table contains details about each store, including its unique identifier, name, address, and contact phone number, with all fields being required. The department table stores information about each department within a store, including its unique identifier, name, the store it belongs to, its location distribution, contact phone number, and the manager's user ID. The department table has a foreign key referencing the store table, meaning that when a store is deleted, all its associated departments will also be deleted, and the department's belonging_store field is required to reference a valid store ID. Additionally, the department table has another foreign key referencing the user table, indicating the manager of the department, which also triggers deletion of the department when the manager is deleted. The user table stores information about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, position, phone number, the department they belong to, and their salary, with all fields being required. The user table has a foreign key referencing the department table, meaning that when a department is deleted, all its associated employees will also be deleted, and the user's belonging_department field is required to reference a valid department ID.
