The rschema_0454 database is designed to manage the supply chain process of a company, specifically focusing on the distribution of products from suppliers to chain supermarkets. The database consists of seven tables: supply_request, product, requested_product, chain_supermarket, distribution_order, purchase_order, and purchased_product. The supply_request table stores information about each request made by a chain supermarket for a specific product, including the requesting supermarket's ID, the time of the request, and the ID of the requested product. The product table contains details about each product, such as its ID, name, price, and current inventory quantity. The requested_product table links supply requests to specific products, indicating the quantity requested. The chain_supermarket table holds information about each chain supermarket, including its ID, name, address, and contact details. The distribution_order table tracks the distribution of products, including the distribution order ID, time, supply request ID, and status. The purchase_order table stores information about each purchase made by the company, including the purchase order ID, date, and status. Finally, the purchased_product table links purchase orders to specific products, indicating the quantity and price of each product purchased. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that supply requests are linked to valid chain supermarkets, distribution orders are linked to valid supply requests, and purchased products are linked to valid purchase orders and products.
