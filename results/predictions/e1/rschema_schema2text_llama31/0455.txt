The rschema_0455 database is designed to track parking information for vehicles. The database contains four tables: parking_space, entry, exit, and vehicle. The parking_space table stores information about each parking space, including its unique identifier, the license plate number of the vehicle occupying it, and the entry and exit identifiers associated with that space. The entry and exit tables store information about each entry and exit event, respectively, with unique identifiers and statuses for each. The vehicle table stores information about each vehicle, including its license plate number, entry time, and exit time. The parking_space table has a primary key constraint on its parking_space_id, and foreign key constraints referencing the entry_id and exit_id columns in the entry and exit tables, respectively, with ON DELETE CASCADE rules to automatically delete related parking space records when the corresponding entry or exit record is deleted. The occupied_vehicle column in the parking_space table also references the license_plate_number column in the vehicle table, with an ON DELETE CASCADE rule to automatically delete related parking space records when the vehicle is deleted.
