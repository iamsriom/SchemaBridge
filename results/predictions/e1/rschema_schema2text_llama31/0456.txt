The rschema_0456 database is designed to manage the operations of a supermarket chain, including inventory management, purchases, payments, and distribution of products. The database consists of seven tables: supermarket, product_inventory, distribution_department, purchase_order, payment_order, supply_request, and distribution. The supermarket table stores information about each individual supermarket, including its unique identifier, name, address, and manager's phone number. The product_inventory table contains details about each product, such as its unique identifier, name, quantity in stock, and storage location. The distribution_department table holds information about each distribution department, including its unique identifier, name, address, and manager's phone number. The purchase_order table tracks purchases made by the supermarkets, including the product identifier, purchase date, supplier, and quantity purchased, with a foreign key referencing the product_inventory table to ensure data consistency. The payment_order table records payments made for these purchases, including the order identifier, payment amount, date, and status, with a foreign key referencing the purchase_order table to link payments to their corresponding orders. The supply_request table stores requests made by supermarkets for products, including the supermarket identifier, product identifier, request time, and requested quantity, with foreign keys referencing the supermarket and product_inventory tables to ensure data consistency. The distribution table tracks the distribution of products to the distribution departments, including the department identifier, product identifier, distribution quantity, time, and a unique identifier, with foreign keys referencing the distribution_department and product_inventory tables to ensure data consistency.
