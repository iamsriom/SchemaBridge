The rschema_0457 database is designed to manage the inventory and supply chain operations of a chain of supermarkets. The Chain_supermarket table stores information about each individual supermarket, including its address, contact details, a unique identifier, and its name. The Product table contains details about the products sold in the supermarkets, such as their inventory quantity, last update date, a unique identifier, and the product name. The Supplier table holds information about the suppliers of these products, including their address, contact details, a unique identifier, and their name. The Supply_request table represents the requests made by supermarkets for specific products, including the product ID, a unique request ID, the date of the request, the requested quantity, and the ID of the supermarket making the request. The Procurement_list table tracks the purchases made by the supermarkets from suppliers, including a unique list ID, the product ID, the purchase date, the quantity purchased, and the supplier ID. The Delivery_notification and Stock-out_notification tables are used to notify the supermarkets about the delivery and stock-out status of the requested products, with the Delivery_notification table containing the delivery date, a unique notification ID, and the ID of the supply request, while the Stock-out_notification table contains the stock-out date and the ID of the supply request.
