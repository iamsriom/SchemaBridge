The rschema_0458 database is designed to manage information about books, their sales, and the customers who purchase them. The Address table stores unique addresses, each identified by a unique Address_ID, which is also used to link customers to their addresses in the Member table. The Member table contains details about each customer, including their name, password, telephone number, and points earned, with the Address_ID referencing the Address table to establish the relationship. The Book table holds information about each book, such as its title, author, ISBN, price, publication date, publisher, and type. The Order table tracks each purchase made by a customer, identified by a unique Order_ID and linked to the Member table through the Member_ID, which references the Member table to establish the customer who made the purchase. The Order_Detail table contains the specifics of each order, including the book purchased, the quantity, and the order it belongs to, with both Book_ID and Order_ID referencing their respective tables to establish the relationships.
