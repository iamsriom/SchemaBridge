The rschema_0459 database is designed to manage information about employees, departments, and projects within an organization. The employee table stores details about each staff member, including their unique employee ID, name, department ID, salary, job position, and education level. The department table contains information about each department, such as its unique department ID, name, and manager's employee ID. The project table holds details about each project, including its unique project ID, name, department ID, and the employee ID of the person responsible for it. The employee_project_participation table tracks the participation of each employee in various projects, with columns for the employee's ID, project ID, and the time period of their participation. Each table has a primary key to uniquely identify its records, and there are foreign keys to establish relationships between tables, such as the department ID in the employee table referencing the department ID in the department table, and the responsible person in the project table referencing the employee ID in the employee table. The relationships between tables are further defined by constraints, including ON DELETE CASCADE rules, which automatically delete related records when a record in the referenced table is deleted, ensuring data consistency throughout the database.
