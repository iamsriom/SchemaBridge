The rschema_0460 database is designed to manage information about malls, their departments, and the employees working within those departments. The Mall table stores details about individual malls, including their address, contact number, unique Mall ID, and name. Each mall is uniquely identified by its Mall ID, which is the primary key. The Department table contains information about the various departments within the malls, such as their contact number, unique Department ID, name, and location distribution. The Department ID serves as the primary key for this table. The Employee table holds data about the employees working in these departments, including their department ID, unique Employee ID, name, phone number, position, and salary. The Department ID is a foreign key referencing the Department table, indicating which department each employee belongs to, and it is not allowed to be null. This relationship is enforced with a cascade update and delete rule, meaning if a department is deleted, all employees associated with it will also be deleted.
