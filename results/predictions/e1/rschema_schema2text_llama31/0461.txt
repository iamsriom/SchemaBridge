The rschema_0461 database is designed to manage a retail business, storing information about products, customers, orders, and delivery personnel. The Product table contains details about the items sold, including their category, description, location, name, product ID, stock quantity, unit price, and unit of measurement. The Delivery_Personnel table stores information about the individuals responsible for delivering products, with fields for their ID, gender, name, phone number, and position. The Customer table holds data about the individuals or businesses making purchases, including their address, age, ID, gender, name, and phone number. The Order table tracks customer transactions, with fields for the customer's ID, delivery address, order creation time, order number, status, and total price, referencing the Customer table for the customer's ID. The Order_Product table links orders to the products they contain, with fields for the order ID, product ID, and quantity, referencing both the Order and Product tables. The Order_Delivery table manages the delivery process, with fields for the delivery personnel's ID, estimated delivery time, and order ID, referencing both the Delivery_Personnel and Order tables.
