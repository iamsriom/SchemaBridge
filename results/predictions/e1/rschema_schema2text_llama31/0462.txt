The rschema_0462 database is designed to manage information about members, books, orders, and purchases. At its core, the database contains a table for Member, which stores details about each individual, including their name, address, phone number, username, and password, as well as their consumption amount, points, and postal code. The Member_ID is the primary key, uniquely identifying each member. The Book table holds information about the books available, including the title, author, category, ISBN, price, publication date, and publisher, with the ISBN serving as the primary key. The Order table records transactions made by members, including the order date, sales amount, and shipping date, with the Member_ID referencing the Member table to link each order to its corresponding member. The Order_ID is the primary key for this table. The Purchase table tracks the specific books purchased by members, linking each purchase to the corresponding order and book through the Order_ID and ISBN, respectively. The ISBN is a foreign key referencing the Book table, and the Order_ID is a foreign key referencing the Order table, ensuring that each purchase is associated with a valid order and book.
