The rschema_0463 database is designed to manage information about malls, their departments, and the employees working within those departments. At the core of this database is the Mall table, which stores details about individual malls, including their address, contact number, unique identifier, and name. The Department table is used to organize the various departments within each mall, with each department having its own unique identifier, name, location, and details about its manager, such as their employee ID and tenure. The Department table is linked to both the Mall table and the Employee table, with the Mall_ID referencing the unique identifier of the mall where the department is located, and the Manager_Employee_ID referencing the employee ID of the department's manager. The Employee table stores information about individual employees, including their department ID, emergency contact details, name, phone number, position, and salary. The Employee table is also linked to the Department table through the Department_ID, which references the unique identifier of the department where the employee works. This database schema ensures that each department is associated with a specific mall and manager, and each employee is associated with a specific department, thereby maintaining a clear organizational structure and relationships between entities.
