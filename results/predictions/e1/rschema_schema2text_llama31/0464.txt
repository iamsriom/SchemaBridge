The rschema_0464 database is designed to manage information about employees, departments, and projects within an organization. The Employee table stores details about each employee, including their ID, name, position, salary, education level, and department ID, which references the Department table to establish their affiliation. The Department table contains information about each department, such as its ID, name, and the ID of its manager, which is linked to the Employee table to identify the manager's role. The Project table holds details about each project, including its ID, name, department ID, and manager's employee ID, which references both the Department and Employee tables to establish the project's scope and leadership. The Participation table tracks the involvement of employees in various projects, with each entry containing the employee's ID, project ID, and serving as a composite primary key to uniquely identify each participation instance.
