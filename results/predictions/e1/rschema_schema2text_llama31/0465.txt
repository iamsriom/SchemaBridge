The rschema_0465 database is designed to manage user accounts and their email communications. The User table stores information about each registered user, including the time their account was created, their email address, and their password. The email address is the primary key, meaning it uniquely identifies each user and cannot be duplicated. The Attachment_Information table holds details about files that have been uploaded, such as the file name, path, size, and the time it was uploaded. Each attachment is assigned a unique identifier, which serves as its primary key. The Email table represents all emails sent and received by users, containing details like the delivery time, content, subject, recipient and sender email addresses, and the time it was sent. The Email table has a foreign key referencing the Attachment_Information table, indicating that an email can be associated with one or more attachments. Additionally, the Email table has foreign keys referencing the User table, meaning that each email is linked to a sender and a recipient, and these relationships are enforced through the database to prevent orphaned emails or users. The database also includes indexes to ensure efficient data retrieval and storage, but no other constraints have been specified.
