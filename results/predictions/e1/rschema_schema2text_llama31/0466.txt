The rschema_0466 database is designed to manage a restaurant's customer and order information. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact number, address, and preferred payment method, with a unique customer ID serving as the primary key. The Employee table contains information about the restaurant's staff, such as their name, contact number, and position, with a unique employee ID as the primary key. The Menu table lists the dishes available for ordering, with a unique menu ID and price for each dish. The Customer_Order table tracks the orders placed by customers, including the customer's ID, delivery location and time, the menu item ordered, order quantity, and status, with a composite primary key consisting of the customer ID, menu ID, and order time. The Customer_ID in the Customer_Order table references the Customer table, ensuring that only valid customer IDs can be used, and the Menu_ID references the Menu table, linking each order to the corresponding menu item. Additionally, the Customer_ID in the Customer_Order table cannot be null, and the Menu_ID also cannot be null, as these are essential details for each order.
