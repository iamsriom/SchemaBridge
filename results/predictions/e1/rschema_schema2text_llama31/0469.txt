The rschema_0469 database is designed to manage information about property owners, their housing, employees, departments, and fee collections. The Property_owner table stores details about the individuals who own properties, including their contact number, name, workplace, and a unique identifier called Owner_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Housing table contains information about the properties themselves, such as their floor area and house number, with the house number being the primary key and the Owner_ID referencing the Property_owner table to establish ownership. The Department table represents different departments within an organization, with each department having a unique identifier, a name, a phone number, and a supervisor's employee ID, which references the Employee table to establish the supervisory relationship. The Employee table stores details about individual employees, including their address, contact number, date of birth, department ID, which references the Department table, and a unique identifier called Employee_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Fee_collection table tracks fee collections from property owners, with each collection recorded by an employee, referencing the Employee table, and associated with a specific house number, which references the Housing table, and a date, with the combination of these three fields serving as the primary key.
