The rschema_0470 database is designed to manage the operations of a food delivery service, encompassing various entities such as employees, menu items, orders, and payment requests. The Enterprise_Employee table stores information about the staff, including their names, phone numbers, and addresses, with each employee uniquely identified by their Employee_ID. The Menu table contains details about the dishes available for delivery, including their names, prices, and whether they are part of any special offers, with each dish identified by a unique Dish_ID. The Delivery_Person table holds information about the delivery personnel, including their names, phone numbers, and addresses, with each delivery person uniquely identified by their Delivery_Person_ID. The Order table represents the orders placed by customers, including the delivery address, time, payment method, total price, and the employee who took the order, with each order uniquely identified by an Order_ID and linked to the corresponding employee through the Ordering_Employee column. The Order_Dish table links orders to the specific dishes ordered, with each entry representing a quantity of a particular dish in an order, and the Order_ID and Dish_ID columns referencing the Order and Menu tables, respectively. The Delivery_Record table tracks the delivery status of each order, including the delivery time and the responsible delivery person, with each entry uniquely identified by a Delivery_Record_ID and linked to the corresponding order and delivery person through the Delivery_Order_ID and Responsible_Delivery_Person_ID columns. The Payment_Request table manages payment requests for orders, including the employee who made the request, the order ID, the requested payment amount, and the payment status, with each payment request uniquely identified by a Payment_Request_ID and linked to the corresponding employee and order through the Paying_Employee_ID and Paying_Order_ID columns.
