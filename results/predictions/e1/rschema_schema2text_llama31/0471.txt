The rschema_0471 database is used to manage customer information and their associated credit card transactions. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact number, application status, and address, with a unique identifier known as the Customer ID. The Customer ID is the primary key, meaning it uniquely identifies each customer and cannot be null. The Credit card table contains information about each credit card, such as its card number, activation status, credit limit, type, and validity period, with the card number serving as the primary key. The Customer ID in the Credit card table references the Customer ID in the Customer table, establishing a relationship between a customer and their credit card. This relationship is enforced through a foreign key constraint, which means that if a customer is deleted from the Customer table, their associated credit card will also be deleted, as specified by the ON DELETE CASCADE rule. The Transaction record table stores details of each transaction made using a credit card, including the card number, transaction ID, amount, date, description, and status, with the transaction ID serving as the primary key. The card number in the Transaction record table references the card number in the Credit card table, establishing a relationship between a credit card and its transactions. This relationship is also enforced through a foreign key constraint, which means that if a credit card is deleted from the Credit card table, all its associated transactions will also be deleted, as specified by the ON DELETE CASCADE rule.
