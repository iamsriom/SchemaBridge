The rschema_0472 database is designed to manage information about students, instructors, and their interactions through homework assignments. The Student table contains details about each student, including their class, email, name, phone number, and a unique Student_ID that serves as the primary key. The Instructor table stores information about each instructor, such as the course they teach, their email, a unique Instructor_ID, name, and phone number, with Instructor_ID being the primary key. The Homework table represents individual homework assignments, featuring a unique Homework_ID, the content of the assignment, the submission time, and the Student_ID of the student who submitted it, which is a foreign key referencing the Student_ID in the Student table, ensuring that only valid student IDs can be used for submissions. The Homework table does not allow null values for the Submitting_student_ID, indicating that every homework submission must be associated with a valid student.
