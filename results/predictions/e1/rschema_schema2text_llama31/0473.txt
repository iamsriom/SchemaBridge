The rschema_0473 database is designed to manage a bookstore's operations, including customer information, publications, and orders. The Publication table stores details about each book, such as its title, type, price, and author, with a unique identifier called Publication_ID serving as the primary key. The Customer table contains information about each customer, including their name, email, phone number, and password, with a unique Customer_ID serving as the primary key. The Administrator table stores details about the bookstore's administrators, including their name, email, phone number, and password, with a unique Administrator_ID serving as the primary key. The Order table tracks each purchase made by a customer, including the customer's ID, order status, payment method, shipping address, and the publication being purchased, with a composite primary key consisting of the customer's ID, publication ID, and order time. The Order table also references the Publication and Customer tables through foreign keys, which ensure that only valid publications and customers can be associated with an order, and that when a customer or publication is deleted, all related orders are automatically deleted as well.
