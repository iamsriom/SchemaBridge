The rschema_0474 database is designed to manage student and instructor information, as well as their interactions through assignments and grading. The Student table stores details about each student, including their class and name, with a unique identifier, Student_ID, serving as the primary key. The Instructor table contains information about each instructor, such as their course and name, with Instructor_ID being the primary key. The Assignment table holds details about each assignment, including its content and a unique identifier, Assignment_ID, which is also the primary key, and it is linked to a student through the Student_ID foreign key, ensuring that each assignment is associated with a specific student. The Assignment_Grading table tracks the grading of each assignment, including the evaluation, grading time, score, and a unique identifier, Assignment_ID, which is the primary key, and it is linked to both the Assignment table through the Assignment_ID foreign key and the Instructor table through the Instructor_ID foreign key, allowing for multiple grading instances per assignment per instructor.
