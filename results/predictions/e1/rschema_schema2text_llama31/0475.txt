The rschema_0475 database is designed to manage information about supermarkets, their departments, employees, products, and sales performance. The supermarket table stores details about individual supermarkets, including their unique identifier, name, address, manager, and phone number. The department table contains information about each department within a supermarket, such as its name, manager, contact number, and the supermarket it belongs to, with a foreign key referencing the supermarket's unique identifier to establish a relationship between the two tables. The employee table holds details about each employee, including their unique identifier, name, contact information, position, salary, and the department they work in, with a foreign key referencing the department's unique identifier to establish a relationship between the two tables. The product table stores information about each product, including its unique identifier, name, model, unit price, and quantity. The salesperson_product_distribution table tracks the sales performance of each product by each employee, including the product's unique identifier, the employee's unique identifier, sales volume, and stock level, with foreign keys referencing the product and employee tables to establish relationships between the three tables.
