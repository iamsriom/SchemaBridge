The rschema_0476 database is used to manage the operations of a pizza business, encompassing customer information, financial transactions, raw materials, suppliers, recipes, sales and purchase orders, product inventory, and production planning. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, contact information, and a unique customer ID. The Raw_Material table contains information about the raw materials used in the pizza-making process, such as the material name and specification. The Financial_Record table tracks financial transactions, including the amount, date, status, and type of transaction. The Supplier table stores information about the suppliers, including their contact information and a unique supplier ID. The Pizza_Recipe table links raw materials to their respective usage amounts in the pizza-making process, with a unique recipe ID. The Sales_Order table represents customer orders, including the expected delivery date and order status, and is linked to the Customer table through the customer ID. The Purchase_Order table manages the purchase of raw materials from suppliers, including the order quantity, supplied quantity, and supplier ID, and is linked to the Raw_Material and Supplier tables. The Product table represents the final pizza products, with a unique product ID, product name, and a link to the Pizza_Recipe table through the recipe ID. The Production_Plan table tracks the production plan, including the order ID, production status, and is linked to the Sales_Order table. The Delivery_Record table tracks the delivery of products to customers, including the delivery location, time, and status, and is linked to the Sales_Order table. The Inventory_Management table manages the inventory of products, including the product ID, quantity, and status, and is linked to the Product table.
