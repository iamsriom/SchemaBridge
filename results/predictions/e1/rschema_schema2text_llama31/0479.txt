The rschema_0479 database is designed to manage information about a company's subsidiaries, departments, and employees. The Subsidiary table stores details about each subsidiary, including its contact address, manager's ID, name, and phone number, with a unique identifier for each subsidiary. The Manager_ID column references the Employee table, establishing a relationship between the subsidiary's manager and the employee who holds that position. The Business_Department table contains information about each department within a subsidiary, such as its address, name, phone number, and the subsidiary it belongs to, with a unique identifier for each department and a foreign key referencing the Subsidiary table to establish the relationship between the department and its subsidiary. The Employee table stores details about each employee, including their department ID, name, family member's name and relationship, phone number, and position, with a unique identifier for each employee and a foreign key referencing the Business_Department table to establish the relationship between the employee and their department.
