The rschema_0480 database is designed to manage information about properties, agents, users, and their responsibilities. The Property table stores details about individual properties, including their address, built area, construction year, whether they are apartments, a unique property ID, property certificate, photos, sale price, and sales status. The Agent table contains information about real estate agents, such as their ID, contact number, home address, and name. The User table holds data about users, including their password, role, unique user ID, and username. The Responsibility table links agents to properties, indicating the start and end times of their responsibilities, and it ensures that each agent is associated with at least one property and vice versa through a composite primary key and foreign keys referencing the Agent and Property tables, with the Agent_ID referencing the Agent table and Property_ID referencing the Property table, and both Agent_ID and Property_ID are not nullable, enforcing referential integrity.
