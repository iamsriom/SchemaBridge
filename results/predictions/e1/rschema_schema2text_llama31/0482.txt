The rschema_0482 database is designed to manage information about clients, employees, vehicles, and maintenance tasks. The Client table stores details about each client, including their contact person, name, client number, type, contact phone number, and discount rate. The Client_Number field serves as the primary key, uniquely identifying each client. The Vehicle table, linked to the Client table through the Client_Number field, contains information about the vehicles owned by each client, such as the license plate number, category, color, and model. The Employee table stores details about the staff, including their home address, name, employee number, phone number, position, and department number, which references the Department table. The Department table contains information about the different departments, including the department name, number, phone number, and the employee number of the supervisor. The Maintenance_Task table tracks maintenance tasks performed on vehicles, including the license plate number, maintenance content, man-hours, order number, and the employee number of the maintenance worker, which references the Employee table. The Maintenance_Task table also references the Vehicle table through the License_Plate_Number field, ensuring that maintenance tasks are associated with the correct vehicle.
