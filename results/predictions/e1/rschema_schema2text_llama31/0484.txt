The rschema_0484 database is designed to manage information about suppliers, customers, products, and purchase records. The Supplier table stores details about each supplier, including their name, contact information, and address, with a unique identifier for each supplier. The Customer table contains similar information for each customer, including their name, contact details, and address. The Product table holds data on the products available, with a unique identifier for each product and its unit price. The Supplier_Product_Supply table links suppliers to the products they supply, with each supplier-product pair having a unique combination. The Purchase_Record table tracks purchase records, including the date of purchase, quantity, and supplier, with each purchase record being unique based on the combination of customer, product, supplier, and purchase date. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys, which uniquely identify each supplier, customer, product, and purchase record, as well as composite primary keys in the Supplier_Product_Supply and Purchase_Record tables. Additionally, foreign keys in the Supplier_Product_Supply and Purchase_Record tables reference the corresponding tables to ensure that only valid supplier, product, and customer IDs are used, with update and delete rules set to cascade in the Supplier_Product_Supply table and restrict in the Purchase_Record table to maintain consistency across the database.
