The rschema_0485 database is designed to manage information about wetland protection areas, users, flora and fauna, and environmental records. The database consists of six tables: wetland protection area, user, flora and fauna, biological record, environmental record, and biological record contains flora and fauna information. The wetland protection area table stores information about protected areas, including their unique identifier, name, location, and administrator, with the administrator being linked to a user through a foreign key that allows for cascading updates and deletions. The user table stores basic information about users, including their unique identifier and name. The flora and fauna table contains information about different species, including their unique identifier, name, and species. The biological record table stores information about observations made in protected areas, including the unique identifier, recorder, recording time, and the protected area where the observation was made, with the recorder being linked to a user and the protected area being linked to the wetland protection area table. The environmental record table stores environmental data, including temperature, humidity, and rainfall, for each protected area, with the protected area being linked to the wetland protection area table. The biological record contains flora and fauna information table stores the details of the flora and fauna observed during each biological record, including the quantity and growth status, with the organism being linked to the flora and fauna table and the biological record being linked to the biological record table.
