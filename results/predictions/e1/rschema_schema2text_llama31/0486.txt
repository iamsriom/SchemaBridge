The rschema_0486 database is used to manage information about mines, mineral resources, and mining records. The mine table stores details about individual mines, including their unique identifier, name, location, and the person in charge, with all fields being required. The mineral_resource table contains information about different types of mineral resources, such as their name, reserves, discovery date, and the mine where they were first discovered, with all fields being mandatory. The first_discovered_mine field in the mineral_resource table references the mine_id in the mine table, meaning that a mineral resource can only be associated with one mine, and if the mine is deleted, all associated mineral resources will also be deleted. The mining_record table stores records of mining activities, including the unique identifier, the type of mineral resource being mined, the date of mining, the volume of mining, the cost of mining, and the mine where the mining took place, with all fields being required. The mineral_resource and mine fields in the mining_record table reference the resource_id and mine_id fields in the mineral_resource and mine tables, respectively, with the same deletion rule applying, meaning that if a mineral resource or mine is deleted, all associated mining records will also be deleted.
