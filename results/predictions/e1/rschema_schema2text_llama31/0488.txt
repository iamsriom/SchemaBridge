The rschema_0488 database is designed to manage various aspects of a company's operations, particularly focusing on departments, employees, and email configurations. The Department table stores information about each department, including its ID, description, name, email address, and whether it allows customer work orders, hides the department, or requires feedback. The Department ID is the primary key, uniquely identifying each department. The Employee_Table stores details about each employee, such as their ID, name, gender, and phone number, with the Employee ID serving as the primary key. The Email_Configuration table contains settings for email servers, including the configuration ID, department ID, host address, login password, login username, and port number, with the department ID referencing the Department table to establish a relationship between email configurations and departments. The Employee_Maintenance_Email_Server table tracks maintenance activities for email servers, listing the employee ID, maintenance time, and server ID, with the primary key being a composite of these three fields, referencing the Employee_Table and Email_Configuration tables to establish relationships between employees, email servers, and their respective maintenance activities.
