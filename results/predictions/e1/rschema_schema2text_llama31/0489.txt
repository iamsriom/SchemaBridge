The rschema_0489 database is designed to manage applications, users, channels, and events. At its core, the Application_Table stores information about the applications, with each application assigned a unique Application_ID and a descriptive Name. The User_Table contains details about the users, identified by a unique ID and a chosen Username. The Channel_Table represents the channels within these applications, with each channel having a unique Channel_ID, a creation time, a creator user ID, a status, and a flag indicating whether the channel is private or not. The Channel_Table is linked to the Application_Table through the Belonging_Application field, which references the Application_ID, and to the User_Table through the Creator field, which references the user's ID. The Channel_Event_Table stores events that occur within these channels, with each event assigned a unique Event_ID, a Channel_ID referencing the parent channel, an Event_Type, a Payload_Content, and a Timestamp.
