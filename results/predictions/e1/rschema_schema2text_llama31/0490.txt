The rschema_0490 database is designed to manage the inventory and supply chain of a company. It consists of seven tables: Part, Geographic_location, Order, Supplier, Customer, Order_detail, and Supply. The Part table stores information about each item in the inventory, including its name, specifications, unit price, and current inventory level. The Geographic_location table contains details about the company's locations, such as country and region, and is referenced by both the Supplier and Customer tables, which store information about the company's suppliers and customers, respectively. The Order table tracks all orders made by customers, including the order status, delivery date, and order time. The Order_detail table links orders to specific parts and suppliers, detailing the quantity and total amount of each order. The Supply table stores quotes from suppliers for specific parts, and is linked to both the Part and Supplier tables. Each table has a primary key to uniquely identify each record, and some tables have additional constraints to ensure data integrity, such as the Location_ID fields in Supplier and Customer tables referencing the Geographic_location table, and the foreign keys in Order_detail referencing the Order, Part, and Supplier tables.
