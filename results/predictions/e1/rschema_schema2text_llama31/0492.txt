The rschema_0492 database is designed to manage organizational data, specifically focusing on departments, positions, and employees within an enterprise. The database consists of three main tables: department_table, position_table, and employee_table. The department_table stores information about each department, with a unique identifier 'id' and a 'name' that cannot be null. The position_table, on the other hand, contains details about each job position, including 'id', 'name', 'salary', and the 'belonging_department' which is a foreign key referencing the 'id' in the department_table, meaning a position must belong to a department. When a department is deleted, all positions belonging to it will also be deleted. The employee_table stores information about each employee, including their 'id', 'first name', 'last name', and 'position_id' which references the 'id' in the position_table, ensuring that each employee has a specific job. Additionally, the 'superior_employee' field references the 'id' in the employee_table, indicating the supervisor of each employee, and when an employee is deleted, their direct reports will also be deleted.
