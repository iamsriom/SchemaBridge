The rschema_0493 database is designed to manage information about enterprises, their roles, employees, attendance records, and tasks. The database contains five tables: enterprise, role, employee, attendance_record, and to-do_item. The enterprise table stores details about each enterprise, including its creation time, a unique enterprise ID, and a name. The role table contains information about roles, such as daily working hours, hourly wage, and lunch time, with a unique role ID. The employee table links employees to the enterprises and roles they belong to, storing their creation and update times, email, identification number, name, password, and role ID, with enterprise and role IDs being non-nullable foreign keys referencing the enterprise and role tables respectively. The attendance_record table tracks employee attendance, including check-in location, time, and any remarks, with a composite primary key of employee ID and check-in time, and a non-nullable foreign key referencing the employee table. The to-do_item table stores tasks assigned to employees, including completion status, creation and update times, task description, and a non-nullable foreign key referencing the employee table, with a composite primary key of employee ID and creation time.
