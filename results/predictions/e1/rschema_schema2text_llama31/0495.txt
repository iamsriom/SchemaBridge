The rschema_0495 database is designed to manage patient care and medical services within a healthcare facility. It consists of seven tables: Ward, Medicine, Patient, Medication_Dispensing, Doctor, Department, and Consultation_Record. The Ward table stores information about the different wards in the facility, including their location, number of beds, and ward level. The Medicine table contains details about the medications available, such as their inventory quantity, name, sales price, and any remarks. The Patient table holds information about the patients, including their age, contact information, gender, name, and the ward they are assigned to, with the ward ID being a non-nullable foreign key referencing the Ward table. The Medication_Dispensing table tracks the dispensing of medications to patients, with each dispensing event uniquely identified by a combination of patient ID, medicine ID, and medication retrieval time, and referencing both the Medicine and Patient tables. The Doctor table stores information about the doctors, including their department ID, gender, name, phone number, title, and years of service, with the department ID being a foreign key referencing the Department table. The Department table contains details about the different departments in the facility, including their name, phone number, location, and the ID of the person in charge, which is a non-nullable foreign key referencing the Doctor table. The Consultation_Record table stores records of consultations between doctors and patients, with each record uniquely identified by a combination of patient ID, doctor ID, and consultation time, and referencing both the Doctor and Patient tables.
