The rschema_0497 database is designed to track student participation in events, with three main tables: Student, Event, and Competition_Status. The Student table contains information about each student, including their Class, Contact_Information, Gender, Grade, Name, and a unique Student_ID, which serves as the primary key. The Event table stores details about each event, such as its Competition_Rules, Event_ID, Event_Time, Name, and Number_of_Participants, with Event_ID being the primary key. The Competition_Status table tracks the status of each student's participation in an event, including the Competition_Result, Event_ID, Registration_Time, and Student_ID, with Student_ID and Event_ID forming a composite primary key. The Student_ID in Competition_Status references the Student_ID in the Student table, and the Event_ID references the Event_ID in the Event table, establishing a relationship between a student's participation and the event they participated in. The Registration_Time in Competition_Status is not nullable, and the Event_ID and Student_ID in this table are also not nullable, ensuring that every participation record is linked to a specific event and student.
