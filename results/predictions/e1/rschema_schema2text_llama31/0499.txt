The rschema_0499 database is designed to manage information about classes, sports teams, dormitories, students, and their relationships. The Class table stores details about each class, including a unique identifier, class name, classroom location, and the major it is associated with. The Sports_team table contains information about the sports teams, including the team name, coach, and a unique team identifier. The Dormitory table stores details about the dormitories, including the building and dormitory numbers, the number of residents, and the administrator in charge. The Student table represents individual students, with attributes such as age, building number, class identifier, dormitory number, gender, name, and a unique student identifier. The Student table also includes foreign keys referencing the Class, Dormitory, and Dormitory tables to establish relationships between students and their respective classes and dormitories. The Team_membership table links students to sports teams, with each student being associated with a unique team and vice versa, and it also includes foreign keys referencing the Student and Sports_team tables to maintain referential integrity. The primary keys in the Class, Sports_team, Dormitory, Student, and Team_membership tables uniquely identify each record, while the composite primary key in the Dormitory table combines the building and dormitory numbers to uniquely identify each dormitory.
