The rschema_0500 database is designed to manage tasks and user roles within an organization. The database consists of six tables: Role_Table, Task_Type, Task_Status, Task_Priority, User, and Task_Table. The Role_Table stores information about different roles within the organization, with each role having a unique ID, a name, and a description. The Task_Type table contains details about the types of tasks that can be assigned, including a unique ID, name, and description. Similarly, the Task_Status and Task_Priority tables store information about the possible statuses and priorities of tasks, respectively. The User table stores information about the individuals within the organization, including their email, password, username, and role ID, with the role ID referencing the Role_Table to establish the relationship between users and their roles. The Task_Table stores information about each task, including its name, description, deadline, assigned user, priority, status, and type, with foreign keys referencing the User, Task_Priority, Task_Status, and Task_Type tables to establish relationships between tasks and these entities. The Task_Table also includes a primary key to uniquely identify each task, and the Assigned_User_ID, Task_Priority_ID, Task_Status_ID, and Task_Type_ID columns are not nullable, indicating that these fields must be populated for each task.
