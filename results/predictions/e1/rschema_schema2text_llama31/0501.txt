The rschema_0501 database is designed to manage information about airlines, their aircraft, crew members, passengers, flights, maintenance records, and ticket orders. The Aircraft table stores details about each aircraft, including its affiliated airline, model, status, manufacture date, and maximum passenger capacity. The Crew_Member table contains information about each crew member, such as their ID, gender, name, phone number, and position. The Passenger table holds details about each passenger, including their gender, identification number, name, phone number, and unique passenger ID. The Flight table stores information about each flight, including the aircraft it uses, departure and landing airports, departure and landing times, and the responsible captain's ID. The Maintenance_Record table tracks maintenance activities for each aircraft, including the date, description, personnel involved, and type of maintenance, with a composite primary key combining the maintenance personnel ID, aircraft ID, and maintenance date. The Ticket_Order table stores details about each ticket purchase, including the booking time, flight ID, passenger ID, seat number, and ticket price, with a composite primary key combining the passenger ID, flight ID, and booking time. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that flights are associated with valid aircraft and captains, maintenance records are linked to valid aircraft and crew members, and ticket orders are tied to valid flights and passengers.
