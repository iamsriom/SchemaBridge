The rschema_0503 database is designed to manage the inventory and transportation of materials across various warehouses and vehicles. At its core, the database consists of seven tables: Material, Warehouse, Driver, Material_Storage, Material_Dispatch, Vehicle, and Material_Transportation. The Material table stores information about the materials being transported, including their ID, name, source location, specification, type, volume, and weight. The Warehouse table contains details about each warehouse, such as its address, current inventory level, maximum capacity, name, remaining capacity, and type. The Driver table holds information about the drivers, including their age, driver's license number, gender, name, and phone number. The Material_Storage table tracks the storage of materials in each warehouse, with columns for material ID, notes, storage quantity, storage time, and warehouse ID, with a composite primary key consisting of material ID, warehouse ID, and storage time. The Material_Dispatch table manages the dispatch of materials from warehouses, with columns for dispatch quantity, dispatch time, material ID, notes, and warehouse ID, also with a composite primary key of material ID, warehouse ID, and dispatch time. The Vehicle table stores information about the vehicles, including their assigned driver ID, current load, license plate number, maximum load, vehicle ID, and type, with a foreign key referencing the Driver table. The Material_Transportation table tracks the transportation of materials, with columns for destination, driver ID, material ID, transportation start time, and vehicle ID, with a composite primary key of vehicle ID, material ID, and transportation start time, and foreign keys referencing the Driver, Material, and Vehicle tables.
