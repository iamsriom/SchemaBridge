The rschema_0504 database is designed to manage information about cultural relics, their exhibitions, repairs, and monitoring records. The Cultural_Relic table stores details about each relic, including its category, ID, excavation site, last repair time, name, and year of origin. The Staff table contains information about the staff members involved in relic maintenance, with columns for their gender, name, phone number, and unique staff ID. The Cultural_Relic_Exhibition table tracks the exhibition history of each relic, recording the exhibition start and end times, as well as the location, and uniquely identifies each exhibition by the relic ID and start time. The Cultural_Relic_Repair_Record table logs the repair history of each relic, including the repair start and end times, method, notes, and the staff member responsible, with a composite primary key combining the staff ID, relic ID, and repair start time, and referencing the Cultural_Relic and Staff tables for integrity. The Cultural_Relic_Monitoring_Record table stores monitoring data for each relic, including air quality, humidity, light, temperature, and notes, with a composite primary key combining the relic ID, monitoring time, and person in charge ID, and referencing the Cultural_Relic and Staff tables for integrity.
