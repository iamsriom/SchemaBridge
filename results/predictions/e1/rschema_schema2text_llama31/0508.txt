The rschema_0508 database is designed to manage information about members, books, and their transactions. The Member table stores details about each member, including their address, consumption amount, name, password, phone number, points, postal code, and username, with the Member_ID serving as the primary key. The Book table contains information about each book, such as its author, title, category, ISBN, price, publication date, and publisher, with the Book_ID being the primary key. The Order table tracks transactions, including the member who made the purchase, the order ID, order date, sales amount, and shipping date, with the Member_ID referencing the Member table and the Order_ID serving as the primary key. The Order_book table links books to orders, with the Book_ID referencing the Book table and the Order_ID referencing the Order table, and together they form a composite primary key. The Place_order table serves as a redundant link between members and orders, with the Member_ID referencing the Member table and the Order_ID referencing the Order table, and together they form a composite primary key.
