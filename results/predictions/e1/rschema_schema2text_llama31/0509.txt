The rschema_0509 database is designed to manage patient information and hospital operations. The Patient table stores details about each patient, including their age, emergency contact phone number, gender, identity card number, medical record number, name, and phone number, with a unique patient ID serving as the primary key. The Department table represents hospital departments, with a unique department ID as the primary key, and includes information such as the department head's ID, name, location, and contact phone number. The Doctor table stores doctor information, including their contact phone number, department ID, gender, name, title, and a unique doctor ID as the primary key, which is also referenced by the Department table. The Ward table represents hospital wards, with a unique ward ID as the primary key, and includes details such as the bed count, head ID, location, and remaining bed count, referencing the Doctor table for the head ID. The Hospitalization_Record table stores information about patient hospitalizations, including the attending physician ID, bed number, discharge time, hospitalization start time, patient ID, and ward ID, with a composite primary key of patient ID, ward ID, and hospitalization start time, referencing the Doctor, Patient, and Ward tables. The Registration_Record table stores consultation records, with a composite primary key of patient ID, doctor ID, and consultation time, referencing the Doctor and Patient tables. The Nurse table stores nurse information, including their department ID, duties, gender, name, phone number, and title, with a unique nurse ID as the primary key, referencing the Department table for the department ID.
