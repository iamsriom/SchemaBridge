The rschema_0510 database is designed to manage information about vehicles and their parking spaces. The Vehicle_information_table stores details about each vehicle, including its contact information, a unique license plate number that serves as the primary key, and the name of its owner. The License_plate_number column is the primary key, ensuring that each vehicle is uniquely identified. The Parking_space_table, on the other hand, contains information about parking spaces, with columns for the owner's name, a unique parking space number that serves as the primary key, and the status of the space. The Owner column in the Parking_space_table is a foreign key referencing the License_plate_number in the Vehicle_information_table, establishing a relationship between the owner of a vehicle and the parking space they occupy. This relationship is enforced by a foreign key constraint, which means that when a vehicle is deleted from the Vehicle_information_table, its associated parking space in the Parking_space_table will also be deleted, following the update rule of CASCADE.
