The rschema_0511 database is designed to manage information about owners, employees, departments, and charges. The Owner_Table stores details about property owners, including their name, contact number, employer, house area, room number, and a unique identifier called Owner_ID. The Department_Table contains information about different departments, such as the department head, ID, name, and phone number, with a unique Department_ID serving as the primary key. The Employee_Table holds data about employees, including their address, contact number, date of birth, department ID, gender, name, password, position, and a unique Employee_ID. The Department_ID in the Employee_Table references the Department_ID in the Department_Table, establishing a relationship between employees and their departments. The Charge_Table tracks charges associated with specific rooms, owners, and employees, with columns for charge amount, date, type, employee ID, owner ID, quantity, and room number. The Charge_Table has a composite primary key consisting of the room number, owner ID, and charge date, ensuring each charge is unique. The Employee_ID and Owner_ID in the Charge_Table reference the Employee_ID and Owner_ID in the Employee_Table and Owner_Table, respectively, enforcing referential integrity by linking charges to the employees and owners responsible for them.
