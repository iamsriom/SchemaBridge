The rschema_0512 database is designed to manage customer information and their financial transactions. At its core, the database contains three main tables: Customer, Account, and Transaction. The Customer table stores details about each individual customer, including their name, ID number, and contact phone number, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Account table is linked to the Customer table through the Customer_ID, which means that each account is associated with a specific customer. This table holds information about each account, such as its account number and balance, with the account number serving as the primary key. The Transaction table is connected to the Account table through the Account_Number, indicating that each transaction is linked to a particular account. This table tracks various transaction details, including the transaction amount, status, time, and type, with the Transaction_ID serving as the primary key.
