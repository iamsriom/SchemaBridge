The rschema_0513 database is designed to store information about electoral constituencies, districts, addresses, and voters. The Constituency table holds details about each electoral area, with a unique identifier (Constituency_ID) that serves as its primary key, along with a description and name. The District table represents individual districts within these constituencies, with a unique identifier (District_ID) as its primary key, and foreign key referencing the Constituency_ID to establish the relationship between the two. Each district has a name and type. The Address table stores information about the physical locations of voters, linked to a district through the District_ID foreign key, which references the District table. The Voter table contains details about individual voters, with a unique identifier (Voter_ID) as its primary key, and a foreign key referencing the Address_ID to associate each voter with their corresponding address. The Address_ID in the Voter table is not nullable, indicating that every voter must have a valid address, and the District_ID in the Address table is also not nullable, meaning every address must be linked to a district.
