The rschema_0514 database is designed to manage customer information and their financial transactions. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, email address, contact number, and the date their account was opened, with a unique identifier for each customer. The Account table holds information about each customer's account, such as the account ID, balance, and the customer ID that it belongs to, which is a foreign key referencing the Customer table, meaning that an account must be associated with a valid customer. The Transaction table records all transactions made by customers, including the transaction ID, amount, status, time, and type, along with the customer ID that initiated the transaction, which is also a foreign key referencing the Customer table, ensuring that each transaction is linked to a valid customer. The Customer table's Customer_ID is the primary key, uniquely identifying each customer, and is also referenced by the Account and Transaction tables, while the Account table's Account_ID and the Transaction table's Transaction_ID serve as their respective primary keys.
