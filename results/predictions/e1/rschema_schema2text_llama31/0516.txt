The rschema_0516 database is designed to manage the operations of a pizza business, encompassing customer information, pizza production, sales, and delivery. The Customer table stores details about each customer, including their name, phone number, and address, with a unique identifier, Customer_ID, serving as the primary key. The Pizza table holds information about the different types of pizzas available, such as their flavor, ingredients, and stock levels, with a unique identifier, Pizza_ID, serving as the primary key. The Sales_Order table tracks each customer's orders, including the expected delivery date, status, and the Customer_ID that links it to the corresponding customer in the Customer table. The Income/Expenditure table records financial transactions related to pizza sales, with a unique identifier, Record_ID, serving as the primary key, and a foreign key referencing the Pizza_ID to track which pizzas are involved in each transaction. The Production_Plan table outlines the production schedule for each pizza order, linking the Order_ID from the Sales_Order table to the Pizza_ID from the Pizza table, with a composite primary key consisting of both Order_ID and Pizza_ID to ensure each production plan is unique. The Delivery_Content table tracks the delivery status of each order, with a unique identifier, Record_ID, serving as the primary key, and a foreign key referencing the Order_ID to link it to the corresponding order in the Sales_Order table.
