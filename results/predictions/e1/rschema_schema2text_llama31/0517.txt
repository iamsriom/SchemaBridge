The rschema_0517 database is designed to manage information about products, employees, supermarkets, and their relationships. The Product table stores details about each product, including its model, name, quantity, and unit price, with a unique identifier for each product. The Supermarket table contains information about individual supermarkets, such as their address, phone number, and manager's employee ID, with a unique identifier for each supermarket. The Department table represents different departments within a supermarket, including their name, responsibility, employee count, establishment time, and manager's employee ID, with a unique identifier for each department and a foreign key referencing the supermarket it belongs to. The Employee table stores details about each employee, including their contact information, department ID, name, position, and salary, with a unique identifier for each employee and a foreign key referencing the department they belong to. The Allocation table tracks the allocation of products to sales employees, including the quantity allocated, allocation time, and product ID, with a composite primary key referencing the product and sales employee. The Establish table represents the establishment of departments within supermarkets, with a composite primary key referencing both the department and supermarket. The Manage_supermarket table stores information about which employees manage which supermarkets, with a composite primary key referencing both the manager and supermarket. The Manage_department table represents the management structure within departments, with a composite primary key referencing both the manager and department. Finally, the Work table tracks which employees work in which departments, with a composite primary key referencing both the employee and department.
