The schemabench_0052 database is used for a forum-like application, where users can create threads and replies, and other users can like and subscribe to these. The database contains 11 tables, each representing a different entity or relationship in the application.
The failed_jobs table stores information about failed jobs, including the connection, queue, payload, exception, and timestamp of when the job failed.
The likes table stores information about likes, including the user who liked a reply or thread, the likeable item (either a reply or thread), and the timestamp of when the like was created.
The migrations table stores information about database migrations, including the migration name and the batch number.
The notifications table stores information about notifications, including the type of notification, the notifiable entity (either a user or a thread), the data associated with the notification, and the timestamp of when the notification was created.
The password_resets table stores information about password resets, including the email address and the token used to reset the password.
The replies table stores information about replies, including the body of the reply, the author, and the timestamp of when the reply was created.
The subscriptions table stores information about subscriptions, including the user who subscribed to a thread or reply, the subscribable item (either a thread or reply), and the timestamp of when the subscription was created.
The taggables table stores information about tags, including the taggable item (either a thread or reply), the tag, and the timestamp of when the tag was created.
The tags table stores information about tags, including the name and slug of the tag.
The threads table stores information about threads, including the author, subject, body, slug, and timestamp of when the thread was created.
The users table stores information about users, including their GitHub ID, username, email, name, and timestamp of when the user was created.
