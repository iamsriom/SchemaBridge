The schemabench_0075 database contains four tables: auth_user, organizations_organization, organizations_organizationowner, and organizations_organizationuser. The auth_user table stores information about users, including their ID and any other relevant details. The organizations_organization table contains information about organizations, including their ID, name, status, creation and modification dates, and slug. The organizations_organizationowner table links organizations to their owners, with columns for the organization ID, owner ID, creation and modification dates, and a foreign key referencing the organizations_organization table. The organizations_organizationuser table links organizations to their users, with columns for the organization ID, user ID, creation and modification dates, and a foreign key referencing the organizations_organization table and the auth_user table.
