The schemabench_0087 database is used for storing information about customers, subscriptions, receipts, and users/teams. The customers table contains information about individual customers, including their ID, billable ID, billable type, trial end date, and updated and created dates. The subscriptions table contains information about customer subscriptions, including their ID, billable ID, billable type, name, Paddle ID, Paddle status, Paddle plan, quantity, trial end date, paused from date, and ends at date. The receipts table contains information about customer receipts, including their ID, billable ID, billable type, Paddle subscription ID, checkout ID, order ID, amount, tax, currency, quantity, receipt URL, paid at date, updated, and created dates. The users/teams table contains information about users and teams, including their ID. The customers, subscriptions, and receipts tables have foreign keys referencing the users/teams table, which ensures referential integrity. The foreign keys are set to cascade on update and delete, which means that if a user or team is deleted, their related customers, subscriptions, and receipts will also be deleted. The schema also includes primary keys and unique constraints to ensure entity integrity.
