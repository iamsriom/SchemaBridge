The schemabench_0092 database contains nine tables that store various information about baseball teams, players, coaches, and awards. The tables are designed to enforce domain and entity integrity through the use of primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints.
The awards_coaches table contains information about coaches who have received awards, including the year, coach ID, award, league ID, and note. The awards_players table contains information about players who have received awards, including the player ID, award, year, league ID, note, and position. The coaches table contains information about coaches, including their ID, year, team ID, league ID, stint, wins, losses, post-season wins, post-season losses, and note. The draft table contains information about players who were drafted, including their ID, draft year, draft round, draft selection, draft overall, team ID, first name, last name, suffix name, player ID, draft from, league ID, and note. The player_allstar table contains information about players who have been selected to the All-Star game, including their ID, last name, first name, season ID, conference, league ID, games played, minutes, points, offensive rebounds, defensive rebounds, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, personal fouls, field goal attempts, field goals made, free throw attempts, free throws made, three-point attempts, and three-pointers made. The players table contains information about players, including their ID, use first, first name, middle name, last name, name given, full given name, name suffix, name nick, position, first season, last season, height, weight, college, college other, birth date, birth city, birth state, birth country, high school, hs city, hs state, hs country, death date, and race. The players_teams table contains information about players and their teams, including their ID, player ID, year, stint, team ID, league ID, games played, games started, minutes, points, offensive rebounds, defensive rebounds, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, personal fouls, field goal attempts, field goals made, free throw attempts, free throws made, three-point attempts, and three-pointers made. The series_post table contains information about post-season series, including their ID, year, round, series, team ID winner, league ID winner, team ID loser, league ID loser, wins, losses, and note. The teams table contains information about teams, including their year, league ID, team ID, franchise ID, conference ID, division ID, rank, conference rank, playoff status, name, offensive field goals made, offensive field goals attempted, offensive free throws made, offensive free throws attempted, offensive three-point field goals made, offensive three-point field goals attempted, offensive rebounds, defensive rebounds, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, personal fouls, points, home wins, home losses, away wins, away losses, neutral wins, neutral losses, conference wins, conference losses, division wins, division losses, pace, wins, losses, games, minutes, arena, and attendance.
