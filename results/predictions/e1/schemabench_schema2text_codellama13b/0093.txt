* coaches: This table contains information about coaches, including their ID, their full name, the year they coached, the team they coached, the league they coached in, the number of times they coached, the number of wins and losses they had, and any additional information.
* draft: This table contains information about players who were drafted, including the year they were drafted, the round they were drafted in, the selection they were drafted at, the overall pick they were drafted as, the team they were drafted by, and any additional information.

* players_teams: This table contains information about the teams a player played for, including the player's ID, their name, the year they played, the stint they played, the team they played for, the league they played in, and any additional information.
* series_post: This table contains information about the playoff series, including the year, the round, the series, the winning team, the losing team, and any additional information.
* teams: This table contains information about teams, including their ID, their league, their franchise ID, their conference ID, their division ID, their rank, their playoff status, their seed, their first round, their semifinals, their finals, their name, and any additional information.
* teams_post: This table contains information about the playoff teams, including the year, the team's ID, their league, their wins, and losses, and any additional information.
