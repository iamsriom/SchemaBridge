The schemabench_0096 database contains six tables: customers, gasstations, products, transactions, transactions_1k, and yearmonth. The customers table stores information about customers, including their unique identifiers, segments, and currencies. The gasstations table contains information about gas stations, including their unique identifiers, chain IDs, countries, and segments. The products table stores information about products, including their unique identifiers and descriptions. The transactions table contains information about transactions, including the date, time, customer ID, card ID, gas station ID, product ID, amount, and price. The transactions_1k table is a copy of the transactions table with an additional column for the transaction ID. The yearmonth table stores information about customer consumption, including the customer ID, date, and consumption amount. The tables are linked through foreign keys, which enforce referential integrity and ensure that data is consistent across tables.
