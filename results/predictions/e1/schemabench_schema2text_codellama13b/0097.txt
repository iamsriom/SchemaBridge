The schemabench_0097 database contains three tables: frpm, satscores, and schools. The frpm table contains information about free and reduced price meals (FRPM) provided to students in schools, including the number of students eligible for FRPM, the number of students receiving FRPM, and the percentage of students eligible for FRPM. The satscores table contains information about standardized test scores, including the number of students taking the tests, the average scores in reading, math, and writing, and the percentage of students scoring above or below a certain score. The schools table contains information about schools, including their name, address, and contact information, as well as information about their status, such as whether they are open or closed, and what type of funding they receive. The frpm table has a foreign key that references the schools table, allowing for a one-to-many relationship between schools and FRPM. The satscores table also has a foreign key that references the schools table, allowing for a one-to-many relationship between schools and test scores.
