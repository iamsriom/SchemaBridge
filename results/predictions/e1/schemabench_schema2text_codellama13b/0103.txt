The schemabench_0103 database is used to store information about a healthcare organization's members, charges, payments, and statements. It contains eight tables: category, charge, corporation, member, payment, provider, region, and statement.
The category table stores information about the different types of charges that can be made, including the charge description, category code, and category number.
The charge table stores information about the charges made to members, including the charge number, member number, provider number, category number, charge date, charge amount, statement number, and charge code.
The corporation table stores information about the healthcare organization, including the corporation number, corporation name, street address, city, state/province, country, postal code, phone number, expiration date, region number, and corporation code.
The member table stores information about the members of the healthcare organization, including the member number, last name, first name, middle initial, street address, city, state/province, country, postal code, phone number, photograph, issue date, expiration date, region number, corporation number, previous balance, current balance, and member code.
The payment table stores information about the payments made by members, including the payment number, member number, payment date, payment amount, statement number, and payment code.
The provider table stores information about the providers who provide services to members, including the provider number, provider name, street address, city, state/province, country, postal code, phone number, issue date, expiration date, region number, and provider code.
The region table stores information about the regions in which the healthcare organization operates, including the region number, region name, street address, city, state/province, country, postal code, phone number, and region code.
The statement table stores information about the statements issued to members, including the statement number, member number, statement date, due date, statement amount, and statement code.
The database uses foreign keys to enforce referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate. For example, the charge table references the category table to ensure that the category number is valid, and the member table references the region table to ensure that the region number is valid. The database also uses primary keys to ensure that each table has a unique identifier.
