The schemabench_0111 database contains seven tables, each with its own unique purpose. The first table, Bio, stores information about the biological characteristics of the patients, including their fibrosis level and activity. The dispat table contains information about the patients' medical history, including their sex, age, and type. The indis table stores information about the patients' medical history, including their blood tests and other laboratory results. The inf table stores information about the patients' medication and treatment. The rel11, rel12, and rel13 tables contain relationships between the different tables, including the relationships between the patients and their medical history, laboratory results, and medication. The database is designed to store and manage information about patients and their medical history, laboratory results, and medication, ensuring that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
