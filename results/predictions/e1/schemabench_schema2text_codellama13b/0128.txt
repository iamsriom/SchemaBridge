The schemabench_0128 database contains three tables: businesses, inspections, and violations. The businesses table stores information about businesses, including their name, address, city, postal code, latitude, longitude, phone number, tax code, business certificate, application date, owner name, owner address, owner city, owner state, and owner zip. The inspections table stores information about inspections, including the business ID, score, date, type, and a foreign key reference to the businesses table. The violations table stores information about violations, including the business ID, date, violation type ID, risk category, description, and a foreign key reference to the businesses table. The primary keys for each table are business_id in the businesses table, business_id in the inspections table, and business_id in the violations table. The business_id column in the inspections and violations tables is a foreign key that references the business_id column in the businesses table. The update and delete rules for the foreign keys are CASCADE and RESTRICT, respectively. There are no other indexes or constraints in the schema.
