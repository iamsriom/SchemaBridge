This schema, named schemabench_0148, is a database for an e-commerce website. It contains eight tables, each representing a different aspect of the website's functionality.
The first table, orders, stores information about each order placed by a customer. It has columns for the order's unique ID, the customer's ID, the total amount of the order, the order's status, the date and time the order was created, and the date and time the order was last updated. The table also has a foreign key that references the customers table, allowing for the association of an order with a specific customer.
The second table, payments, stores information about each payment made for an order. It has columns for the payment's unique ID, the invoice ID, the amount paid, the date and time the payment was made, the payment method, and the date and time the payment was last updated. The table also has a foreign key that references the invoices table, allowing for the association of a payment with a specific invoice.
The third table, refunds, stores information about each refund made for an order. It has columns for the refund's unique ID, the order ID, the amount refunded, the reason for the refund, the date and time the refund was made, and the date and time the refund was last updated. The table also has a foreign key that references the orders table, allowing for the association of a refund with a specific order.
The fourth table, order_items, stores information about each item in an order. It has columns for the item's unique ID, the order ID, the product ID, the quantity of the item, the price of the item, and the date and time the item was last updated. The table also has foreign keys that reference the products and orders tables, allowing for the association of an order item with a specific product and order.
The fifth table, products, stores information about each product offered on the website. It has columns for the product's unique ID, the product's name, the category ID, the price of the product, the stock of the product, a description of the product, and the date and time the product was last updated. The table also has a foreign key that references the categories table, allowing for the association of a product with a specific category.
The sixth table, categories, stores information about each category of product offered on the website. It has columns for the category's unique ID, the category's name, a description of the category, and the date and time the category was last updated.
The seventh table, users, stores information about each user of the website. It has columns for the user's unique ID, the user's name, the user's email address, the date and time the user's email was verified, the user's password, a remember token, and the date and time the user was last updated.
The eighth table, invoices, stores information about each invoice issued for an order. It has columns for the invoice's unique ID, the order ID, the amount of the invoice, the date and time the invoice was issued, the date and time the invoice is due, the status of the invoice, and the date and time the invoice was last updated. The table also has a foreign key that references the orders table, allowing for the association of an invoice with a specific order.
In summary, this schema is a comprehensive database for an e-commerce website, containing tables for orders, payments, refunds, order items, products, categories, users, and invoices. Each table has its own unique columns and foreign keys, allowing for the association of data across different aspects of the website's functionality.
