The schemabench_0153 database is used to manage a library system, with six tables that store information about books, staff members, members, issued books, returned books, and fines. The Books table contains information about the books in the library, including their titles, authors, publishers, publication dates, ISBNs, total copies, and available copies. The Staffs table contains information about the staff members, including their names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and dates of joining. The Members table contains information about the library members, including their names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and membership statuses. The IssuedBooks table contains information about the books issued by members, including the member's ID, book ID, issue date, due date, and the staff ID of the person who issued the book. The ReturnBooks table contains information about the books returned by members, including the issued ID, return date, and the staff ID of the person who received the book. The Fine table contains information about the fines incurred by members for late returns, including the issued ID, member ID, and the amount of the fine. The database ensures data integrity through primary keys, foreign keys, and unique constraints, and it also ensures referential integrity through the use of foreign keys and update and delete rules.
