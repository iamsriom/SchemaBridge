The schemabench_0164 database is a comprehensive schema that stores information about customers, products, sales, and inventory. It contains nine tables, each with its own unique purpose and structure.
The Customer table stores information about individual customers, including their ID, home store, first name, email, customer since date, loyalty card number, birthdate, gender, and birth year. The table also includes a primary key constraint on the customer ID column.
The Generation table stores information about customer generations, including the birth year and generation name.
The Product table stores information about products, including their ID, product group, category, type, product name, description, unit of measure, current wholesale price, current retail price, tax exempt status, promo status, and new product status. The table also includes primary key and unique constraints on the product ID column.
The Date table stores information about transaction dates, including the transaction date, date ID, week ID, week description, month ID, month name, quarter ID, quarter name, and year ID.
The Pastry Inventory table stores information about pastry inventory, including the sales outlet ID, transaction date, product ID, start of day, quantity sold, waste, and waste percentage.
The Sales Target table stores information about sales targets, including the sales outlet ID, year and month, beans goal, beverage goal, food goal, merchandise goal, and total goal.
The Sales Outlet table stores information about sales outlets, including the sales outlet ID, type, square feet, address, city, state, telephone, postal code, longitude, latitude, manager, and neighborhood. The table also includes a primary key constraint on the sales outlet ID column.
The Staff table stores information about staff members, including their ID, first name, last name, position, start date, and location. The table also includes a primary key constraint on the staff ID column.
The Sales Receipt table stores information about sales receipts, including the transaction ID, transaction date, transaction time, sales outlet ID, staff ID, customer ID, in-store status, order number, line item ID, product ID, quantity, line item amount, unit price, and promo item status. The table also includes primary key and foreign key constraints on the transaction ID, sales outlet ID, staff ID, customer ID, and product ID columns.
