The schemabench_0165 database contains 11 tables, each representing a different entity or relationship in the Olympic Games. The athlete table contains information about the athletes who participate in the games, including their name, gender, function, country, nationality, height, weight, disciplines, events, birth date, birth place, and more. The coach table contains information about the coaches who lead the athletes, including their name, gender, function, country, disciplines, and events. The event table contains information about the events that take place during the games, including the name, tag, sport, and more. The medalist table contains information about the athletes who win medals, including their name, gender, country, nationality, discipline, event, and more. The medal table contains information about the medals that are awarded, including the type, code, date, and more. The medal_total table contains information about the total number of medals awarded by country, including gold, silver, and bronze medals. The schedule table contains information about the schedule of the games, including the start and end dates, day, status, discipline, event, and more. The schedule_preliminary table contains information about the preliminary schedule of the games, including the start and end dates, estimated start time, venue, and more. The team table contains information about the teams that participate in the games, including their name, gender, country, disciplines, events, athletes, and more. The torch_route table contains information about the torch relay route, including the title, city, start and end dates, tag, and more. The venue table contains information about the venues where the events take place, including the name, sports, date, and more.
