The schemabench_0177 database is a collection of six tables that represent various aspects of a company's organizational structure. The departments table contains information about the different departments within the company, including their department number and name. The employees table contains information about the employees within the company, including their employee number, birth date, first and last name, gender, and hire date. The dept_manager table contains information about the managers of each department, including their employee number, department number, and the dates they were assigned to their current position. The dept_emp table contains information about the employees who are assigned to each department, including their employee number, department number, and the dates they were assigned to their current position. The salaries table contains information about the salaries of each employee, including their employee number, salary amount, and the dates they were paid. The titles table contains information about the titles held by each employee, including their employee number, title, and the dates they were assigned to their current title. The foreign keys in the tables ensure that the data is consistent and accurate, with the primary keys in each table serving as references to the corresponding data in other tables.
