The schemabench_0186 database is a relational database that contains information about a company that sells and supplies mobile phones. The database has seven tables: Supplier, Phones, Customer, Employee, Purchase, Supply, and Contract.
The Supplier table contains information about the companies that supply the phones, including their name and contact phone number. The Supplier table has two columns: supplierName (primary key) and contactPhone.
The Phones table contains information about the phones themselves, including their product code, make, model, stock quantity, and retail price. The Phones table has five columns: productCode (primary key), make, model, stockQuantity, and retailPrice.
The Customer table contains information about the customers who purchase the phones, including their ID and name. The Customer table has two columns: custID (primary key) and custName.
The Employee table contains information about the employees who work at the company, including their ID, name, and position. The Employee table has three columns: empID (primary key), empName, and position.
The Purchase table contains information about the purchases made by customers, including the purchase number, date, customer ID, product code, and employee ID. The Purchase table has six columns: purchaseNo (primary key), purchaseDate, custID, productCode, empID, and foreign keys to the Customer and Phones tables.
The Supply table contains information about the supplies received from suppliers, including the supplier name, product code, supply price, supply quantity, and supply date. The Supply table has six columns: supplierName, productCode (primary key), supplyPrice, supplyQuantity, supplyDate, and foreign keys to the Supplier and Phones tables.
The Contract table contains information about the contracts between the company and customers, including the contract number, purchase number, contract term, and contract price. The Contract table has four columns: contractNo (primary key), purchaseNo (foreign key to the Purchase table), contractTerm, and contractPrice.
In summary, the schemabench_0186 database contains information about a company that sells and supplies mobile phones, including information about suppliers, phones, customers, employees, purchases, supplies, and contracts. The database is structured using a relational model, with each table representing a different entity and relationships between them defined using foreign keys and primary keys.
