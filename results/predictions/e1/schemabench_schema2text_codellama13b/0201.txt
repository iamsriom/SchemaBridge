The schemabench_0201 database is used to store information about authors, books, customers, orders, and reviews. The authors table contains information about the authors of the books, including their first and last names, biography, birthday, nationality, and website. The books table contains information about the books, including their title, ISBN, genre, price, published date, author ID, and added date. The customers table contains information about the customers, including their first and last names, email, passcode, address, and phone number. The orders table contains information about the orders, including the order ID, customer ID, order date, total amount, status, and foreign key to the customers table. The review table contains information about the reviews, including the review ID, book ID, customer ID, rating, comment, and review date. The authors, books, and customers tables have primary keys, while the orders and review tables have primary keys and foreign keys to the corresponding tables. The foreign keys are used to enforce referential integrity, ensuring that the data in the child tables is consistent with the data in the parent tables.
