The schemabench_0203 database is used to store information about products, suppliers, and customers. It contains five tables: category, supplier, product, sale, and customer.
The category table contains information about the categories of products, including the category ID, category name, number of products, and description. The category ID is the primary key and is used to link to the product table.
The supplier table contains information about the suppliers of products, including the supplier ID, supplier name, contact person, contact number, and email. The supplier ID is the primary key and is used to link to the product table.
The product table contains information about the products, including the product ID, product name, category ID, supplier ID, quantity in stock, unit price, description, and foreign keys to the category and supplier tables. The product ID is the primary key and is used to link to the sale table.
The sale table contains information about the sales of products, including the sale ID, product ID, customer ID, quantity sold, total amount, sale date, and foreign keys to the product and customer tables. The sale ID is the primary key and is used to link to the customer table.
The customer table contains information about the customers, including the customer ID, first name, last name, phone number, email, and address. The customer ID is the primary key and is used to link to the sale table.
The database also contains foreign key constraints to enforce referential integrity, such as the foreign key from the product table to the category and supplier tables, and the foreign key from the sale table to the product and customer tables. The database also contains composite and unique constraints to enforce entity integrity, such as the primary key and foreign key constraints.
