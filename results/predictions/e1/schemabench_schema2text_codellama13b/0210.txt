The schemabench_0210 database is a library management system that stores information about authors, genres, books, members, librarians, transactions, and book reviews. The authors table contains information about the authors of books, including their names, birth dates, nationalities, and biographies. The genres table contains information about the genres of books, including their names and descriptions. The books table contains information about the books themselves, including their titles, authors, genres, publication dates, ISBNs, available and total copies, and descriptions. The members table contains information about the members of the library, including their names, enrollment dates, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and descriptions. The borrow table contains information about the books that members have borrowed, including the book IDs, member IDs, borrow dates, due dates, and return dates. The librarians table contains information about the librarians who work at the library, including their names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and descriptions. The transactions table contains information about the transactions that have taken place in the library, including the book IDs, member IDs, librarian IDs, transaction dates, transaction types, and descriptions. The book_reviews table contains information about the reviews that members have left for books, including the book IDs, member IDs, ratings, comments, and review dates.
