The schemabench_0212 database is a collection of six tables that store information about movies, their genres, directors, actors, and ratings. The movie table contains information about each movie, including its title, year of release, duration, country of production, worldwide gross income, and the languages spoken in the movie. The genre table lists the genres associated with each movie, while the director_mapping table maps each movie to its director. The role_mapping table lists the actors and their roles in each movie, along with their category (e.g., leading actor, supporting actor). The names table contains information about the actors, including their name, height, date of birth, and the movies they are known for. Finally, the ratings table contains the average rating, total votes, and median rating for each movie. The primary keys and foreign keys are used to ensure data integrity, with the movie_id in the role_mapping table referencing the movie table, and the name_id in the role_mapping table referencing the names table.
