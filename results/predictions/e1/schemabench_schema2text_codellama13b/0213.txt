The schemabench_0213 database is a schema used for a retail business that sells products to customers. It contains six tables: Products, Orders, Sales, Address, Shipping, and Sales_.
The Products table contains information about the products sold by the business, including their unique identifiers, names, categories, and subcategories. The ProductID column is the primary key and is not nullable. The ProductName, Category, and Sub-category columns are also not nullable.
The Orders table contains information about the orders placed by customers, including their unique identifiers, product identifiers, customer identifiers, order dates, markets, order priorities, quantities, and shipping costs. The OrderID column is the primary key and is not nullable. The ProductID and CustomerID columns are foreign keys that reference the Products and Customers tables, respectively.
The Sales table contains information about the sales made by the business, including the unique identifiers of the orders, sales amounts, discounts, profits, and shipping costs. The OrderID column is the primary key and is not nullable. The Sales, Discount, Profit, and ShippingCost columns are also not nullable.
The Address table contains information about the addresses of customers and shipping locations, including their unique identifiers, cities, states, countries, postal codes, and regions. The AddressID column is the primary key and is not nullable.
The Shipping table contains information about the shipping of orders, including the unique identifiers of the orders, address identifiers, shipping dates, shipping modes, and shipping costs. The OrderID column is the primary key and is not nullable. The AddressID column is a foreign key that references the Address table.
The Sales_ table contains information about the sales made by the business, including the unique identifiers of the sales, prices, costs, discounts, shipping costs, quantities, location identifiers, time identifiers, and product identifiers. The SalesID column is the primary key and is not nullable. The LocationID, TimeID, and ProductID columns are foreign keys that reference the Location, Time, and Products tables, respectively.
Overall, the schemabench_0213 schema is designed to store and manage information about a retail business's products, orders, sales, customers, and shipping. It uses primary keys, foreign keys, and other constraints to ensure data integrity and consistency.
