The schemabench_0220 database is a comprehensive schema that includes 14 tables, each representing a different entity or relationship in the hotel industry. The tables are designed to store information about hotels, reservees, rooms, reservations, booking agencies, activities, offers, guests, departments, employees, gyms, equipment, restaurants, and facilities.
The Hotel table contains information about individual hotels, including their location, name, rating, and ID number. The Reservee table stores information about individuals who make reservations, including their ID number. The Room table contains information about individual rooms, including their room number, price, hotel ID, view, availability, and room type. The Reservation table stores information about individual reservations, including the board type, start date, duration, paid amount, children number, reservee ID, and room ID. The Booking_Agency table contains information about booking agencies, including their name, location, discount, and reservee ID.
The Activities table contains information about various activities that guests can participate in, including their name, date, and price. The Offers table contains information about offers that hotels make to guests, including the hotel ID and activity ID. The Guest table contains information about individual guests, including their SSN, name, reservee ID, gender, date of birth, reservation ID, and participation in activities. The Participates_in table contains information about the activities that guests participate in, including their guest SSN and activity ID.
The Department table contains information about departments within hotels, including their ID number, name, and hotel ID. The Employee table contains information about employees, including their ESSN, name, management ESSN, department ID, hotel ID, salary, and participation in activities. The Gym table contains information about gym facilities, including their ID number, hotel ID, name, working hours, and equipment. The Equipment table contains information about the equipment available in gyms, including their facility ID and equipment name.
The Restaurant table contains information about restaurants, including their ID number, hotel ID, name, rating, price range, food type, and facilities. The Facility table contains information about facilities, including their ID number and hotel ID.
Overall, the schemabench_0220 database schema provides a comprehensive and detailed representation of the hotel industry, with tables that are designed to store information about hotels, reservees, rooms, reservations, booking agencies, activities, offers, guests, departments, employees, gyms, equipment, restaurants, and facilities.
