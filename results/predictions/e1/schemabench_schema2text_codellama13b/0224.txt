The schemabench_0224 database is used to store information about employees and their departments. It contains six tables: departments, employees, dept_manager, salaries, dept_emp, and titles.
The departments table contains information about the different departments in the company, including their department number (dept_no) and name (dept_name). The primary key is the department number, and there is a unique constraint on the department name.
The employees table contains information about the employees, including their employee number (emp_no), birth date, first name, last name, gender, and hire date. The primary key is the employee number.
The dept_manager table contains information about the managers of each department, including their employee number (emp_no), department number (dept_no), from date, and to date. The primary key is the combination of employee number and department number.
The salaries table contains information about the salaries of each employee, including their employee number (emp_no), salary, from date, and to date. The primary key is the employee number.
The dept_emp table contains information about the employees in each department, including their employee number (emp_no), department number (dept_no), from date, and to date. The primary key is the employee number.
The titles table contains information about the titles of each employee, including their employee number (emp_no), title, from date, and to date. The primary key is the employee number.
All tables have foreign key constraints that reference the primary keys of other tables. For example, the dept_manager table has foreign keys to the employees and departments tables, and the dept_emp table has foreign keys to the employees and departments tables. The foreign key constraints ensure that the data in the tables is consistent and accurate.
