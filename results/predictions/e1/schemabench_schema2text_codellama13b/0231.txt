The schemabench_0231 database is used to store information about football (soccer) teams, stadiums, players, managers, matches, and goals. The database has seven tables: cities, stadiums, teams, players, managers, matches, and goals.
The cities table contains information about football cities, including the city name and the country where the city is located. The stadiums table contains information about football stadiums, including the stadium ID, name, city, capacity, and foreign key to the cities table. The teams table contains information about football teams, including the team ID, name, country, stadium, and foreign key to the stadiums table.
The players table contains information about football players, including the player ID, first name, last name, nationality, date of birth, team, jersey number, position, height, weight, and foot. The managers table contains information about football managers, including the manager ID, first name, last name, nationality, date of birth, and team.
The matches table contains information about football matches, including the match ID, season, date and time, home team, away team, stadium, home team score, away team score, penalty shootout score, attendance, and foreign keys to the teams and stadiums tables. The goals table contains information about football goals, including the goal ID, match ID, player ID, duration, assist, and goal description.
Each table has primary keys and foreign keys to enforce entity and referential integrity. The primary keys are used to uniquely identify each row in the table, while the foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables. The tables also have data types, such as VARCHAR, INT, and DATE, and constraints, such as NOT NULL and CHECK, to ensure data consistency and integrity.
