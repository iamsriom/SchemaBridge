The schemabench_0235 database is used to store information about a company's departments, positions, employees, projects, and project assignments. The database has five tables: department, positions, employees, projects, and project_assignment.
The department table contains information about the different departments within the company, including their unique identifiers and names. The positions table contains information about the different positions within the company, including their unique identifiers, names, and salaries.
The employees table contains information about the company's employees, including their unique identifiers, first and last names, date of birth, department identifier, and position identifier. The employees table also has foreign key constraints that reference the department and position tables.
The projects table contains information about the company's projects, including their unique identifiers, names, descriptions, start and end dates. The projects table also has foreign key constraints that reference the employees table.
The project_assignment table contains information about the assignments of employees to projects, including their unique identifiers, project identifier, and employee identifier. The project_assignment table also has foreign key constraints that reference the projects and employees tables.
Overall, the schemabench_0235 database is designed to store and manage information about a company's departments, positions, employees, projects, and project assignments, ensuring that all data is accurate and consistent.
