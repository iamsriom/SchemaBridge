The database schema named "schemabench_0099" consists of three tables: "cites", "content", and "paper".
The "cites" table represents the relationship between papers. It has two columns: "cited_paper_id" and "citing_paper_id". Both are varchar(40) and cannot be null. The primary key of this table is a composite key made up of "cited_paper_id" and "citing_paper_id".
The "content" table represents the content of the papers. It has two columns: "paper_id" and "word_cited_id". Both are varchar(40) and cannot be null. The primary key of this table is a composite key made up of "paper_id" and "word_cited_id". The "paper_id" column is a foreign key referencing the "paper" table.
The "paper" table represents the papers. It has two columns: "paper_id" and "class_label". Both are varchar(40) and cannot be null. The primary key of this table is "paper_id".
This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing the foreign key constraint on the "content" table. It also ensures entity integrity by enforcing the primary key constraints on all tables. The composite primary keys in the "cites" table ensure entity integrity by ensuring that a pair of papers cannot be cited more than once.
In summary, this schema is designed to manage a database of papers, their citations, and the content of the papers.
