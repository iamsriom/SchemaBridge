The database named "schemabench_0108" has 3 tables. The schema is designed to store and manage gene classification information.
The `Classification` table represents the primary classification of genes. It has a single primary key, `GeneID`, which is a string of 7 characters and is not nullable. The `Localization` column is also a string of up to 255 characters and is not nullable.
The `Genes` table stores detailed information about each gene. It has a foreign key `GeneID` that references the `Classification` table. The `GeneID` is a string of 7 characters and is not nullable. The `Essential`, `Class`, `Complex`, `Phenotype`, `Motif`, `Chromosome`, `Function`, and `Localization` columns are all strings of up to 255 characters and are not nullable.
The `Interactions` table stores information about gene interactions. It has two foreign keys, `GeneID1` and `GeneID2`, that reference the `Classification` table. Both `GeneID1` and `GeneID2` are strings of 7 characters and are not nullable. The `Type` column is a string of up to 255 characters and is not nullable. The `Expression_Corr` column is a decimal number with 10 digits and 9 of them can be after the decimal point and is not nullable. The primary key of this table is a composite of `GeneID1` and `GeneID2`.
The schema enforces referential integrity by setting up cascading delete and update rules. If a gene is deleted or updated in the `Classification` table, the corresponding records in the `Genes` and `Interactions` tables will also be deleted or updated.
