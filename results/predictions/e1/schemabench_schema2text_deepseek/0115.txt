The database schema named "schemabench_0115" consists of five tables: `legalact_link`, `legalact_people`, `legalacts`, `people`, and `scrapefix`.
The `legalact_link` table is used to represent the relationship between legal acts. It has two columns, `actId1` and `actId2`, which are foreign keys referencing the `legalacts` table. The primary key is a composite key of `actId1` and `actId2`.
The `legalact_people` table represents the relationship between people and legal acts. It has two columns, `peopleId` and `actId`, which are foreign keys referencing the `people` and `legalacts` tables respectively. The primary key is a composite key of `peopleId` and `actId`.
The `legalacts` table represents the legal acts. It has various columns such as `id`, `hash`, `update`, `Court`, `CaseKind`, `CaseNumber`, `ActYear`, `Judge`, `ActKind`, `ActNumber`, `StartDate`, `LegalDate`, `Status`, `ActLink`, `MotiveDate`, `MotiveLink`, `HighCourt`, `OutNumber`, `YearHigherCourt`, `TypeOfDocument`, `SendDate`, and `ResultOfAppeal`. The `id` column is the primary key.
The `people` table represents the people. It has two columns, `personId` and `name`, which are the primary key and name of the person respectively.
Finally, the `scrapefix` table represents the scraped fixes. It has three columns, `actId`, `fix_description`, and `contributor`. The `actId` column is the primary key and a foreign key referencing the `legalacts` table.
