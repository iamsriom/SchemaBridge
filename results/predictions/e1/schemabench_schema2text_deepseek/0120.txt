The database schema named "schemabench_0120" consists of 9 tables. The primary function of this schema is to store and manage various types of sports data, including regular season results, detailed season results, seasons, targets, teams, tourney compact results, tourney detailed results, tourney seeds, and tourney slots.
The "regular_season_compact_results" table stores the compact results of the regular season. It includes columns for the season, day number, winning team, winning score, losing team, losing score, winning location, and number of overtimes. The primary key is a composite of the season, day number, and the winning team. The foreign keys reference the "seasons" and "teams" tables.
The "regular_season_detailed_results" table stores the detailed results of the regular season. It includes additional columns for winning and losing team's field goals made, field goals attempted, three-point field goals made and attempted, free throws made and attempted, offensive rebounds, defensive rebounds, assists, turnovers, steals, blocks, and personal fouls. The primary key is a composite of the season, day number, and the winning team. The foreign keys reference the "seasons" and "teams" tables.
The "seasons" table stores information about each season. It includes columns for the season, the date of the first day, and region information. The primary key is the season.
The "target" table stores prediction targets for each season. It includes columns for the target ID, season, team IDs, prediction, and win counts for each team. The primary key is the target ID. The foreign keys reference the "teams" table.
The "teams" table stores information about each team. It includes columns for the team ID and team name. The primary key is the team ID.
The "tourney_compact_results" and "tourney_detailed_results" tables store the compact and detailed results of the tournament. They have the same structure as the "regular_season_compact_results" and "regular_season_detailed_results" tables, respectively.
The "tourney_seeds" table stores the seeds for each team in each season. It includes columns for the season, seed, and team. The primary key is a composite of the season and seed. The foreign keys reference the "seasons" and "teams" tables.
The "tourney_slots" table stores the slots for each season. It includes columns for the season, slot, strong seed, and weak seed. The primary key is a composite of the slot and season. The foreign key references the "seasons" table.
All tables have foreign key constraints that cascade on delete and update operations, ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the consistency of the data.
