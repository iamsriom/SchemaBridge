The database schema named 'schemabench_0135' contains two tables: 'cars' and 'trains'.
The 'cars' table is used to store information about different types of vehicles, including their id, train id, position, shape, length, sides, roof, wheels, load shape, and load number. The 'id' column is the primary key and does not allow null values. The 'train_id' column is a foreign key referencing the 'id' column in the 'trains' table. If a train is deleted or updated, all associated cars will be cascade deleted or updated.
The 'trains' table stores information about different types of trains, including their id and direction. The 'id' column is the primary key and does not allow null values.
This schema ensures that the integrity of the data is maintained by enforcing referential integrity through foreign keys. The 'cars' table references the 'trains' table, ensuring that a train cannot be deleted or updated unless all associated cars are also deleted or updated. This prevents orphaned records from being created in the 'cars' table.
