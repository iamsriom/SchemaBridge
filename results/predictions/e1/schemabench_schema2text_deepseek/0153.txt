The database schema named "schemabench_0153" contains six tables: Books, Staffs, Members, IssuedBooks, ReturnBooks, and Fine.
The Books table represents a collection of books, with each book identified by a unique BookID. The Title, Author, Publisher, PublicationDate, ISBN, TotalCopies, and AvailableCopies columns store information about the book's title, author, publisher, publication date, ISBN, total copies, and available copies respectively. The Author column has a constraint that ensures that either the Author or Publisher must be filled, but not both.
The Staffs table stores information about the library staff members. Each staff member is identified by a unique StaffID, and the Name, Address, Phone, Email, and DoJ (Date of Joining) columns store their name, address, phone number, email, and date of joining respectively.
The Members table contains information about the library members. Each member is identified by a unique MemberID, and the Name, Address, Phone, Email, and MembershipStatus columns store their name, address, phone number, email, and membership status respectively. The MembershipStatus column is an enumerated type that can only take values 'Active' or 'NotActive'.
The IssuedBooks table records the books issued to members by staff. Each record is identified by a unique IssuedID, and the MemberID, BookID, IssueDate, DueDate, and IssuedBy columns store the MemberID of the member, the BookID of the book, the date the book was issued, the due date, and the StaffID of the staff member who issued the book respectively.
The ReturnBooks table records the books returned by members. Each record is identified by a unique ReturnID, and the IssuedID, ReturnDate, and ReceivedBy columns store the IssuedID of the issued book, the date the book was returned, and the StaffID of the staff member who received the book respectively. The IssuedID column in the ReturnBooks table is a foreign key referencing the IssuedID in the IssuedBooks table.
The Fine table records fines for overdue books. Each record is identified by a unique ReturnID and IssuedID, and the MemberID and FineAmount columns store the ReturnID and IssuedID of the issued book, the MemberID of the member, and the amount of the fine respectively. The ReturnID and IssuedID columns in the Fine table are foreign keys referencing the ReturnID and IssuedID in the ReturnBooks table respectively.
All tables have primary keys that uniquely identify each record. Foreign keys are used to link these tables together, ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the relationships between the tables.
