The database schema named "schemabench_0160" consists of 8 tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage various financial transactions and interactions.
The "company_info" table holds information about various companies, including their names, address, established date, and other details. The "exchange" table represents various financial exchanges. The "broker_info" table contains information about brokers, including their names, contact details, and address. The "trader_info" table stores information about traders, including their names, contact details, and address.
The "Account_info" table holds information about trading accounts, including the cash and security amounts, and it references the "trader_info" and "broker_info" tables. The "broker_exchange_deals" table records broker-exchange deals, including the fees given, and it references the "broker_info" and "exchange" tables.
The "stock_info" table contains information about stocks, including their names, share numbers, symbol, launch date, and price, and it references the "company_info" and "exchange" tables. The "transaction_info" table records transactions, including the price, number of shares, and brokerage fees, and it references the "broker_exchange_deals", "stock_info", "broker_info", "exchange", "trader_info", and "Account_info" tables.
This schema ensures referential integrity as all foreign keys are properly defined and referenced, ensuring that data integrity is maintained throughout the database.
