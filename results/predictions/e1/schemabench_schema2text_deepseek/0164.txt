The database schema named 'schemabench_0164' contains 9 tables. The 'Customer' table is used to store customer information, including their ID, home store, first name, email, since when they joined, their loyalty card number, birthdate, gender, and birth year. The 'Generation' table contains information about the birth year and generation of customers.
The 'Product' table holds information about various products, including their ID, group, category, type, name, description, unit of measure, wholesale price, retail price, tax exempt status, promo status, and new product status. The 'Date' table contains information about various dates, including the transaction date, date ID, week ID, week description, month ID, month name, quarter ID, quarter name, and year ID.
The 'Pastry_Inventory' table records the inventory of pastries sold at various sales outlets, including the sales outlet ID, transaction date, product ID, start of day, quantity sold, waste, and waste percentage. The 'Sales_Target' table sets sales targets for various sales outlets, including the sales outlet ID, year and month, beans goal, beverage goal, food goal, merchandise goal, and total goal.
The 'Sales_Outlet' table contains information about various sales outlets, including their ID, type, size, address, city, state, telephone number, postal code, longitude, latitude, manager, and neighborhood. The 'Staff' table holds information about the staff, including their ID, first name, last name, position, start date, and location.
Finally, the 'Sales_Receipt' table records sales receipts, including the transaction ID, date, time, sales outlet ID, staff ID, customer ID, whether the transaction was in-store or not, order number, line item ID, product ID, quantity, line item amount, unit price, and whether the item was a promo item or not.
