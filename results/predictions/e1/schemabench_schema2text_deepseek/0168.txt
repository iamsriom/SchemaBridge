The database schema named 'schemabench_0168' consists of six tables: 'patients', 'medical_histories', 'invoices', 'treatments', 'invoice_items', and 'medical_treatments'.
The 'patients' table holds information about patients, including their unique id, name, and date of birth. The 'medical_histories' table records medical history data, including the admission time, patient id, and status. The 'invoices' table contains invoice details, including the total amount, generation time, payment time, and the id of the associated medical history. The 'treatments' table contains information about different treatments, including their type and name. The 'invoice_items' table holds details about individual items on an invoice, including the unit price, quantity, total price, invoice id, and treatment id. The 'medical_treatments' table links the 'medical_histories' and 'treatments' tables, associating a treatment with a medical history. All tables have primary keys, foreign keys, and constraints to ensure data integrity and referential integrity.
