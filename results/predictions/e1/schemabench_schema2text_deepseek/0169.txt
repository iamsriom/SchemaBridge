The database schema named "schemabench_0169" consists of 18 tables. The database is designed to manage a bookstore, with tables representing various entities such as regions, addresses, publishers, books, staff, warehouses, clients, bank accounts, sales, orders, and more.
The 'Region' table holds information about different regions, including postal codes, state, and country. The 'Address' table stores addresses with a unique identifier, building number, street, city, and postal code, and a foreign key referencing the 'Region' table.
The 'Publisher' table contains information about publishers, including their ID, name, email, phone number, and address ID. The 'Book' table contains information about books, including their ISBN, title, author, genre, publisher ID, number of pages, cost, price, publisher's percentage, stock, and threshold.
The 'Staff' table stores information about staff members, including their ID and email. The 'Warehouse' and 'Client' tables store information about warehouses and clients, respectively, including their IDs and addresses.
The 'BankAccount' table contains information about bank accounts, including the account number and amount. The 'Sales', 'Orders', 'manage', 'update_sales', 'handle_account', 'client_account', and 'publisher_account' tables store information about sales, orders, managing books, updating sales, handling bank accounts, client accounts, and publisher accounts respectively.
The 'tracks', 'emails', and 'checkout' tables store information about tracking orders, emails between staff and publishers, and checkout information, respectively.
All these tables are interconnected, with foreign keys ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the relationships between different entities. The database is designed to be exhaustive and readable, with each table representing a specific entity and containing all necessary fields for that entity.
