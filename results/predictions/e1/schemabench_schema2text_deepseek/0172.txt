The database schema named "schemabench_0172" consists of 17 tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage various social interactions, including user profiles, friendships, pages, group memberships, chats, multimedia content, activities, messages, posts, comments, reactions, and events.
The "Publisher" table represents the publishers of the social network. Each publisher is identified by a unique 'publisherID' and has a 'name' that cannot be null.
The "User" table holds information about the users of the platform. Each user is identified by a unique 'userID', has a unique 'phoneNumber', a 'gender' that must be either 'M' or 'F', a 'birthDate' that cannot be null, an 'age' that must be at least 13, and an 'address'. The 'userID' is a foreign key referencing the 'publisherID' in the 'Publisher' table.
The "Friendship" table represents the friendships between users. Each friendship is identified by a unique pair of 'senderID' and 'receiverID', has a 'state' that must be between 1 and 3, and a 'date' that cannot be null. The 'senderID' and 'receiverID' are foreign keys referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table.
The "Page" table represents the pages on the social network. Each page is identified by a unique 'pageID', has a 'website', and an 'adminID' that is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table.
The "PageFollower" table represents the followers of the pages. Each follower is identified by a unique pair of 'followerID' and 'pageID', where 'followerID' is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table and 'pageID' is a foreign key referencing the 'pageID' in the 'Page' table.
The "Group" table represents the groups on the social network. Each group is identified by a unique 'groupID', has a 'name' that cannot be null, and an 'adminID' that is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table.
The "GroupMember" table represents the members of the groups. Each member is identified by a unique pair of 'memberID' and 'groupID', where 'memberID' is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table and 'groupID' is a foreign key referencing the 'groupID' in the 'Group' table.
The "Chat" table represents the chats on the social network. Each chat is identified by a unique 'chatID' and has a 'name' that cannot be null.
The "ChatParticipant" table represents the participants in the chats. Each participant is identified by a unique pair of 'participantID' and 'chatID', where 'participantID' is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table and 'chatID' is a foreign key referencing the 'chatID' in the 'Chat' table.
The "Multimedia" table represents the multimedia content on the social network. Each piece of content is identified by a unique 'multimediaID', has a 'title', a 'uri', a 'size' that must be greater than 0, a 'format' that must be either '.mp3', '.jpg', '.png', '.wav', or '.mp4', and a 'length' that must be greater than 0.
The "Activity" table represents the activities on the social network. Each activity is identified by a unique 'activityID' and has a 'activityText' and an 'activityDate'.
The "Message" table represents the messages on the social network. Each message is identified by a unique 'messageID', has a 'dateSent' that cannot be null, a 'multimediaID', an 'authorID', and a 'chatID' that are foreign keys referencing the 'activityID', 'multimediaID', 'authorID', and 'chatID' in the 'Activity', 'Multimedia', 'User', and 'Chat' tables, respectively.
The "Post" table represents the posts on the social network. Each post is identified by a unique 'postID', has a 'publisherID', a 'multimediaID', a 'pageID', and a 'groupID' that are foreign keys referencing the 'activityID', 'publisherID', 'multimediaID', 'pageID', and 'groupID' in the 'Activity', 'Publisher', 'Multimedia', 'Page', and 'Group' tables, respectively.
The "Comment" table represents the comments on the social network. Each comment is identified by a unique 'commentID', has an 'authorID' and a 'postID' that are foreign keys referencing the 'activityID' and 'authorID' in the 'Activity' and 'User' tables, respectively, and 'postID' in the 'Post' table.
The "Reaction" table represents the reactions on the social network. Each reaction is identified by a unique pair of 'activityID' and 'userID', has a 'type' that must be between 1 and 6, and 'activityID' is a foreign key referencing the 'activityID' in the 'Activity' table and 'userID' is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table.
The "Event" table represents the events on the social network. Each event is identified by a unique 'eventID', has a 'name' and a 'description' that cannot be null, an 'occurrenceDate' that cannot be null, and an 'creatorID' that is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table.
The "EventParticipant" table represents the participants in the events. Each participant is identified by a unique pair of 'participantID' and 'eventID', where 'participantID' is a foreign key referencing the 'userID' in the 'User' table and 'eventID' is a foreign key referencing the 'eventID' in the 'Event' table.
