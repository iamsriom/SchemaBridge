The 'driver' table represents a driver. It has columns for 'driver_id', 'name', 'password', 'current_booking', 'phone_number', 'car_id', and 'current_location'. The 'transaction' table represents a transaction. It has columns for 'transaction_id', 'payment_type', and 'amount'. The 'transaction_id' is the primary key and auto-increments.
The 'booking' table represents a booking. It has columns for 'booking_id', 'pickup_location', 'destination', 'number_of_people', 'pickup_time', 'booking_status', 'transaction_id', 'sharing', 'customer_id', 'driver_id', and 'car_id'. The 'booking_id' is the primary key and auto-increments. The 'transaction_id', 'customer_id', 'driver_id', and 'car_id' all reference their respective tables' primary keys. The 'booking_status' column can have one of the following values: 'pending', 'confirmed', 'cancelled', 'completed'.
