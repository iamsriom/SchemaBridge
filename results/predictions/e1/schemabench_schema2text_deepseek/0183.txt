The database schema named "schemabench_0183" consists of 10 tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage various aspects of a music industry, including the locations of employees, the details of albums, songs, genres, and artists.
The "Location" table holds information about different locations, including their ID, name, phone number, and address. The "Employee" table records information about the employees, including their ID, name, and the location they work in. The "Album" table contains information about the albums, including their ID, title, release date, and cost. The "Song" table contains details about the songs, including their ID, title, duration, lyrics, and the album they belong to. The "Genre" table contains information about the genres, including their name and description. The "SongGenreList" table links genres to songs, allowing for a many-to-many relationship between songs and genres.
The "Artist" table holds information about the artists, including their ID, stage name, solo/band flag, artist name, date of birth, band flag, and established date. The "MemberOf" table links artists to groups, allowing for a many-to-many relationship between artists and groups. The "Inventory" table keeps track of the amount of albums available at different locations, linking the album and location IDs. The "SongFeatureList" table links songs to artists, allowing for a many-to-many relationship between songs and artists.
All tables have been designed with entity integrity in mind, with primary keys and foreign keys ensuring that the relationships between tables are maintained. The "Location", "Employee", "Album", "Song", "Genre", "Artist", and "Inventory" tables allow null values, while the "Employee", "MemberOf", "SongFeatureList", and "SongGenreList" tables do not. Default values are also set for some columns, but this is not specified in the schema. The schema also includes cascading delete rules, meaning that when a referenced record in the "Location", "Album", "Genre", or "Artist" tables is deleted, all associated records in the "Employee", "MemberOf", "SongFeatureList", "SongGenreList", "Inventory", and "Song" tables will also be deleted.
