The database schema named "schemabench_0189" consists of 12 tables. The primary function of this schema is to store and manage various types of data related to music, including acoustic features of songs, information about albums, charts, popularity scores, artists, and their charts, and the releases of artists and their albums.
The "acoustic_features" table contains detailed information about each song, including its duration, key, mode, time signature, and various acoustic properties like acousticness, danceability, energy, instrumentalness, liveness, loudness, speechiness, valence, and tempo. The "albums" table stores information about each album, including its name, billboard rankings, artists, popularity, total tracks, album type, and image URL.
The "album_chart" table represents the chart data for albums, including the rank score, peak position, weeks on chart, and the week of the chart entry. The "album_pop" table provides popularity scores and information about whether an album is popular or not, along with the year.
The "artists" table contains information about each artist, including their name, followers, popularity, artist type, main genre, genres, and image URL. The "artist_chart" table represents the chart data for artists, including the rank score, peak position, weeks on chart, and the week of the chart entry. The "artist_pop" table provides popularity scores and information about whether an artist is popular or not, along with the year.
The "releases" table links the artists and their albums, providing the release date and precision. The "songs" table contains information about each song, including its name, billboard rankings, artists, popularity, explicit status, and song type. The "song_chart" table represents the chart data for songs, including the rank score, peak position, weeks on chart, and the week of the chart entry. The "song_pop" table provides popularity scores and information about whether a song is popular or not, along with the year.
Finally, the "tracks" table links songs and their albums, providing the track number and release date, along with the primary key of the song and album. This schema ensures data integrity by enforcing primary keys and referential integrity constraints, allowing for efficient data management and analysis.
