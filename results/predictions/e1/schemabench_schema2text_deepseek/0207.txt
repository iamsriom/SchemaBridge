The database schema named "schemabench_0207" consists of seven tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage various aspects of an e-commerce platform, including user information, product categories, products, orders, order items, and reviews.
The "users" table holds information about the users of the platform, including their unique ID, name, contact details, and password. The "categories" table contains information about the product categories, including their unique ID and description.
The "products" table holds information about the products available on the platform, including their unique ID, name, category ID, price, stock quantity, and description. The "product_category" table is a junction table that links products and categories together.
The "orders" table contains information about the orders placed by users, including the unique ID of the user, the date of the order, the total price, and the status of the order. The "order_items" table holds information about the items included in each order, including the order ID, product ID, quantity, and subtotal.
Finally, the "reviews" table contains information about user reviews for products, including the unique ID of the user, the product ID, the rating, the comment, and the date of the review. The schema is designed to ensure referential integrity by setting foreign keys to link related data together.
The schema is exhaustive, but it could be further optimized for better performance and efficiency.
