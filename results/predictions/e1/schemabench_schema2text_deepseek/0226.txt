The database schema named "schemabench_0226" consists of 13 tables. The primary purpose of this schema is to manage various aspects of a social media platform, including user profiles, photos, videos, posts, comments, likes, follows, hashtags, and bookmarks.
The "users" table represents the main entities of the system, with each user identified by a unique user_id. The username, profile_photo_url, bio, and email fields are used to store additional information about each user. The profile_photo_url field has a default value of 'https://picsum.photos/100'.
The "photos" and "videos" tables store information about photos and videos posted by users. Each photo and video is identified by a unique photo_id or video_id, and is associated with a post_id. The size field is a float and has a CHECK constraint to ensure that the size of the photo or video is less than 5 for photos and less than 10 for videos.
The "post" table serves as a junction table that links the "users", "photos", and "videos" tables. It represents a post made by a user, which can contain a photo or video.
The "comments" table stores information about comments made by users on posts. Each comment is associated with a post_id and a user_id.
The "post_likes" and "comment_likes" tables represent the likes given by users to posts and comments, respectively. Each like is associated with a user_id and a post_id or comment_id.
The "follows" table represents the followers and followees of users. Each follower-followee pair is unique.
The "hashtags" table stores information about hashtags used in posts. Each hashtag is identified by a unique hashtag_id.
The "hashtag_follow" table represents the users who follow hashtags. Each user-hashtag pair is unique.
The "post_tags" table links posts to hashtags. Each post-hashtag pair is unique.
The "bookmarks" table represents bookmarks made by users to posts. Each bookmark is associated with a user_id and a post_id.
The "login" table logs the login activities of users. Each login is associated with a user_id and an ip address. The login_time field records the time of the login.
All tables have foreign keys that reference the primary keys of other tables, ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the relationships between tables. The schema is designed to be exhaustive and covers all aspects of a social media platform.
