The database schema named schemabench_0233 consists of seven tables. The main purpose of this schema is to manage various aspects of incident handling, including personnel, vehicles, equipment, victims, tasks, and medical records.
The Incident table represents the main entity which has an IncidentID as the primary key. It contains fields for the type of incident, its location, a description, and the date of the incident.
The Personnel table stores information about the personnel involved in the incident handling. Each record has a unique PersonnelID, the name of the personnel, their contact details, designation, and shift.
The Vehicle table contains information about the vehicles used in the incident handling. Each vehicle is identified by a unique VehicleID, its type, model, and availability status.
The Equipment table stores information about the equipment used in the incident handling. Each piece of equipment is identified by an EquipmentID, its type, quantity, condition, and a description.
The Victim table records information about the victims of the incidents. Each victim is identified by a unique VictimCNIC, their name, and age.
The Task table keeps track of tasks related to incident handling. Each task is identified by a unique TaskID, its type, and completion status.
The MedicalRecord table stores medical records related to the incidents. Each record is identified by a unique RecordID, the treatment details, and the diagnosis.
All these tables are interconnected through primary and foreign keys, ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the relationships between the entities.
