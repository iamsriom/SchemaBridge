The database schema named 'schemabench_0238' consists of 7 tables. The 'customers' table represents a list of customers with various details such as their first name, last name, phone number, and address. The 'accounts' table contains information about different types of accounts, including the type of account, account number, and balance. The 'transactions' table records all transactions made on the accounts, including the time of transaction, type of transaction (withdrawal or deposit), and the amount involved. The 'transfers' table keeps track of all the money transfers between different accounts. The 'cards' table contains information about the credit cards held by the customers, including the type of card, card number, and credit limit. The 'loans' table contains information about all the loans taken by the customers, including the loan amount, interest rate, term, and status of the loan. Finally, the 'investments' table contains information about all the investments made by the customers, including the type of investment (mutual-fund or stock), the amount invested, the date of investment, and the current value of the investment. All these tables are interconnected with foreign keys, ensuring referential integrity and maintaining the relationships between the tables.
This schema is designed to simulate a banking system, where customers can have multiple accounts, make transactions, apply for loans, and invest in different types of investments. It also allows for the tracking of credit card usage and money transfers between different accounts.
