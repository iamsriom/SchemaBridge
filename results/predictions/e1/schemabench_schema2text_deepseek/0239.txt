The database schema named "schemabench_0239" consists of 6 tables: 'major', 'departments', 'students', 'courses', 'instructors', and 'enrollment'.
The 'major' table represents the majors offered by the university. It has two columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), and 'name' (a variable character field of maximum length 255).
The 'departments' table represents the departments within the university. It has three columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), 'name' (a variable character field of maximum length 255), and 'chairperson' (an integer).
The 'students' table represents the students enrolled in the university. It has seven columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), 'first_name', 'last_name', 'email', 'phone', 'address', 'dob' (date of birth), and 'major_id' (an integer, foreign key referencing the 'major' table).
The 'courses' table represents the courses offered by the university. It has four columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), 'name' (a variable character field of maximum length 255), 'dep_id' (an integer, foreign key referencing the 'departments' table), and 'credits' (an integer).
The 'instructors' table represents the instructors teaching at the university. It has six columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), 'first_name', 'last_name', 'email', 'dep_id' (an integer, foreign key referencing the 'departments' table).
Finally, the 'enrollment' table represents the enrollment of students in courses. It has four columns: 'id' (an integer, auto-incrementing and primary key), 'course_id' (an integer, foreign key referencing the 'courses' table), 'student_id' (an integer, foreign key referencing the 'students' table), and 'term' (a variable character field of maximum length 255). This schema ensures referential integrity by enforcing foreign key constraints between tables, ensuring data consistency and preventing orphaned records.
