The schemabench_0008 database is designed to manage a content management system, storing information about various components of a website. At its core, the database contains a table for sites, which stores details such as the site's identifier, hostname, and locale, along with timestamps for when the site was created and last updated. Each site can have multiple layouts, which are stored in the comfy_cms_layouts table, each with a unique identifier, label, and content, as well as a reference to the site it belongs to and its parent layout. Pages are also stored in the comfy_cms_pages table, which includes details such as the page's label, full path, and content cache, along with references to the site and layout it belongs to, as well as its parent page. The comfy_cms_translations table stores translations of page and layout content for different locales. The comfy_cms_fragments table stores reusable content fragments, while the comfy_cms_snippets table stores snippets of content that can be used across multiple sites. The comfy_cms_files table stores files associated with a site, and the comfy_cms_revisions table stores revisions of snippets, pages, and layouts. The comfy_cms_categories table stores categories for a site, and the comfy_cms_categorizations table stores the relationships between categories and the items they categorize, such as snippets, pages, and files. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign key constraints, ensuring that relationships between tables are maintained, and it also includes various indexes and constraints to ensure data consistency and performance.
