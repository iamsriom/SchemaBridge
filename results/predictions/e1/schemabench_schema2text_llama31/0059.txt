The schemabench_0059 database is designed to store information about websites and their associated hostnames. The 'websites' table serves as the central hub, containing essential details about each website, such as a unique identifier (id), a universally unique identifier (uuid), timestamps for creation, update, and deletion, and a field to track the database connection used for management. The 'hostnames' table, on the other hand, stores information about the hostnames associated with each website, including the fully qualified domain name (fqdn), a redirect URL, a flag to indicate whether HTTPS should be forced, a timestamp for when the hostname is under maintenance, and timestamps for creation, update, and deletion. The 'hostnames' table is linked to the 'websites' table through a foreign key referencing the 'id' field in 'websites', meaning that each hostname is associated with a specific website, and any changes to the website will automatically affect the associated hostnames.
