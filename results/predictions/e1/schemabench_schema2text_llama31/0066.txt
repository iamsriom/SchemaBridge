The schemabench_0066 database is a comprehensive system for managing various entities and their relationships. At its core, it contains a table called attachments, which stores information about uploaded files, including their names, original names, MIME types, sizes, and paths, as well as details about the users and groups they belong to. The attachmentable table links attachments to other entities in the system, such as users or roles, allowing for the association of files with these entities. The notifications table tracks messages sent to users, including the type of notification, the entity being notified, and the data associated with the notification. The role_users table establishes relationships between users and roles, enabling the assignment of roles to users. The roles table stores information about available roles, including their slugs, names, and permissions. The settings table contains key-value pairs for storing system-wide settings, while the users table stores information about individual users, including their names, emails, passwords, and permissions.
