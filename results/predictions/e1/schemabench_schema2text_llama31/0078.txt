The schemabench_0078 database is designed to track financial transactions and billing information for various entities. At its core is the billable table, which stores information about each entity, including the processor used, processor ID, trial end date, card type, last four digits of the card number, expiration month and year, and any extra billing information. The pay_charges table is used to record individual charges, with each charge having a unique ID, owner type, owner ID referencing the billable entity, processor, processor ID, amount, amount refunded, card type, last four digits of the card number, expiration month and year, and timestamps for creation and update. The pay_subscriptions table stores subscription information, including the owner ID referencing the billable entity, subscription name, processor, processor ID, processor plan, quantity, trial end date, end date, status, and timestamps for creation and update. The pay_charges and pay_subscriptions tables both reference the billable table, establishing a relationship between charges and subscriptions.
