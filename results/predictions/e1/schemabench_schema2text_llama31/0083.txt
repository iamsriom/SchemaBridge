The schemabench_0083 database is designed to manage various aspects of a customer support system, including conversations, emails, users, and settings. The database consists of 19 tables, each representing a specific entity or concept. The activity_logs table stores records of system activities, including log names, descriptions, and timestamps. The attachments table contains information about uploaded files, such as their directory, name, type, and size, and is linked to the threads and users tables. The conversation_folder table establishes relationships between conversations and folders, while the conversations table stores details about individual conversations, including their number, type, status, and participants. The customers table holds information about customers, including their name, email, and contact details, and is linked to the emails and conversations tables. The emails table stores email addresses associated with customers, and the folders table manages mailboxes and their contents. The followers table tracks users who follow specific conversations, and the ltm_translations table stores translations for the system. The mailbox_user table links mailboxes to users, and the mailboxes table stores settings for mailboxes, including their name, email, and configuration. The modules table manages system modules, and the notifications table stores notifications sent to users. The options table stores system settings, and the polycast_events table logs events in the system. The send_logs table tracks sent emails, and the subscriptions table manages user subscriptions to events. The threads table stores information about individual threads, including their conversation ID, user ID, and status, and is linked to the conversations and users tables. Finally, the users table stores information about system users, including their name, email, password, and permissions.
