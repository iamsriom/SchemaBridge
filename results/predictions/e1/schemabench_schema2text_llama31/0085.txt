The schemabench_0085 database is designed to manage subscriptions and related financial information for users. At its core, the database contains five tables: subscriptions, users, subscription_items, receipts, and tax_rates. The subscriptions table stores information about each subscription, including the user who owns it, the subscription's name, its status, price, and quantity, as well as when it ends and was last updated. The user table holds details about each user, such as their Stripe ID, payment method type, billing address, and VAT ID. The subscription_items table links subscriptions to specific items, including their Stripe IDs, product names, prices, and quantities. The receipts table tracks financial transactions, including the user who made the payment, the provider, the amount paid, tax, and the date it was paid. The tax_rates table stores tax information, including the Stripe ID, tax percentage, and the date it was last updated. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: a subscription is linked to a user through the user_id field, a subscription item is linked to a subscription through the subscription_id field, and a receipt is linked to a user through the user_id field. Additionally, the database enforces referential integrity by restricting the deletion of a subscription if it has associated subscription items, and the deletion of a user if they have associated subscriptions or receipts.
