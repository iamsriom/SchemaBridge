The schemabench_0086 database is designed to manage subscriptions and related financial information for users. At its core, the database consists of five tables: subscriptions, users, subscription_items, receipts, and tax_rates. The subscriptions table stores information about each subscription, including its unique identifier, the user it belongs to, the subscription name, and its status, price, and quantity. It also tracks when the subscription ends and when it was last updated or created. The user table contains details about each user, such as their Stripe ID, payment method type, and billing address. The subscription_items table links specific items within a subscription to their corresponding subscription, including their Stripe ID, product name, price, and quantity. The receipts table stores records of payments made by users, including the amount paid, tax, and date of payment. Finally, the tax_rates table stores information about tax rates, including their unique identifier, Stripe ID, and percentage. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: the subscriptions table references the users table by user ID, the subscription_items table references the subscriptions table by subscription ID, and the receipts table references the users table by user ID.
