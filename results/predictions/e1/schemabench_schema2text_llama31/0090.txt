The schemabench_0090 database is designed to store information about flight data, specifically on-time performance for the year 2016. It contains 19 tables that categorize and organize various aspects of flights, airports, and airline information. The L_AIRLINE_ID table holds airline codes and descriptions, while L_AIRPORT stores airport codes and descriptions, and L_AIRPORT_ID and L_AIRPORT_SEQ_ID are used to uniquely identify airports. The L_CANCELLATION table lists cancellation codes and descriptions, and L_CITY_MARKET_ID contains city market codes and descriptions. The L_DEPARRBLK table holds departure and arrival block codes and descriptions, and L_DISTANCE_GROUP_250 lists distance group codes and descriptions. The L_DIVERSIONS table stores diversion codes and descriptions, and L_MONTHS, L_ONTIME_DELAY_GROUPS, L_QUARTERS, L_STATE_ABR_AVIATION, L_STATE_FIPS, L_UNIQUE_CARRIERS, L_WEEKDAYS, and L_WORLD_AREA_CODES contain codes and descriptions for months, on-time delay groups, quarters, state abbreviations, state FIPS codes, unique carriers, weekdays, and world area codes, respectively. The L_YESNO_RESP table holds yes/no response codes and descriptions. The On_Time_On_Time_Performance_2016_1 table is the main data storage table, containing information about flights, including year, quarter, month, day of the month, day of the week, flight date, unique carrier, airline ID, carrier, tail number, flight number, origin airport ID, origin airport sequence ID, origin city market ID, origin airport code, origin city name, origin state, origin state FIPS, origin state name, origin WAC, destination airport ID, destination airport sequence ID, destination city market ID, destination airport code, destination city name, destination state, destination state FIPS, destination state name, destination WAC, CRSDepTime, DepTime, departure delay, departure delay minutes, departure delay 15 minutes, departure delay groups, departure time block, taxi out, wheels off, wheels on, taxi in, CRSArrTime, ArrTime, arrival delay, arrival delay minutes, arrival delay 15 minutes, arrival delay groups, arrival time block, cancelled, cancellation code, diverted, CRSElapsedTime, actual elapsed time, air time, flights, distance, distance group, carrier delay, weather delay, NAS delay, security delay, late aircraft delay, first departure time, total add GTime, longest add GTime, diversion airport landings, diversion reached destination, diversion actual elapsed time, diversion arrival delay, diversion distance, diversion airport code, diversion airport ID, diversion airport sequence ID, diversion wheels on, diversion total GTime, diversion longest GTime, diversion wheels off, and diversion tail number. The tables are connected through foreign keys, which ensure data consistency and relationships between tables, such as the relationship between flights and airlines, airports, and destinations.
