The schemabench_0091 database is designed to store information about patients and their medical records. The Contr table represents a collection of medical contraindications, with each record containing a unique identifier (ICO), a patient's ROKVYS and MESVYS values, a PORADK number, and various other medical details such as ZMCHARZA, ZMTELAKT, AKTPOZAM, and ZMDIET. The primary key of the Contr table is a composite of the ICO and PORADK columns, and it references the ICO column in the Entry table, which is the primary key of the Entry table. The Entry table stores information about each patient, including their KONSKUP, ROKNAR, ROKVSTUP, MESVSTUP, STAV, VZDELANI, ZODPOV, TELAKTZA, AKTPOZAM, DOPRAVA, DOPRATRV, KOURENI, DOBAKOUR, BYVKURAK, ALKOHOL, PIVO7, PIVO10, PIVO12, VINO, LIHOV, PIVOMN, VINOMN, LIHMN, KAVA, CAJ, CUKR, IM, IML, HT, HTD, HTL, ICT, ICTL, DIABET, DIABD, DIABL, HYPLIP, HYPLD, HYPLL, IMTRV, HTTRV, ICTTRV, DIABTRV, HYPLTRV, BOLHR, BOLDK, DUSNOST, VYSKA, VAHA, SYST1, DIAST1, SYST2, DIAST2, TRIC, SUBSC, CHLST, TRIGL, MOC, RARISK, OBEZRISK, KOURRISK, HTRISK, and CHOLRISK. The Death table stores information about patient deaths, with each record containing a unique identifier (ICO), DENUMR, MESUMR, ROKUMR, and PRICUMR. The primary key of the Death table is the ICO column, which references the ICO column in the Entry table. The Letter table stores information about patient letters, with each record containing a unique identifier (ICO), MESDOT, ROKDOT, LEKCHOL, LEKTK, NEMOC1, ROK1, NEMOC2, ROK2, NEMOC3, ROK3, NEMOC4, ROK4, NEMOC5, ROK5, HYPTK, ROKHYPTK, HYPLP, ROKHYPLP, CUKROVKA, ROKCUKR, CUKRTAB, ODCUTAB, DOCUTAB, CUKRINS, ODCUINS, DOCUINS, AP, SI, ROKSI, MM, ROKMM, BDK, ROKBDK1, DUSNOST, ROKDUS, DUSCHUZE, DUSBEH, DUSROVIN, DUSNOC, KURAK, KURAKBYV, ROKPRKOUR, KOURLET, CIGDENMIN, CIGDEN, KOURODLET, DYMKA, PASED, PAMIRNA, PAVELKA, PAPRED10, DIETA, DIECUOD, DIETUKOD, JINADIE, JINADIEOD, VAHA, and VAHAPRED10. The primary key of the Letter table is the ICO column, which references the ICO column in the Entry table.
