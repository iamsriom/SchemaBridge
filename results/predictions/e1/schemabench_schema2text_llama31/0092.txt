The schemabench_0092 database is designed to store information about sports teams, players, coaches, and awards. The database contains nine tables, each representing a different entity. The awards_coaches table stores information about awards won by coaches, with each record including the coach's ID, year, award, league ID, and a note. The awards_coaches table references the coaches table, which stores information about coaches, including their ID, year, team ID, league ID, stint, wins, losses, post-season wins, and post-season losses. The coaches table, in turn, references the teams table, which stores information about teams, including their ID, year, franchise ID, conference ID, division ID, rank, conference rank, playoff status, name, and various statistics such as field goals made, attempted, and points scored. The teams table also references the series_post table, which stores information about post-season series, including the year, round, series, winning team ID, league ID, losing team ID, league ID, wins, and losses. The players table stores information about players, including their ID, first name, middle name, last name, full given name, name suffix, position, first season, last season, height, weight, college, birth date, birth city, birth state, birth country, high school, high school city, high school state, high school country, death date, and race. The players table is referenced by the awards_players table, which stores information about awards won by players, including the player's ID, award, year, league ID, note, and position. The awards_players table is also referenced by the player_allstar table, which stores information about players' all-star performances, including the player's ID, last name, first name, season ID, conference, league ID, games played, minutes, points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, personal fouls, field goal attempts, made field goals, free throw attempts, made free throws, three-point attempts, and made three-pointers. The players_teams table stores information about players' team affiliations, including the player's ID, year, stint, team ID, league ID, games played, games started, minutes, points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, personal fouls, field goal attempts, made field goals, free throw attempts, made free throws, three-point attempts, and made three-pointers, as well as post-season statistics. The players_teams table references the teams table and the players table. The draft table stores information about draft picks, including the draft ID, year, round, selection, overall pick, team ID, first name, last name, suffix name, player ID, draft source, and league ID. The draft table references the teams table.
