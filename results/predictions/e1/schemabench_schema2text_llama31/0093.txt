The schemabench_0093 database is designed to store information about sports teams, players, coaches, and awards. The players table contains detailed information about individual players, including their bioID, first name, middle name, last name, name given, full given name, married name, nickname, position, first season, last season, height, weight, college, birth date, birth city, birth state, birth country, high school, high school city, high school state, and high school country. The players_teams table stores information about a player's stint with a team, including the player's ID, name, year, stint, team ID, league ID, games played, games started, minutes played, points scored, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, and other statistics. The awards_players table links players to awards they have received, including the player's ID, award, year, league ID, note, position, and name. The coaches table contains information about coaches, including their coach ID, full name, year, team ID, league ID, stint, wins, losses, post-season wins, post-season losses, and player-coach status. The draft table stores information about draft picks, including the draft year, round, selection, overall pick, team ID, first name, last name, full name, suffix name, player ID, draft source, league ID, whether the player played professionally, and notes. The teams table contains information about teams, including the year, league ID, team ID, franchise ID, conference ID, division ID, rank, playoff status, seeded, first-round opponent, semi-final opponent, final opponent, name, and statistics such as field goals made, attempted, free throws made, attempted, three-pointers made, attempted, and rebounds. The series_post table stores information about post-season series, including the year, round, series, winning team ID, winning league ID, losing team ID, losing league ID, wins, and losses. The teams_post table contains information about a team's post-season performance, including the year, team ID, league ID, wins, and losses.
