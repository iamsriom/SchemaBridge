The schemabench_0094 database is designed to store information about molecules and their atomic composition. The database contains five tables: atom, bond, gmember, group, and molecule. The atom table stores information about individual atoms within a molecule, with each atom identified by a unique atom_id, and linked to a molecule through the molecule_id. The type of atom is also recorded. The bond table represents the connections between atoms within a molecule, with each bond defined by two atom_ids and a bond type, and is linked back to the atom table through foreign keys. The gmember table links atoms to groups, with each atom belonging to a group identified by a group_id, and is linked to both the atom and group tables. The group table stores information about the different types of groups, with each group identified by a unique group_id and a type. The molecule table stores information about the properties of each molecule, including its activity, logp, and molecular weight, with each molecule identified by a unique molecule_id.
