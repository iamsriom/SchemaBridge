The schemabench_0095 database is designed to store information about chemical compounds and their atomic structures. The 'canc' table contains a list of drugs, each identified by a unique 'drug_id' and categorized by a single character 'class'. The 'atom' table represents individual atoms within these compounds, with each atom having a unique 'atomid', a corresponding 'drug' it belongs to, an 'atomtype', a 'charge', and a two-character 'name'. The 'atom' table is linked to the 'canc' table through a foreign key, indicating which drug each atom belongs to. The 'sbond_1', 'sbond_2', 'sbond_3', and 'sbond_7' tables all represent chemical bonds between atoms within these compounds, with each bond identified by a unique 'id', a corresponding 'drug' it belongs to, and two 'atomid' values representing the atoms connected by the bond. Each of these bond tables is linked to the 'canc' and 'atom' tables through foreign keys, indicating which drug and atoms the bond belongs to.
