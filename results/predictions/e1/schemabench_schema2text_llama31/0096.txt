The schemabench_0096 database is designed to track and analyze customer transactions at gas stations. The database contains six tables, each representing a different aspect of the data. The customers table stores information about individual customers, including their unique customer ID, segment, and currency. The gasstations table holds details about gas stations, such as their ID, chain ID, country, and segment. The products table lists the various products sold at the gas stations, identified by their unique product ID and description. The transactions table records each transaction, including the date, time, customer ID, card ID, gas station ID, product ID, amount, and price, with foreign keys linking it to the customers, gasstations, and products tables to ensure data consistency. The transactions_1k table is a duplicate of the transactions table, likely used for testing or benchmarking purposes. The yearmonth table tracks the total consumption of each customer by month, with a composite primary key combining the customer ID and date, and a foreign key linking it to the customers table to maintain data integrity.
