The schemabench_0097 database is designed to store information about schools and their performance. The database contains three tables: frpm, satscores, and schools. The frpm table represents the Free and Reduced Price Meal (FRPM) data, which includes information about the school's enrollment, meal counts, and eligibility percentages for both kindergarten to 12th grade and ages 5-17. Each record in the frpm table is uniquely identified by the CDSCode, which is a 14-character code that references the CDSCode in the schools table. The schools table stores detailed information about each school, including its name, address, phone number, website, and administrative contacts. The satscores table contains standardized test scores for each school, with each record identified by the CDSCode, which again references the schools table. The satscores table includes information about the number of students who took the test, their average scores in reading, math, and writing, as well as the percentage of students who scored 1500 or higher. The schools table has a primary key of CDSCode, which is also the primary key in the frpm and satscores tables, establishing a relationship between the tables. The schools table also has a unique constraint on the CDSCode to ensure data integrity.
