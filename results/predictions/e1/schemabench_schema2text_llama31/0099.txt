The schemabench_0099 database is designed to track relationships between research papers and the citations within them. The paper table stores information about individual papers, with each paper assigned a unique identifier and a class label. The content table contains information about the words within each paper, with each word linked to its corresponding paper through the paper_id column, which references the paper table. The cites table tracks the citations within the papers, linking each citing paper to the paper it cites through the cited_paper_id and citing_paper_id columns, which together form the primary key of the table. The cited_paper_id and citing_paper_id columns in the cites table are also foreign keys referencing the paper_id column in the paper table, ensuring that only valid paper identifiers are used in the citation relationships.
