The schemabench_0103 database is designed to manage information about various entities, including categories, corporations, members, providers, regions, and financial transactions. The database consists of eight tables: category, charge, corporation, member, payment, provider, region, and statement. The category table stores information about different categories, with each category having a unique category number, description, and code. The corporation table contains details about corporations, including their number, name, address, and contact information, while the member table stores information about individual members, including their number, name, address, and contact details. The provider table holds information about service providers, including their number, name, address, and contact information. The region table stores information about geographic regions, including their number, name, and contact details. The charge table tracks financial charges made to members, including the charge number, member number, provider number, category number, date, amount, and statement number. The payment table stores information about payments made by members, including the payment number, member number, date, amount, and statement number. The statement table contains details about statements sent to members, including the statement number, member number, date, due date, amount, and code. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, which ensure data consistency and integrity. For example, a charge is linked to a category, a member, a provider, and a statement, while a member is linked to a corporation and a region, and a provider is also linked to a region.
