The schemabench_0104 database is designed to store and manage data related to incidents, officers, and subjects involved in these incidents. The incidents table serves as the central hub, containing essential information about each case, including a unique case number, the date and location of the incident, the status and type of weapon involved, the number of subjects and officers present, and details about the grand jury disposition, attorney general forms, and a summary of the incident. The table also includes geographic coordinates for the incident location. The officers table stores information about the officers involved in each incident, including their case number, race, gender, last and first names, and a full name. Each officer is linked to a specific incident through the case number, which serves as the primary key in both the incidents and officers tables. The subjects table contains similar information about the individuals involved in each incident, including their case number, race, gender, last and first names, and a full name, also linked to the incident through the case number. The primary key for the incidents table is the case number, which is a unique identifier for each incident, and the officers and subjects tables have a composite primary key consisting of the case number, which ensures that each officer and subject is uniquely associated with a specific incident.
