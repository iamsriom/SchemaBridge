The schemabench_0105 database is designed to store and manage information about sentences and their constituent terms. The database contains two tables: sentences and terms. The sentences table stores information about individual sentences, with each sentence given a unique identifier, an id, which is the primary key of the table and cannot be null. The class of the sentence is also stored, represented by a three-character string, which can be null if not specified. The terms table, on the other hand, stores the individual terms that make up each sentence. Each term is uniquely identified by a combination of the id of the sentence it belongs to and its own unique id, which together form the primary key of the table and cannot be null. The id of the sentence that a term belongs to is also stored, which references the id of the sentence in the sentences table, and this relationship is enforced by a foreign key constraint that requires the term to be deleted when the sentence it belongs to is deleted, and also updates the term's id_sentence when the sentence's id is updated.
