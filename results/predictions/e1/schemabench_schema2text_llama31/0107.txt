The schemabench_0107 database is designed to manage various financial and client information. It contains eight tables: account, card, client, disp, district, loan, order, and trans. The account table stores information about individual accounts, including a unique account ID, district ID, frequency, and date, with a foreign key referencing the district table to establish a relationship between accounts and districts. The card table holds details about cards, such as card ID, disp ID, type, and issue date, linked to the disp table through the disp ID. The client table stores client information, including client ID, gender, birth date, and district ID, with a foreign key referencing the district table. The disp table serves as a bridge between accounts and clients, containing disp ID, client ID, account ID, and type, with foreign keys referencing both the account and client tables. The district table stores district information, including district ID, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13, A14, A15, and A16, with A12 and A15 allowing null values. The loan table stores loan information, including loan ID, account ID, date, amount, duration, payments, and status, with a foreign key referencing the account table. The order table stores order information, including order ID, account ID, bank to, account to, amount, and k symbol, with a foreign key referencing the account table. Finally, the trans table stores transaction information, including transaction ID, account ID, date, type, operation, amount, balance, k symbol, bank, and account, with a foreign key referencing the account table.
