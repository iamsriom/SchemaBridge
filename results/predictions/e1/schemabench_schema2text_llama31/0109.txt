The schemabench_0109 database is designed to track sales data for various products across different retailers. The go_retailers table stores information about each retailer, including their unique code, name, type, and country. The go_products table contains details about each product, such as its number, product line, type, name, brand, color, unit cost, and unit price. The go_methods table lists different order methods, identified by a unique code and type. The go_daily_sales table records daily sales data, including the retailer code, product number, order method code, date, quantity, unit price, and unit sale price. The go_1k table is a summary of sales data, linking retailers, products, and dates, and is a composite key of the retailer code, product number, and date. The go_daily_sales table has a foreign key referencing the go_methods table for order method codes, and the go_1k table references go_retailers and go_products for retailer codes and product numbers, respectively, with the additional constraint that the combination of retailer code and product number in go_1k must also exist in go_daily_sales.
