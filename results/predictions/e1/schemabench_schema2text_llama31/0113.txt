The schemabench_0113 database is designed to store information about movies and the people involved in their production. The database contains 19 tables, each representing a different entity or relationship. The actors table stores information about individual actors, including their name and sex, with a full-text index on their name for efficient searching. The movies table contains details about each movie, such as its title and release year, with a full-text index on the title for fast searching. The directors table holds information about film directors, including their name, rating, gross revenue, and number of movies they have directed. The distributors table lists the companies that distribute each movie, while the editors table stores information about film editors. The genres, language, and countries tables contain details about the genres, languages, and countries associated with each movie. The movies2actors, movies2directors, movies2editors, movies2producers, and movies2writers tables establish relationships between movies and the people involved in their production, such as actors, directors, editors, producers, and writers. The prodcompanies table lists the production companies involved in each movie. The producers table stores information about individual producers, with a full-text index on their name for efficient searching. The ratings table contains information about the ratings and votes for each movie, while the runningtimes table stores details about the running times of each movie. The writers table holds information about individual writers. Foreign keys are used to establish relationships between these tables, ensuring data consistency and integrity, with cascade delete and update rules applied to ensure that related data is updated or deleted when a primary record is modified.
