The schemabench_0115 database is designed to store information about legal acts and their associated people, links, and fixes. The legalacts table is the core of the database, containing detailed information about each legal act, including its hash, update timestamp, court, case kind, case number, act year, judge, act kind, act number, start date, legal date, status, act link, motive date, motive link, high court, out number, year higher court, type of document, send date, and result of appeal. The people table stores information about individuals associated with these legal acts, including their name, jury status, and court affiliation. The legalact_link table contains links between different legal acts, with each link represented by a pair of act IDs. The legalact_people table stores the relationships between people and legal acts, with each entry representing a person's involvement in a specific legal act. The scrapefix table contains notes about fixes made to the legal acts, including a description of the fix, the contributor who made the fix, and the ID of the legal act being fixed. The database enforces referential integrity through cascade deletes and updates, ensuring that when a legal act is deleted or updated, any associated links, people, or fixes are automatically updated or deleted accordingly.
