The schemabench_0117 database is a comprehensive system for storing and organizing information about chemical compounds, specifically drugs. At its core, the database contains six tables that work together to provide a detailed understanding of the molecular structure and properties of these compounds. The 'atoms' table holds information about individual atoms within a molecule, including their element, type, and charge, and is linked to the 'drugs' table, which stores properties such as the drug's activity, logP value, and lumo value. The 'bonds' table represents the connections between these atoms, specifying the type of bond and the two atoms involved, and references both the 'atoms' and 'drugs' tables. The 'ring_atom' table links atoms to specific rings within a molecule, referencing both the 'atoms' and 'rings' tables, while the 'rings' table itself is linked to the 'ring_strucs' table, which stores information about the structure of the ring. Finally, the 'ring_strucs' table is connected to the 'drugs' table, allowing for the association of a specific ring structure with a particular drug.
