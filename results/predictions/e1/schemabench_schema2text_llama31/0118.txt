The schemabench_0118 database is designed to store information about various countries, their relationships, and statistical data. The database consists of three tables: country, relation, and stat. The country table contains information about individual countries, with a unique country_id serving as the primary key, and a country name that can be null. The country_id is also referenced in the relation table, which stores the relationships between countries, with each relationship having a unique combination of country_id1, country_id2, and relation. The stat table, on the other hand, contains detailed statistical data about each country, including telephone, agricultural population, energy consumption, and many other metrics. The country_id in the stat table serves as the primary key and is referenced in the relation table to establish relationships between countries. The relation table has a composite primary key consisting of country_id1, country_id2, and relation, ensuring that each relationship is unique. The relation table also has foreign keys referencing the country table, ensuring referential integrity. The stat table has numerous columns with various data types, including integers and default values, representing a wide range of statistical data.
