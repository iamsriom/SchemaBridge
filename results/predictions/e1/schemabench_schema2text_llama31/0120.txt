The schemabench_0120 database is designed to store information about sports seasons, teams, and games. The database contains nine tables that work together to provide a comprehensive view of the data. The seasons table stores information about each season, including the season number, day zero date, and regional designations. The teams table holds information about each team, with a unique team ID and team name. The regular season compact results and detailed results tables store information about each game played during the regular season, including the season, day number, winning and losing teams, scores, and locations. The tourney compact results and detailed results tables store similar information for tournament games. The target table stores predictions and win-loss records for specific teams in a given season. The tourney seeds table stores information about the seeding of teams in the tournament, including the season, seed, and team ID. Finally, the tourney slots table stores information about the slots in the tournament, including the season, slot, strong seed, and weak seed. Each of these tables is linked to the teams table through foreign keys, which ensures data consistency and allows for efficient querying of related information. The database also enforces referential integrity through ON DELETE CASCADE and ON UPDATE CASCADE rules, which automatically update or delete related data when a record is deleted or updated.
