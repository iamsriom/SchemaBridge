The schemabench_0121 database is a comprehensive system for managing customer relationships, sales, and inventory. It consists of 13 interconnected tables that store information about customers, employees, orders, products, and suppliers. The Customers table holds details about individual customers, including their company name, contact information, and address. Each customer is associated with a unique customer ID, which is also referenced in the CustomerCustomerDemo table, which links customers to their respective customer types. The CustomerDemographics table stores information about customer types, such as their descriptions. The Employees table contains details about company employees, including their last name, first name, job title, and contact information, with each employee having a unique employee ID that can be referenced in the EmployeeTerritories table, which links employees to the territories they manage. The Orders table stores information about customer orders, including the order date, required date, shipped date, and shipping details, with each order linked to a customer and an employee. The Order Details table contains details about the products ordered, including the product ID, unit price, quantity, and discount. The Products table stores information about products, including their name, supplier ID, category ID, and unit price, with each product linked to a supplier and a category. The Suppliers table contains details about suppliers, including their company name, contact information, and address. The Categories table stores information about product categories, including their name and description. The Region table stores information about geographic regions, including their ID and description, while the Territories table links regions to their respective territories. The Shippers table stores information about shipping companies, including their company name and phone number. Finally, the EmployeeTerritories table links employees to the territories they manage, and the Order Details table links orders to their respective products.
