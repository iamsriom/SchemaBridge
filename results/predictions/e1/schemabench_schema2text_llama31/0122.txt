The schemabench_0122 database is designed to manage information about authors, publishers, books, sales, and employees in the publishing industry. The database contains 11 tables: authors, discounts, employee, jobs, pub_info, publishers, roysched, sales, stores, titleauthor, and titles. The authors table stores information about individual authors, including their ID, last name, first name, phone number, address, city, state, zip code, and contract status. The discounts table contains details about discounts offered by stores, including the discount type, store ID, low and high quantity thresholds, and the discount percentage. The employee table holds information about employees, such as their ID, first name, middle initial, last name, job ID, job level, publisher ID, and hire date. The jobs table lists job descriptions, minimum and maximum job levels, and their corresponding IDs. The pub_info table stores additional information about publishers, including their logo and press information, linked to their publisher ID. The publishers table contains details about publishers, including their ID, name, city, state, and country. The roysched table outlines royalty schedules for books, including the title ID, royalty ranges, and royalty rates. The sales table tracks sales transactions, including the store ID, order number, order date, quantity sold, payment terms, and title ID. The stores table lists store information, including their ID, name, address, city, state, and zip code. The titleauthor table connects authors to their published titles, including the author ID, title ID, author order, and royalty percentage. Finally, the titles table stores information about books, including their ID, title, type, publisher ID, price, advance, royalty rate, sales, notes, and publication date.
