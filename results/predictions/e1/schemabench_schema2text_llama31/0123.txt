The schemabench_0123 database is designed to store information about molecules and their properties. The database contains two tables: 'molecule' and 'position'. The 'molecule' table holds information about individual molecules, with each molecule assigned a unique identifier, 'molecule_id', which is a non-nullable integer that defaults to 0. The 'activity' column, representing a float value, is also stored in this table and can be null. The 'position' table, on the other hand, contains data about the positions of atoms within a molecule, with each position identified by a unique combination of 'molecule_id' and 'position', which together form the primary key. The 'molecule_id' column in the 'position' table references the 'molecule_id' in the 'molecule' table, establishing a relationship between the two tables, and this relationship is enforced with a foreign key constraint that cascades both updates and deletions. The 'position' table also includes several columns to store various properties of the atoms at each position, such as 'flex', 'h_acceptor', 'h_doner', 'pi_acceptor', 'pi_doner', 'polar', 'polarisable', and 'sigma', all of which are floats and can be null, as well as 'size', another float that can be null.
