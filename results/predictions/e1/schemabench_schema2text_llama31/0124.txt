The schemabench_0124 database is designed to store information about restaurants, their locations, and geographic details. The generalinfo table holds the core details of each restaurant, including a unique identifier, a label, the type of food served, the city in which it is located, and a review rating on a scale of 0.0 to 9.9. The city column in this table references the geographic table, which contains a list of cities, their corresponding counties, and regions. The location table provides more specific details about each restaurant's physical address, including the street number and name, as well as the city in which it is located, which is also referenced from the geographic table. The id_restaurant column in the location table serves as a foreign key, linking each location to its corresponding restaurant in the generalinfo table. The id_restaurant column in the generalinfo table serves as the primary key, uniquely identifying each restaurant, and the city column in the geographic table is also a primary key, ensuring that each city is uniquely represented. The foreign key constraints in the generalinfo and location tables are set to cascade, meaning that if a city is deleted from the geographic table, all associated restaurants and locations will also be deleted, and if a restaurant is deleted from the generalinfo table, all associated locations will also be deleted.
