The schemabench_0125 database is designed to manage a movie rental system, storing information about actors, films, customers, staff, and stores. The actor table contains details about individual actors, including their first and last names, and is linked to the film_actor table, which associates actors with the films they have appeared in. The film table holds information about each movie, such as its title, description, release year, and rental duration, and is connected to the film_category table, which links films to their respective categories. The film_text table stores the title and description of each film in a full-text index for efficient searching. The inventory table tracks the availability of films in each store, while the language table lists the languages in which films are available. The payment table records transactions made by customers, including the amount paid and the date of payment, and is linked to the customer and staff tables. The rental table tracks the rental history of each customer, including the date they rented a film and the date they returned it, and is connected to the inventory and staff tables. The staff table contains details about each staff member, including their first and last names, address, and username, and is linked to the address and store tables. The store table holds information about each store, including its manager and address, and is linked to the address and staff tables. The address table stores the addresses of customers, staff, and stores, while the city table contains information about each city, including its name and country, and is linked to the country table. The country table lists the countries in which stores are located.
