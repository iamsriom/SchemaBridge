The schemabench_0126 database is designed to manage customer and employee information, as well as sales data for a company. The Customers table stores details about each customer, including their unique CustomerID, first name, middle initial, and last name. The Employees table holds information about the company's staff, with a unique EmployeeID, first name, middle initial, and last name. The Products table lists the items sold by the company, identified by a unique ProductID, along with the product name and price. The Sales table tracks each sale, including a unique SalesID, the SalesPersonID of the employee who made the sale, the CustomerID of the buyer, the ProductID of the item sold, the quantity sold, and the date of the sale. The Sales table has foreign keys referencing the Employees, Customers, and Products tables, meaning that when an employee, customer, or product is deleted, all related sales will also be deleted.
