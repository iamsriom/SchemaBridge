The schemabench_0127 database is a comprehensive system for managing customer data and sales information. At its core is the Customers table, which stores details about each individual customer, including their ID, sex, marital status, geographic location (GEOID), education level, occupation, and age. The GEOID field references the Demog table, which contains demographic data about various geographic locations, including the number of inhabitants and income levels. The Customers table also has foreign key relationships with the Mailings1_2 and Sales tables, which track customer responses to mailings and sales events, respectively. The Mailings1_2 table stores information about mailings, including the date and response, while the Sales table records the date and amount of each sale. The mailings3 table, which is also related to the Customers table, stores additional mailing information. The database maintains referential integrity through cascade delete and update rules, ensuring that when a customer is deleted or updated, their associated mailings and sales are also updated accordingly.
