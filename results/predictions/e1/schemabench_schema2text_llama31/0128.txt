The schemabench_0128 database is used to store information about businesses and their related data. The businesses table contains detailed information about each business, including its unique business ID, name, address, city, postal code, latitude and longitude coordinates, phone number, tax code, business certificate number, application date, and the name and address of the business owner. The owner's city, state, and zip code are also recorded. The inspections table stores data about the inspections conducted on each business, including the business ID, inspection score, date, and type. The business ID in the inspections table is a foreign key referencing the business ID in the businesses table, establishing a relationship between the two tables. The violations table contains information about any violations found during inspections, including the business ID, date, violation type ID, risk category, and a description of the violation. The business ID in the violations table is also a foreign key referencing the business ID in the businesses table, linking the two tables.
