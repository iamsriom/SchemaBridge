The schemabench_0136 database is designed to store information about students, professors, courses, and their relationships. The RA table contains details about the capabilities of students, with each record tied to a specific professor and student, identified by their unique IDs, which are also used to link them to other tables. The professor ID is a composite primary key, ensuring that each professor-student pair is unique, and it references the prof table, which stores information about professors, including their popularity and teaching ability. The student ID in the RA table also references the student table, which holds details about students, such as their intelligence and ranking. The course table stores information about individual courses, including their rating and difficulty level, and is referenced by the registration table, which contains records of students enrolled in courses, along with their grades and SAT scores. The student ID in the registration table also references the student table, ensuring that each student's information is linked to their course enrollments.
