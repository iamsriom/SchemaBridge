The schemabench_0137 database is designed to store information about YouTube videos and their attributes. The database consists of two tables: utube_states and utube_attributes. The utube_states table stores basic information about the states of YouTube videos, with each state represented by a unique identifier, id, which is the primary key and cannot be null. The class column stores additional information about the state, but it is optional and can be null. The utube_attributes table, on the other hand, stores the attributes of the YouTube videos, with each attribute identified by a unique combination of id_states and name, which together form the primary key. The id_states column references the id column in the utube_states table, indicating that each attribute belongs to a specific state, and the relationship is enforced with a cascade delete and update rule, meaning that if a state is deleted or updated, all its associated attributes will be automatically deleted or updated. The name column stores the name of the attribute, which is required and cannot be null. The value1, value2, and direction columns store additional information about the attribute, but they are optional and can be null.
