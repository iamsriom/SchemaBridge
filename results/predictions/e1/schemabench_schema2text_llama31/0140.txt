The schemabench_0140 database is designed to track various types of entities and their relationships, particularly focusing on voyages and the people associated with them. The database contains eight tables: craftsmen, impotenten, invoices, passengers, seafarers, soldiers, total, and voyages. Each of these tables represents a distinct entity, with craftsmen, impotenten, passengers, seafarers, and soldiers all sharing a similar structure, which includes a unique identifier number, a supervisor number, trip number, and trip supervisor, along with several date fields tracking their onboard and departure status at various points during a voyage. The invoices table, on the other hand, contains information about financial transactions related to voyages, including the voyage number, supervisor number, trip number, and trip supervisor, as well as an invoice number and chamber. The total table appears to be a duplicate of the other entity tables, but with a trip number field that can be null. The voyages table serves as the central hub, containing detailed information about each voyage, including its artificial ID, number, supervisor number, trip number, trip supervisor, boat name, master, tonnage, type, and various dates and locations. The voyages table also serves as the primary key for the other tables, with each of the entity tables referencing the voyages table through a composite foreign key of number and number_sup, indicating that each entity is associated with a specific voyage. The relationships between these tables are established through these foreign keys, ensuring data integrity and consistency throughout the database.
