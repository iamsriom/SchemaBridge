The schemabench_0144 database is designed to store information about employees, their salaries, job titles, departments, and employment history. The employees table contains detailed information about each employee, including their employee number, birth date, first and last names, gender, and hire date. The salaries table stores the salary history of each employee, including their employee number, salary amount, and the dates they were paid that salary, with a default salary of $13.21. The titles table tracks the various job titles held by each employee, including the title, start date, and end date, with the option to leave the end date blank if the title is still current. The departments table contains a list of all departments in the organization, with each department identified by a unique four-character code and a descriptive name. The dept_manager table shows which employees are managers of which departments, with the employee number, department code, start date, and end date of their management tenure. The dept_emp table tracks which employees are members of which departments, with the employee number, department code, start date, and end date of their department membership. The foreign keys in these tables ensure that an employee can only be associated with one department at a time, and that an employee can only hold one job title at a time, while the primary key in the employees table ensures that each employee has a unique employee number.
