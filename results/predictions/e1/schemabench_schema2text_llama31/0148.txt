The schemabench_0148 database is designed to manage an e-commerce system, storing information about users, products, orders, payments, refunds, and invoices. The users table contains details about registered customers, including their names, emails, and passwords, with each user's email address being unique. The categories table stores information about product categories, with each category having a unique name. The products table holds details about the products available for sale, including their names, prices, and stock levels. The order_items table links products to orders, specifying the quantity and price of each item in an order, while the orders table contains information about each order, including the total amount, status, and the user who made the order. The invoices table stores details about invoices generated for orders, including the amount, issued date, and due date. The payments table holds information about payments made towards orders, including the payment method and date. The refunds table stores details about refunds issued for orders, including the amount refunded and the reason for the refund. Each of these tables is linked to others through foreign keys, ensuring that data integrity is maintained, for example, an order can only be linked to a user who exists in the users table, and a payment can only be linked to an invoice that exists in the invoices table.
