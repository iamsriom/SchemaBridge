The schemabench_0149 database is a comprehensive system for managing user accounts, subscriptions, and financial transactions. At its core is the account table, which stores information about each user, including their email address, date of creation, and a unique identifier from Stripe, a payment processing service. Each account is associated with a user, who is identified by a unique ID and has a name, email address, password, and permissions level. The user's account ID is also linked to a session, which represents an active login session and includes a unique token and expiration date. The account can also be associated with a subscription, which is stored in the subscription table and includes details such as the plan, status, and start and end dates. The subscription is linked to a Stripe subscription ID and is associated with a specific account. The account can also have multiple payments, which are stored in the payment table and include the amount, currency, and status of each transaction. The payment table is linked to the account table, and each payment is associated with a unique Stripe payment ID. The plan_feature table links specific plans to features, which are stored in the feature table and include a unique name and description. The invite table stores information about pending invitations, including the email address and account ID of the recipient, while the audit_log table tracks all changes made to the system, including the user ID, account ID, action taken, and details of the change.
