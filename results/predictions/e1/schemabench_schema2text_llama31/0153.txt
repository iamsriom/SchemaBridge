The schemabench_0153 database is designed to manage a library's operations, encompassing information about books, staff members, library members, issued books, returns, and any associated fines. The Books table stores details of the library's collection, including a unique identifier, title, author, publisher, publication date, ISBN, total copies, and available copies, with a check constraint ensuring that either the author or publisher is always provided. The Staffs table holds information about library staff, such as their ID, name, address, phone number, email, and date of joining. The Members table contains details of library members, including their ID, name, address, phone number, email, and membership status. The IssuedBooks table tracks issued books, with a unique identifier, member ID, book ID, issue date, due date, and the staff member who issued the book, referencing the Members and Books tables for these details. The ReturnBooks table records book returns, including a unique identifier, the associated issued book ID, return date, and the staff member who received the return, referencing the IssuedBooks table for the issued book ID and the Staffs table for the staff member. The Fine table stores any fines associated with returned books, referencing the ReturnBooks table for the return ID, the IssuedBooks table for the issued book ID, and the Members table for the member ID, with a decimal field for the fine amount.
