The schemabench_0154 database is designed to manage a retail business, storing information about customers, products, orders, and customer services. The Customers table holds details about each customer, including their unique CustomerID, email address, password, full name, and gender. The Products table stores information about the products sold, including the ProductID, product name, price, description, the number of items in stock, and up to three images. The Shelves table is used to manage the physical storage of products, with each shelf having a unique ShelfID, location coordinates, and a reference to the ProductID it holds, along with the number of orders it has received. The Robots table tracks the location and status of robots used in the business, including their speed, battery percentage, current location, and whether they are charging, with a unique ShelfID referencing the shelf they are currently on. The Orders table stores information about each order, including the OrderID, ProductID, and quantity ordered, with a composite primary key to ensure each order is unique. The Orders_details table holds additional details about each order, including the customer's ID, total cost, order date, phone number, address, payment method, and order status. The Wishlist table allows customers to save products they are interested in, with a composite primary key to ensure each customer can only add a product once. The Customer_Services table stores messages sent to customers, including the message ID, customer ID, phone number, and the message itself. The Notifications table is used to send notifications to customers, with a unique notification ID, notification message, and the date and time it was sent.
