The schemabench_0155 database is designed to manage information related to residents, employees, robots, and various entities involved in a crime scene investigation. The Residents table stores details about individuals, including their first and last names, gender, birth date, role, and a unique bracelet ID that links them to the Bracelet table. The Employee table is similar, but also includes a hire date and function. The Robots table contains information about robots, such as their name, company, function, hire date, price, and a unique bracelet ID. The House table stores details about houses, including a unique ID, room name, CCTV ID, and a crime scene description. The Location table tracks the location of residents, employees, and robots, with a house ID, time, and bracelet ID. The Bracelet table simply stores the ID and name of each bracelet. The Cctv table contains information about CCTV cameras, including the camera room, date, switch on and off status, breakdown, and modification details. The Modification_type table links CCTV cameras to their modification descriptions. The Evidence tables (Evidence_One, Evidence_Two, and Evidence_Three) store descriptions of evidence found at a crime scene. The Ransack tables (Ransack_One, Ransack_Two, and Ransack_Three) store descriptions of items ransacked during a crime. The Crime_scene table stores details about a crime scene, including the victim, date, house ID, and links to evidence and ransacked items. The Suspect_Analyse_Post_Crime, Innocent, Real_Suspect, and Culprit tables store information about suspects, including their bracelet ID, analysis, and status. Each of these tables has a unique bracelet ID that links them to the Bracelet table, allowing for easy tracking of individuals involved in the investigation.
