The schemabench_0157 database is designed to manage a library's collection and operations. It contains 17 tables that work together to store information about countries, publishers, book languages, book statuses, customers, books, book inventory, storage locations, loans, authors, and various logs. The country table stores information about different countries, with each country having a unique identifier and a name. The category table contains categories of books, while the publisher table stores details about the publishers of the books. The book_language table lists the languages in which books are written, and the book_status table tracks the status of each book. The customer table holds information about the library's patrons, including their name, date of birth, and contact details. The book table stores details about each book, including its title, publication date, and language, and links to the publisher and language tables. The book_inventory table tracks the availability of each book, including its status and storage location. The storage_location table stores the shelf and section where each book is kept. The loan table records when a book is borrowed, including the customer who borrowed it and the due date. The author table contains information about the authors of the books, and the book_author table links authors to the books they have written. The book_category table links books to their categories. The book_return table tracks when a book is returned, and the log_statement, log_loan, and log_deleted_book_return tables store logs of various events, such as user queries and book returns.
