The schemabench_0159 database is a comprehensive system for managing information about actors, films, and related entities. It consists of nine tables: actor, address, category, city, country, film, film_actor, film_category, and language. The actor table stores details about individuals involved in the film industry, including their first and last names, address, and a unique identifier. Each actor is associated with an address, which is stored in the address table, containing information such as the physical address, district, city, postal code, and phone number. The category table categorizes films into different genres, with each category having a unique name and a timestamp for the last update. The city table stores information about cities, including their names and associated country, while the country table contains a list of countries with their respective names. The film table holds details about movies, including their titles, descriptions, release years, languages, rental durations, rental rates, lengths, replacement costs, ratings, and special features. The film_actor table links actors to films they have been involved in, while the film_category table connects films to their respective categories. The language table stores information about languages, including their names and a timestamp for the last update. The database maintains referential integrity through foreign keys, such as the actor_id in the actor table referencing the address_id in the address table, and the film_id in the film_actor table referencing the film_id in the film table. Additionally, the database enforces data integrity through constraints like NOT NULL, DEFAULT, and UNIQUE, ensuring that all required fields are populated and preventing duplicate entries.
