The schemabench_0160 database is designed to manage information about companies, their stock exchanges, brokers, traders, and financial transactions. The database contains eight tables: company_info, exchange, broker_info, trader_info, account_info, broker_exchange_deals, stock_info, and transaction_info. The company_info table stores details about companies, including their unique identifier, name, address, and establishment date. The exchange table holds information about stock exchanges, with each exchange having a unique identifier and a name. The broker_info table stores details about brokers, including their first and last names, contact information, and physical address. The trader_info table contains information about traders, including their first and last names, account number, contact information, and physical address. The account_info table links traders to their accounts, with each account having a unique identifier, cash balance, and security balance, and referencing the trader and broker information. The broker_exchange_deals table tracks deals made by brokers with exchanges, with each deal having a unique identifier, fees paid, and referencing the broker and exchange information. The stock_info table stores details about stocks, including their unique identifier, name, number of shares, symbol, launch date, and price, and referencing the company and exchange information. The transaction_info table tracks financial transactions, including the order ID, transaction price, number of shares, and fees paid by the seller and buyer, and referencing the broker, exchange, trader, account, stock, and deal information.
