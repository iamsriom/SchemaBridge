The schemabench_0161 database is designed to manage customer orders and their associated items. It contains four main tables: Customers, Items, Orders, and Order_Details. The Customers table stores information about each customer, with a unique Customer_Code that serves as the primary key and a Customer_Name field that can hold up to 255 characters. The Items table lists the products available for sale, with a unique Item_ID as the primary key, a descriptive Item_Description, and a Price that can have up to 10 digits with 2 decimal places. The Orders table tracks each customer's orders, with a unique Order_ID as the primary key, a Date and Time for the order, and a Customer_Code that references the Customer_Code in the Customers table, establishing a link between the two. The Order_Details table contains the specifics of each order, with a composite primary key consisting of the Order_ID and Item_ID, referencing the Order_ID in the Orders table and the Item_ID in the Items table, respectively. This establishes a many-to-many relationship between orders and items, allowing multiple items to be part of a single order and vice versa.
