The schemabench_0162 database is designed to manage information about products, customers, cities, and orders. The products table stores details about individual products, including a unique 11-character product ID, category, subcategory, and product name. The cities table contains information about cities, with a unique auto-incrementing ID, city name, state, country, region, and market. The customers table holds customer data, featuring a unique 50-character customer ID, customer name, and segment. The orders table is used to track customer purchases, with a unique auto-incrementing order ID, order and ship dates, ship mode, order priority, and details about the customer, city, and product involved in the transaction, including unit price, unit cost, quantity, discount, and shipping cost. The orders table also includes a field to indicate whether the order was returned. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: the orders table references the customers table by customer ID, the cities table by city ID, and the products table by product ID, ensuring that each order is linked to a valid customer, city, and product.
