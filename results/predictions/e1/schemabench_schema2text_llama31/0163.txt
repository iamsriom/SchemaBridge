The schemabench_0163 database is designed to manage event planning and resource allocation for various facilities and customers. It consists of eight tables: CUSTOMER, FACILITY, LOCATION, EVENTREQUEST, EMPLOYEE, EVENTPLAN, RESOURCETBL, and EVENTPLANLINE. The CUSTOMER table stores information about each customer, including their customer number, name, address, internal status, contact details, phone number, city, state, and zip code. The FACILITY table holds data on each facility, with a unique facility number and name. The LOCATION table links facilities to their respective locations, with a location number, facility number, and location name. The EVENTREQUEST table contains details of event requests, including the event number, date held, date requested, facility number, customer number, authorization date, status, estimated cost, estimated audience, and budget number. The EMPLOYEE table stores information about each employee, including their employee number, name, department, email, and phone number. The EVENTPLAN table links event requests to their corresponding plans, with a plan number, event number, work date, notes, activity, and employee number. The RESOURCETBL table lists available resources, with a resource number, name, and rate. The EVENTPLANLINE table details the planned activities for each event, including the plan number, line number, start and end times, number of fields, location number, and resource number. The database enforces various constraints to ensure data integrity, such as primary keys, unique constraints, foreign keys with cascade and restrict rules, check constraints, and indexes to ensure that data is accurate and consistent.
