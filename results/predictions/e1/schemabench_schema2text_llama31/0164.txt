The schemabench_0164 database is designed to manage data for a retail business, specifically a coffee shop. It contains nine tables that work together to store information about customers, products, sales, and staff. The Customer table holds details about each customer, including their ID, home store, name, email, date of birth, loyalty card number, and birth year. The Generation table is used to categorize customers by their birth year into different generations. The Product table stores information about each product sold, such as its ID, group, category, type, description, unit of measure, wholesale and retail prices, and whether it's tax-exempt or on promotion. The Date table contains information about dates, including the date, week, month, quarter, and year, with each date assigned a unique ID. The Pastry_Inventory table tracks the sales of pastries, including the sales outlet, transaction date, product ID, quantity sold, and waste. The Sales_Target table sets sales targets for each sales outlet by year and month, including goals for beans, beverages, food, merchandise, and total sales. The Sales_Outlet table stores information about each sales outlet, including its ID, type, square footage, address, city, state, telephone number, postal code, longitude, latitude, manager, and neighborhood. The Staff table holds details about each staff member, including their ID, first and last name, position, start date, and location. The Sales_Receipt table tracks each transaction, including the transaction ID, date, time, sales outlet ID, staff ID, customer ID, whether the sale was made in-store or not, the order number, line item ID, product ID, quantity sold, line item amount, unit price, and whether the item was on promotion.
