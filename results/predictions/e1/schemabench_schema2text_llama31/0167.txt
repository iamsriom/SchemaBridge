The schemabench_0167 database is designed to manage hotel reservations and customer information. The reservations table stores details about each booking, including a unique reservation ID, the customer who made the reservation, the check-in and check-out dates, the date the reservation was made, and the room number assigned. The customer ID references the customers table, which contains information about each guest, such as their first and last name, email address, and phone number. Each customer is uniquely identified by their customer ID, which is also referenced in the reservations table to link a reservation to its corresponding customer. The room table stores information about each room in the hotel, including its room number, the number of beds, the maximum number of occupants, whether it is a smoking or pet-friendly room, and the nightly cost. The room number in the reservations table references the room table to link a reservation to its assigned room. The database ensures data integrity by enforcing not null constraints on all columns, meaning that every reservation must have a customer, room, and reservation date, and every customer and room must have a unique ID. The database also enforces primary keys on the reservation ID, customer ID, and room number to prevent duplicate entries, and uses foreign keys to ensure that a reservation can only be linked to an existing customer and room.
