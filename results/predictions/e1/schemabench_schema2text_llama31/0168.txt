The schemabench_0168 database is designed to store information about patients, their medical histories, and the treatments they receive. The patients table holds basic information about each patient, including their id, name, and date of birth. The medical_histories table stores details about each patient's medical history, including the date they were admitted, their status, and a reference to the patient's id in the patients table. This establishes a relationship between the two tables, ensuring that each medical history is linked to a specific patient. The invoices table contains information about payments made to the hospital, including the total amount, the date the invoice was generated, and the date it was paid, as well as a reference to the medical history id in the medical_histories table. The treatments table lists the different types of treatments available, including their names. The invoice_items table stores details about each item on an invoice, including the unit price, quantity, total price, and references to the invoice id in the invoices table and the treatment id in the treatments table. The medical_treatments table links medical histories to specific treatments, allowing for multiple treatments to be associated with a single medical history. This table also references the medical history id and treatment id in the medical_histories and treatments tables, respectively, ensuring that each treatment is linked to a valid medical history and vice versa.
