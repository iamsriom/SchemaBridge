The schemabench_0169 database is designed to manage a bookstore's operations, encompassing various entities and their relationships. The database consists of 18 tables, each representing a distinct entity or concept. The Region table stores information about postal codes, states, and countries, with postal code serving as the primary key. The Address table contains details about buildings, streets, cities, and postal codes, with address_id as the primary key, and it references the Region table for postal code information. The Publisher table holds data about publishers, including their names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses, with publisher_id as the primary key and address_id referencing the Address table. The Book table stores information about books, such as ISBN, title, author, genre, publisher, number of pages, cost, price, publisher's percentage, stock, and threshold, with ISBN as the primary key and publisher_id referencing the Publisher table. The Staff table contains staff members' email addresses, with staff_id as the primary key. The Warehouse table stores information about warehouses, including their addresses, with warehouse_id as the primary key and address_id referencing the Address table. The Client table holds client information, including names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses, with client_id as the primary key and address_id referencing the Address table. The BankAccount table stores bank account details, with account_number as the primary key. The Sales table tracks sales data, including ISBN, month, year, and quantity, with a composite primary key of ISBN, month, and year, and it references the Book table for ISBN information. The Orders table manages orders, including order numbers, order placement dates, statuses, final totals, addresses, and warehouse IDs, with order_number as the primary key and address_id and warehouse_id referencing the Address and Warehouse tables, respectively. The manage table tracks staff members responsible for managing books, with a composite primary key of ISBN and staff_id, referencing the Book and Staff tables. The update_sales table updates sales data, including order numbers, ISBNs, months, and years, with a composite primary key of order_number, ISBN, month, and year, referencing the Orders and Sales tables. The handle_account table manages bank accounts for orders, with a composite primary key of order_number and account_number, referencing the Orders and BankAccount tables. The client_account table tracks bank accounts for clients, with a composite primary key of client_id and account_number, referencing the Client and BankAccount tables. The publisher_account table manages bank accounts for publishers, with a composite primary key of publisher_id and account_number, referencing the Publisher and BankAccount tables. The tracks table tracks client orders, with a composite primary key of order_number and client_id, referencing the Orders and Client tables. The emails table stores email addresses for staff members and publishers, with a composite primary key of staff_id and publisher_id, referencing the Staff and Publisher tables. Finally, the checkout table tracks book checkouts, with a composite primary key of ISBN and order_number, referencing the Book and Orders tables.
