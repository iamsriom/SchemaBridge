The schemabench_0170 database is designed to manage a business's transactions and financial activities. The customers table stores information about each customer, including their unique customer ID, name, address, city, country, phone number, and email address. The items table contains details about the products being sold, such as the item ID, type, name, width, weight, and HS code. The orders table represents the transactions between customers and the business, with each order having a unique order ID, referencing the customer who made the purchase, the item being purchased, the export ID if applicable, the import ID if applicable, the quantity of items ordered, and the price paid. The payment table tracks the payments made by customers, with each payment having a unique payment ID, referencing the order it relates to, the remittance amount, and the date of payment. The exports and imports tables store information about the business's export and import activities, including the export ID, date, destination, and freight costs. The production table tracks the production of items, with each production record having a unique production ID, referencing the order it relates to, the factory name, the quantity produced, and the price per unit. The exchanges table stores the exchange rates, with each record having a unique date and a rate. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, with the orders table referencing the customers, items, exports, and imports tables, the payment table referencing the orders table, the production table referencing the orders table, and the exchanges table having a unique date as its primary key.
