The schemabench_0171 database is used to track and manage financial trades. The audit table stores records of all activities related to trades, including the trade ID, the type of activity, and the timestamp of the activity, with each record automatically assigned a unique audit ID. The counterparties table contains information about the counterparties involved in trades, including their ID, name, and exposure limit, and is linked to the trades table through the trade ID. The instrument table holds details about the financial instruments used in trades, such as the instrument ID, label, maturity date, settlement type, and currency, and is also linked to the trades table through the trade ID. The trader table stores information about the traders involved in trades, including their ID, name, email, and trade ID, and is also linked to the trades table through the trade ID. The trades table itself contains comprehensive information about each trade, including the trade ID, portfolio, trade date, instrument ID, quantity, price, typology, counterparty ID, trader ID, notional, fixed rate, float rate index, and spread. The tradestatus table tracks the status of each trade, including the trade ID, status ID, and status name, and is linked to the trades table through the trade ID. The trade ID is the primary key for the audit, counterparties, instrument, trader, trades, and tradestatus tables, while the combination of trade ID and counterparty ID is the primary key for the counterparties table, and the combination of trade ID and status ID is the primary key for the tradestatus table. The trade ID is also used as a foreign key in the audit, counterparties, instrument, trader, and tradestatus tables to establish relationships between these tables and the trades table, with the update and delete rules set to cascade, meaning that any changes to the trade ID in the trades table will automatically be reflected in the related records in these tables, and any deletion of a trade ID in the trades table will also delete the related records in these tables.
