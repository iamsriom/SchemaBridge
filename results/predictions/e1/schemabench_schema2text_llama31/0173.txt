The schemabench_0173 database is designed to manage information related to blood donations and transfusions. It consists of nine tables, each representing a different entity within the system. The blood table stores information about the different types of blood, with each type having a unique identifier and a list of compatible types. The hospital and private_facility tables contain details about medical facilities, including their names, addresses, contact information, and services offered, as well as whether they provide emergency services. The donor table holds information about individuals who donate blood, including their personal details, contact information, and date of birth. The donor_medical table stores medical information about donors, such as their allergies, weight, blood type, medical conditions, and medical history, and is linked to the donor table through a foreign key. The patient table contains information about individuals who receive blood transfusions, including their personal details, contact information, and date of birth. The patient_medical table stores medical information about patients, such as their blood type, allergies, medical conditions, and medical history, and is linked to the patient table through a foreign key. The blood_transfusion table tracks blood transfusions, including the patient's medical information, the hospital or private facility where the transfusion took place, and the dates of admission and discharge. The blood_donations table stores information about blood donations, including the donor's medical information, the hospital or private facility where the donation was made, and details about the blood itself, such as its description, component type, and quantity. The database enforces referential integrity through foreign keys, ensuring that data is consistent and accurate across the different tables, and uses primary keys and unique constraints to uniquely identify each record.
