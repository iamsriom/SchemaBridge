The schemabench_0175 database is designed to manage information about employees, customers, and music-related data. The employee table stores details about the staff, including their employee ID, last and first names, job title, supervisor's ID, birth and hire dates, address, and contact information. The customer table contains information about clients, including their ID, name, company, address, and contact details, with a foreign key referencing the employee ID of the support representative assigned to them. The invoice table holds records of transactions, including the invoice ID, customer ID, date, billing address, and total amount, with a foreign key referencing the customer ID. The artist, album, and media_type tables store data about music artists, albums, and types of media, respectively, with foreign keys linking albums to artists and tracks to albums, media types, and genres. The genre table lists music genres, and the track table contains information about individual songs, including their ID, name, album ID, media type, genre, composer, duration, and price. The invoice_line table links invoices to specific tracks, and the playlist_track table connects playlists to tracks. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys in each table, foreign keys that link related data, and constraints that ensure data consistency, such as the requirement that a customer must have a support representative assigned to them, and that an invoice must be linked to a customer and a track.
