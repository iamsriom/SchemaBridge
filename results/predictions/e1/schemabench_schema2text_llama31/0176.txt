The schemabench_0176 database is designed to manage various aspects of water supply and labor management. At its core, it contains information about different types of water, which are categorized into distinct types with unique identifiers and descriptions. Each water type is associated with a registration number, which serves as a primary key, and is linked to a supplier through a foreign key referencing the SUPPLIERS table. The SUPPLIERS table stores information about the suppliers, including their codes, names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses. The DELIVERIES table tracks the deliveries made by suppliers to different water types, recording the delivery date, supplier code, registration number, amount, and unit of measurement. The UNIT_MEASURES table defines the possible units of measurement, with each unit having a unique identifier and title. The JOBS table lists various job titles and their expected number of workers, while the DEPARTMENTS table stores information about different departments, including their codes, titles, and expected number of workers. The WORKERS table contains details about individual workers, including their security numbers, names, last names, shifts, birth dates, addresses, phones, and employment status. Each worker is assigned to a department and a job, and their records are linked to the DELIVERIES table through the RECORDS_OF_WATER_BALANCE table, which tracks the water balance for each worker on a given date, along with the initial and end states of the water balance. The database also includes constraints to ensure data integrity, such as primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and a check constraint to limit the possible shift values for workers to 1, 2, or 3.
