The schemabench_0177 database is designed to store information about employees and their work history within a company. The database contains six tables: departments, employees, dept_manager, dept_emp, salaries, and titles. The departments table holds information about the different departments within the company, with each department identified by a unique four-character code and a descriptive name. The employees table stores details about each employee, including their unique employee number, birth date, first and last name, gender, and hire date. The dept_manager table links employees to the departments they manage, with each manager assigned to a specific department for a particular period of time, and the employee's unique number and department code serve as the primary key for this table. The dept_emp table tracks the departments each employee has worked in, with each employee assigned to a department for a specific period of time, and the employee's unique number is the primary key. The salaries table stores the salary history of each employee, with each employee's unique number serving as the primary key, and the from and to dates indicating the period during which the salary was in effect. The titles table records the different job titles held by each employee, with the employee's unique number serving as the primary key, and the from and to dates indicating the period during which the title was held. Each of these tables is linked to the employees table through foreign keys, ensuring that only valid employee numbers can be associated with departments, salaries, and job titles, and that any changes to an employee's status in one table are reflected in the other tables.
