The schemabench_0178 database is designed to manage various aspects of an organization's operations, including employee information, job postings, and performance evaluations. The database contains 27 tables that store data on employees, their job history, salaries, bonuses, and performance evaluations, as well as information on job openings, job candidates, and company divisions. The employees table stores details about each employee, including their personal information, job title, department, and division, while the job table stores information about job openings, including the job title, description, and salary range. The job_candidates table stores information about job applicants, including their resume and cover letter, while the job_offers table stores information about job offers made to candidates. The appraisal_types and appraisal_period_types tables store information about the different types of performance evaluations and appraisal periods, respectively. The appraisal table stores information about each performance evaluation, including the employee's overall evaluation and any comments from the appraiser. The absence_types table stores information about the different types of absences, such as vacation or sick leave, while the absences table stores information about each absence, including the start and end dates and the type of absence. The absence_totals table stores information about the total number of days absent for each employee. The bonus_distribution table stores information about bonuses granted to employees, including the amount and currency, while the bonuses table stores information about the bonuses themselves, including the title and date granted. The currencies table stores information about the different currencies used by the organization, including the symbol and fraction. The curr_rates table stores information about currency exchange rates, including the from and to currencies and the exchange rate. The departments table stores information about the different departments within the organization, including the department name and manager. The division_change table stores information about changes to an employee's division, including the date and reason for the change. The divisions table stores information about the different divisions within the organization, including the division name and location. The job_history table stores information about an employee's job history, including the job title and start and end dates. The locations table stores information about the different locations within the organization, including the location name and country. The memos table stores information about memos written to employees, including the date and type of memo. The person_relation_types table stores information about the different types of relationships between people, including family relationships and friendships. The person_relations table stores information about the relationships between people, including the type of relationship and the start date. The persons table stores information about individuals, including their personal details and contact information. The regions table stores information about the different regions within the organization, including the region name. The sal_history table stores information about an employee's salary history, including the salary amount and currency. The team_change table stores information about changes to an employee's team, including the date and reason for the change.
