The schemabench_0179 database is designed to store information about racing events, drivers, cars, teams, and results. The database contains 12 tables, each representing a different entity. The drivers table holds information about individual drivers, including their title, first name, last name, nickname, suffix, sex, birthdate, and country of origin. The circuits table stores details about the racing circuits, such as their name, the date they were established, length in kilometers and miles, layout, and any changes made to the circuit. The races table contains information about each racing event, including the circuit it was held at, the date and start time of the event, whether it was cancelled, the distance covered, the number of laps completed, and the average speed in kilometers per hour and miles per hour. The cars table stores information about the cars that participated in the racing events, including their class, chassis, and engine. The car_numbers table links cars to their corresponding racing events, with each car assigned a unique number for each event. The results table stores the outcome of each racing event, including the position of each car, the number of laps completed, distance covered, racing time, and any reason for not completing the event. The results_in table contains aggregated results for each car class, including the position, car number, team country, team name, drivers' country and name, car chassis, car engine, car tyres, laps completed, distance covered, racing time, and any reason for not completing the event. The teams table stores information about the teams that participated in the racing events, including their title, country, and whether they are a private entrant. The tyres table lists the different tyre brands used in the racing events, with each brand assigned a unique identifier. The car_tyres table links cars to their corresponding tyre brands, with each car assigned a specific tyre for each racing event. The driver_results table links drivers to their corresponding results, with each driver assigned an order number for each event. The team_results table links teams to their corresponding results, with each team assigned an order number for each event.
