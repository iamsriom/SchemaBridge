The schemabench_0180 database is designed to track and manage information about aircraft and their traffic patterns. The aircraft table stores detailed information about each aircraft, including its unique identifier, registration number, manufacturer, model, and operational details such as the owner, operator, and status. The table also captures the aircraft's technical specifications, including its serial number, engine type, and whether it is equipped with ADS-B, ACARS, and other advanced systems. The registration and deregistration dates, as well as the aircraft's first flight date and built date, are also recorded. Additionally, the table includes a category description and notes field for any miscellaneous information. The traffic table, on the other hand, tracks the movement of aircraft, storing information such as the callsign, flight number, and type code, as well as the aircraft's current and previous locations, including latitude and longitude coordinates, and altitude. The table also captures the origin and destination of each flight, along with the date and time of the first and last sightings.
