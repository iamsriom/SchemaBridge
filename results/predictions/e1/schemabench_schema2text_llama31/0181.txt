The schemabench_0181 database is designed to manage information about various entities in a retail business, including customers, employees, stores, products, and suppliers. The database consists of 11 tables, each representing a distinct entity or relationship. The address table stores information about physical locations, including address lines, city, zip code, state, and country, with a unique address ID serving as the primary key. The category table contains a list of product categories, identified by a unique category ID. The chain store table stores information about chain stores, including their name, email, phone number, website, and office address, with a unique chain store ID as the primary key. The customers table stores customer information, including name, phone number, email, and the date they became a customer, with a unique customer ID as the primary key and a foreign key referencing the address table to link customers to their physical locations. The employee table stores employee information, including name, phone number, email, and the date they became an employee, with a unique employee ID as the primary key and foreign keys referencing the address and store tables to link employees to their physical locations and stores. The products table stores product information, including name, price, type, description, and the store and category they belong to, with a unique product ID as the primary key and foreign keys referencing the store and category tables. The products_off table stores information about products on offer, including name and price, with a unique product ID as the primary key. The products_suppliers table stores information about product suppliers, including the supplier ID, product ID, and supply date, with foreign keys referencing the products and suppliers tables. The store table stores store information, including name, description, phone number, capacity, and the chain store and address they belong to, with a unique store ID as the primary key and foreign keys referencing the chain store and address tables. The store_customers_buy table stores information about customer purchases, including the store, customer, employee, and product involved, with foreign keys referencing the store, customers, employee, and products tables. Finally, the suppliers table stores supplier information, including name, phone number, email, and the address they belong to, with a unique supplier ID as the primary key and a foreign key referencing the address table to link suppliers to their physical locations.
