The schemabench_0182 database is designed to manage a car rental service, storing information about customers, drivers, cars, bookings, and transactions. The customer table contains essential details about each customer, including their name, password, phone number, and the ID of their current booking. The car table holds information about the available vehicles, such as their brand, model, registration number, capacity, and price per kilometer, with a unique registration number ensuring each car is distinct. The driver table stores details about the drivers, including their name, password, phone number, current booking, and the ID of the car they are driving, with a foreign key referencing the car table to establish the relationship between drivers and vehicles. The transaction table records financial transactions, including the type of payment and the amount. The booking table is the central hub, containing information about each booking, such as the pickup and destination locations, number of people, pickup time, booking status, and whether the booking is a shared ride, along with foreign keys referencing the customer, driver, and car tables to establish relationships between bookings and the entities involved.
