The schemabench_0183 database is designed to manage information about music albums, songs, artists, and their relationships. The Location table stores details about physical locations, including a unique identifier, name, phone number, and address. Each Employee is associated with a specific Location, and when an employee is deleted, the corresponding location is also deleted. The Album table contains information about music albums, with a unique identifier, title, release date, and cost. Each album is linked to a Song, which has a unique identifier, title, duration, and lyrics, and when a song is deleted, its associated album is also deleted. The Genre table stores information about music genres, with a unique name and description. The SongGenreList table links songs to genres, and when a song or genre is deleted, the corresponding link is also deleted. The Artist table stores information about individual artists, including a unique identifier, stage name, solo flag, artist name, date of birth, and band flag, and whether they are established. The MemberOf table represents the relationships between artists, with each entry linking a group to an individual artist, and when an artist is deleted, their associated groups are also deleted. The Inventory table tracks the quantity of albums stored at each location, and when an album or location is deleted, the corresponding inventory entry is also deleted. Finally, the SongFeatureList table links songs to artists, and when a song or artist is deleted, the corresponding link is also deleted.
