The schemabench_0186 database is designed to manage information about suppliers, customers, employees, products, and purchases. The Supplier table stores the names and contact phone numbers of suppliers, with each supplier's name serving as a unique identifier. The Phones table contains details about the products available, including their product codes, makes, models, stock quantities, and retail prices. The Customer table holds information about customers, with each customer's ID serving as a unique identifier. The Employee table stores details about the employees, including their IDs, names, and positions. The Purchase table tracks purchases made by customers, with each purchase linked to a specific customer, product, and employee, and includes the purchase date and number. The Supply table records the supplies received from suppliers, with each entry linked to a specific supplier and product, and includes the supply price, quantity, and date. The Contract table stores details about contracts related to purchases, with each contract linked to a specific purchase and including the contract term and price. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, ensuring that a supplier can only supply products that exist in the Phones table, a customer can only make purchases that are recorded in the Purchase table, an employee can only be associated with a purchase that is recorded in the Purchase table, a supplier can only supply products that exist in the Phones table, and a purchase can only have one contract.
