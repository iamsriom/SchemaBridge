The schemabench_0187 database is designed to manage information about vehicles, dealerships, customers, and sales agents. The database consists of nine tables: Region, Distributor, Dealership, Vehicle, Manager, Sales_Agent, Customer, Insurance, and Deal. The Region table stores information about geographic regions, including a unique identifier, ZIP code, and name. The Distributor table contains details about distributors, with a unique identifier and name. The Dealership table represents individual dealerships, linking them to their respective distributors and regions, and includes information such as dealership name and ID. The Vehicle table stores data about vehicles, including make, model, body type, model year, price, and the dealership and distributor they belong to. The Manager table holds information about dealership managers, including their salary, bonus, and the dealership and distributor they manage. The Sales_Agent table represents sales agents, linking them to their managers, dealerships, and including details such as their salary. The Customer table stores customer information, including their first and last names, phone number, email, and the sales agent they work with. The Insurance table contains details about insurance policies, including policy type and renewal date. Finally, the Deal table represents sales transactions, linking vehicles, customers, sales agents, and insurance policies, and includes the date of the deal. Throughout the database, relationships between tables are established through foreign keys, ensuring data consistency and integrity, with constraints such as primary keys, composite keys, unique constraints, and referential integrity rules like CASCADE and RESTRICT applied to maintain data accuracy and prevent inconsistencies.
