The schemabench_0188 database is designed to manage various aspects of a hospital's operations. It contains eight tables: DEPARTMENTS, EMPLOYEES, SCHEDULES, HOSPITAL_RESOURCES, PATIENTS, CASES, HANDLES, and HOUSED_UNDER. The DEPARTMENTS table stores information about the hospital's departments, including their IDs, available rooms, locations, number of employees, and budgets. Each employee is represented in the EMPLOYEES table, which includes their first name, middle initial, last name, position, address, salary, and department ID, with a composite primary key combining their ID and department ID to ensure each employee is uniquely associated with a department. The SCHEDULES table tracks the shifts worked by employees, including the start and end times, employee ID, and date, with a primary key combining the employee ID and shift ID to prevent duplicate schedules for the same employee. The HOSPITAL_RESOURCES table lists the hospital's supplies, including their names, types, counts, and department IDs, with a primary key combining the supply name and department ID to ensure each supply is uniquely associated with a department. The PATIENTS table stores patient information, including their first name, middle initial, last name, social security number, patient ID, date of birth, address, and insurance details, with a primary key combining the social security number and patient ID to uniquely identify each patient. The CASES table tracks patient cases, including case IDs, room numbers, admitting physicians, prescriptions, patient IDs, admission and discharge dates, with a primary key combining the case ID and patient ID to prevent duplicate cases for the same patient. The HANDLES table links employees to cases they are handling, with a primary key combining the employee ID and case ID to ensure each employee is uniquely associated with a case. Finally, the HOUSED_UNDER table tracks patients who are being housed under a specific department for a particular case, with a primary key combining the patient ID, department ID, and case ID to ensure each patient is uniquely associated with a department and case.
