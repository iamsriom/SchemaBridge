The schemabench_0189 database is designed to store information about music data, including acoustic features, albums, artists, and songs. The acoustic_features table contains detailed information about the acoustic properties of songs, such as duration, key, mode, time signature, and various metrics like acousticness, danceability, and energy. The albums table stores information about music albums, including their names, billboard rankings, artists involved, and other metadata like popularity and total tracks. The album_chart table tracks the performance of albums on a chart, with columns for rank score, peak position, weeks on the chart, and the week the album reached its peak. The album_pop table provides additional information about the popularity of albums over time, including year-end scores and whether they were considered popular. The artists table contains information about individual artists, including their names, followers, popularity, and genres, while the artist_chart table tracks the performance of artists on a chart, similar to the album_chart table. The artist_pop table provides year-end scores and popularity information for artists. The releases table links artists to their albums, with columns for the artist ID, album ID, release date, and release date precision. The songs table stores information about individual songs, including their names, billboard rankings, artists involved, and other metadata like popularity and explicit content. The song_chart table tracks the performance of songs on a chart, with columns for rank score, peak position, weeks on the chart, and the week the song reached its peak. The song_pop table provides year-end scores and popularity information for songs. The tracks table links songs to their corresponding albums, with columns for the song ID, album ID, track number, release date, and release date precision. The database maintains relationships between these tables through foreign keys, ensuring that data is consistent and accurate, and indexes are used to improve query performance.
