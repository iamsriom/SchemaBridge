The schemabench_0191 database is designed to manage the operations of a restaurant, including customer information, staff details, bookings, orders, and menu items. The Customers table stores customer data, with each customer assigned a unique Customer ID, which is the primary key, and must be provided along with their full name and phone number. The Staff table contains information about the restaurant staff, including their ID, name, role, salary, address, contact number, and email. The Bookings table records all the bookings made by customers, with each booking assigned a unique ID, date, customer ID, table number, number of persons, and staff ID. The Orders table stores the orders placed by customers, with each order assigned a unique ID, date, total cost, and a reference to the corresponding booking ID. The Orders Delivery Status table tracks the delivery status of each order, with each entry linked to a specific order ID. The Menu table lists all the items available on the menu, with each item assigned a unique ID, name, category, cuisine, and price. The Orders Details table contains the details of each order, including the order ID, item ID, and quantity, with each entry linked to a specific order and menu item. The database maintains referential integrity through foreign keys, ensuring that orders are linked to valid bookings and menu items, and that orders are linked to valid orders delivery status.
