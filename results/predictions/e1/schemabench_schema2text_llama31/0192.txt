The schemabench_0192 database is designed to manage a bookstore's inventory and customer information. The database contains 21 tables that work together to store data about authors, books, publishers, customers, orders, and more. The author table holds information about the writers behind the books, including their name, a brief summary, and a picture. The book table stores details about each book, such as its product ID, name, description, price, and ISBN number. The publisher table contains information about the companies that publish the books, including their name, address, email, and phone number. The storeuser table holds customer data, including their username, first and last name, email, address, and login history. The role table defines the different roles within the bookstore, such as administrator or customer. The warehouse table stores information about the physical storage locations, including their name, address, phone number, and square footage. The storeorder table tracks customer orders, including the order number, status, billing and shipping information, and the time the order was placed. The cart table stores the items currently in a customer's shopping cart, while the cart_coupon and cart_product tables link customers to coupons and products they have added to their cart. The order_coupon and order_product tables store the coupons and products that have been applied to a customer's order. The publishes table links books to their publishers, while the stored_in table tracks the stock levels of each product in each warehouse. The user_role table assigns roles to customers, and the writes table links authors to the books they have written.
