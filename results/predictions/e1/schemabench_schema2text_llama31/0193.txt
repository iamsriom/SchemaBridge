The schemabench_0193 database is designed to manage information about books, their authors, themes, qualities, customers, and their interactions with the books. The database contains ten tables: titles, authors, themes, qualities, cities, customers, prices, orders, ratings, and viewings. The titles table stores book information with a unique identifier, isbn, and a title. The authors, themes, and qualities tables each contain a list of books with their respective authors, themes, and qualities, with each combination of book and attribute being unique. The cities table holds a list of cities with their names and countries, while the customers table stores customer information, including their first and last names, date of birth, sex, email, password, and the city they reside in. The prices table contains the prices of the books, linked to their isbn. The orders table records the purchases made by customers, including the book isbn, customer id, quantity, purchase date, and cost. The ratings and viewings tables store the ratings and viewing history of customers for each book, respectively. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, ensuring that a book can only be associated with one author, theme, quality, price, and a customer can only purchase, rate, or view a book once. The database also enforces referential integrity by cascading updates and deletes, meaning that if a book is deleted, all associated data in the other tables will be automatically updated or deleted accordingly.
