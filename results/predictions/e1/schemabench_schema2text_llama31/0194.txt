The schemabench_0194 database is designed to manage sales data and customer information. The sales table holds records of individual orders, with each entry containing a unique customer identifier, the date the order was made, and the product being purchased. The customer_id column in the sales table is a one-character string, while the order_date is a date and the product_id is an integer. The menu table stores information about the products available for sale, including a unique product identifier, the product name, and its price, all of which are represented as a five-character string, an integer, and an integer respectively. The members table contains details about customers who have joined the system, with each entry featuring a unique customer identifier and the date they joined. The customer_id column in the members table is a one-character string and the join_date is a date. The customer_id columns in both the sales and members tables are linked, indicating that a customer can have multiple orders and a single join date. The product_id column in the sales table references the product_id in the menu table, showing which product was purchased in each order.
