The schemabench_0196 database is designed to manage customer plans and their associated subscriptions. The plans table stores information about the available plans, with each plan having a unique identifier (plan_id), a name that is up to 13 characters long, and a price that is a decimal value with up to 5 digits before the decimal point and 2 digits after. The plan_id is the primary key, meaning it uniquely identifies each plan and cannot be null. The subscriptions table, on the other hand, contains details about the plans that customers have subscribed to, including the customer's identifier (customer_id) and the plan they are subscribed to (plan_id), as well as the start date of their subscription. The customer_id and plan_id in the subscriptions table are foreign keys that reference the plan_id and customer_id in the plans and subscriptions tables, respectively, ensuring that a customer can only subscribe to existing plans and each plan can only be subscribed to by existing customers. The customer_id in the subscriptions table is also the primary key, uniquely identifying each subscription and cannot be null. The plan_id in the subscriptions table is a foreign key that references the plan_id in the plans table, with an ON DELETE RESTRICT rule, meaning that if a plan is deleted, no new subscriptions can be added to it, and any existing subscriptions to that plan will remain.
