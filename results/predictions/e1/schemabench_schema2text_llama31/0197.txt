The schemabench_0197 database is designed to track customer transactions across different regions and nodes. The regions table stores information about the various geographic regions, with each region identified by a unique region_id and having a corresponding region_name with a maximum length of 9 characters. The customer_nodes table contains details about the nodes associated with each customer, including the customer's unique customer_id, the region they belong to (identified by the region_id), the node_id, and the start and end dates of their association with the node. The customer_transactions table holds records of all transactions made by customers, with each transaction identified by a unique customer_id, the date the transaction was made (txn_date), the type of transaction (txn_type), and the amount of the transaction (txn_amount). The customer_id in the customer_nodes and customer_transactions tables references the customer_id in the customer_nodes table, establishing a relationship between a customer's node association and their transactions, and the region_id in the customer_nodes table references the region_id in the regions table, linking a customer's node to their associated region.
