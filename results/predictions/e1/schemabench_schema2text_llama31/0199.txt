The schemabench_0199 database is designed to store information about products and their sales. The product_hierarchy table contains a hierarchical structure of products, with each record representing a product and its parent product, identified by the id and parent_id columns, respectively. The level_text and level_name columns provide additional information about the product's position in the hierarchy. The product_prices table stores the prices of products, with each record linked to a specific product by the product_id column, which is a six-character string. The product_details table holds more comprehensive information about each product, including its product_id, name, category, segment, style, and corresponding names for these categories, segments, and styles. The sales table tracks sales transactions, with each record representing a sale of a product, identified by its product_id, quantity, price, discount, and whether the sale was made to a member or not. The sales table also includes a unique transaction ID and the start time of the transaction. The product_details table's category_id, segment_id, and style_id columns reference the product_hierarchy table, establishing relationships between products and their categories, segments, and styles.
