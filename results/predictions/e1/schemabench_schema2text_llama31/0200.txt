The schemabench_0200 database is designed to store information about various interests and their associated metrics. The interest_map table is used to catalog different types of interests, with each interest represented by a unique id, a descriptive name, a brief summary, and timestamps for when it was created and last modified. The interest_metrics table, on the other hand, tracks the performance of these interests over time, with columns for the month and year of the data, a combined month and year field, an interest id that references the corresponding entry in the interest_map table, and several metrics including composition, index value, ranking, and percentile ranking. The interest_id column in interest_metrics is a foreign key that references the id column in interest_map, establishing a relationship between the two tables, and the data in interest_metrics is organized by month and year, with each month and year combination represented by a unique entry in the _month and _year columns, and a combined month and year field for easier querying.
