The schemabench_0201 database is designed to manage information about authors, their books, customers, and reviews. The authors table stores details about each author, including their first and last name, biography, birth day, nationality, and website. The books table contains information about each book, such as its title, ISBN, genre, price, published date, and the ID of the author who wrote it. The books table also includes a foreign key referencing the authors table, establishing a relationship between authors and their written works. The customers table holds data about each customer, including their first and last name, email, passcode, address, and phone number. The orders table tracks each customer's purchases, with a unique order ID, customer ID, order date, total amount, and status. The orders table also references the customers table to link each order to its corresponding customer. The reviews table stores customer feedback about books, with a unique review ID, the ID of the book being reviewed, the ID of the customer who wrote the review, a rating, and a comment. The reviews table includes foreign keys referencing both the books and customers tables, establishing relationships between customers, books, and their reviews.
