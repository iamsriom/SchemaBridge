The schemabench_0202 database is designed to store information about employees, their departments, positions, and relationships within the organization. The departments table contains details about each department, including its unique identifier, name, manager's identifier, location, and a description. Each department is led by a manager, whose identifier is referenced from the employees table, which stores information about every employee, such as their unique identifier, first and last names, email, phone number, address, birth date, salary, hire date, department identifier, and position identifier. The positions table lists all available positions within the organization, including their unique identifier, name, description, and salary range. The employee_position table serves as a bridge between employees and positions, linking each employee to their current position, while the employee_department table tracks the departments each employee has worked in, including the start and end dates of their tenure. The employee_manager table establishes the reporting relationships between employees, with each entry representing a manager-employee pair.
