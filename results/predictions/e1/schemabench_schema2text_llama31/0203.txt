The schemabench_0203 database is designed to manage information about products, suppliers, customers, and sales. The database contains five tables: category, supplier, product, customer, and sale. The category table stores information about product categories, including a unique category ID, category name, the number of products in the category, and a detailed description. The supplier table holds details about suppliers, such as supplier ID, supplier name, contact person, contact number, and email. The product table contains information about individual products, including a unique product ID, product name, category ID, supplier ID, quantity in stock, unit price, and a detailed description. The product table also references the category and supplier tables, meaning that each product is associated with a specific category and supplier. The customer table stores customer information, including a unique customer ID, first name, last name, phone number, email, and address. The sale table records sales transactions, including a unique sale ID, product ID, customer ID, quantity sold, total amount, and sale date, which defaults to the current date and time. The sale table references the product and customer tables, meaning that each sale is associated with a specific product and customer.
