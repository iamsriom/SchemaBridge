The schemabench_0204 database is designed to manage customer information and their associated financial transactions. The customers table stores details about each customer, including their unique identifier, first and last names, birth date, the date they became a customer, email and phone number, the number of accounts they have, a description of their account, and their address. The accounts table links customers to their individual accounts, which are identified by a unique account ID, account type, balance, consideration, and a foreign key referencing the customer ID in the customers table. The transactions table tracks all transactions made on these accounts, including a unique transaction ID, the account ID associated with the transaction, the type of transaction, its status, the amount, the date of the transaction, and a description. The balances table stores the current balance of each account, identified by a unique balance ID, the account ID it belongs to, the balance amount, the date the balance was recorded, and a description. The database enforces data integrity through primary keys in the customers, accounts, transactions, and balances tables, as well as foreign keys in the accounts and balances tables referencing the customers and accounts tables, respectively, with update and delete rules set to CASCADE, ensuring that when a customer or account is deleted, their associated records in the other tables are also deleted.
