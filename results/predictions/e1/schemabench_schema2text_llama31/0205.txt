The schemabench_0205 database is a comprehensive system for managing information about movies, their genres, directors, actors, and ratings. At its core, the database contains a table for genres, which stores unique identifiers for different types of movies, along with their names and descriptions. The directors table holds information about individual directors, including their first and last names, birth dates, nationalities, websites, and biographies, as well as a foreign key referencing the actors table, indicating that some directors are also actors. The rates table stores ratings for movies, with each rating linked to a specific movie, genre, and director through foreign keys. The movies table contains detailed information about each film, including its title, release date, duration, genre, director, and rating, as well as a timestamp for when the record was created. The actors table stores information about individual actors, including their first and last names, birth dates, nationalities, websites, and biographies, along with a foreign key referencing the directors table, indicating that some actors have also directed movies. The movie_actor table serves as a bridge between the movies and actors tables, linking each movie to the actors who have appeared in it. This schema ensures data integrity through primary keys, unique constraints, and foreign keys, which enforce relationships between tables and prevent inconsistencies, such as a movie being linked to a non-existent genre or director.
