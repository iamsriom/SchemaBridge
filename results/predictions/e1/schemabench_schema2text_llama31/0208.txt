The schemabench_0208 database is a comprehensive system for managing events, including their venues, organizers, participants, and feedback. At its core, the database consists of ten tables, each representing a distinct entity. The venues table stores information about the physical locations where events take place, including their names, addresses, capacities, and descriptions. The organizers table contains details about the individuals or organizations responsible for organizing events, such as their names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, and descriptions. The events table is where event-specific information is stored, including the event name, start and end dates, venue ID, organizer ID, and a description. Each event is linked to a venue through the venue ID, and to an organizer through the organizer ID, ensuring that events are properly associated with their respective venues and organizers. The participants table holds information about individuals who attend events, including their first, middle, and last names, email addresses, phone numbers, and a description. The event_categories table lists the various categories that events can be classified under, such as workshops, conferences, or seminars, while the event_category_mapping table connects events to their respective categories. The event_speakers table stores information about speakers at events, including their names, email addresses, phone numbers, and a description, and is linked to the participants table through the participant ID. The event_speaker_mapping table maps speakers to specific events, allowing for multiple speakers to be associated with a single event. The event_participants_mapping table links participants to events, enabling multiple participants to be associated with a single event. Finally, the event_feedback table captures feedback from participants, including the event ID, participant ID, rating, comment, and feedback date, with the feedback date defaulting to the current time.
