The schemabench_0211 database is designed to store information about companies, their job postings, and the skills required for those jobs. The company_dim table contains details about individual companies, including a unique identifier, name, link to their website, link to their Google page, and a thumbnail image. The skills_dim table stores information about different skills, with each skill having a unique identifier, a description of the skill, and a type. The job_postings_fact table holds data about job postings, including a unique identifier, the company they belong to, the job title, location, and other details such as the job schedule type, whether it can be done from home, and whether it requires a degree or health insurance. This table also includes the date the job was posted, the average salary rate, and the average salary per hour. The company_id in this table references the company_dim table, establishing a relationship between the two. The skills_job_dim table acts as a bridge between job postings and skills, linking each job posting to the skills required for that job, with each combination of job and skill being unique. This table references both the job_postings_fact and skills_dim tables, ensuring that only valid job postings and skills are linked together.
