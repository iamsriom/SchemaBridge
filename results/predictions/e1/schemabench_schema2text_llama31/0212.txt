The schemabench_0212 database is designed to store information about movies, their genres, directors, cast members, and ratings. The movie table contains details about each film, including its unique identifier, title, release year, date of publication, duration, country of origin, worldwide box office earnings, languages spoken, and production company. The genre table links a movie to its respective genre, with each genre being identified by a unique identifier. The director_mapping table connects a movie to its director, referencing the director's unique identifier in the names table, which stores information about individuals, including their name, height, date of birth, and notable movies they've been in. The role_mapping table assigns a cast member to a specific movie, specifying their role, and is linked to both the movie and names tables. The ratings table tracks the average, total, and median ratings of each movie, with each rating being associated with a unique movie identifier. The primary keys in the database ensure that each record in a table is unique, and foreign keys like movie_id in the genre, director_mapping, role_mapping, and ratings tables reference the corresponding movie_id in the movie table, enforcing referential integrity and preventing orphaned records.
