The schemabench_0213 database is designed to manage sales and orders for a company, storing information about products, customers, orders, and shipping details. At its core, the Products table contains a list of products, each identified by a unique ProductID, with a ProductName, Category, and Sub-category. The Orders table links products to specific orders, with each order having a unique OrderID, referencing the ProductID of the product sold, the CustomerID of the buyer, the date of the order, the market where the order was placed, the priority of the order, and the quantity of products ordered. The Sales table stores financial information about each order, including the sales amount, discount, profit, and shipping cost, referencing the OrderID of the corresponding order. The Address table stores the physical addresses of customers, identified by a unique AddressID, with fields for city, state, country, postal code, and region. The Shipping table links orders to their corresponding shipping addresses, with each shipping event identified by an OrderID, referencing the OrderID of the order being shipped and the AddressID of the shipping address. The Sales_ table, however, appears to be a duplicate of the Sales table, with additional fields for price, cost, and quantity, but with foreign keys referencing non-existent tables Location and Time, and a ProductID referencing a Products_ table, which is not present in this schema.
