The schemabench_0214 database is designed to manage the operations of a restaurant, encompassing customer information, staff details, bookings, orders, and menu items. The Customers table stores essential customer data, including a unique Customer ID, full name, and phone number, all of which are required fields. The Staff table contains information about the restaurant's employees, such as their ID, name, role, salary, address, contact number, and email, all of which are mandatory. The Bookings table tracks reservations, with a unique booking ID, date, customer ID, table number, number of persons, and staff ID, referencing the Customers and Staff tables for customer and staff details. The Orders table records orders made by customers, including a unique order ID, table number, order date, total cost, and booking ID, with a composite primary key of order ID and table number, and a foreign key referencing the Bookings table. The Orders_Delivery_Status table tracks the delivery status of orders, with a unique order ID, delivery date, and delivery status, referencing the Orders table for order details. The Menu table lists the restaurant's menu items, with a unique item ID, name, category, cuisine, and price, all of which are required fields. The Orders_Details table links orders to menu items, with an order ID, item ID, and quantity, referencing the Orders and Menu tables for order and item details, and featuring a composite primary key of order ID and item ID.
