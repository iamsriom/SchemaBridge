The schemabench_0215 database is designed to manage information related to airline operations, including passenger details, flight schedules, aircraft information, and luggage handling. The Passenger table stores details about individuals who have booked flights, with each passenger assigned a unique AccountID, which is used to link them to their ticket and luggage information. The Passenger table also includes the passenger's name, age, and frequent flyer ID. The Airport table contains information about the airports served by the airline, with each airport identified by a unique AirportCode, which is used to link airports to flights and aircraft. The Aircraft table stores details about the planes used by the airline, including the AirplaneNumber, make, weight limit, and flight number, which is linked to the Flight table. The FlightCrew table holds information about the airline's employees, including their EmployeeID, name, title, age, and address, with the AirportCode linking them to the airport where they are based. The Flight table contains details about each flight, including the flight number, departure and arrival times, price, airport code, gate number, and the employee ID of the pilot, which links them to the FlightCrew table. The Ticket table stores information about each ticket sold, including the ticket ID, from and to locations, seat number, price, flight status, class, and the AccountID of the passenger who booked the ticket, which links to the Passenger table. The Luggage table stores information about each piece of luggage, including the LuggageID, carousel number, weight, and the AccountID of the passenger who checked the luggage, which links to the Passenger table, as well as the AirplaneNumber, which links to the Aircraft table.
