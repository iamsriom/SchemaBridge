The schemabench_0219 database is designed to manage the operations of a fuel station, encompassing various aspects such as fuel types, suppliers, customers, employees, fuel pumps, fuel tanks, transactions, payments, tank refills, and maintenance records. The Fuel_type table stores information about the different types of fuel available, including a unique identifier, the type name, and the supplier ID. The Supplier table contains details about the fuel suppliers, including their ID, name, contact number, email, and address. The Fuel_pump table represents the fuel pumps at the station, with attributes such as pump ID, location, capacity, fuel type, status, and the ID of the fuel type it dispenses. The Customer table stores customer information, including their ID, name, contact number, email, and address. The Employee table contains details about the station's employees, including their ID, name, contact number, email, and position. The Fuel_Tank table represents the fuel tanks at the station, with attributes such as tank ID, location, capacity, fuel type, and the current level of fuel. The Transaction table stores records of fuel transactions, including the transaction ID, date and time, fuel amount, payment method, customer ID, pump ID, fuel type ID, and the employee ID of the attendant. The Payment table contains information about the payments made, including the payment ID, amount, payment method, date and time, and the transaction ID it relates to. The Tank_Refill table stores records of fuel refills, including the refill ID, date and time, refill amount, tank ID, supplier ID, and fuel type ID. The Maintenance_Record table stores maintenance records for the fuel pumps, including the maintenance ID, date and time, description, cost, pump ID, employee ID, and supplier ID. Each table has a primary key to uniquely identify each record, and foreign keys are used to establish relationships between tables, such as the Fuel_type ID in the Fuel_pump and Tank_Refill tables referencing the Fuel_type table, and the Supplier_ID in the Fuel_type and Tank_Refill tables referencing the Supplier table. Additionally, some tables have unique constraints to prevent duplicate values, and indexes are likely created to improve query performance.
