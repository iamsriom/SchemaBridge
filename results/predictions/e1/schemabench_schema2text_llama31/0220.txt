The schemabench_0220 database is designed to manage information about hotels, their rooms, guests, and various facilities and services they offer. The Hotel table stores details about each hotel, including its location, name, and rating. Each hotel has a unique ID, which is used as a foreign key in other tables to link them to the hotel. The Room table contains information about each room, such as its number, price, view, and availability, and is linked to the Hotel table through the Hotel_ID foreign key. The Reservation table stores details about each reservation, including the board type, start date, duration, paid amount, and number of children, and is linked to the Reservee and Room tables through foreign keys. The Reservee table stores information about each guest, including their ID, and is linked to the Booking_Agency table through a foreign key. The Booking_Agency table stores details about each booking agency, including its name, location, and discount, and is linked to the Reservee table. The Activities table stores information about each activity, including its name, date, and price, and is linked to the Offers table through a foreign key. The Offers table stores details about each offer, including the hotel and activity involved, and is linked to the Hotel and Activities tables. The Guest table stores information about each guest, including their SSN, name, date of birth, and reservation ID, and is linked to the Reservee and Reservation tables through foreign keys. The Participates_in table stores details about each guest's participation in activities, including their SSN and activity ID, and is linked to the Guest and Activities tables. The Department table stores information about each department, including its ID, name, and hotel ID, and is linked to the Hotel table through a foreign key. The Employee table stores details about each employee, including their SSN, name, management SSN, department ID, hotel ID, and salary, and is linked to the Department and Hotel tables through foreign keys. The Gym table stores information about each gym facility, including its ID, hotel ID, name, and working hours, and is linked to the Hotel table through a foreign key. The Equipment table stores details about each piece of equipment, including its facility ID and equipment type, and is linked to the Gym table. The Restaurant table stores information about each restaurant, including its ID, hotel ID, name, rating, price range, and food type, and is linked to the Hotel table through a foreign key.
