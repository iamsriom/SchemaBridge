The schemabench_0221 database is designed to store information about customers and their interactions with restaurants. The Customer_Ratings table holds ratings given by customers to restaurants, with each rating consisting of an overall rating and separate ratings for food and service. The Customer_Details table contains demographic information about each customer, including their city, state, country, and age, as well as their smoking and drinking habits, transportation method, marital status, and occupation, along with their budget. The Customer_Preference table stores the preferred cuisine of each customer, linked to their Consumer_ID. The Restaurants table holds details about each restaurant, including its name, location, and amenities such as alcohol service and smoking policy, as well as its price range and parking options. The restaurant_cuisines table links each restaurant to the cuisines it offers, with each restaurant able to serve multiple cuisines. The Consumer_ID in the Customer_Ratings and Customer_Preference tables references the Consumer_ID in the Customer_Details table, establishing a relationship between a customer's ratings and preferences and their demographic information. The Restaurant_ID in the Customer_Ratings and restaurant_cuisines tables references the Restaurant_ID in the Restaurants table, linking customer ratings to the restaurants they were given to, and restaurant offerings to the restaurants themselves. Each table has a primary key, with Consumer_ID in Customer_Ratings, Customer_Details, and Customer_Preference, and Restaurant_ID in Restaurants and restaurant_cuisines, ensuring that each customer and restaurant is uniquely identified.
