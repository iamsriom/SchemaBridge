The schemabench_0222 database is designed to manage various aspects of an organization, including artist information, employee details, customer data, product inventory, and financial transactions. The database contains 17 tables, each representing a specific entity or relationship within the organization. The ArtistInfoT table stores information about individual artists, including their name, date of birth, address, email, and phone number. The EventT table holds details about events, such as the event name, description, start and end dates, and start and end times. EmployeeInfoT stores employee information, including their name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, start date, end date, salary, and job title. CustomerInfoT contains customer data, including their name, date of birth, phone number, email, address, and zip code. ProductInfoT is a simple table that stores product names. The CustomerInterestT table links customers to products they are interested in, referencing the CustomerInfoT and ProductInfoT tables. InventoryArtT stores information about artwork, including the item name, artist ID, creation date, move-in date, and move-out date, referencing the ArtistInfoT table. PartnerOrgT represents partner organizations, including their name, address, email, phone number, start date, and type, with a check constraint to ensure the type is either 'janitorial', 'catering', or 'waste disposal'. The DisplayArtT table links artwork to display locations, referencing the InventoryArtT table, and has a check constraint to ensure the display location is either 'H1', 'H2', or 'H3'. The LedgerT table stores financial transactions, including the transaction amount and timestamp. The CreditT and DebitT tables record credit and debit transactions, referencing the LedgerT table. The CollabT table stores collaboration information, including the collaboration name, description, start date, and end date. The ArtistContractT table represents artist contracts, including the contract ID, artist ID, sign date, start date, end date, and commission percentage, referencing the ArtistInfoT table. The SalesT table links sales transactions to customers, items, salesmen, and artists, referencing the LedgerT, InventoryArtT, CustomerInfoT, EmployeeInfoT, and ArtistInfoT tables. The artistcontract_auditT and partnerOrg_auditT tables store audit logs for artist contracts and partner organizations, respectively, including the action taken, timestamp, and relevant details.
