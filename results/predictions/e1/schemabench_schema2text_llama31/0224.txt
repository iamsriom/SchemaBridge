The schemabench_0224 database is designed to store information about employees and their work history within a company. The database contains six main tables: departments, employees, dept_manager, salaries, dept_emp, and titles. The departments table holds information about the different departments within the company, including a unique identifier for each department and its corresponding name. The employees table stores details about each employee, such as their employee number, birth date, first and last name, gender, and hire date. The dept_manager table links employees to the departments they manage, specifying the start and end dates of their management tenure, and it references the employees and departments tables to ensure data consistency. The salaries table contains information about the salaries of each employee, including the amount, start and end dates, and it references the employees table to link it to the corresponding employee. The dept_emp table stores the departments each employee has worked in, along with the start and end dates of their employment, and it references both the employees and departments tables to ensure data integrity. Finally, the titles table lists the job titles held by each employee, including the title, start and end dates, and it references the employees table to link it to the corresponding employee.
