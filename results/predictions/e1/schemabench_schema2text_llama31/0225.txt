The schemabench_0225 database is designed to manage various aspects of a training organization, including its employees, clients, courses, hardware, software, and vendors. The database contains 23 tables, each representing a distinct entity within the organization. The trainer table stores information about the employees who conduct training sessions, including their personal details, employment history, and contact information. The hardware table lists the equipment used for training, such as computers and laptops, while the software table catalogues the software programs used in the training sessions. The classroom table links the hardware to the physical classrooms where the training takes place. The client table stores information about the individuals or organizations that participate in the training, including their contact details and initial dates of participation. The membership table outlines the different types of membership available, including their durations and prices, while the membership_purchase table tracks the purchases made by clients. The author table lists the authors of course materials, and the course_book table stores information about the books used in the training sessions. The author_book_listing table links authors to the books they have written, and the course table outlines the details of each course, including its length, fee, and prerequisites. The qualification table records the qualifications earned by employees, and the course_prerequisites table lists the prerequisites for each course. The course_software table links courses to the software required for them, and the class table schedules the training sessions, including the dates, times, and trainers. The enrollment_request table tracks the requests made by clients to enroll in courses, and the vendor table lists the suppliers of course materials. The ordert table records the orders placed by the organization, and the shipment table tracks the shipments of course materials. The order_line and shipment_line tables detail the items ordered and shipped, respectively, and the course_book_vendor table links course books to their suppliers.
