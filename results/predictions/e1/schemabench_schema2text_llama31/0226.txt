The schemabench_0226 database is designed to manage a social media platform where users can share photos, videos, and posts, interact with each other, and track their activities. The database consists of 13 tables that work together to store user information, media content, and relationships between users and their posts. The users table stores information about each user, including their unique username, profile photo URL, bio, and email address. The photos and videos tables store the URLs and sizes of the media content uploaded by users, while the post table links users to their posts, which can be either photos or videos, and includes additional details such as captions and locations. The comments table stores comments made by users on posts, and the post_likes and comment_likes tables track the likes on posts and comments, respectively. The follows table stores the relationships between users who follow each other, and the hashtag_follow table stores the relationships between users and the hashtags they follow. The post_tags table links posts to the hashtags they are associated with, and the bookmarks table stores the posts that users have bookmarked. The login table tracks the login activities of users, including their IP addresses and login times. The database also includes various constraints to ensure data integrity, such as primary keys, unique constraints, foreign keys, and check constraints, to prevent data inconsistencies and ensure that relationships between tables are maintained correctly.
