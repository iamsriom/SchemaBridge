The schemabench_0227 database is designed to manage information about airlines, airports, flights, and various related entities. The Airline table stores details about different airlines, including their names, headquarters locations, and logos. Each airport is represented in the Airport table, which includes its abbreviation, name, location, and radio operator, with the airport abbreviation serving as a foreign key referencing the AirportAbbreviation table. The AirportAirline table links airports with airlines, specifying the location where the airline operates from the airport. The Amenity table lists various amenities, such as food and beverage services, with a unique amenity name and code. The FlightCrew table stores information about the crew members, including their IDs and crew sizes. The FlightSchedule table contains details about flight schedules, including the flight number, airline name, departure and arrival times, crew ID, and origin and destination airports, with foreign keys referencing the Airline, FlightCrew, and Airport tables. The Model table lists different airplane models, including their names, manufacturers, and passenger capacities, with a composite primary key of model name and manufacturer. The Airplane table stores information about individual airplanes, including their tail numbers, airline names, model names, manufacturers, and nicknames, with foreign keys referencing the Airline and Model tables. The Role table lists different roles, such as pilot or flight attendant, and the CrewMember table stores information about individual crew members, including their FAA numbers, names, sexes, and roles, with a foreign key referencing the Role table. The FlightCrew_CrewMember table links crew members with their respective flight crews, specifying their roles. The Status table lists different statuses, such as on-time or delayed, and the FlightInstance table stores information about individual flight instances, including their IDs, flight numbers, tail numbers, statuses, departure dates and times, arrival times, and registered passenger numbers, with foreign keys referencing the FlightSchedule, Airplane, and Status tables. The ChargableInstance, AmenityChargableInstance, DomesticInstance, InternationalInstance, and LocalInstance tables are used to track various types of instances, such as chargeable instances, amenity instances, domestic instances, international instances, and local instances, with foreign keys referencing the FlightInstance table. The IncidentReport table stores information about incident reports, including incident IDs, flight instance IDs, reporter and reported FAA numbers, incident types, report times, and descriptions, with a foreign key referencing the FlightInstance table. The PersonnelReport table links incident reports with their respective sentiments, reported FAA numbers, and primary keys referencing the IncidentReport table.
