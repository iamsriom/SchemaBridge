The schemabench_0231 database is designed to store information about sports teams, players, and matches. The database contains seven tables: cities, stadiums, teams, players, managers, matches, and goals. The cities table stores the names of cities and their corresponding countries, with each city name serving as a unique identifier. The stadiums table holds information about sports stadiums, including their names, capacities, and the cities in which they are located, with the stadium ID as the primary key and the city referencing the city name in the cities table. The teams table stores details about sports teams, including their names, countries, and the stadiums in which they play, with the team ID as the primary key and the stadium referencing the stadium ID in the stadiums table. The players table contains information about individual players, including their first and last names, nationalities, dates of birth, team affiliations, jersey numbers, positions, heights, weights, and preferred feet, with the player ID as the primary key and the team referencing the team ID in the teams table. The managers table stores details about team managers, including their first and last names, nationalities, dates of birth, and team affiliations, with the manager ID as the primary key and the team referencing the team ID in the teams table. The matches table holds information about sports matches, including the season, date and time, home and away teams, stadiums, scores, penalty shootouts, and attendances, with the match ID as the primary key and the home and away teams referencing the team ID in the teams table, and the stadium referencing the stadium ID in the stadiums table. The goals table stores details about goals scored in matches, including the match ID, player who scored, duration of the goal, assisting player, and goal description, with the goal ID as the primary key and the match, player, and assisting player referencing the corresponding IDs in the matches, players, and players tables, respectively.
