The schemabench_0232 database is a comprehensive system for managing various types of data related to oceanic signals. At its core, it contains three fundamental tables: channel_name, channel_type, and ocean_region, each representing a distinct entity with a unique identifier and a descriptive name. The channel_name table stores information about the names of channels, while the channel_type table categorizes these channels into different types, and the ocean_region table lists various ocean regions. The signalling_unit table is the central hub of the database, linking these entities together as it stores data about individual signalling units, including their time stamps, channel names, ocean regions, and various other attributes such as burst frequency and timing offsets. This table also references other tables, including stage and su_type, which represent additional entities that are crucial to the signalling process, with stage referring to the different stages involved and su_type representing the types of signalling units. The signalling_unit table also references the channel_name, ocean_region, channel_type, and su_type tables, establishing relationships between these entities and ensuring data consistency through foreign key constraints, which dictate that when a signalling unit is deleted or updated, the corresponding records in the referenced tables are either updated or restricted from being deleted.
