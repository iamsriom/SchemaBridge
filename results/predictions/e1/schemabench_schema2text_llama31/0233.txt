The schemabench_0233 database is designed to manage and track various incidents, personnel, vehicles, equipment, victims, tasks, and medical records. The Incident table stores information about each incident, including its unique identifier, type, location, description, and date. The Personnel table contains details about individuals involved in the incidents, such as their ID, name, contact information, designation, and shift. The Vehicle table holds information about the vehicles used, including their ID, type, model, and availability status. The Equipment table tracks the equipment used in the incidents, with details such as its ID, type, quantity, condition, and description. The Victim table stores information about the individuals affected by the incidents, including their unique CNIC, name, and age. The Task table manages tasks associated with the incidents, including their ID, type, and completion status. The MedicalRecord table stores medical records related to the incidents, with details about the treatment and diagnosis provided. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys: IncidentID in Incident references TaskID in Task; PersonnelID in Personnel references TaskID in Task; VehicleID in Vehicle references TaskID in Task; EquipmentID in Equipment references TaskID in Task; VictimCNIC in Victim references IncidentID in Incident; and RecordID in MedicalRecord references TaskID in Task.
