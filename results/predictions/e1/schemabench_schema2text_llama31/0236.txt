The schemabench_0236 database is designed to manage a simple e-commerce system, storing information about products, customers, orders, and customer reviews. The database contains five tables: categories, products, customers, orders, and reviews. The categories table stores a list of product categories, with each category identified by a unique id and named with a descriptive title. The products table holds details about individual products, including their name, description, price, and the category they belong to, which is linked to the categories table through the category_id field. The customers table stores information about each customer, such as their name, address, email, and phone number. The orders table tracks customer purchases, with each order identified by a unique id, including the date it was made, the customer who made it, the amount spent, its status, and the product purchased, which is linked to the products table through the product_id field. The reviews table stores customer feedback on products, with each review identified by a unique id, including the customer who made the review, the product being reviewed, a rating out of some unspecified scale, a brief comment, and the date the review was made, which is linked to both the customers and products tables through the customer_id and product_id fields, respectively.
