The schemabench_0237 database is designed to manage a library's collection of books, authors, genres, patrons, and their interactions. The genres table stores information about the different categories of books, with each genre identified by a unique id and described by a name. The authors table contains details about the individuals who wrote the books, including their id, name, birthday, and nationality. The books table links authors and genres to specific books, with each book having a unique id, title, isbn, and references to the id of its author and genre. The patrons table stores information about individuals who borrow books, including their id, name, email, and phone number. The reservations table tracks the borrowing history of patrons, with each reservation identified by a unique id, referencing the id of the borrowed book and patron, along with the date of the reservation and the patron's email. The relationships between these tables are established through foreign keys, with the author_id and genre_id in the books table referencing the id in the authors and genres tables, and the book_id and patron_id in the reservations table referencing the id in the books and patrons tables, ensuring that a book can only be borrowed by a patron who has made a reservation for it and that a patron can only make a reservation for a book that exists in the collection.
