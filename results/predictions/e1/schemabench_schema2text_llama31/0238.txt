The schemabench_0238 database is designed to manage customer information and their financial activities. It contains seven tables: customers, accounts, transactions, transfers, cards, loans, and investments. The customers table stores basic customer details, including their first and last names, phone number, and address, with each customer uniquely identified by a unique ID. The accounts table links customers to their bank accounts, which can be either checking or savings, and includes the account number, balance, and a foreign key referencing the customer ID to establish the relationship. The transactions table records all financial transactions, including withdrawals and deposits, with each transaction linked to a specific account through its ID, and includes the time of the transaction and the amount. The transfers table manages money transfers between accounts, referencing both the source and destination account IDs, and the time of the transfer. The cards table stores information about customers' credit or debit cards, including the card type, customer ID, card number, and spending limit, with the customer ID linking the card to the corresponding customer. The loans table tracks customer loans, including the loan amount, interest rate, term, and status, with the customer ID linking the loan to the borrower. Finally, the investments table records customers' investments in mutual funds or stocks, including the investment amount, type, date, and current value, with the customer ID linking the investment to the investor.
