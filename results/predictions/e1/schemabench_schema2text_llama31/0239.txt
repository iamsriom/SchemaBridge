The schemabench_0239 database is designed to manage information about a university's departments, students, instructors, courses, and enrollments. The major table stores the names of available majors, with each major assigned a unique identifier. The departments table contains information about the various departments within the university, including their names and the identifier of the chairperson, which is linked to the major table through the chairperson's id. The students table holds details about each student, such as their first and last names, email, phone number, address, date of birth, and the major they are enrolled in, which is referenced by the major's id. The courses table lists the available courses, including their names, department identifiers, and credit values, with the department identifier linking to the departments table. The instructors table stores information about the instructors, including their first and last names, email, and department identifier, which is also linked to the departments table. The enrollment table tracks which students are enrolled in which courses, with the course and student identifiers referencing the courses and students tables, respectively, allowing for the management of student enrollment across different terms.
